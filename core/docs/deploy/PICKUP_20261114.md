The deploy itself: core (the migration runs in the normal migrate step; nothing changes for shoppers while the switch is
off) AND both storefront builds (the checkout, cart, product page and confirmation changes ship with them; with pickup

For EACH shop (Watchizer, then Brand Fashion):

1. **Payments screen → "Pay at the store"** (الدفع في الفرع): it is seeded DISABLED with that shop's cash-on-delivery

2. **Pick up in store screen** (Settings → الاستلام من الفرع): check each store's name, address, opening hours, phone,
   WhatsApp and map link, in Arabic and English, and the terms (hold 48 h, one 24 h extension, ready 1–2 / 4–7

3. **Switch the feature on** on that screen (it refuses while an active store has no opening hours).
4. **Place one test pickup order** on the shop's live site (pay at the store), check the confirmation page and the
   e-mail, then cancel it from the dashboard (the stock comes back).

## Decisions (Michael, 2026-11-14)

1. Payment: online now, or pay at the store (a payment method capped like cash on delivery).
2. Hold: 48 hours from "ready"; one 24-hour extension by the team on request, not self-service.
3. Every product may be picked up — our stock (express) and sourced items (market); the team brings the item to the
   chosen store and only then marks it ready. The customer is promised an honest ready window: express 1–2 business
   days, market 4–7 (both editable per shop on the dashboard).
4. No minimum; pay-at-store capped like cash on delivery; at most 2 open pay-at-store reservations per phone.
5. At pickup: order number + 4-digit pickup code; the team checks the phone's last digits; no ID card.
6. No branch-only dashboard accounts in this version.
7. Both stores for both shops; each shop's own number as the stores' phone and WhatsApp; Arkadia's map link per shop.
8. E-mail + the dashboard's WhatsApp button; no SMS.
9. A free-shipping code on a pickup order is refused with a clear message (and not spent).
10. A pickup order has NO address (no invented one); the readers of `orders.address_id` handle NULL.

### Part 2 — the checkout path

- `POST /api/v2/{shop}/orders` with `fulfilment=pickup` + `pickup_store_id`: no address, no delivery fee; refusals

- A free-shipping code on a pickup is refused with its own sentence and not spent; money codes apply.

### Part 3 — the lifecycle

- Order page, a "Pick up in store" panel: store, code, payment (collect at the store / paid online), the promised
  window, reserved / ready / collect-by / extended / collected with who; buttons **Ready for pickup** (only once the
  order is `processing`, i.e. paid or pay-at-store), **Collected** (asks for the 4-digit code; the order becomes
  `delivered`, then `completed` as usual), **Extend** (once, the shop's extension hours), **WhatsApp the customer**
  (wa.me with a ready-made Arabic + English message, sent by a person), **Print the pickup slip**. Cancel is the normal
  cancel button. All three steps take the fulfilment ability (as the status buttons); scoped grants get 404 outside
  their shop. A pickup order is never offered "shipped", and the server refuses it.
- E-mails: "Ready for pickup" and "Held for you longer" (new kind `pickup_update`); the status e-mail says "being
  prepared for pickup", "You collected your order from …", and for an expired reservation "not collected in time …
  released" with an "Order again" link.
- `orders:expire-pickups`, every five minutes: a READY **pay-at-the-store** pickup past its collect-by time is claimed
  (ready → expired) and its order cancelled through the one cancel door (stock back through the ledger, the e-mail
  above). Claimed per row: two runs never cancel or release twice. Every other cancel path (dashboard, unpaid card
  expiry, a card payment that never started) closes the reservation; a late-paid card order that is reinstated is
  reserved again.
- Order list: filters "Delivery or pickup", "Store", "Pickup status" (reserved, late to prepare, ready, expires today,
  past collect-by time, collected, expired) — the per-store view; a "Pickup · Arkadia Mall" badge, red when past
  collect-by, amber when late to prepare. The CSV export gains the column.

**Decision D-P3-1 (confirmed by Michael, 2026-11-14):** a reservation PAID ONLINE is NOT cancelled by the expiry job. Cancelling it
means a refund somebody has to make, and a job cannot make it; the order page and the list show it as "past collect-by
time" with a note to contact the customer (extend, or cancel and refund). Alternative: cancel it too and rely on the
team to refund from the cancelled list — cheaper to run, but a customer who paid would get "released" with no money
back until someone notices.

**Decision D-P3-2 (approved by Michael, 2026-11-14):** "late to prepare" = still reserved after the slowest promised business day, counted as the
promised days plus two calendar days per five (no holiday calendar). It only flags; nothing is cancelled before ready.

### Fix — the dashboard's order page on phones

Every order page was wider than a phone (677 px on a 390 px screen) in BOTH languages — in English it simply opened on
the left edge. Cause: the grids of cards (Order/Customer/Shipping, Payment attempts/Stock movements) let a card grow to
its table's full width, because a grid item's minimum width is its content's. `[&>*]:min-w-0` on the two grids lets the
tables scroll inside their own boxes with the existing edge fade (the Table component's own design). Measured after:
360 of 360, 390 of 390, 1440 of 1440 — delivery, ready pickup and collected pickup, AR and EN. Screens:
checkpoints\2026-11-14_pickup\screens-orderpage.
