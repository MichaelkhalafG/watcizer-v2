## 1. Brand and category page texts

### 1.1 What existed (measured first)

- A brand or category page had no words of its own: the meta description was the template "Browse N … at Watchizer —
  luxury watches and accessories …" (`listingSeo.js`), and nothing was printed above or below the grid.
- `catalog_brand_translations` and `storefront_category_translations` are CLEAN tables, rebuilt from the legacy
  database on switch night (`CoreChecksumCommand::CLEAN_TABLES`). A text written into them would be wiped.

### 1.4 Decisions taken alone — RECOMMENDATIONS, each reversible

- **D-S1 own table keyed by slug/path**, not columns on the brand/category tables — those are rebuilt on switch night.
  Cost: a brand slug or category path renamed in the legacy data orphans its text (the dashboard then shows the page
  as "No text yet" and the old row stays unused).
- **D-S2 the introduction under the grid**, not above it: the products stay the first thing on the page and the text
  is still in the HTML. To move it above: one line in `src/lib/facetListing.jsx` (both apps).
- **D-S3 the dashboard wins over the file**: `seo:seed-texts` never overwrites without `--overwrite`.
- **D-S4 the ability is the banners'** (`manage-legacy-content`), storefront-scoped.
- ~~**D-S5 Brand Fashion's FAQ payment answer says what its checkout offers today**~~ — **REVERTED 2026-10-30 by
  Michael's decision:** the FAQ answer and the draft article `shopping-at-brand-fashion` now say "All payment methods
  and instalment programmes are available" / «كل طرق الدفع وبرامج التقسيط متاحة». (What was measured stays true: on
  2026-10-30 storefront 2 had one payment provider, `cod`.)
- **D-S6 the first request of a category page costs one query more: 7 → 8** (`QueryBudgetTest`). The page text is
  one SELECT on its unique key, only for a page that is one category or one brand, cached per page under the
  storefront's version (cache family `page_text`, wrapped so a page with no text is cached too); the second request
  of the same page measured 6. The budget of the unfiltered listing and the search stays 7. Found by the final gate,
  not before it. The alternative — folding the texts into the cached category tree — would push the cold meta
  (measured 20, budget 20) over its own budget. If you would rather keep 7 everywhere, that is the change to make.
