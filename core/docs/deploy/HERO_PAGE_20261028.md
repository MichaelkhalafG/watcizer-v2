### 3.2 Explode readiness

Every part the brief names is a separate node with the exporter's own disassembly plan (`part.step`, `explodeOffsetMM`,
`internalOffsetMM`), positions in parent space in mm — pivots are the part's own origin, which is right for translation
explodes. Steps as the file has them: 0 case + lugs (stay) · 1 bracelet, end links, spring bars, 14 links with their pins
fanning out, clasp (body, engraving plate, two blades, two release buttons, three hinge pins) · 2 caseback + four notches ·
3 caseback gasket · 4 movement holder · 5 the 31 movement parts, each with an internal offset that spreads them ·
6 dial base + dial face · 7 three sub-dial faces + rings, 12 hour indices, date frame · 8 hour, minute, chrono-seconds
hands, pivot cap, three sub-dial hands · 10 crystal, crystal gasket, tachymeter ring · 11 bezel · 12 crown, stem, two
pusher caps + tubes + shafts. Nothing is missing for the brief's seven steps; the page groups them as bracelet &
clasp (1), caseback (2–4), movement (5), crystal & bezel (10–11), dial & sub-dials (6–7), hands (8), crown & pushers (12).
Not edited: the model's steps are read at load (`watchModel.js`) and the order is the page's (`explode.js`).

Two observations reported, not fixed: the dial texture is stored mirrored (it reads correctly once mapped — checked in
the renders); the movement is labelled illustrative (D1).

### 3.3 Optimisation (gltf-transform 4.5.1 in a scratch folder, not a project dependency)

Audit: `@gltf-transform/{cli,core,functions,extensions}` 4.5.1, MIT, one maintainer (Don McCurdy, the three.js glTF
maintainer), published 2026-09-28; codecs `draco3dgltf` 1.5.9 (Apache-2.0, Google) and `meshoptimizer` 1.3.0 (MIT);
`sharp` 0.35 for textures. Installed with `npm install` into the session scratchpad `gltf/`; the project's package.json
and lockfile are untouched. KTX2 was not possible: the `ktx` CLI (KTX-Software) is not installed and installing it is a
system change — textures went to **WebP** (`EXT_texture_webp`, decoded natively by three's GLTFLoader).

Steps, all appearance-preserving: drop `extras.baseMat` (keep `extras.part`, `extras.note`) → `dedup` + `weld`
(exact duplicates only) + `prune` → textures WebP, longest side 2048, quality 90 → geometry compression.

| file | raw | gzip | **brotli (the wire)** | drawn triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| original | 41,530,780 | — | — | 94,367 |
| extras.baseMat dropped | 10,918,932 | 3,579,732 | 2,716,569 | 94,367 |
| + welded | 4,277,380 | 2,811,596 | 2,549,147 | 94,367 (164 unique meshes, 273 drawn) |
| + WebP 2048 | 2,475,448 | 1,156,654 | 921,891 | 94,367 |
| + **Draco** (edgebreaker) | 1,063,236 | 810,400 | 798,245 | **94,305** (125 of 183,356 rendered lost to quantisation) |
| + **Meshopt** (medium) — **chosen, desktop** | 1,199,296 | 838,572 | **815,506** | 94,367 |
| **phone**: WebP 1024 + Meshopt | 880,848 | 526,073 | **503,173** | 94,367 |

the model and ~230 KB smaller in total, with no third-party host and no lost triangles. Both are under the 3 MB target.
