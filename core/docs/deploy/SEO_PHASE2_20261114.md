# SEO Phase 2 — landing pages that match how people search (2026-11-14, unattended run)

Nothing here is live: it goes out with the combined deploy (`SEO_PHASE1_20261114.md`, "The combined deploy"), whose
script gains this phase's migration and copy step (listed at the end).

## What changed

### Landing pages (the brief's item 1) — core + both shops

- **Which exist** (`App\Domain\Seo\LandingPages`): per shop, brand × gender (`/brand/tommy-hilfiger/men`), brand ×
  family (`/brand/guess/bags`) and brand × family × gender (`/brand/guess/bags/women`), where the page holds **6 or more
  LIVE products** search engines are shown (visible, active, not deleted, not hidden by SearchExposure; out of stock
  counts — the STOCK RULE) AND **narrows its parent** (a brand whose products are all men's has no `/men` page: it would
  be the brand page twice). Families: watches, bags, wallets, perfumes. Built from three grouped queries per shop,
  cached under the build lock with the last good copy (cache family `landing_pages`), built with the sitemaps by
  `catalog:warm`. On the copy: **Watchizer 47 landing pages, Brand Fashion 93**.
- **Which are indexable**: only once the page's own copy (page text type `landing`, keyed `brand/family/gender`) has
  **passed the quality gate in both languages**. Until then a landing page is served with `noindex, follow` and is
  left out of the sitemaps; once it passes, both follow on their own (the sitemaps list it, the page drops noindex).
  A page that falls under 6 LIVE products leaves the list and goes back to noindex — automatically, both ways.
- **v2's listing** takes a `family` filter (validated in the controller); for a brand + one gender and/or one family it
  answers `meta.landing` {key, exists, indexable} and the page's text as `meta.context`; on a brand page it answers
  `meta.landings` (the brand's landing pages) so the shop can link them.
- **The shops** (both; twins `src/lib/landing.js`, the routes `app/(main)/brand/[brand]/[a]/page.jsx` and
  `…/[a]/[b]/page.jsx`): each landing page has its own H1, title ("Guess Women's Bags in Egypt | Brand Fashion" /
  «حقائب جيس للنساء في مصر | براند فاشون»), self-canonical (`/brand/guess/bags/women`, a case variant redirects to it),
  hreflang, a three-level BreadcrumbList (Home › Guess › Guess Women's Bags), noindex until core says it passed; a
  segment that is not a family or a gender is a 404. The brand page shows **real links to its landing pages** ("Shop by:
  Bags · Men · Wallets · Women's wallets · Watches · Men's watches").
- Checked locally on production builds of both shops: brand pages indexable with their landing links; landing pages 200,
  their H1/title/canonical/breadcrumbs in both languages, `noindex, follow`; `/brand/tommy-hilfiger/shoes` 404;
  `/brand/Tommy-Hilfiger/Men` → 301 to `/brand/tommy-hilfiger/men`.

### Editable copy (item 2) — core + both shops + the dashboard

- Page texts (brand, category, landing) gain an **intro** (60–90 words, shown ABOVE the products, under the H1 —
  `FacetLead`, a twin) beside the existing guide under them (`FacetIntro`: sections, the catalogue's "in numbers"
  section, the questions as FAQPage data), and the **quality gate's verdict** per language (`gate_status`,
  `gate_report`, `gate_checked_at`). A saved text loses its verdict and is checked again. Migration
  `2026_11_14_000006_page_text_intro_and_gate`.
- **Dashboard → Page texts**: a third tab, "Landing pages", listing the shop's landing pages with their product
  counts; every page's form has the intro field per language; a badge says "Passed the check" or "Held — see the
  reasons", and a held language shows the gate's reasons.

### The quality gate (built here for Phase 2's copy; Phase 4's articles use the same) — core

`App\Domain\Content\QualityGate` with `config/content_gate.php` (rules, banned phrases, forbidden statements, the
business facts, the approved source list). Per language: the lengths and structure (intro 60–90 words; guide 800–1,200;
4+ sections; 4–6 questions with 40–80-word answers; Arabic minimums 85 %); banned filler phrases (EN and AR, the
brief's list and more); forbidden statements (human review, authorized dealer/agent, the free-shipping threshold); the
business facts (a return window other than 14 days, a delivery time other than 24 hours, a warranty over 3 years fail);
the catalogue (every model number named is a LIVE product of the shop; every price named lies within the page's LIVE
price range); every internal link resolves to a page the shop has, external links only to approved sources; an external
fact (a year other than 2013) needs a Sources section citing an approved source; every section carries a concrete
detail (a number, a link, a named line or model, a proper name other than the brand's); English and Arabic name the
same model numbers and amounts; no more than 25 % of a text's 5-word phrasing repeats any other text (page texts of
both shops and articles). The generated "in numbers" section is the catalogue's own and is not re-checked.

- `php artisan seo:gate [--storefront=] [--type=] [--key=] [--dry-run]` — scheduled hourly (`:25`).
- `php artisan seo:load-copy [--apply] [--only=]` — loads written copy from
  `database/seeders/content/seo/phase2/<shop>-<type>-<key>.<lang>.md` (front matter `intro:` / `meta:`), fills
  `{facts}` and `{shop}`, saves (logged), runs the gate, records the verdict.

- **Not yet written**: the other brand pages of the list (Watchizer 20, Brand Fashion 28) and the landing pages'

  before these rules) are held by the gate (they are short and the two shops' texts share 88–91 % of their phrasing),
  which changes nothing on those pages: a brand or category page's indexing does not depend on the gate.
