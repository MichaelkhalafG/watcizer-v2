# SEO Phase 1, group 1 — record (2026-11-14): Brand Fashion's sitemap 404s and its broken internal links

Baseline: `new branding/docs/seo/PHASE1_BASELINE_2026-11-13.md` (private). Crawler: `scripts/seo/crawl.mjs`.

## 1. What was wrong (measured)

1. **Brand Fashion's sitemaps named product pages that do not exist.** Live, 2026-11-13: 76 of 160 sampled product
   URLs answered 404 (~4,000 of 8,415 per language). Cause: core built every shop's sitemap with WATCHIZER's product
   route — the listing index's English-title slug (`CompatLocaleSitemap`) — while Brand Fashion's product page looks
   a product up by its own `storefront_product.slug` (v2 `products/{slug}`). Where the two differ (the old WooCommerce
   slugs the import kept, e.g. `tommy-cross-bag-black` vs the title slug `tommy-cross-bag-black-menbag005`), the
   sitemap named a 404. Reproduced on the production copy (`scratch_seo_20261114` = `scratch_bf_f_20261112`, Brand
   Fashion's domain set to production's, visibility as production's rule): **3,781 of 9,273** product URLs dead, and
   **256 visible products not listed at all** (the index lists products that share a title once).
2. **Every sitemap request rebuilt the whole sitemap** — 1.2–4 s per language over the growing catalogue, uncached
   (the 2026-11-12 lesson).
3. **Brand Fashion's product page linked pages that do not exist:**
   - the breadcrumb and the "See all" of the Similar rail linked a THIRD-level category (`/subtypes/shoulder-bag`,
     404 — only the first two levels have pages): 320 products on the copy (3%);
   - its BreadcrumbList named every sub-category as `/category/{parent}/{child}` (404, a one-segment route): 104 of
     244 middle crumbs in the crawl; and the Arabic page named the English addresses.
4. **`/product-category`** (bare, linked from an imported article) answered 404: the redirect map has rows for the old
   categories, none for the old index itself.
5. **A home banner links `/category/perfumes`** (404). Data, not code — see §5.

## 2. What changed

**core** (ships with the archive below):
- `CompatLocaleSitemap`: a storefront other than the default (Watchizer) lists every product its product page answers
  — `ProductCards::visibleOn`, the SAME rule as v2's product page — under its own slug, each once, in id order; two
  queries (products, then covers), no sub-select per row. Watchizer keeps the title slug: **byte-identical** output.
  `urlset()` is cached per storefront and language (`compat_locale_sitemap`). **A request never rebuilds a sitemap that
  has a last good copy** (the 2026-11-12 lesson: anything heavy runs from the CLI) — it serves that copy; only a shop
  whose sitemap was never built builds it in a request, once, under the build lock. `warm()` builds a cold one.
- `ProductCards::visibleOn()` — the visibility rule, moved out of `base()` so the sitemap asks it instead of copying it.
- `catalog:warm` (cron, every 5 minutes) builds each active shop's sitemaps when they are not cached
  (`CatalogWarmer::warmSitemaps`); it prints `storefront N: sitemaps ready (en … ms, ar … ms)`.
- `BlogWriter` REBUILDS the shop's sitemaps right after an article is saved, published or deleted (an admin's save or
  the publishing job pays the 1–2 s, never a shopper's or crawler's request), so a new article is listed at once as before;
  a failure is logged and never fails the save.
- `StorefrontCache::INVALIDATION_MAP`: the new family. A `compat_` family, so a campaign starting does not re-key it.

**Brand Fashion app** (ships with its next build):
- `src/lib/categoryHref.js` (new): a node's page only for the first two levels; `nearestCategoryPage()` for "See all".
- `BfProductPage.jsx`: a third-level crumb is text, not a link; "See all" goes to the nearest page.
- `app/(main)/product/[slug]/page.jsx`: the BreadcrumbList lists only levels that have a page, at their address in the
  page's language.
- `middleware.js`: a bare old base (`/product-category`, `/brand-category`, `/gender-category`, `/collection`,
  `/product-tag`, with or without the slash) → one 301 to `/listing` (`/ar/listing` for `?lang=ar`).
