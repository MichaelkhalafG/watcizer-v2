## Who makes Michael Kors watches, and why that shapes the price

Michael Kors is an American fashion house founded in New York by the designer of the same name, and its watches are made under licence by Fossil Group, the American company behind several fashion watch brands. That arrangement explains most of what you see in the price. The watches use reliable quartz movements, stainless steel cases and a consistent design language, and they are priced as fashion pieces rather than as Swiss mechanical watches.

It also explains why prices are broadly similar between established sellers. The cost of a Michael Kors watch in Egypt is set largely by its distribution, its import costs and the exchange rate, so two reputable shops rarely differ by a great deal on the same reference. When they do, the difference usually comes from what is included: the length of the warranty, who gives it, the delivery terms and the return policy.

## Three-hand or chronograph: the first price step

The biggest single step in the price comes from the function. A three-hand watch shows hours, minutes and seconds, sometimes with a date; a chronograph adds a stopwatch with three sub-dials and two pushers beside the crown. The chronograph movement has more parts, and the dial needs more printing and more hands, so the same case costs more as a chronograph.

That does not make the chronograph the better buy. A three-hand watch is easier to read, slimmer under a shirt cuff and simpler to look after. ![Michael Kors Runway Slim with a gold dial on a gold stainless steel mesh bracelet](/Uploads_Images/Product_image/1793016073_2026-10-26_6adf4109dafae.webp)
*The Runway Slim: a three-hand watch on a gold mesh bracelet.*

If you never plan to use the stopwatch, a three-hand model gives you the same brand, the same steel case and the same warranty for less. If you like the busy, sporty look of the sub-dials, the step up is worth it, but buy it for the look rather than for the function you will rarely use.

On the women's side the Parker and the Bradshaw are the chronographs; on the men's side most models are. A good way to compare is to open two product pages with the same metal and size, one three-hand and one chronograph, and look at the difference in the price shown on each.

## Crystals, ceramic, two-tone and mesh: the second price step

After the function, the finish decides the price. Four finishes appear again and again in the Michael Kors range at Watchizer, and each adds to the cost of a plain silver steel watch.

Crystals are the most visible. A full ring of crystals around the dial, as on the Darci, or a crystal-set bezel, as on the Parker, adds material and setting work. Ceramic, which appears on a white women's chronograph, is harder and more scratch-resistant than steel. Two-tone bracelets, silver with gold or rose gold, need extra finishing. Mesh bracelets, a fine woven band found on several Runway Slim and Darci models, often cost a little more than links.

None of these is a matter of quality alone; they are matters of taste. A plain silver steel watch is just as well made as a two-tone one. Choose the finish you will enjoy wearing every day, and you will spend your budget where it matters to you.

### Size matters less than you think

Case size has a smaller effect on price than most people expect. A 36 mm women's watch and a 39 mm one from the same line often sit close together, and the same is true of men's watches from 42 to 45 mm. What changes with size is how the watch wears on the wrist, not what it costs.

That is good news for buyers: you can choose the size that suits your wrist without paying more for it. On a slim wrist, below about 15 cm for women or 17 cm for men, a smaller case looks better balanced; on a larger wrist, the bigger cases sit comfortably. ## Why prices differ between shops, and when they change

Even with prices set by distribution, you will see differences between sellers. The honest reasons are few. One shop may include a longer warranty or a warranty from the brand's agent rather than its own; one may include delivery while another adds it at checkout; one may be clearing an older model at a lower price. Ask what is included before you compare the numbers.

The dishonest reasons are more dangerous. A Michael Kors watch offered at a fraction of the usual range, by a seller with no address, no invoice and no returns, is very rarely an original. Our blog guide on how to tell an original Tommy Hilfiger watch explains the checks that apply to any fashion watch: the model number on the case back, the finish of the dial and clasp, a working chronograph, and a seller you can find again.

Michael Kors prices in Egypt are not fixed for ever. They move with new shipments, with the exchange rate and with the end of a model's life, when the last pieces of an older reference may be offered at a lower price.
