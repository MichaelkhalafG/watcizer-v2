## Why Tommy Hilfiger works well as a first branded watch

The brand was founded in New York in 1985, and its watches carry the same clean American styling as its clothing: navy, red and white accents, a flag logo on the dial, and cases that look substantial without being flashy. That combination makes Tommy Hilfiger a natural first branded watch. It looks like a serious watch at a wedding or an interview, yet it is priced well below the Swiss brands.

The second reason is choice. The two looks most people picture, a blue dial on a steel bracelet and a black chronograph, both exist in several versions, and the catalogue is wide enough that two brothers can each buy one without owning the same watch. The blocks in this guide are live, so the models you see are the ones in our shop today, available ones first.

The third reason is practical. Quartz movements need little care, keep accurate time, and run for years on a battery, which suits someone who wants to wear a watch every day without winding it or sending it for servicing. For a first branded watch that simplicity matters more than a mechanical movement would.

## Getting the size right for your wrist

Size is where most returns start, so we spend time on it with every customer who asks. Most of the Tommy Hilfiger watches in our catalogue have a 44 mm case, which is the brand's standard men's size. The sport lines are mostly 46 mm, and a smaller group of dress and chronograph models sits at 40 to 43 mm.

A simple test helps. Measure your wrist with a strip of paper and a ruler just below the wrist bone. Under about 16 cm, a 40 to 42 mm case will look balanced and a 46 mm case will overhang. Between 16 and 18 cm, the standard 44 mm sits well. Over 18 cm, the 46 mm Bank and Decker cases look proportionate rather than oversized. If you fall between two sizes, take the smaller one if you dress formally most days and the larger one if you mostly wear casual clothes, because a big case needs a loose cuff to look right.

Case thickness matters too. The Kane and Daniel cases are 10 to 11 mm thick, which slides under a shirt cuff. The TH85 chronographs are about 12 mm, still comfortable for daily wear but noticeably taller.

## Bracelet, leather or silicone: choosing the strap

The strap changes how a watch feels more than any other detail, and in Egypt the climate makes the decision sharper than it is in Europe.

A stainless steel bracelet is what most of our Tommy Hilfiger watches come on, and it is the strap we recommend for everyday wear in Cairo. It does not absorb sweat, it can be rinsed, and it looks right with formal and casual clothes. The one thing to know is that a bracelet usually needs links removed to fit; within the warranty, we size bracelets for free at both our stores, so this is a five-minute job rather than a cost.

Leather straps, which appear on roughly a quarter of the Tommy Hilfiger watches we stock, feel warmer and more classic. They are lovely in winter and with a suit, but leather darkens and wears quickly with daily summer sweat. If you choose leather, take the watch off before the gym and keep it away from perfume sprayed directly on the wrist.

Rubber and silicone straps, found mostly on the Bank and Decker sport lines, such as the Bank 1791722 with its red silicone strap, are the practical choice for the beach in Sahel, for the gym, and for anyone who works outdoors. They are light, they dry quickly, and the bright red, blue and white versions are where Tommy Hilfiger's colour identity shows most.

Colour of the bracelet is worth a thought as well. Silver steel is the most versatile; black-coated steel, as on the Daniel, looks modern but shows scratches sooner; gold-tone and two-tone bracelets make a statement and suit evening wear.

## Looking after a Tommy Hilfiger watch in Egyptian weather

Heat, dust and humidity are harder on a watch than most people expect, and a few habits keep a Tommy Hilfiger looking new for years.

**Wipe it after a hot day.** Sweat contains salt, and in July and August it collects between bracelet links and under the case. Once a week, wipe a steel bracelet with a soft cloth and a little lukewarm water, then dry it. A soft toothbrush reaches between the links. Do not use alcohol or perfume to clean it.

**Rinse it after the sea.** If you swim in the Mediterranean or the Red Sea with a 50-metre model, rinse it under fresh water afterwards, with the crown pushed in, and let it dry before putting it back on. Salt left to dry on a silicone strap or steel bracelet leaves white marks and wears the seals faster.

**Keep it away from heat and magnets.** A car dashboard in a Cairo summer can reach temperatures that age a battery and harden a strap. Phone speakers, magnetic clasps on bags and laptop covers can disturb a quartz movement for a while. A drawer or the box it came in is the right place for a watch you are not wearing.

**Let the strap breathe.** A leather strap worn tight in summer stays damp. Wear it a notch looser in hot weather, and alternate it with a steel or silicone watch if you wear one daily.

**Have the seals checked when the battery is changed.** Water resistance depends on rubber gaskets that age. When we replace a battery at our stores, we close the case properly so the seal sits as it should. If the glass ever fogs from inside, bring the watch in rather than waiting.

None of this takes more than a minute, and it is the difference between a watch that looks tired after one summer and one that still looks new after three.

## What the specifications on our product pages mean

Every Tommy Hilfiger product page in our shop lists a few specifications, and understanding them saves surprises after delivery.

**Movement.** Almost every Tommy Hilfiger men's watch we list is quartz; only a handful are automatic. Quartz means a battery-powered movement that is accurate to a few seconds a month and needs no winding. When the battery runs down after a few years, the second hand often starts jumping in four-second steps, which is the sign to replace it. Battery replacement is free at our stores within the warranty.

**Water resistance.** Most of these watches are rated to 50 metres, often written as 5 ATM. Despite the number, that is not a diving rating. In practice it means the watch handles rain, hand washing and splashes, and a short swim, but it should not be worn for diving, and its crown and buttons should never be pressed under water. A handful of models are rated to 30 metres, which means splashes only; the rating is shown on each page.

**Glass.** Most models use mineral glass, which resists everyday knocks well but can scratch against stone or metal. Keep that in mind if you work with your hands.

**Display.** Every Tommy Hilfiger men's watch we stock is analogue. If you want a digital display, look at other brands in our shop rather than this one.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Tommy Hilfiger watches waterproof?

Most of the Tommy Hilfiger watches we stock are rated to 50 metres, which makes them water resistant rather than waterproof. You can wash your hands, walk in the rain and take a short swim while wearing one, but do not dive with it and never press the crown or chronograph buttons under water. A few models are rated to 30 metres, splashes only.

### How long does the battery in a Tommy Hilfiger watch last?

A quartz Tommy Hilfiger battery usually lasts two to three years, and chronographs used often can drain it sooner. When the second hand starts jumping in four-second steps, the battery is low. Within the warranty we replace batteries free of charge at our Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis stores, usually while you wait.

### Which Tommy Hilfiger watch size is best for men?

For most adult men, 44 mm is the safest size, and it is the size of most models we carry. A wrist under about 16 cm suits a 40 to 42 mm case better, while a wrist over 18 cm can wear the 46 mm Bank and Decker models comfortably. Measure below the wrist bone with a strip of paper if you are unsure.

### Can I exchange a Tommy Hilfiger watch if I choose the wrong one?

Yes. You can return or exchange any watch within 14 days of delivery, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging with its tags, and we pay the return shipping. Message our customer service on WhatsApp with your order number and we will arrange the exchange or the refund, which is made within 24 hours of the watch reaching us.

### Do you resize the bracelet of a Tommy Hilfiger watch?

Yes. Steel bracelets usually need one or more links removed to fit, and within the warranty we size them for free at both our stores. Bring the watch with its invoice and the job takes a few minutes. If you order online and live outside Cairo, a local watchmaker can also remove links using the pins already in the bracelet.

### Can I pay for a Tommy Hilfiger watch by card?

Yes. You can pay online by card when you order, or in cash when the watch arrives. Cash on delivery lets you open the parcel and check the model number, the case and the bracelet in front of the courier before you pay, and if you refuse the watch after inspecting it, you pay no delivery fee. Both ways, the warranty is the same and is stated on the product page.
