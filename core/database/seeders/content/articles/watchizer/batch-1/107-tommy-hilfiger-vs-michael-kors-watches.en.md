## Two American brands, two different ideas

Both names come from New York fashion. Michael Kors was founded in 1981 by the designer of the same name, and Tommy Hilfiger in 1985. Their watches are made under licence by two of the large American watch companies: Michael Kors watches by Fossil Group, Tommy Hilfiger watches by Movado Group. That is why both are solid quartz watches in stainless steel, priced as fashion pieces rather than as Swiss mechanical watches.

The ideas behind them are different, though. Tommy Hilfiger carries the brand's preppy, sporty clothing style onto the wrist: clean dials, the flag logo, navy and red details, and many models on silicone or rubber straps. Michael Kors carries its handbags' polished, slightly glamorous look: gold and rose gold, crystal bezels, mother-of-pearl dials and bracelets that read as jewellery.

Neither idea is better. They suit different people, different wardrobes and different occasions.

You can see the difference in the details. A Tommy Hilfiger dial usually keeps things simple, often with a touch of red or navy and a small logo. A Michael Kors dial is more likely to shine: a sunray finish, applied markers, crystals at the hours or around the bezel, and a polished bracelet that catches the light. Hold one of each and the difference in mood is obvious before you notice a single specification.

## For men: which brand?

For most men, Tommy Hilfiger offers the wider choice. Its men's range covers three-hand dress watches such as the Kane and the Daniel, chronographs such as the TH85, and sporty models such as the Bank and the Decker on silicone straps. The typical case is 44 mm, the dials are easy to read, and the look works from the office to the weekend.

Michael Kors men's watches are fewer and more focused. Nearly all are chronographs, from 42 to 45 mm, in lines such as the Dylan, the Lexington, the Bayville and the Saunder. They have a bolder, more luxurious feel, often in gold tone, gunmetal or black steel. Choose Michael Kors if you want a statement chronograph; choose Tommy Hilfiger if you want a versatile everyday watch or a sport model.

Think about where the watch will be worn most. For a man who works in an office and spends weekends with friends or at the gym, a Tommy Hilfiger on a steel bracelet, with a silicone-strap model as a second watch, covers the whole week. For a man who dresses up often, for meetings, dinners or weddings, a gold-tone Michael Kors chronograph makes a stronger impression with a suit.

## For women: which brand?

For women, the balance shifts the other way. Michael Kors has the deeper range at Watchizer: the Runway and Runway Slim for every day, the Darci with its ring of crystals for evenings, the Parker and Bradshaw chronographs, the small Mini models and the softer Layton. Gold, rose gold, silver and two-tone are all available, many on steel or mesh bracelets.

Tommy Hilfiger's women's watches are simpler and sportier, with clean dials and the brand's colours, and they suit a woman who prefers a casual look to sparkle. If you want a watch that looks like jewellery, Michael Kors is the natural choice; if you want a relaxed everyday watch that pairs with trainers and denim, look at Tommy Hilfiger.

For a gift, the brand often decides itself once you think about her jewellery. A woman who wears gold rings and bracelets, or who loves a little sparkle, will usually be happier with a Michael Kors in gold or rose gold. A woman who wears little jewellery, dresses casually and is always on the move may prefer the simplicity of a Tommy Hilfiger. If you are unsure, a Michael Kors Runway in her usual metal tone is the safest of all.

For a slim wrist, both brands have an answer, but Michael Kors has more of them. Its Mini models and the Runway Slim are designed to sit neatly on a small wrist and slide under a fitted sleeve, while most Tommy Hilfiger women's watches are a little larger and sportier. If her wrist is slim and she likes a delicate look, start with Michael Kors.

## Size and fit on the wrist

The two brands also wear differently. Tommy Hilfiger men's watches are mostly 44 mm, with 46 mm sport models, which suits an average to large wrist; on a wrist below about 17 cm, look for the 40 to 43 mm models. Michael Kors men's chronographs are similar in size but often look bolder, because of their busy dials and metal finishes.

Michael Kors women's watches are mostly 38 to 39 mm, with smaller cases in the Mini lines for a slim wrist below about 15 cm. A crystal bezel makes a watch look larger than a plain one of the same diameter, so if you like the Darci but have a slim wrist, compare it with a Runway Slim before you decide.

To measure your wrist, wrap a strip of paper just below the wrist bone, mark where it meets, and measure the length with a ruler. Compare it with the case size on the product page: as a rough guide, a men's watch of 40 to 43 mm suits a wrist of 15 to 17 cm, and 44 mm and above suits a larger wrist. Better still, try both brands on in our stores.

## Straps, bracelets and daily wear

Tommy Hilfiger offers more strap variety for daily and sporty wear: steel bracelets, leather and many silicone and rubber straps, which cope well with heat, sweat and the beach. That makes it a practical brand for hot Egyptian summers and for men who wear one watch everywhere.

Michael Kors focuses on bracelets: steel links in gold, rose gold, silver or two-tone, and fine mesh bracelets on several women's models. They look elegant and are easy to keep clean, but they are dressier than a rubber strap. Within the warranty we size steel bracelets of both brands free of charge at our stores.

In the heat of an Egyptian summer, the strap matters more than it seems. Steel and rubber cope well with heat and sweat; leather straps last longer if you keep them for cooler months and evenings. Whichever brand you choose, rinse a rubber or silicone strap after the beach, wipe a steel bracelet with a dry cloth after a hot day, and keep perfume away from leather.

## Which to choose: a quick guide

Choose Tommy Hilfiger if you are a man looking for a first branded watch, if you want a sporty model on a silicone strap, if you like navy and red details, or if you want one watch for the office and the weekend. It is the brand with the widest men's choice in our shop.

Choose Michael Kors if you are buying for a woman who loves gold tones or sparkle, if you want a watch for weddings and evenings, or if you are a man who wants a bold gold or gunmetal chronograph. And if you are buying matching watches for a couple, Michael Kors's women's range and Tommy Hilfiger's men's range can sit happily side by side when they share the same metal tone.

If you are still undecided after all of this, the deciding question is simple: do you want the watch to be noticed, or to be worn without thinking? Michael Kors is designed to be noticed; Tommy Hilfiger is designed to be worn every day. Both are good answers, and the right one is the one that suits your life. If the answer is both, two watches, one from each brand, will serve you better than one that tries to do everything.
