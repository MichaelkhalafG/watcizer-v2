The holiday season in Egypt is long and full of gifts. It starts with Christmas on 25 December, continues with New Year's Eve and New Year's Day, and ends with Coptic Christmas on 7 January, with office parties, family dinners and visits in between. That makes December one of the busiest months for gift shopping, and the time when a little planning saves the most stress. This guide covers what to give in the holiday season, from original watches and ready-made gift sets to perfume, wallets and bags, together with a simple calendar, budget ideas and how to make sure every gift arrives on time. We carry {{count family=watch}} watches and {{count family=wallet}} wallets and card holders at Brand Fashion.

## Key points

- Make your list in the first week of December and order by about 20 December for Christmas and New Year.
- Gift sets, a watch with a bracelet or a belt with a wallet, are the easiest complete holiday gifts.
- A watch suits close family and partners; a wallet, a card holder or a set suits colleagues and wider family.
- When giving perfume, a 100 ml bottle of a fragrance the person already likes is safer than a new scent.
- Keep the invoice with every gift: unused items can be exchanged within 14 days, and we pay the return shipping.

## A holiday gift calendar

Good holiday gifts start with a list. In the first week of December, write down everyone you will give to, from close family to colleagues, and next to each name the occasion, the budget and one idea. This takes 15 minutes, and it stops the last-minute rush that leads to generic gifts and overspending.

Then work backwards from the dates. For Christmas on 25 December and New Year's Eve, aim to order by about 20 December, so there is time for delivery, a possible exchange and wrapping. For Coptic Christmas on 7 January, order by the end of December. We deliver to every governorate within 24 hours, but the days before the holidays are busy for everyone, so a few days of margin make the season calmer.

Finally, think about who will receive the gift and where. For family you will see at a dinner, order to your own address and wrap it yourself. For someone in another city, order directly to their address with a gift note, and tell us when we call to confirm the order, so we can check the delivery day with you.

## Watches for her

A watch is the most personal holiday gift, best kept for a wife, a fiancée, a mother, a daughter or a sister. For the holidays, warm metals feel right: gold tone, rose gold and two-tone watches catch the light at evening gatherings. Michael Kors, Guess and Tommy Hilfiger make many women's watches in these tones, from simple three-hand models to crystal-set dials.

If you do not know her wrist size, a steel bracelet in a medium case of 36 to 39 mm is the safest choice, because the bracelet can be sized afterwards; within the warranty we size steel bracelets free of charge at our stores. Match the metal to the jewellery she wears every day, and you will choose well.

If she already owns a watch she loves, give her a second one in a different mood rather than a copy of the first. A woman who wears a steel watch every day may enjoy a gold-tone or rose gold watch for evenings; one who wears a large chronograph may enjoy a small, elegant three-hand watch for formal dinners. The holiday season, with its many evenings out, is the moment when a dressier second watch gets worn the most.

## Watches for him

For a husband, a father, a brother or a son, a steel chronograph with a blue or black dial is a gift that suits work and weekends alike. Hugo Boss, Tommy Hilfiger and Emporio Armani are good places to start, with many models of 42 to 44 mm on steel bracelets. For a man who already owns a steel watch, a second watch on a leather strap suits the cooler months and the formal dinners of the season.

Think about how he dresses. A man in suits most days will enjoy a thin watch with a clean dial; a man in jeans and jackets will enjoy a chronograph or a watch with a coloured dial. If he wears gold-tone cufflinks or a gold ring, a two-tone or gold-tone watch completes his look; otherwise, steel is the safest choice.

Size is the one detail to get roughly right. For most men a case of 42 to 44 mm looks balanced, while a man with a slim wrist, under about 17 cm, will be more comfortable with 40 to 42 mm. If you can, look at the watch he already wears and note its size from the case back or the product page of the same model; choosing within 2 mm of it is almost always safe.

## Gift sets: complete in one box

Gift sets are the easiest holiday gifts, because the pieces have already been chosen to go together. For women, sets that pair a watch with a matching bracelet, such as the Michael Kors Pyper set, look generous and need no extra wrapping thought. For men, belt and wallet sets from Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger combine two everyday leather pieces in the same colour.

![Calvin Klein black belt and wallet gift set](/Uploads_Images/Product_image/1793873715_2026-11-05_6aec5733a833b.webp)

Sets also make budgeting easier, because one price covers a complete gift. They suit fathers, brothers and colleagues you know well, and they are a safe choice when you are not sure what someone already owns. If he wears black shoes most days, choose a black set; if brown, a brown one; a reversible belt covers both.

Check two details before you buy a belt set. The first is length: belts in sets usually come in one length, and most can be shortened at the buckle end by a leather shop, so choose a set whose belt is long enough rather than too short. The second is the buckle: a polished silver-tone buckle goes with a steel watch, a matt black or gunmetal buckle with a black watch, and a gold-tone buckle with a gold-tone watch.

## Perfume as a gift

Perfume is one of the classic holiday gifts, and it is also one of the most personal, because scent is a matter of taste. The safest perfume gift is a fragrance the person already wears and likes, in a larger bottle. If you want to give something new, choose a fragrance family close to what they wear: fresh and citrus scents for people who like light perfumes, woody and amber scents for those who like warm ones, floral scents for those who like soft and romantic ones.

Concentration matters too. Eau de parfum contains roughly 15 to 20 percent perfume oils and lasts longer on the skin than eau de toilette, which usually contains 5 to 15 percent. A 100 ml bottle of eau de parfum, such as Emporio Armani Stronger With You Absolutely, is a generous gift that lasts through the winter. Our perfume selection is small, so check what is available before you plan a fragrance gift.

If you are giving perfume, a little advice on wearing it makes the gift even better. Two or three sprays on the pulse points, the neck and the inside of the wrists, are enough for an eau de parfum; rubbing the wrists together breaks down the top notes faster, so it is better to let the scent dry on its own. Kept away from heat and sunlight, in its box or a drawer rather than on a bathroom shelf, a bottle keeps its character for years.

## Wallets and small leather goods

Wallets and card holders are the most useful gifts for people you know a little less well: colleagues, uncles, cousins and friends. They are used every day, they need no sizing, and a good leather wallet from a known brand feels like a real gift rather than a token. For men, a bifold with 6 to 10 card slots suits most; for those who pay by card or phone, a slim card holder is more practical.

For women, a zip-around wallet suits a large bag, while a compact wallet or a zip card case suits a small crossbody. Coach and Guess make women's wallets in signature canvas, quilted finishes and colours, which make cheerful holiday gifts. If you know the bag she carries, choose a wallet that matches its brand or its colour.

A wallet is also a good gift to combine with something else. A slim card holder slipped inside a gift bag with a watch, or a zip card case with a shoulder bag, turns a single gift into a small set without doubling the budget, and the second piece is often the one that gets used every day.

## Bags for the season's outings

The holiday season is full of evenings out: dinners, parties, family visits and New Year's celebrations. A small crossbody or a shoulder bag with a chain strap is the bag of the season, because it holds a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick and leaves the hands free. In winter colours, black, burgundy, navy and deep green look festive without being loud, and gold or silver hardware adds a touch of shine.

For a woman who already has an evening bag, a structured tote for work or a weekend bag for winter trips can be a more useful gift. Look at the bag she carries most days: if it is worn, a new bag in a similar size and style is a gift she will use from the first day.

Size the evening bag to what she carries out at night: a crossbody of 18 to 24 cm wide holds a phone, a card holder and keys; one of 25 cm or more also fits a small make-up pouch and a power bank. A detachable chain strap is a bonus, because it lets the same bag be carried on the shoulder, across the body or by hand as a clutch.

## Gifts for the office

Office gifts and gift exchanges are part of the season in many workplaces, and they call for something useful, neutral and within a set budget. A card holder, a slim wallet or a small leather accessory is ideal: personal enough to be appreciated, neutral enough for any colleague. For a manager or a team leader, a group of 4 or 5 colleagues can share the cost of a watch or a belt and wallet set.

Keep office gifts unisex or choose them per person, avoid perfume unless you know the colleague's taste well, and add a short card. A simple line such as "thank you for a great year" makes even a small gift feel thoughtful.

Agree on a budget before the exchange so that nobody feels awkward. ## Wrapping, notes and delivery

Presentation matters as much in the holidays as at any time of year. Keep the brand's box or packaging, add a ribbon in a festive colour and a handwritten card with one sentence about why you chose the gift. Put the invoice in a separate envelope with the gift, so the person can exchange it or use the warranty without having to ask you.

With cash on delivery you can open the parcel in front of the courier and check the gift before you pay, and refuse it with no delivery fee if it is not right. An unused gift in its original packaging with its tags can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping, which takes the risk out of holiday shopping.
