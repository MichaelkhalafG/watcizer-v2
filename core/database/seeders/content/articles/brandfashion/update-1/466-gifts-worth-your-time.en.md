The gifts that are worth the time it takes to choose them are the ones the person will use every day: a watch in the metal of their jewellery, a bag that suits the way they live, a wallet that fits their pocket or their bag, a belt that matches their shoes. Those four gifts also have something else in common: you can choose them well without asking a single question that spoils the surprise, if you know what to look at. We carry {{count family=watch}} watches and {{count family=bag}} bags at Brand Fashion, and this guide shows how to choose each kind of gift for the people on your list.

## Key points

- Look at what the person already wears: their jewellery metal, their bag size and their shoe colour tell you most of what you need.
- Bags, wallets and many belts need no sizing at all, which makes them the safest surprise gifts.
- For a watch, choose a medium case: 36 to 39 mm for a woman, 42 to 44 mm for a man; steel bracelets can be sized afterwards.
- Matching sets, such as a wallet and a belt, make an easy, complete gift.
- Keep the invoice: an unused gift can be exchanged within 14 days, and we pay the return shipping.

## Start by looking, not asking

A good gift begins with two minutes of attention. Look at the jewellery the person wears every day: if their ring and earrings are yellow gold, they will enjoy a gold-tone watch or a bag with gold hardware; if they are silver or white gold, steel and silver tones. Look at their bag: is it a large tote, a shoulder bag or a small crossbody? That tells you how they carry their day.

For a man, look at his wallet when he pays and his belt and shoes when he dresses up. A slim card holder means he likes to travel light; a thick bifold means he carries cash and receipts. Black shoes and a black belt suggest a black wallet and a steel or black watch; brown shoes, warmer leathers and gold or rose gold tones.

Shoes and a watch strap tell you about colour; a phone case and a key ring often tell you about taste. Someone with a plain black phone case and a single key on a leather fob tends to like quiet, classic pieces; someone with a bright case and a charm on every zip will enjoy colour, prints and a visible logo. Write down three things you notice, for example "gold rings, a large black tote, brown shoes", and keep the note on your phone while you shop.

These small observations are worth more than any budget. They turn a generic present into one that looks as if it was chosen for that person, which is exactly what makes a gift worth your time.

## Watches: the gift that is worn every day

A watch is the most personal of these gifts, because it is worn all day and seen every time the person checks the time. For a woman, a classic three-hand watch such as a Michael Kors Runway in her usual metal is a safe and elegant choice; for an occasion such as an engagement, a crystal-set model such as a Darci or a Parker feels special.

![Michael Kors Parker chronograph with a rose gold dial on a stainless steel bracelet](/Uploads_Images/Product_image/1792935813_2026-10-25_6ade0785318cb.webp)
*A Michael Kors Parker in rose gold: crystals and a chronograph for a gift that feels like an occasion.*

For a man, a steel chronograph with a blue or black dial, from brands such as Tommy Hilfiger, Hugo Boss or Emporio Armani, suits the office and the weekend alike. Size is the one thing to get roughly right: choose 36 to 39 mm for a woman and 42 to 44 mm for a man if you do not know the wrist. Within the warranty, we size steel bracelets free of charge at our two Cairo stores, so the fit can be perfected after the gift is given.

Two more details make a watch gift easier. First, the strap: a steel bracelet is the most versatile and the easiest to size, a leather strap is warmer and lighter for someone who has never worn a watch, and a mesh bracelet adjusts to almost any wrist with its sliding clasp. Second, the dial: white, silver and black dials are the safest, blue is a versatile colour for men, and mother-of-pearl or rose gold dials suit an occasion. If you are unsure between two watches, choose the simpler one; it will be worn more often.

## Bags: the gift with no sizing

A bag is the safest surprise gift of all, because it needs no sizing and it is used every day. The key is to choose the right kind of bag for the person's life. For a student, a backpack or a crossbody bag; for a working woman, a structured shoulder bag or a tote that holds a notebook and a water bottle; for someone who goes out often, a small crossbody or a handbag for evenings.

![Tommy Hilfiger beige monogram embossed crossbody bag with gold-tone hardware](/Uploads_Images/Product/1787156503_2026-08-19_6a85d817df809.webp)
*A Tommy Hilfiger beige crossbody with gold-tone hardware: a compact gift for everyday outings.*

Colour is the second choice. Black is the safest and goes with everything; navy, beige and brown are warm, versatile alternatives; bright colours make a statement and suit someone who already wears colour. Match the hardware to her jewellery, gold or silver, and the bag will look like it was always meant for her.

Size is the third choice, and it follows from how she spends her day. A tote of about 30 to 40 cm wide holds a laptop or a notebook for work or university; a shoulder bag of 25 to 30 cm holds a phone, a wallet, keys and make-up; a crossbody of 18 to 24 cm holds the essentials for an evening or a day out. Brands such as Guess, Coach and Michael Kors make the same model in two or three sizes, so once you know the style she likes, you can choose the size that fits her day.

## Wallets: small, useful and personal

A wallet is a gift that is used dozens of times a week, and it is easy to get right if you look at how the person pays. For a man who carries cards and some cash, a bifold with card slots and a note section; for one who travels light, a slim card holder; for one who carries many cards, a larger bifold or a wallet with a coin pocket.

For a woman, a long zip-around wallet suits a tote or a large shoulder bag, while a compact wallet or a card case fits a small crossbody. Choose the wallet with the bag in mind: matching the material and colour of the bag she carries, or the bag you are giving her, makes the gift feel complete.

![Tommy Hilfiger men's reversible belt and leather wallet gift set in black and brown](/Uploads_Images/Product_image/1791643062_2026-10-10_6aca4db67db1d.webp)
*A Tommy Hilfiger reversible belt and wallet set: black on one side, brown on the other, with a wallet to match.*

A good wallet is also a matter of leather and finish. Smooth leather looks classic and softens with use; pebbled leather hides small scratches; coated canvas with a monogram, as on many Coach and Guess wallets, is light and easy to wipe clean. Count the card slots: 4 to 6 suit most people, 8 to 12 suit someone who carries loyalty and bank cards together. A wallet with RFID protection is a thoughtful extra for someone who travels.

## Belts and matching sets

A belt is a practical gift that is often overlooked. The safest choice is black leather with a polished buckle in silver or gold tone, matching the metal of his watch. Belt size matters, so check his trouser size if you can, or choose a style that can be shortened at the buckle end by a leather shop.

Matching sets, such as a wallet and a belt in the same leather and colour, are among the easiest complete gifts, because the pieces already go together. A set also saves you from matching two pieces yourself. If he wears black shoes most days, a black set; if brown, a brown one. A reversible belt, black on one side and brown on the other, solves the question entirely, because it goes with both colours of shoes.

For a woman, a slim belt in black or tan leather with a small gold buckle works with dresses, jeans and coats, and a belt from the same brand as her bag ties an outfit together. Choose 2 to 3 cm wide for dresses and trousers, and 3.5 to 4 cm for jeans.

Set the budget before you start browsing, then choose the best piece within it rather than the cheapest piece of a more expensive kind. A well-made wallet or card holder from a brand such as Tommy Hilfiger or Calvin Klein makes a better gift than an entry-level watch from an unknown name, because it will be used every day and will last for years.

## Gifts by person

For a mother, comfort and elegance: a watch with a clear dial in her jewellery's metal, or a structured shoulder bag in black or brown. For a wife or fiancée, a crystal-set watch or a bag with gold hardware for evenings. For a sister or a friend, a crossbody bag in a colour she loves, or a slim wallet. For a daughter at university, a backpack or a watch with a coloured dial.

For a father, a steel watch with a black dial or a wallet-and-belt set. For a husband, a chronograph in the metal of his wedding band. For a brother or a young man starting work, a crossbody bag and a slim card holder. For a colleague, a quality wallet or a card case is a safe, appreciated gift that does not feel too personal.

For a couple, matching watches in the same metal, one 38 mm and one 42 mm, make a lasting gift for an anniversary. For a teenager, a watch with a coloured dial or a small crossbody bag in a bright colour feels grown-up without being formal. For someone who already has everything, a matching set or a second bag in a colour they do not own yet is a safe way to surprise them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the safest gift if I do not know their size?

A bag or a wallet, because neither needs sizing. If you prefer a watch, choose a medium case, 36 to 39 mm for a woman or 42 to 44 mm for a man; steel bracelets can be sized for free at our stores within the warranty, and an unused gift can be exchanged within 14 days.

### How do I choose the right metal for a gift?

Look at the jewellery the person wears every day. If it is yellow gold, choose a gold-tone watch or a bag with gold hardware; if rose gold, rose gold; if silver or white gold, steel and silver tones. If they wear both, a two-tone watch is a safe and thoughtful choice.
