## Start with the person, not the watch

A good gift starts with a little observation. Look at the jewellery the person wears every day: the ring they never take off, their earrings, the bracelet on their wrist. If it is yellow gold, choose a gold-tone watch; if rose gold, a rose gold one; if silver or white gold, a steel watch in silver. If they wear both, a two-tone model is a safe and thoughtful choice.

Then look at the wrist and the style. A slim wrist suits a smaller case; a woman who loves sparkle will enjoy crystals; a man who wears suits every day needs a watch that slides under a shirt cuff. Two minutes of attention to these details is worth more than any price tag.

It also helps to think about the watch they already wear, if any. A person who wears a watch every day knows what they like, and a second watch should add something new rather than repeat the first: an evening watch next to an everyday one, a bracelet next to a strap, a gold watch next to a silver one. A person who has never worn a watch will be happiest with something light, simple and easy to read, which they can get used to without effort.

## For her: a first Michael Kors

For a woman receiving her first Michael Kors, the Runway is the safest and most elegant choice. Its round steel case, clean dial and link bracelet in gold, rose gold or silver look polished with everything, from office clothes to an evening dress, and the size, usually 38 to 39 mm, suits most wrists.

The Runway Slim is the alternative for a woman who prefers a finer watch: the same idea in a thinner case, often on a mesh bracelet that sits flat on the wrist. Both are easy to read, easy to wear every day and easy to love, which is exactly what a first watch from a brand should be.

If you are choosing between metals and cannot decide, look at her phone case, her bag hardware or the frames of her glasses, which often reveal a preference for gold or silver tones. And if she wears very little jewellery, a two-tone Runway is a safe bet that will work with whatever she adds later.

## For a bride or a big occasion

For an engagement, a wedding or an anniversary, the gift should feel like an occasion. The Darci, with a full ring of crystals around the dial, is made for those moments: it sparkles at every movement and sits beautifully with evening dresses and jewellery sets. A rose gold Darci with a mother-of-pearl dial is especially romantic.

If she prefers something bolder than the Darci, the Parker combines a crystal-set bezel with a chronograph, so it has both sparkle and a sportier face. Match the metal to the jewellery she will wear on the day: if her wedding set is gold, choose gold; if it is rose gold or diamonds set in white gold, choose rose gold or silver.

A bridal gift is often given before the wedding, so the watch should suit the months of engagement as much as the day itself. That is a good reason to choose a crystal-set watch with a clean dial rather than an extremely decorated one: it will look right at the engagement party, the wedding and every evening out afterwards.

## For a young woman or a student

For a young woman, a student or a graduate, colour and comfort matter more than formality. A Runway Slim with a pink, purple, blue or turquoise dial adds personality to everyday clothes and looks fresh on a young wrist. The Camille, with a crystal dial in a gold or rose gold case on a coloured rubber strap, is fun, light and comfortable in the heat.

For a graduation, a watch is a gift that marks the moment and lasts into the first job. A plain Runway in her usual metal tone is the choice that will still suit her in an office in five years' time, while a coloured dial is the one she will enjoy most at university. Think about which of those two lives the gift is for.

Comfort matters a lot for a young woman who will wear the watch all day at university, on public transport and at the gym. A light case, a mesh or rubber strap and a medium size of about 36 to 38 mm are the most comfortable choices. If she is very active, check the water rating on the product page: 50 m (5 ATM) allows a short pool swim, while 30 m means splashes only.

## For a mother

For a mother, comfort and elegance go together. A watch with a clear, easy-to-read dial, in the metal of her jewellery, on a bracelet that is simple to put on and take off, is a gift she will wear every day. A two-tone model is a good choice if she wears both gold and silver, and a leather-strap model such as the Layton offers a softer, more classic look.

For Mother's Day on 21 March, a watch is one of the gifts that she will see and use many times a day, which makes it more personal than most. If she already owns a watch she loves, choose a different style rather than a similar one: a sparkling Darci for evenings next to her everyday watch, for example.

Think about practical details too. A bracelet with a simple fold-over clasp is easier to put on with one hand than a bracelet with a hidden clasp, and a dial with clear hour markers is easier to read without glasses. These small details turn a beautiful watch into one she reaches for every morning.

## For him: the Michael Kors chronographs

Most Michael Kors men's watches are chronographs from 42 to 45 mm, and the line decides the mood. For a man who wears suits, a Lexington in gold tone or a Bayville in gunmetal on a steel bracelet looks polished and confident. For a man who prefers casual clothes, a Dylan on a silicone or rubber strap, in navy, black or with a bright accent, is relaxed and sporty.

Look at his wrist and his shirts before choosing the size. On a wrist below about 17 cm, or for a man who wears fitted shirts every day, a 42 to 43 mm model slides under the cuff more easily; on a larger wrist, 44 to 45 mm sits comfortably. If he wears a gold wedding band, match it with a gold-tone watch.

For a father or an older man, a black dial on a black or silver steel bracelet is a classic, understated choice that suits work and family occasions alike. For a young man starting his first job, a blue-dial chronograph on steel looks smart at the office and relaxed at weekends. For a groom, a gold-tone chronograph matched to his cufflinks on the day is a gift he will associate with the wedding for years.

## For a couple

A pair of watches for a couple, his and hers, is one of the most meaningful gifts for an engagement or an anniversary. The secret is to match the metal, not the model: a women's Runway or Darci in rose gold with a men's chronograph in rose gold or two-tone, or a gold Runway with a gold Lexington. The two watches then look like a pair while suiting each wrist.

![Michael Kors Bayville chronograph with a blue dial on a gunmetal stainless steel bracelet](/Uploads_Images/Product_image/1793113319_2026-10-27_6ae0bce70c2e7.webp)

If you prefer silver, a women's Runway Slim in silver with a men's Bayville in gunmetal or a black steel model makes a modern, understated pair. Whatever the metal, choose the two watches together, side by side, so the tones match exactly.

Pairs can also be built across sizes. A practical approach is a women's watch around 36 to 38 mm and a men's chronograph around 42 to 44 mm, so that each looks right on its own wrist. If one of you prefers sparkle and the other a plain dial, that is no problem: the shared metal is what makes them a pair, not identical dials.

## Getting the size right without asking

The hardest part of a watch gift is the size, because asking spoils the surprise. Three tricks help. First, choose a medium case: 36 to 39 mm for a woman, 42 to 44 mm for a man, which suits most wrists. Second, for a woman, consider a mesh bracelet, which usually adjusts with a sliding clasp and fits a wide range of wrists without removing links.

Third, remember that steel link bracelets can be sized after the gift is given. Within the warranty we size them free of charge at our two Cairo stores, usually while you wait, so the person receiving the watch can bring it in with the invoice and leave with a perfect fit. And if the size or style is not right at all, an unused watch can be exchanged within 14 days.

If you can borrow one of their watches for a moment, or a bracelet they wear, measure its inside length or compare its case with the case sizes on our product pages. Even a rough idea of their wrist, small, average or large, is enough to choose between a 36 mm and a 39 mm case for a woman, or a 42 mm and a 45 mm one for a man.
