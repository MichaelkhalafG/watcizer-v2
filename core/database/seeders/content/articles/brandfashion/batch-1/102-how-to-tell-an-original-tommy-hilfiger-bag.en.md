## Key points

- Look at the logo first: the flag, the TH monogram or the name should be sharp, centred and evenly spaced.
- Inside, an original usually carries a label with a style code; Tommy Hilfiger women's bags use codes that begin with AW0AW, men's with AM0AM.
- Stitching should be straight, even and tight, with no loose threads at the corners or the handles.
- Zips, clips and buckles should feel solid, run smoothly and match the colour of the other hardware.
- Buy where you can inspect the bag before paying and return it within a stated period.

## The logo: flag, monogram and name

Tommy Hilfiger uses three main marks on its bags: the rectangular flag in navy, white and red; the TH monogram, often interlocking; and the brand name in capital letters. On an original, each mark is crisp at the edges, correctly proportioned and placed straight on the bag. A flag whose colours bleed into each other, letters of uneven thickness or a monogram that leans to one side are warning signs.

![Tommy Hilfiger beige monogram embossed crossbody bag with gold-tone hardware](/Uploads_Images/Product/1787156503_2026-08-19_6a85d817df809.webp)
*A Tommy Hilfiger beige crossbody with an embossed monogram and gold-tone hardware: the pattern should line up cleanly.*

On monogram bags, look at the pattern itself. An embossed or printed TH monogram should repeat evenly across the surface, with the same spacing between the letters and the rows. Where two panels meet, at a seam or a flap, the pattern should continue neatly rather than being cut off at an odd angle. Metal logos, such as a TH plaque on the front, should be smooth, evenly finished and firmly attached, with no glue visible around the edges.

Men's bags tend to keep the branding smaller: a small flag on a pocket, an embossed name on the flap or a discreet metal logo near the zip. Small does not mean careless, though. On an original, even a tiny flag has clean, separate colours and straight edges, and an embossed name is pressed evenly into the material rather than smudged.

## The label and the style code

Open the bag and look for the inside label. Original Tommy Hilfiger bags usually carry a small label or tag with the brand name and a style code, and the code follows a clear format: women's bags begin with AW0AW, men's with AM0AM, followed by five digits. You will also often find a care label with the materials and the country of manufacture.

The style code is useful in two ways. First, its format is a quick sign that the label is the brand's own. Second, you can search it online, and the result shows you what that style is supposed to look like: its shape, colour and details. If the code on the label belongs to a completely different bag, or there is no label at all on a bag sold as new, ask the seller to explain before you go further.

Keep the absence of a label in proportion. Labels are sometimes cut out by previous owners, and a bag sold as pre-owned may have lost its tags. On a new bag sold with its tags, though, the label should be there, and it should be stitched in neatly rather than glued or hanging by a thread. Combine the label with the other checks rather than relying on it alone.

## The lining and the inside

The inside of a bag tells you as much as the outside. On an original, the lining is fitted smoothly with no loose wrinkles, the seams inside are neat and finished, and the pockets sit straight. Many Tommy Hilfiger bags have a printed or branded lining, or a branded zip pocket, but a plain lining is also normal on some styles; what matters is that it is well made.

Look into the corners, where poor workmanship hides: a lining that pulls away from the seam, threads hanging loose or glue showing through are signs of a hurried copy. Feel the inside of the base too. A structured bag should keep its shape because it was built that way, not because it was stuffed with paper to look full in the photos.

Count the pockets and compartments too, and compare them with the product description. If the description promises a zip pocket inside and a slip pocket for a phone, they should be there, sewn straight and finished at the edges. Many of our Tommy Hilfiger shoulder bags, for example, have 1 main compartment with a secure zip closure, and the page says so.

## Stitching and edges

Stitching is one of the most reliable checks, because it is hard to fake well. On an original Tommy Hilfiger bag, the stitches are evenly sized and spaced, run in straight lines along the edges and curve smoothly around the corners. The thread colour matches or contrasts on purpose, and there are no double stitches, skipped stitches or loose ends.

Pay special attention to the places that carry weight: where the handles join the bag, where the strap rings are attached and along the base. These should be stitched firmly, often with extra rows. Look at the edges too. Painted or folded edges should be even and smooth, without cracks, bubbles or rough patches. A bag that will carry your phone, wallet and keys every day needs these joins to be strong.

A simple test is to compare the stitching with the close-up photos on the product page or the brand's site. If the photos show 2 neat rows of stitching along the top and the bag in your hands has a single, wandering row, the difference is not a matter of opinion. Good stitching also stays good: after a few weeks of daily use, it should look exactly as it did on the first day.

## Hardware: zips, clips and buckles

Pick up the bag and work every piece of hardware. Zips should run smoothly from end to end without catching, and the pulls should feel solid in your fingers rather than light and thin. Clips, rings and buckles should open and close firmly, and the turn-locks or magnetic clasps should hold.

![Tommy Hilfiger navy monogram embossed crossbody bag with a gold-tone TH logo](/Uploads_Images/Product/1787157376_2026-08-19_6a85db806a6ee.webp)
*A Tommy Hilfiger navy crossbody with a gold-tone TH logo and clips: the hardware should match in tone.*

Look at the finish of the metal as well. On an original, all the hardware on a bag shares the same tone, gold-tone or silver-tone, and the plating is even, without spots, peeling or a rough texture. A gold-tone logo next to a silver-tone zip pull on the same bag is unusual and worth a question. Logo details on the hardware, where present, should be cleanly engraved or embossed.

Check the smaller pieces as well. Metal feet on the base of a structured bag, where present, should be firmly fixed and evenly placed; magnetic snaps should close with a clear click and hold the flap in place; key clips inside should open and close without sticking. These small parts are where cheaper bags save money first.

## Materials: leather, leather-look and textile

Tommy Hilfiger makes bags in at least 4 kinds of material: leather, coated leather-look materials, polyester and other textiles. All of these can be original; a polyester Tommy Hilfiger bag is not a copy because it is not leather. What matters is that the material matches what the seller describes, and that it looks and feels consistent all over the bag.

Read the product description, then check the bag against it. A bag described as leather should feel and smell like leather and show its natural grain; a polyester bag should have a tight, even weave without loose fibres; a coated material should have an even surface without peeling. If the description and the bag disagree, ask the seller before you accept it.

Each material also needs slightly different care, which is worth knowing when you choose. Leather likes a soft, dry cloth and a little rest between uses; coated materials wipe clean with a slightly damp cloth; polyester and textiles are the easiest to live with and the lightest to carry. None of them likes direct sun, perfume or dark denim rubbing against light colours.

## Straps and handles

Straps and handles are where a bag is held all day, so they are worth a close look. Shoulder straps should be adjustable smoothly and stay at the length you set; many of our Tommy Hilfiger shoulder bags have straps that extend to about 120 cm, so check the length on the product page if you plan to wear the bag across the body.

![Tommy Hilfiger black leather tote bag with logo webbing handles](/Uploads_Images/Product/1787156309_2026-08-19_6a85d755d2d2e.webp)
*A Tommy Hilfiger black tote with logo-print webbing handles: the print should be sharp and the handles firmly stitched.*

Logo-print webbing handles, a sporty Tommy Hilfiger detail, should have a crisp, evenly repeated print and edges that do not fray. Detachable straps should clip on and off firmly, and their clips should match the rest of the hardware. A strap that twists, frays or loosens on its own after a few minutes of wear is not what you should be paying for.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the style code on a Tommy Hilfiger bag?

Usually on a small label or tag inside the bag, often in the main compartment or an inside pocket, together with the brand name. Women's bag codes begin with AW0AW and men's with AM0AM, followed by five digits. Searching the code online shows what that style should look like, which helps you compare it with the bag in your hands.

### Is a polyester Tommy Hilfiger bag fake?

No. Tommy Hilfiger makes bags in leather, coated leather-look materials, polyester and other textiles, and all of them can be original. What matters is that the material matches the seller's description and feels consistent all over the bag, with even stitching, solid hardware and a well-finished lining.

### What should the stitching look like?

Even and straight, with stitches of the same size and spacing along every edge, smooth curves at the corners and no loose threads, skipped stitches or glue showing. The places that carry weight, where the handles and strap rings join the bag, should be stitched firmly, often with extra rows.
