## Finding the right size for your wrist

Size is the single most common reason a watch comes back to us, so it is worth two minutes before you order. Of the women's Michael Kors watches in our catalogue, the largest group has a 39 mm case, followed by 38 mm, then 40 and 42 mm, with the crystal lines often smaller at 36 to 37 mm.

A quick way to choose is to measure your wrist with a strip of paper just below the wrist bone, then compare it with a ruler. A wrist under about 14.5 cm usually looks best with a 36 to 38 mm case; the Sofie and Portia lines sit beautifully there. Between 14.5 and 16.5 cm, which covers most women, the 38 to 40 mm Darci and Parker sizes are right. Above that, or if you simply like a watch that is noticed, the 42 mm cases and the 43 mm Bradshaw chronograph look proportionate rather than oversized.

If you are between two sizes, the smaller suits work and formal clothes, the larger casual wear and evenings. Think about how you will wear it, too. A watch worn under a long sleeve at the office is easier in a slimmer, smaller case. A watch worn with short sleeves or a sleeveless evening dress can be a size larger, because nothing hides it.

If you are buying a gift and cannot measure, 38 to 39 mm is the safest choice for most adult women. A bracelet can always be shortened, and our 14-day exchange covers a size that turns out wrong, as long as the watch is unworn and in its original box.

## Gold, rose gold, silver or two-tone

Colour is where Michael Kors is at its best, and it is the decision that makes a watch feel like yours.

**Rose gold** is the colour most associated with the brand, and the one we sell most for gifts. It flatters warm and olive skin tones, which suits many women in Egypt, and it sits well with both gold and rose gold jewellery. It is also forgiving with outfits: it works with white, black, beige and pastel colours alike.

**Gold-tone** steel is more classic and more formal. If your everyday jewellery is yellow gold, a gold-tone watch completes it; it looks particularly good with evening wear and with the traditional gold sets worn at engagements.

**Silver** stainless steel is the most versatile and the easiest to wear every day. It works with silver and white-gold jewellery, with sporty clothes and with office wear, and it shows small marks less than polished gold tones.

**Two-tone** bracelets, silver links with gold or rose gold centre links, solve a common problem: they match whichever jewellery you are wearing that day. If you own both gold and silver pieces, two-tone is the practical choice.

Dial colour matters as well. White and champagne dials are the easiest to read and the most timeless. Blush pink, purple and blue dials are more playful and are often chosen to match a favourite outfit or a bag.

## Link bracelet, mesh, leather or rubber

The bracelet decides how a watch feels through a long day, and Michael Kors offers more variety here than most fashion brands.

**Steel link bracelets** are the classic choice and the one most of our women's models come on. They are durable, they do not absorb sweat, and they match jewellery in the same metal. Their one practical point is fit: a link bracelet nearly always needs one or two links taken out for a woman's wrist, which our stores do for free within the warranty.

**Mesh bracelets** are finer and softer, woven from thin steel. They slide to any length on a sliding clasp, so they fit a slim wrist without removing links, and they look delicate next to rings and thin bangles.

**Leather straps** feel warm and light and give a softer, more casual look. They are lovely in winter, but in a Cairo summer daily sweat darkens leather quickly, so they suit women who rotate between two or three watches.

**Rubber and silicone straps**, as on the Camille with its coloured strap, are the sporty, carefree option. They survive the beach, the gym and the sun, and the colours make the watch a fashion accent rather than a piece of jewellery.

If you are unsure, choose steel: it is the strap that works with the most outfits and lasts the longest.

## Crystal dials and everyday care

Many women's Michael Kors watches carry crystals, on the bezel, the dial or the bracelet, and they need a little more care than a plain watch to keep their sparkle.

**Wipe them after wearing.** Lotion, perfume and sweat dull crystals and collect in their settings. A soft, dry microfibre cloth after a long day keeps them bright; once a month, a soft toothbrush with a drop of lukewarm water reaches between the stones. Avoid alcohol, perfume and household cleaners.

**Put perfume on before the watch.** Spray perfume and let it dry before you put the watch on, rather than spraying your wrist with the watch already there. Perfume is one of the main reasons gold tones fade early.

**Mind the heat and the beach.** Most of these watches are rated to 50 metres, which handles hand washing and rain, but crystal-set pieces are best kept away from the sea and the pool. Salt and chlorine cloud the stones and attack the plating. The Runway MK7329, at 100 metres, is the better choice for summer in Sahel.

**Store it on its own.** Keep the watch in its box or a soft pouch, apart from other jewellery, so harder stones and metal chains do not scratch the crystals or the polished case.

**Use the warranty for the small jobs.** Within the warranty, battery replacement and bracelet sizing are free at our two Cairo stores, so there is no reason to let a watch sit in a drawer because it stopped or the bracelet is too loose.

## Three-hand or chronograph?

Once you know the size and the colour, the last choice is the dial layout, and it changes the character of the watch more than people expect.

A **three-hand watch**, like the Parker MK4725 or most Darci models, shows only hours, minutes and seconds. It looks cleaner and more elegant, it is easier to read at a glance, and it pairs best with jewellery. For a first Michael Kors, or for a watch meant for weddings and evenings, this is usually the right choice.

A **chronograph**, like the Runway MK7329 or the Bradshaw, adds small sub-dials for a stopwatch. It looks busier and sportier, and it gives the watch more presence on the wrist, which is why many women who like a bolder style prefer it. Chronograph cases are often a little thicker, so check the sleeve test above.

Water resistance follows the same split in our catalogue: the sportier Runway reaches 100 metres, while most dress models are 50 metres. If you swim or spend summers at the coast, that difference matters more than the dial.

## Wearing it, and giving it

A Michael Kors watch is meant to be worn with the rest of your look, and a few simple pairings make it work.

**At work**, a silver or two-tone watch with a white or champagne dial is the most versatile. It sits under a blazer sleeve, does not clash with a laptop or a desk, and looks right in a meeting. A 38 or 39 mm case keeps it discreet.

**In the evening**, crystals come into their own. A rose gold Darci or a Sofie with a crystal dial works like a bracelet with a black dress or an abaya, and it catches the light at dinner or a party.

**At weddings and engagements**, it helps to match the watch to the jewellery set: gold-tone with yellow gold, rose gold with rose gold. Stacking a thin bracelet next to the watch, on the same wrist, is a popular look; keep the bracelet in the same metal so the two do not compete.

**With a bag**, the brand's style carries across. If you already own a Michael Kors bag, a watch in the same hardware colour ties the look together; we also carry a few of the brand's bags.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are women's Michael Kors watches waterproof?

Most of the women's Michael Kors watches we stock are rated to 50 metres, which makes them water resistant rather than waterproof. You can wash your hands and wear them in the rain, but avoid swimming with crystal-set models, because salt and chlorine cloud the stones. The Runway MK7329 is rated to 100 metres and handles a swim far better.

### What size Michael Kors watch suits a small wrist?

For a wrist under about 14.5 cm, choose a 36 to 38 mm case; the Sofie and Portia lines are the best fit. Most women, with wrists between 14.5 and 16.5 cm, are comfortable with 38 to 40 mm, the size of most Darci and Parker models. Measure just below the wrist bone with a strip of paper if you are not sure.

### How long does a Michael Kors watch battery last?

A quartz Michael Kors battery usually lasts two to three years, and chronographs used often can drain it sooner. When the watch stops or the second hand starts jumping every few seconds, the battery is low. Within the warranty we replace batteries free of charge at our Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis stores, usually while you wait.

### Can I exchange a Michael Kors watch if the colour is wrong?

Yes. You can return or exchange any watch within 14 days of delivery, as long as it is unused and in its original packaging with its tags, and we pay the return shipping. Message our customer service on WhatsApp with your order number and we will arrange the exchange, or a refund made within 24 hours of the watch reaching us.

### Do you shorten the bracelet of a women's Michael Kors watch?

Yes. Most steel bracelets need one or more links removed to fit a woman's wrist, and within the warranty we size them for free at both our stores while you wait. Bring the watch with its invoice. If you live outside Cairo, a local watchmaker can also remove links using the pins already in the bracelet.
