## Two brands, two ideas of glamour

Both brands sell glamour, but of different kinds. Michael Kors was founded in New York in 1981, and its watches carry the polished, slightly luxurious look of its handbags: gold and rose gold bracelets, crystal bezels, mother-of-pearl dials and clean round cases that suit an office as well as a wedding.

Just Cavalli is the younger line of Roberto Cavalli, the fashion house opened in Florence in 1970 and famous for animal prints, bold colours and unashamed glamour. Its watches bring that spirit to the wrist: bracelets shaped like snakes, seven-sided Septagon cases, Animalier dials with animal-print patterns and Glam models with crystals. They are designed to be noticed.

The two houses also take different approaches to their names on the dial. A Michael Kors usually carries the brand name or the MK logo neatly on the dial or the clasp, while a Just Cavalli often makes the design itself the signature: the snake head, the seven-sided case or the animal pattern tells you the brand before you read a single letter. That is part of the appeal for people who prefer jewellery that is recognised by its shape.

The difference is easy to feel. A Michael Kors is a watch that happens to look like jewellery; a Just Cavalli is a piece of jewellery that happens to tell the time. Neither is better; they suit different personalities and different wardrobes.

Think about the rest of your style. If you like classic clothes, neat handbags and fine jewellery, a Michael Kors will slot straight into your wardrobe. If you like statement earrings, bold prints and accessories that start conversations, a Just Cavalli will feel more like you. Many wardrobes have room for both: one watch for every day and one for the moments when you want to stand out.

## Design and lines

Michael Kors organises its women's watches into lines with clear characters. The Runway and Runway Slim are the everyday classics; the Darci adds a ring of crystals for evenings; the Parker and Bradshaw are chronographs; the Mini models are small and delicate; and lines such as the Layton and the Jaryn add softer or more unusual faces.

## Size on the wrist

Size is the clearest practical difference. Michael Kors women's watches are mostly 38 to 39 mm, with smaller cases in the Mini lines, which reads as a modern, readable watch on most wrists. Just Cavalli cases are much smaller, mostly 23 to 34 mm, and they read as a bracelet with a dial rather than as a watch.

![Just Cavalli women's watch with a silver dial on a two-tone silver and gold stainless steel bracelet](/Uploads_Images/Product/1789045164_2026-09-10_6aa2a9acdabc6.webp)
*A Just Cavalli in two-tone silver and gold: a small round case on a steel bracelet.*

A small case looks delicate and elegant on a slim wrist and slides under any sleeve, but it is harder to read at a glance. If you need to check the time quickly at work, a Michael Kors with a clear dial is the practical choice; if the watch is mainly an accessory for evenings and occasions, a small Just Cavalli is lovely.

Your wrist size helps decide as well. On a slim wrist, below about 15 cm, a 38 mm Michael Kors can look slightly large, and its Mini models or a small Just Cavalli are better balanced. On an average or larger wrist, a tiny Just Cavalli can look delicate to the point of disappearing, while a Michael Kors Runway sits naturally. Measure your wrist with a strip of paper before you order, and compare it with the case size on the product page.

## Bracelets and comfort

Every Just Cavalli watch at Brand Fashion sits on a stainless steel bracelet, and some bracelets are shaped as snakes that coil around the wrist like a bangle. These are beautiful but fit a narrower range of wrists, so check the bracelet length on the product page or ask us before you order.

Michael Kors offers more variety: steel link bracelets in gold, rose gold, silver and two-tone, fine mesh bracelets with sliding clasps on several models, and some leather and rubber straps. Link bracelets can be sized by removing links, which we do free of charge at our stores within the warranty, and mesh bracelets adjust easily at home.

Comfort over a long day is worth considering. A light, slim watch is easy to forget on the wrist, which is what you want for work and travel. A decorated bracelet, such as a snake that wraps the wrist, is a delight for a few hours at an event but may feel more noticeable during a full working day. If you will wear the watch from morning to night, try it on before you decide, or choose the simpler option. A watch you forget you are wearing is usually the one you end up wearing most.

## Water and daily wear

The two brands differ on water resistance, and it matters in an Egyptian summer. Most Michael Kors women's watches are rated to 50 m (5 ATM), which allows a short pool swim. Many Just Cavalli models are rated to 30 m (3 ATM), which means splashes and rain only, while some are rated to 50 m. Check the rating on each product page before wearing a watch near water.

In daily terms, a Just Cavalli is best treated like jewellery: take it off before washing dishes, swimming or the shower, and put on perfume and creams before the watch. A Michael Kors rated to 50 m is more forgiving, but even then, never press a chronograph's pushers under water, and keep leather straps dry.

Crystals and decorated bracelets need a little extra care whichever brand you choose. Wipe them with a soft, dry cloth after wearing, keep them apart from harder jewellery that can scratch them, and store the watch in a soft pouch. Within the warranty we replace batteries free of charge at our stores, so a watch that stops is a quick visit rather than a problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Michael Kors or Just Cavalli better?

Neither is better overall; they serve different purposes. Michael Kors is polished and versatile, with readable 38 to 39 mm cases and a large range in many metals. Just Cavalli is bold and decorative, with small 23 to 34 mm cases, snake bracelets and Septagon shapes, and it is best worn as jewellery.

### Which brand is better for everyday wear?

Michael Kors, for most people. Its dials are larger and easier to read, most women's models are rated to 50 m, and it offers mesh, link, leather and rubber options for comfort. Just Cavalli watches are smaller and often rated to 30 m, which makes them better suited to evenings and occasions.

### Why are Just Cavalli watches so small?

Because they are designed as jewellery first. Just Cavalli cases are mostly 23 to 34 mm, so they read as a bracelet with a dial, especially on the snake models that coil around the wrist. That makes them elegant on a slim wrist and under sleeves, but harder to read at a glance.

### Are Just Cavalli watches water resistant?

Many are rated to 30 m (3 ATM), which means splashes and rain only, and some to 50 m (5 ATM), which allows a short swim. Check the rating on each product page. Treat a Just Cavalli like jewellery: take it off before swimming, washing or showering, and keep perfume away from it.

### Can I return or exchange a watch?
