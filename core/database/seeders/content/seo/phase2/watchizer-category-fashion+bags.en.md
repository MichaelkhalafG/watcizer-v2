A bag carries your day, so it has to be practical as well as beautiful. This guide explains the main shapes on this page, what each brand does best, and how to pick a size, a material and a colour that will still feel right in a few years.

## The main shapes

Shoulder bags sit under the arm and suit work and evenings. Crossbody bags hang across the body on a long strap and leave both hands free, ideal for shopping and travel. Totes are large and open, good for a laptop or a long day out. Backpacks and messenger bags suit students and commuters. Each product title names the shape, and the photos show it from 2 or 3 angles.

## Tommy Hilfiger bags

Tommy Hilfiger supplies the largest group, about 110 bags. Women's totes and shoulder bags carry the TH monogram or the brand's stripe in navy, red and white. Men's crossbody bags, backpacks and messenger bags are cleaner, mostly black or navy. They suit a smart casual style and are a good first designer bag at a reasonable price.

## Le Pliage

Le Pliage is the famous folding tote: light nylon with leather handles that folds flat when you do not need it. We carry it in sizes from S to L, in canvas and leather versions, and in classic colours. It is the bag for travel, university and anyone who carries a lot without wanting weight. The University and Xtra lines add more structure and a shoulder strap.

## Choosing a size

For daily essentials, a phone, keys, a wallet and sunglasses, a small crossbody bag of about 20 cm is enough. For work with a tablet or documents, choose a shoulder bag or tote of 30 cm or more. For a laptop, check that the bag is described as fitting one, usually 35 to 40 cm wide. When in doubt, the product page lists the dimensions.

## Leather, canvas or nylon

Leather looks rich and lasts the longest with care, but it is heavier and dislikes rain. Coated canvas, like Coach's Signature, resists scratches and stains and stays lighter. Nylon, like Le Pliage, is the lightest of all and easy to clean. If you want 1 bag for everything, coated canvas or nylon with leather trim is the most forgiving choice.

## Colours that last

Black is the most common colour on this page, with about 187 bags, then brown, blue, beige, white and pink. Black and brown go with everything and hide marks. Beige and white look fresh in summer but need more care. Pink, red and blue add personality to a neutral outfit. If you already own a black bag, a brown or beige one is the best second choice.

Black and navy are the safest colours.

## Caring for a bag

Store a bag stuffed with paper inside its dust bag, away from direct sun, so it keeps its shape and colour. Wipe leather with a dry, soft cloth and avoid rain. Clean canvas and nylon with a slightly damp cloth. Do not hang heavy bags by the straps for long periods. With these habits, a good bag looks new for 3 years and more.

## Questions and answers

### Which bag shape is the most practical?

A medium crossbody bag is the most practical for daily life. It leaves both hands free, holds a phone, wallet, keys and sunglasses, and stays close to the body in crowded places. Many models have a detachable strap, so the same bag becomes a shoulder bag or a clutch for the evening.

### Will a Le Pliage tote hold a laptop?

The larger sizes usually do. Sizes M and L of the Le Pliage tote fit most 13 to 14 inch laptops, while size S is meant for daily essentials. Check the dimensions in the product description before you order, and consider a laptop sleeve inside the tote to protect the computer from knocks.

### How do I keep a leather bag looking new?

Keep it away from rain, direct sun and heat, and store it stuffed with paper in its dust bag. Wipe it with a soft dry cloth after use, and avoid placing it on wet or dirty surfaces. Light-coloured leather should be kept away from dark denim, which can transfer dye onto it.

### Can I return a bag if I change my mind?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused, with its tags and original packaging. Send your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund returns by the payment method you used.
