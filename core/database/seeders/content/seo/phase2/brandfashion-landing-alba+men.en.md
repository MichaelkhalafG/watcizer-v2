A men's Alba is a watch you buy for the long term: a steel case, a Japanese movement and a sober design that will still look right in ten years. With a range this wide, the specifications do most of the choosing for you, so this guide is built around them: the water rating, the size, the movement, the strap and the glass, then the collections and the practical side of ordering.

## Built for water: 100 metres

Many of our men's Albas list **10 ATM**, which equals 100 metres. That is a genuinely useful rating: you can swim in a pool or in the sea, as long as you never press the crown or pushers under water and rinse the watch in fresh water afterwards. Others list 5 ATM, or 50 metres, which allows a short pool swim but not the sea.

## Sizes from 40 to 45 mm

The most common size is **43** mm, followed by 42 and 40 mm, with 44 and 45 mm for a bolder look. On a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres, 42 to 43 mm looks balanced; 40 mm suits a slimmer wrist or a dress watch; 44 to 45 mm suits a broad wrist. A few smaller cases suit anyone who prefers a classic size.

## Quartz for zero fuss

Most models run on Japanese **quartz**: accurate to within seconds a month, unaffected if left in a drawer, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. A quartz Alba is the right choice if you want a watch you never have to think about beyond an occasional battery change.

## Automatic for the pleasure of mechanics

About one in seven of our men's Albas is **automatic**, a mechanical watch that winds itself as you wear it. It needs no battery and has a smoothly sweeping seconds hand, but it gains or loses a few seconds a day and stops if left off the wrist for a day or two; a few turns of the crown restart it.

## Steel bracelets

**Steel** bracelets are the most common strap, and with a 100-metre case they make a true all-weather watch: rinse it under the tap after the beach and it is clean. They are sized by removing links, which any watch shop does in minutes. Steel suits both a suit and a T-shirt.

## Leather, fabric and silicone

**Leather** straps, about a quarter of the range, look warmer and dressier; keep them dry. **Fabric** and nylon straps, such as the dark green Active, are light, casual and comfortable in summer. **Silicone** straps, on some automatics, are the sporty choice. Remember that the strap, not the case, often decides how close to water you can go.

## Mineral or sapphire glass

Most models list **mineral** glass, clear and resistant to everyday knocks. Some list sapphire, harder and much more resistant to scratches; it is worth looking for if you work with your hands or wear the watch every day for years. The specifications on each product page name the glass.

## The collections

**Active** is the everyday and sporty line, with three-hand models, chronographs and automatics. **Prestige** is the dressier line, often on leather. **Standard** keeps things simple and practical. **Flagship Signa** includes a silver-dial chronograph on steel. The product title names the line of each watch.

## Chronographs

An Alba **chronograph** adds a stopwatch run by 2 pushers beside the crown, with sub-dials that count the elapsed time. In our range they come in the Active, Prestige and Flagship Signa lines, on steel or leather. A chronograph looks technical and sporty, but its pushers should never be pressed under water.

## Dial colours

**Black**, blue and silver-white are the most practical dials, easy to read and to match. **Grey** looks modern, **brown** warm, and a **gold** dial on silver steel brings a vintage touch. For a first Alba, black or blue on steel; for a dressier second watch, grey on leather.

## By occasion

For the office, a **Prestige** on leather or a three-hand Active on steel. For the beach and holidays, a 100-metre model on steel or silicone. For weekends, a fabric-strap Active. For a watch lover, an automatic. One Alba on steel with a 100-metre rating covers almost all of these on its own.

## Questions and answers

### Can I swim in the sea with an Alba watch?

Yes, if its page lists 10 ATM or 100 metres and it is on a steel, silicone or rubber strap. Rinse it with fresh water afterwards and never press the crown or pushers under water. A 5 ATM or 50-metre model is for a short pool swim only, and a leather strap should always stay dry.

### Is an Alba automatic as accurate as quartz?

No. A quartz Alba is accurate to within seconds a month, while an automatic may gain or lose a few seconds a day. That is normal for a mechanical watch. Its appeal is different: it needs no battery, winds itself as you wear it and has a smoothly sweeping seconds hand.

### Which size of men's Alba should I choose?

Measure your wrist. At about 17 to 18 centimetres, 42 or 43 mm looks balanced, which is why 43 mm is the most common size here. Below that, choose 40 mm; above about 18.5 centimetres, 44 or 45 mm looks natural. A leather strap makes a watch look slightly smaller than steel.
