Guess started as a denim label in Los Angeles in 1981 and grew into one of the most recognisable fashion names of the decades that followed. In our shop the brand means two things above all: handbags with the triangle logo or the quilted, hardware-heavy look Guess is known for, and fashion watches with bold cases and colourful straps. Below you will find what we carry, how to choose, and what to expect when you order from us.

## The Guess bag lines we carry

Most of our Guess bags come from a handful of named lines, and the name on the product page tells you a lot before you even open the photos. **Noelle** pieces are the classic Guess shape: a structured body, a top handle and a detachable strap, usually in the logo-printed fabric. **Amara** and **Giully** lean softer and more everyday, with quilting and chain details that make them easy to dress up for an evening. **Cordelia** is the larger, work-friendly option with room for a laptop sleeve or a folder. You will also see pieces described simply as "elegant" or "everyday" — those words come from the way the team sorts the range, not from a separate collection.

Colour matters as much as the line. Black, coal and latte tones are the safest because they go with everything; the lighter shades suit summer best. If a colour you want is out of stock, the product page lets you ask to be told when it is back.

## Choosing a Guess bag for how you use it

Start with how you carry a bag on a normal day. A crossbody keeps your hands free on public transport or in a mall like Arkadia, and a strap you can shorten turns it into a shoulder bag for an evening. A satchel with a top handle looks more formal and holds its shape, which suits an office or a meeting. A tote is the practical choice if you carry a water bottle, a notebook and a charger — check the inner pockets on the product page, because a tote with no divider quickly becomes a black hole.

Then look at the closure. A zip top is the safest for crowded places; a magnetic flap is quicker to open but less secure. Finally, look at the hardware: gold-tone buckles and chains are part of the Guess look, so pick a bag whose metal matches the jewellery and watch you wear most.

## Guess watches for men and women

Our Guess watch range is split almost evenly between men's and women's models, and it is a different world from the bags. Men's Guess watches tend to be large, sporty and dressed in silicone or rubber straps, with multi-dial faces that read like chronographs. Women's models range from slim bracelets with crystal markers to bold coloured cases that are meant to be noticed. Both run on quartz movements, which keep accurate time without daily wear and need only a battery change every couple of years.

When you compare two watches, look at the case size on the product page first — a 44 mm men's case wears very differently from a 40 mm one on a slim wrist — and then the strap material: silicone is comfortable in the heat and easy to clean, steel looks dressier and needs sizing, which we do for free in our stores within the warranty.

## Wallets and small leather goods

Guess wallets in our shop are mostly women's: zip-around wallets that hold cards, notes and a phone, and smaller card cases that fit a crossbody bag. A few men's bifolds complete the range; all of them are on the [Guess wallets](/brand/guess/wallets) page. If you are buying a bag and a wallet together, choosing the same line keeps the logo print and the hardware consistent.

## Looking after a Guess bag in Egypt's climate

Heat, dust and sun are the three things that age a bag fastest here. Summer days in Cairo pass 40 degrees, and a parked car gets far hotter, so keep a bag out of a hot car, because the coated fabric and the glue behind the trims soften in the heat. Wipe dust off with a dry, soft cloth after a day out, and use a slightly damp cloth for marks — never a household cleaner, which can strip the print. When you store a bag, stuff it lightly with paper so it keeps its shape, and keep it in the dust bag or a cotton pillowcase rather than plastic, which traps moisture. Rotating two bags through the week also gives each one time to recover its shape.

### How do I know the size of a bag before ordering?

Each product page lists the measurements of the bag, usually width, height and depth in centimetres, and the photos show it from several sides. If you are unsure, compare it with a bag you already own: measure it with a tape and hold the two sets of numbers side by side.
