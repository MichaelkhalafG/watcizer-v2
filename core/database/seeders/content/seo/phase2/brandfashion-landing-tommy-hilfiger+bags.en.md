Families often shop for bags together: a shoulder bag for a mother, a crossbody for a father, a backpack for a student, a travel pouch for the trip coming up. This page lists every Tommy Hilfiger bag we carry so you can do exactly that. The guide below goes person by person, then covers the choices that apply to every bag: material, closure, colour and care.

## For her, every day

A medium **shoulder bag** in black, navy or beige is the bag a woman uses most: it holds a wallet, a phone, keys, sunglasses and a small pouch, sits under the arm and leaves both hands free. A **handbag** with a top handle and a detachable strap does the same job with a more polished look for meetings. The [women's bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/women) explains the shapes in detail.

## For him, every day

For a man, the everyday bag is a small **crossbody** or chest bag: phone, cards, keys and earphones close to the body and out of trouser pockets. For work, a **messenger** or a laptop bag takes a tablet or a 13 to 15-inch laptop. The [men's bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/men) covers each type and how it is carried.

## For students

A **backpack** is the natural student bag, for either sex: it spreads books, a laptop and a water bottle across both shoulders and keeps a clean, smart look that suits university as well as school. Choose one with a padded laptop sleeve and a front pocket for small things. A small crossbody bag is a good second bag for evenings and weekends.

## For travellers

Every traveller needs two bags: a **crossbody** worn in front for the passport, boarding pass, phone and cards, and a **tote** or **backpack** for everything else. Add a **travel pouch** for chargers or toiletries so nothing tangles at the bottom of the bag. Monogram canvas and nylon handle airports best, because they shrug off scuffs and wipe clean.

## For a couple

A couple can coordinate without looking identical: a women's shoulder bag and a men's crossbody in the same colour, such as navy, or one black and one beige from the same monogram line. The shared flag or monogram detail ties them together. It is also a thoughtful anniversary or engagement gift, chosen once for 2 people, and ordered together they arrive in a single delivery.

## For parents and grandparents

Older family members usually want a bag that is light, simple to open and easy to find things in. A medium shoulder bag with a zip and 2 or 3 inner pockets suits a mother or grandmother; a flat crossbody or a hand pouch with a wrist strap suits a father or grandfather who carries a phone, glasses, keys and a wallet. Avoid deep, open totes, where small things disappear, and choose black or navy, which go with everything and hide wear. A lighter bag also means less strain on the shoulder over a long day.

If the bag is a gift for a parent, choose a model with a clear, easy closure rather than small snaps that can be hard to open; comfort in use is what they will appreciate every day.

## Material: monogram, smooth or nylon

**Monogram canvas** is light, scuff-resistant and easy to wipe, ideal for daily use and travel. **Smooth** finishes, in leather or leather-look materials, look the most polished and suit work and evenings, with a little more care. **Nylon** and textured fabric are the lightest and most practical for sport, travel and long days. Choose by how hard the bag will work.

## Closure: zip, flap or open

A **zip** is the most secure in crowds and on public transport. A **flap** with a magnetic snap opens quickly with one hand and suits the car, the office and evenings. An **open** tote is the fastest and the least secure, best kept for places where the bag stays close to you. Inside, a zipped pocket for cash and keys makes any bag safer.

## Colour for the whole family

**Black** suits everyone and every outfit. **Navy** is the brand's own colour and looks fresh with white and denim. **Beige** is soft and summery for women's bags. **Red** is the confident accent for a second bag. If you buy several bags at once, keep them in one or two of these colours and they will always look like a set.

## Sizes to check

The product pages list width, height and depth in centimetres. As a guide: a crossbody holds 4 or 5 small essentials; a medium shoulder bag adds a wallet and sunglasses; a tote, a messenger or a backpack takes a tablet, and many take a laptop. A 13-inch laptop usually needs about 33 centimetres of inside width; a 15-inch about 38.

## Caring for any bag

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use, or a slightly damp one on canvas and nylon, and blot spills straight away. Keep it out of a hot parked car in summer, where a 35-degree day softens coatings and glue, and keep light colours away from dark denim. Store bags empty and stuffed with paper, in their dust bags. Never use household cleaners or wet wipes.

## Questions and answers

### Can I buy bags for several people in one order?

Yes. Add them all to the cart and they arrive in one delivery after one confirmation call. With cash on delivery you can inspect each bag before paying, and every bag keeps its own right to be returned or exchanged within 14 days.

### Which bag suits both men and women?

A plain black backpack or a simple black crossbody bag works for anyone. The product title says whether a bag is designed for men or women, but these two shapes in black are the most neutral choices on the page.

### Which bag is best for travel?

A crossbody bag worn in front for your passport, phone and cards, plus a backpack or a tote for the rest, and a small pouch for chargers. Monogram canvas or nylon is the most practical material, because it resists scuffs and wipes clean.

### Will a laptop fit in a tote?

Some totes take a small laptop. Check the inside width on the product page, which should be at least 33 centimetres for that size, and about 38 for a 15-inch laptop. If you are unsure, send our customer service the product link and your laptop model on WhatsApp.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
