Pinko is an Italian fashion brand known for feminine, confident designs and, above all, for its bags with a distinctive metal buckle shaped like two birds facing each other. Where many brands play it safe, Pinko likes a touch of drama: quilting, chains, glossy hardware and shapes that look good in photos. Our range is compact, so this guide focuses on how to choose between the two main shapes we carry, how to match the colours and how to look after the hardware and the leather.

## Crossbody bags

Most of our Pinko bags can be worn **crossbody**: a long strap carries the bag across the body, so your hands are free and your phone and cards sit in front of you, where you can see them. That is the most practical way to carry a bag in a busy mall, at an airport or on a night out. Pinko crossbody bags are compact, with room for a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick, and many use a chain strap or a chain-and-leather strap that can also be doubled and worn on the shoulder.

## Shoulder bags

The **shoulder bags** in our range are slightly larger and more structured, with a flap or a top closure and the brand's metal hardware on the front. They sit comfortably under the arm and look elegant with a dress, a blazer or a simple shirt and jeans. If you carry more each day, such as a wallet, a power bank and sunglasses, a shoulder bag is the better choice. Check the width, height and depth on the product page, and look at the photos of the bag open to see the inside pockets.

## The hardware and the bird buckle

Metal is central to Pinko's style. The best-known detail is the **bird buckle**, two stylised birds facing each other, used as a clasp on many of the brand's bags; other models use a logo plaque, chain straps or quilted panels. The finish is usually gold-tone or silver-tone, and the product photos show which. Gold-tone pairs naturally with gold jewellery and a gold or rose-gold watch; silver-tone suits steel watches and white gold. Keep perfume and creams away from the metal, which can dull its shine.

## Choosing the colour

Black is the most common colour in our Pinko range and the easiest to wear every day, from the office to dinner. **White** is fresh and striking in summer and photographs beautifully, but it needs more care: keep it away from dark denim and wipe it after each use. **Burgundy** is a rich, warm alternative for autumn and evenings, and it pairs well with black, beige and grey clothes. If you can only choose one, black is the safest; if you already own a black bag, burgundy or white is the more interesting second one.

## Wearing a Pinko bag with a watch

Because Watchizer is a watch shop first, a bag and a watch can be chosen together. With a gold-tone Pinko clasp, a gold or rose-gold watch looks deliberate, such as the women's watches from [Michael Kors](/brand/michael-kors) in our range; with silver hardware, a steel watch matches better. A crossbody bag on a chain and a slim bracelet watch make an elegant evening pair. Let either the bag or the watch be the statement, and keep the other piece calm, so they complement each other.

## A Pinko bag for an occasion

Pinko bags are at their best at events: weddings, engagements, birthdays and dinners, where a small, striking bag finishes an outfit. A black crossbody on a chain goes with almost every evening dress; a white bag lifts a summer look and photographs beautifully; burgundy adds warmth to a black or beige outfit in winter. Because the bags are compact, plan what you need to carry: a phone, a card holder, keys and one lipstick usually fit, a large wallet often does not. If you are choosing a Pinko as a gift for a bride or a friend's celebration, pick the colour of the event's dress code and keep the invoice, so the bag can be exchanged within 14 days if needed.

At a long evening, a crossbody bag is more comfortable than a handbag, because it leaves your hands free for greeting people, taking photos and holding a drink, without worrying about where your bag is all night.

## Caring for leather and chains

Wipe the bag with a soft, dry cloth after each use, and blot any spill immediately instead of rubbing. Do not use household cleaners or wet wipes, which can mark the finish. Keep the bag out of a hot parked car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside gets far hotter, and heat can soften glue and coatings. Store it empty, stuffed with paper and inside its dust bag, with the chain strap laid inside so it does not scratch the leather. Polish the metal now and then with a dry cloth to keep its shine.

## Questions and answers

### Can a Pinko chain bag be worn on the shoulder?

Often, yes. Many Pinko chain straps are long enough to wear crossbody and can be doubled to wear on the shoulder for a shorter, dressier look. The product photos show the strap and the product page gives its length. If a model has a fixed short strap, it is designed to be carried on the shoulder or in the hand.

### Is a white bag a good choice for daily use?

It can be, with care. White looks fresh and elegant, especially in summer, but it shows marks sooner than black or burgundy. Avoid dark denim, which transfers dye, keep pens and make-up in a pouch inside the bag, and wipe it gently after each use. If you want a low-maintenance daily bag, black is the easier choice.

### How do I keep the metal hardware shiny?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use, and keep perfume, hand cream and sun cream away from it, because chemicals dull the finish. Do not use metal polish or abrasive cloths, which can strip the plating. Store the bag in its dust bag so the hardware does not rub against other items.
