SKMEI watches are bought for what they do: tell the time clearly, wake you up, time a run and survive a school bag. This guide sorts the page into 4 kinds of SKMEI, explains the functions, and helps you choose for a student, an athlete or an everyday wearer.

## Military-style digital

The largest group, about 10 models, is the military-style digital watch: a chunky case, a large screen, raised buttons and a strap in black, olive green or blue. They show the time, date and day, and run alarms, a stopwatch and a countdown. They are favourites for sport, scouting, camping and young men who like a rugged look.

## Sports models

Sports models on rubber and resin straps, around 40 to 45 mm, are lighter and slimmer than the military designs, with a digital dial and sometimes a chronograph display. They suit running, the gym and football practice. Resin and rubber dry fast and can be rinsed after training.

## Metal-band watches

About 4 SKMEI models come on stainless steel or a small metal band, in silver and black or gold tones. They look smarter than the resin models and suit students who want something neat for university, or women and teenagers who prefer a slimmer watch. They are still light and simple to use.

## Simple watches for school and exams

Some SKMEI watches are deliberately plain: a quiet analog or digital face without smart features, made for exam halls where phones and smartwatches are not allowed. They are a practical buy before exam season for pupils and university students who need to keep track of time during a 2 or 3 hour paper.

## Functions explained

Depending on the model: a daily alarm, an hourly chime, a stopwatch, a countdown timer, a dual time display, 12 or 24 hour format and an LED backlight. The leaflet in the box explains the 4 buttons. Most owners set the time, 1 alarm and the backlight on the first day and never need the leaflet again.

## Water resistance in practice

SKMEI ratings on this page range from splash resistance to 50 m. A 50 m model survives rain, hand washing and a shower; lower ratings are for splashes only. Check the rating on the product page, never press the buttons under water, and dry the watch after it gets wet.

## Sizes for everyone

Cases range from small, slim designs to about 48 mm. Military and sports models are large and suit teenage boys and men. Metal-band and simple models are smaller and suit women and younger children. Check the case size on each product page, and choose an adjustable resin strap if the wrist is very small.

A SKMEI battery usually lasts 1 to 2 years; frequent use of the backlight and alarms shortens it. When the screen fades, bring the watch to one of our stores in Arkadia Mall or Heliopolis for a new battery. The team also checks that the case is closed tightly so the water resistance holds.

## Questions and answers

### Can I wear a SKMEI in an exam?

The simple SKMEI models without smart features are made for exam halls, where phones and smartwatches are not allowed. Check your school's or university's own rules first; most allow a basic analog or digital watch. Silence the alarms and the hourly chime before the exam.

### Can I swim with a SKMEI?

Only models rated 50 m can handle a shower or a brief swim; most others are for splashes only. Check the rating on the product page and never press the buttons under water. For regular swimming, a watch rated 100 m or more is the safer choice.

### How do I set the time?

Press the mode button until the time setting appears, hold the adjust button until the digits flash, then use the other buttons to change hours, minutes and date. The leaflet in the box shows the exact steps for each model, and our team can help on WhatsApp.
