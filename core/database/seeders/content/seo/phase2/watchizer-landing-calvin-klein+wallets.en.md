## Zip-around: why it works

A **zip-around** wallet closes on three sides, so nothing slips out, even at the bottom of a full bag. When it opens, everything is visible at once: notes in the middle, cards on either side. That makes it the most secure everyday shape for women, and the easiest to use at a till or a kiosk.

For a man, a **bifold** wallet slips into a trouser pocket and is the most common shape. The gift sets add a matching belt, which makes them the easiest complete gift. Match the belt to his shoes: black for black shoes and suits, which is why the black sets suit almost everyone.

## What fits inside

A long zip-around wallet holds a typical set of bank cards, an ID card, some loyalty cards, folded notes and coins. A bifold holds fewer cards and folded notes. Keep it to about **10** cards; an overfilled wallet strains the zip or the fold and stretches the card slots.

## Looking after a wallet

Wipe the wallet with a soft, dry cloth, never with alcohol or strong cleaners. Open and close the **zip** gently along its line, never at an angle, and never force it over a bulging stack of cards. Keep a beige wallet away from dark jeans and newspaper ink, which can transfer colour.

## Signs it is time for a new wallet

A wallet usually tells you when it is tired. Card slots that no longer grip, a **zip** that catches, frayed edges or a fold that has cracked are the signs. Replacing it before it fails protects your cards and cash, and a new wallet is one of the simplest ways to refresh your everyday look.
