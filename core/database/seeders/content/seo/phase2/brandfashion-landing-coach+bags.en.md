Buying a Coach bag online is easier when you know what to check. Beyond the colour, four details decide whether a bag suits you: its size, its strap, its closure and its material. This guide walks through those four checks using the bags in our range, then covers colours by season, care, matching and ordering, so you can choose with the same confidence as in a shop.

## Check 1: the size

Coach bags on this page fall into four sizes. **Mini** totes and small crossbody bags carry a phone, cards and keys. **Medium** shoulder bags take a wallet, sunglasses and a power bank as well. **Large** shoulder bags and handbags add a notebook or a tablet. The backpack holds a full day's things. Each product page lists the measurements.

## Check 2: the strap

A **single** shoulder strap sits under the arm and suits quick errands. A long **crossbody** strap frees both hands. Two top handles, with or without a detachable strap, let a handbag go from the hand to the shoulder. Backpack straps spread the weight. If you can, choose a bag with an adjustable or detachable strap for the most flexibility.

## Check 3: the closure

A **zip** keeps everything inside and is the safest in crowds and on public transport. A flap with a clasp, as on the Tabby, looks smart and opens quickly. A snap closure is the fastest to open. Open totes are easy to reach into but are best carried in front of you in busy places.

## Check 4: the material

**Leather**, smooth or pebbled, softens with use and looks refined; it prefers dry days. **Signature coated canvas**, printed with the interlocking C pattern, is lighter, more resistant to light rain and easier to wipe clean, often with leather trims. For everyday rough and tumble, Signature canvas is the more forgiving choice.

## The hardware tone

Titles often start with the hardware tone: **brass**, gold or silver. Brass is warm with a slightly antique look, gold is bright and dressy, and silver is cool and modern. Choose it to match your watch and jewellery, or the zips and clasps of the shoes and belt you wear most.

**Tabby** is a structured Coach bag with the signature C hardware on the front flap, neat enough for the office and easy with jeans. **Lana** and **Teri** are other named styles in the range, each with its own shape; the product photos show the exact form and size of every model we carry.

## Colours for summer

For the hot months, light colours keep a bag looking fresh: **chalk**, khaki, sand, ivory and powder pink, and Signature canvas in light khaki. Light bags pair beautifully with linen and white, but they show marks sooner, so wipe them now and then and keep them away from dark jeans.

## Colours for winter

For the cooler months, rich colours feel warm: **maple**, walnut, dark brown, burgundy and navy. They suit coats, knitwear and boots, and hide marks well. Black works in every season and with every outfit, which is why it is the safest choice if you are buying only one Coach bag.

## Carrying a bag for work

For the office, a **large** leather shoulder bag or a top-handle handbag in black, navy or maple looks polished and holds the day's essentials. A zip closure keeps papers and valuables secure on the commute, and a detachable strap lets you carry the bag by hand in meetings.

## Carrying a bag for evenings

For dinners and celebrations, a **mini** tote or a small crossbody bag is enough: a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick. Gold hardware adds a festive touch, and chalk, ivory or pink feel light and pretty. Leave the long wallet at home and carry a card case instead.

## Caring for leather and canvas

Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth and Signature canvas with a slightly damp one, then dry both in the shade. Avoid alcohol and strong cleaners. Store the bag stuffed with paper in its **dust bag**, and never leave it in a hot car, where heat can mark leather and the canvas coating.

## Questions and answers

### Is a leather or a Signature canvas Coach bag better?

Neither is better; they suit different needs. Leather looks refined and softens beautifully with use but prefers dry days and gentle care. Signature coated canvas is lighter, resists light rain and wipes clean easily, which makes it ideal for a bag used every day in heat and dust.

### Which Coach bag size should I choose?

Think about the largest thing you carry daily. A phone and cards need only a mini tote or a small crossbody. A wallet, sunglasses and a power bank need a medium shoulder bag. A notebook or a tablet needs a large shoulder bag, a handbag or the backpack. Compare the measurements on the product page.

### Which closure is the safest?

A zip, which keeps everything inside even when the bag is jostled in a crowd. A flap with a clasp is secure too and quicker to open. Open totes are the least secure, so keep them in front of you on public transport and in busy markets.
