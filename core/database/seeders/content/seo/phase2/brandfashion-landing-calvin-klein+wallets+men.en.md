A man's wallet lives in a pocket for years, so small differences matter: how thick it is, how many cards it takes, whether it keeps coins and how it looks when it comes out at a till. This guide helps you choose a men's Calvin Klein wallet by habit, not just by look, and explains the gift sets, the finishes and how to keep a wallet in shape.

## Start with how you pay

If you still pay for taxis, kiosks and shops in **cash**, choose a bifold with a coin pocket; notes stay flat and change stays separate. If you pay mostly by card or phone, a slim card holder or a vertical wallet carries what you need without bulk. Your payment habit is the best guide to the right shape.

## Front pocket or back pocket

A slim **card holder** or a thin bifold sits comfortably in a front trouser pocket, which is also the safest place in a crowd. A fuller bifold or trifold is usually carried in a back pocket. Try not to sit on a thick wallet for hours: it wears the wallet out faster and is uncomfortable on a long drive.

## The jacket pocket

If you wear a jacket or a blazer most days, a **vertical** wallet is designed for its inside pocket. It is tall and slim, so it does not spoil the line of the jacket, and it opens upright, which looks neat when you pay at a restaurant or take out a business card.

## How many cards

A bifold usually holds a row of cards on each side. A **trifold** folds twice and holds more cards in a compact square, which suits anyone with many bank, loyalty and ID cards. Whatever the shape, keep it to about 10 cards so the wallet stays slim and the slots keep their grip.

## RFID protection

One trifold we list has an **RFID** lining. It blocks the radio signal of contactless bank cards, so a reader held close to your pocket cannot read them through the wallet. It is a small but useful extra on crowded transport and at busy markets, and the product page states which wallets have it.

## Slim or full

A **slim** wallet looks smart and keeps pockets flat, but holds less. A full wallet carries everything but adds bulk. If you are between the two, choose a bifold without a coin pocket and carry coins separately, or a card holder for workdays and a bifold for weekends.

## The logo question

Calvin Klein wallets range from **discreet** to bold. A small metal logo on a textured surface is the most formal; a vertical embossed logo is modern and clean; a large embossed CK is confident; and an all-over monogram, often from Calvin Klein Jeans, is the most casual. Choose the level of branding that suits the wearer.

The wallet mostly stays in a pocket, so it can be black whatever you wear, while the belt should follow your **shoes**: black with black, brown with brown. That is why black wallet and belt sets suit nearly every man and almost every outfit, from a suit to jeans.

Some men's wallets are listed as **leather**; many are described as leather-look, a coated material with the appearance of leather that is light and wipes clean easily. The product page names the material. Leather softens and darkens slightly with use; leather-look keeps its colour and shape well.

## Keeping a wallet in shape

Clear out receipts every week, never force a fold over too many cards, and do not leave the wallet in a hot car or on a sunny dashboard. Wipe it with a dry **cloth** if it gets wet, and let it dry naturally, away from direct heat, before putting cards back in.

## When to replace it

Card slots that no longer grip, frayed edges, a cracked fold or a **coin pocket** that has split are signs a wallet is tired. Replacing it in time protects your cards and cash. A new wallet is also one of the simplest gifts to give yourself at the start of a new job or a new year.

A card holder, which carries a few cards and folded notes without a coin pocket and fits a front pocket. Among full wallets, a vertical wallet or a bifold without a coin pocket is the slimmest. The product photos show the inside, so you can see how many slots each one has.

### Is a trifold better than a bifold?

It is not better, it is different. A trifold holds more cards in a smaller, squarer shape but is a little thicker when full. A bifold is flatter and is the classic choice for a trouser pocket. If you carry many cards, choose a trifold; if you want a flat pocket, a bifold.
