More than half of the pieces here are **bags**: shoulder bags, crossbody bags, handbags with a top handle, totes, mini bags and a backpack. Next come 16 **watches**, then 7 **wallets**, almost all zip-around designs. Black leads across all three families, followed by rose gold on the watches and latte, beige, off-white and pink on the bags and wallets.

## Start with the bag

The bag is the largest piece and sets the colour of the whole look, so choose it first. Named lines such as **Noelle**, **Assia**, Fedana, Latona and Kerima cover everything from a practical crossbody bag to a quilted evening bag and a bamboo-style handbag. The [Guess bags](/brand/guess/bags) page explains every shape and line.

## Then the watch

Once the bag is chosen, pick a watch in the same metal tone as its buckles and clasps. Our women's Guess watches run from the delicate 31 mm **Fame** to the 42 mm **Crush**, with crystal models such as G Twist and Afterglow for evenings. The [women's Guess watches](/brand/guess/watches/women) page covers sizes, straps and crystals.

## Finish with the wallet

A wallet is the piece you show most often, every time you pay. Our Guess wallets are quilted, monogrammed or pebbled, in black, white, pink and brown, with one double-zip wristlet. The [Guess wallets](/brand/guess/wallets) page explains what fits inside and which bags they suit.

## Matching gold-tone details

Many Guess pieces carry **gold-tone** hardware: buckles, zip pulls, logos and studs. When the watch, the bag clasp and the wallet logo share the same tone, the whole look appears planned. Gold-tone details suit a gold or rose-gold watch; if your watch is silver, choose a bag and a wallet with silver details or none at all.

## The 4G monogram, once

The **4G** pattern looks best on one piece at a time. A monogram bag with a plain black wallet and a simple watch reads as confident; a monogram bag, a monogram wallet and a crystal watch together can feel busy. If you love the pattern, put it on the bag, which is the most visible piece, and keep the rest quiet.

## Quilting for a soft, dressy look

Quilted pieces repeat well together. A quilted off-white **Assia** or **Fedana** bag with a white or black quilted wallet looks coordinated and dressy, and a rose-gold watch on steel or mesh completes it. Quilting suits weddings, engagements and family dinners, and still works with jeans during the day.

## A soft palette: rose gold and pink

Rose gold, pink and off-white belong together. A rose-gold watch on white silicone, such as **Confetti** or **Raven**, with a pink zip-around wallet and a beige or off-white bag gives a light, cheerful look for spring and summer, and photographs beautifully at outdoor events.

## The black everyday set

For one set that works every day, choose black for the bag and the wallet and a watch in your usual metal tone. A black crossbody bag with a zip, a black **pebbled** wallet and a silver or gold steel watch go with every outfit, hide marks and look smart from the office to a café. It is also the easiest set to give as a gift, since black never clashes with what she already owns.

## A set for evenings and weddings

For evenings, carry little and let the watch shine. A mini bag such as **Kasinta**, small enough for a phone, a card holder and keys, pairs well with a crystal watch like Afterglow or G Twist. Leave the long wallet at home; a mini bag has no room for it, and the wristlet can stand in as a small clutch.

## A set for work and university

A long day needs room. A tote such as **Noelle II** or Latona holds papers, a water bottle and a long zip-around wallet. Pair it with a 36 to 38 mm steel watch such as Constellation or Lenox, easy to read in meetings and comfortable at a keyboard.

## Caring for a mixed set

Keep the watch out of the bag's outer pockets, where keys and coins can scratch its glass and crystals. Store each piece separately: bags stuffed with paper in their dust bags, the wallet in its box, the watch in its case. Wipe all three with a soft cloth, never alcohol, and keep perfume away from plating and coated surfaces. Change the watch battery every 2 to 3 years, as soon as it slows, so the whole set stays ready to wear.

### Which Guess piece makes the best gift?

A wallet is the safest, because there is no size to guess. A bag is the most visible and is easy to choose in black or her favourite colour. A watch is the most personal; choose it in the metal tone of the jewellery she already wears, and a 36 or 38 mm case if you are unsure of her wrist.

### Should the watch match the bag exactly?

No. Matching the metal tone is enough: a gold-tone clasp on the bag with a gold or rose-gold watch, silver with steel. The colours of the bag and the watch strap can differ freely. A black bag goes with every watch, which is why it is the easiest starting point.
