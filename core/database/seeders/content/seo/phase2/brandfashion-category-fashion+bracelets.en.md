## Wearing a bracelet with a watch

Put the bracelet on the same wrist as the watch, further up the arm, and match the metals: silver beads and chains with steel, gold-tone with gold. Leave a little space between them so the bracelet does not scratch the case. With a large watch of 44 mm or more, keep to 1 slim bracelet.

## Stacking

A stack of 2 or 3 bracelets looks deliberate when they share 1 colour. Start with the thinnest next to the hand, then a beaded strand, then a charm bracelet. Mixing textures, smooth chain with beads, works better than 3 identical pieces.

## Size and fit

Elastic beaded bracelets fit most wrists of about 15 to 18 cm. Chain bracelets usually close with a clasp and a short extension chain. The Lacoste Arthor adjusts by sliding. Measure your wrist just above the bone and add 1 to 2 cm for a comfortable fit.

## Looking after beads and chains

Put bracelets on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take them off for showers, swimming and sport. Wipe them with a dry soft cloth. Store each one separately in a pouch, away from humidity and below 30 degrees, so chains do not tangle and beads do not scratch each other. Roll elastic bracelets on and off rather than stretching them.

## Questions and answers

### What does the evil eye charm mean?

The evil eye is a traditional symbol of protection, worn in many cultures around the Mediterranean to keep away bad luck. On this page it appears as a blue crystal-style charm on silver beaded bracelets, including an infinity design. It is a popular gift for a new baby, a new job or a new home.

### Will an elastic bracelet fit my wrist?

Most elastic beaded bracelets here fit wrists of about 15 to 18 cm. If your wrist is outside that range, choose a chain bracelet with a clasp and extension, or the adjustable Lacoste Arthor for men. Measure your wrist just above the bone and add 1 to 2 cm before choosing.

### Can I wear a bracelet every day?

Yes, if you take it off for water, sport and sleep and put it on after perfume and lotion. These habits protect the beads, the elastic and the metal finish. Wipe it in the evening and store it in a pouch, and it will keep its shine for a long time.
