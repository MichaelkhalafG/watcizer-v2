When Giorgio Armani launched Armani Exchange in 1991, he wanted a line that spoke to young people in big cities: denim, T-shirts, bold graphics and accessories made to be used hard. That is still the spirit of A|X. Its bags are less about quiet luxury and more about everyday usefulness with a clear logo. This guide looks at the range by situation — the office, the weekend, travel and a night out — so you can choose the right bag for how you live.

{facts}

## For the office and university

For long days with a laptop, notes and a water bottle, a **tote** is the answer. The A|X totes in our range are wide and open, with an embossed or printed logo, mostly in black, which hides daily marks. If you commute by bus or metro, look for a zipped top; if you drive, an open tote is quicker to use. Men who carry a laptop every day will find the **backpack** more comfortable, with the weight on both shoulders and the logo embossed discreetly on the front.

## For the weekend and errands

On the weekend, a small **crossbody bag** is the most useful thing you can carry: phone, cards, keys and sunglasses on a strap across the body, with your hands free for shopping bags or a coffee. Our range has crossbody bags for women with a logo strap or a monogram print, and textured crossbody bags for men, flat enough to sit close to the body. The **camera bag**, a boxy crossbody with a logo plaque, is the most fashionable version.

## For travel

At an airport or in a new city, keep passports, cards and a phone in a crossbody bag that stays in front of you, and pack the rest in a backpack or a tote. The men's **hand pouch** is a practical extra: it holds documents, a charger and earphones, and slides into a larger bag or works on its own. Coated materials, which many A|X bags use, shrug off light rain and are easy to wipe clean after a dusty day.

## For a night out

When you need something smaller and dressier, the **chain bags** are the evening option: compact flap bags on metal chain straps, some woven with leather, in black, white or a monogram pattern. They hold a phone, a card holder and a lipstick, and the chain can often be doubled for a shorter strap. Pair a silver chain with silver jewellery and a steel watch for a consistent look.

## Black, white or monogram

Black is the core colour of the range and the most versatile. White and off-white pieces look fresh and modern in summer but need more care, especially near dark denim, which can transfer its dye. **Monogram** prints in black and grey or white and grey add pattern without strong colour, and they are the most recognisably A|X. The [Armani Exchange bags page](/brand/armani-exchange/bags) shows every colour and shape.

## Pairing an A|X bag with a watch

Armani Exchange bags carry silver-tone hardware almost everywhere, so they sit naturally beside steel watches. A black A|X crossbody bag with a black-dial steel watch from [Emporio Armani](/brand/emporio-armani) or [Diesel](/brand/diesel) in our range gives a sharp, monochrome city look. For women, a white or grey monogram bag goes well with a silver or two-tone bracelet watch. Avoid mixing strong gold watches with silver-heavy bags if you want a clean look; one metal tone across bag, watch and jewellery is what makes an outfit look deliberate rather than accidental.

Because the brand speaks to a younger, urban audience, A|X bags are excellent gifts for students starting university and young people starting their first job. A backpack or a tote is the practical choice for daily study or work; a crossbody bag is the one they will carry every weekend. For a birthday or a graduation, choose black, which never goes out of style and suits any outfit. For a young man in his first job, a black backpack with a quiet logo works for the office and the weekend alike, so it becomes a gift he uses every day for years.

## Simple care

Wipe coated bags with a slightly damp cloth and leather details with a dry one. Do not use household cleaners, alcohol or wet wipes, which can strip the print and the finish. Keep the bag out of a hot parked car, where a 35-degree day can soften the coating, and store it empty and stuffed with paper so it keeps its shape. Light colours last longest when they are stored in a dust bag, away from sunlight.

## Questions and answers

### Who are Armani Exchange bags for?

They are made for people who like a modern, urban look and want a practical bag with a visible logo. Students, young professionals and anyone who dresses casually most of the week will get the most use from them. If you prefer a quieter, more formal style, the Emporio Armani bags in our catalogue are the more refined choice.

### Are A|X bags leather?

Many are made of coated or synthetic materials, chosen for lightness, water resistance and easy cleaning, sometimes with leather details. The product page states the material of each model. If leather matters to you, check the page or ask our customer service on WhatsApp before ordering.

### Which bag is best for travel?

A crossbody bag for your passport, phone and cards, worn in front of you, plus a backpack for everything else. The pair keeps valuables secure and your hands free at airports and stations. The men's pouch is a useful third piece for documents and chargers.

### How do I stop a white bag from getting marked?

Keep it away from dark denim and new dark clothes, whose dye can transfer, store pens and make-up in a pouch inside it, and wipe it gently after each use. Store it in a dust bag, out of the sun. If you want a light bag with less care, a grey-and-white monogram hides small marks better than plain white.

### Can I return an Armani Exchange bag?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund is made by the payment method you used once the bag reaches us.
