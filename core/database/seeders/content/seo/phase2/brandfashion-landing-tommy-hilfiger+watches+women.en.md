Tommy Hilfiger's women's watches are easy to love because they do not try too hard: a clean dial, a small flag or logo, a bracelet or strap that suits daily life. They are watches for the office, university, weekends and travel rather than for a single evening, and they make dependable gifts. This guide explains the sizes, the metals, the straps and the details that set one model apart from another.

## The 38 mm standard

More than half of the women's watches on this page measure 38 millimetres, which is the brand's most balanced women's size. On a typical women's wrist of 14 to 16 centimetres, it is large enough to read at a glance and modern in proportion, without looking like a man's watch. Models at 40 and 41 mm give a bolder, more contemporary look, and they suit someone who likes oversized watches.

## Smaller cases: 32 and 36 mm

If you prefer a finer, more classic look, the 32 and 36 mm models are the ones to see. A smaller case sits delicately on a slim wrist and looks closer to a piece of jewellery, which suits formal occasions and anyone who wears bracelets alongside the watch. Measure your wrist and compare with a watch you already own: under about 14 centimetres, 32 to 36 mm usually looks best.

## Silver, gold or rose gold

Steel bracelets in three tones lead the range. **Silver** steel is the easiest to wear every day and goes with any jewellery. **Gold** tone looks warm and festive, at its best with yellow-gold earrings and rings. **Rose gold** is the softest and most feminine, and it flatters warm skin tones and pink, nude and white clothes. Two-tone bracelets mix silver with gold for someone who wears both metals.

## Silicone straps for summer

A group of models comes on **silicone** straps in light colours such as white, pastel blue and blush. Silicone is soft, light and comfortable in the heat, it does not absorb sweat and it rinses clean in seconds, which makes it ideal for the beach, the gym and travel. It also fastens with a simple buckle, so it fits almost any wrist without sizing, a good point if the watch is a surprise gift.

## Leather for a softer look

A smaller number of women's models are on **leather**. Leather is the warmest and most classic strap: it softens with wear and suits office clothes and autumn and winter outfits. Keep it dry, give it a rest after a hot day and store it away from direct sun so its colour lasts.

## Sizing a steel bracelet

Most bracelets on this page are stainless steel with removable links and a folding clasp. Links are taken out in pairs, one from each side of the clasp, so the clasp stays centred under the wrist; most women need 2 to 4 links removed. We size bracelets in our stores in a few minutes, and any watch shop can do it too. Keep the spare links in the box, in case you want the bracelet longer later.

## Water and daily care

Most women's watches here list water resistance on the product page at the 30 or 50 metre level. At 30 metres, rain and washing your hands are fine; at 50 metres, a short swim in a pool too. Take the watch off for the sea and the shower either way. Put the watch on after perfume and creams, which dull plating, and wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day.

## Wearing a watch with bracelets and rings

A women's watch is often worn with other jewellery, so plan the mix. Keep one metal tone across the watch, the bracelets and the rings, or choose a two-tone watch that ties silver and gold together. Wear a thin bangle or a chain bracelet on the same wrist for a stacked look, leaving a little space so metal does not scratch metal, or put bracelets on the other wrist to let the watch stand alone. A small 32 or 36 mm case stacks more easily than a 40 mm one, because it leaves room on the wrist for the rest.

## Day to evening with one watch

Many women want one watch that works from morning to night. A 38 mm silver or two-tone steel bracelet does that best: calm enough for the office, bright enough for dinner. For evenings, swap a silicone strap model for a gold or rose-gold bracelet, or pair a silver watch with a statement ring. If you own only 2 watches, make one a steel bracelet for every day and the other a lighter silicone model for summer and travel.

If the watch is a gift for a bride or a special occasion, rose gold is a delicate choice that matches most women's jewellery and stays wearable long after the day.

## Questions and answers

### Is 38 mm a good size for a woman?

For most women, yes. On a wrist of 14 to 16 centimetres, 38 mm is balanced and easy to read, which is why it is the most common size on this page. Women with very slim wrists, or who prefer a jewellery look, may prefer 32 or 36 mm; those who like a bold look, 40 or 41 mm.

### Will the rose-gold colour fade?

The rose-gold tone is a plating over stainless steel, and with normal care it lasts for years. What wears it is friction and chemicals: perfume, creams, sea water and chlorine. Put the watch on after applying them, wipe it after a hot day and store it apart from other jewellery.

### Can I swim with a Tommy Hilfiger women's watch?

Only if the product page lists 50 metres or more, and then just for a short swim in a pool. Models at 30 metres are for splashes and rain only. Never wear any of them in the sea or a hot shower, and rinse the watch with fresh water if it gets wet with salt or chlorine.
