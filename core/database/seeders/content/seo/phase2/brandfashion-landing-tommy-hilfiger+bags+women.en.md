A Tommy Hilfiger bag is built around three ideas: a practical shape, a clean finish and a little touch of the brand, such as the flag, the monogram or the red, white and navy stripe. This page gathers every women's model we carry, and the guide below sorts them by what matters day to day: the shape, what it holds, the material, the colour, the closure and how it pairs with the rest of your wardrobe.

## The shapes on this page

**Shoulder bags** are the largest group: worn under the arm, they leave both hands free and suit a full day out. **Handbags** with a top handle look the most polished and suit meetings and occasions. **Crossbody bags** are compact and secure, worn across the body. **Totes** are the roomiest, for work and university. **Backpacks** spread weight over both shoulders for long days. Many models combine two of these, for example a handbag with a detachable crossbody strap.

## What fits in each shape

As a guide, a crossbody bag holds a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick. A medium shoulder bag adds a full wallet, sunglasses and a small pouch. A large shoulder bag or a tote takes a tablet, a notebook and a water bottle, and some take a small laptop; check the inside width on the product page, which should be at least 33 centimetres for that. A backpack takes the most, comfortably.

## Monogram canvas or smooth finish

The **monogram** bags are covered in the brand's repeated initials on a coated canvas. They are light, resist light rain and scratches, and wipe clean easily, which makes them some of the most practical bags for daily use. **Smooth** finishes, in leather or leather-look materials, look more polished and formal and suit the office and evenings, but need a little more care. If you use one bag every day in dust and heat, monogram canvas is the most forgiving choice.

## Colours: black, navy, beige and red

**Black** is the most common and the safest; it goes with everything. **Navy**, the brand's own colour, is softer and beautiful with white, beige and denim. **Beige** and light neutrals look fresh in spring and summer and with light-coloured abayas and dresses, though they show marks sooner. **Red** is the statement shade, a confident touch of the brand's flag that lifts a black or white outfit. Choose your first bag in black or navy and a second in beige or red.

## Closures and security

A **zip** top keeps everything inside and is the safest choice on public transport and in crowds. A **flap** with a magnetic snap opens quickly with one hand but is less secure. An open tote is the fastest and the least protected. Inside, a zipped pocket for cash and keys and a slip pocket for your phone save time at the till. The product photos show most bags open, so you can see the layout.

## Bags for work

For the office, choose a structured shoulder bag or a tote in black, navy or beige, with a zip and room for a tablet or a laptop. A structured bag keeps its shape when it is full and looks neat on a desk or a meeting-room chair. If you carry a laptop every day, a backpack with a padded sleeve is kinder to your shoulders, because it spreads the weight of about 1.5 kilograms over 2 straps instead of 1.

## Bags for weekends and travel

For weekends, a crossbody bag is the most useful: light, secure and hands-free for shopping or for children. For travel, it keeps your passport, boarding pass and phone in front of you at airports, while a tote or a backpack carries the rest. Monogram canvas is the best travel material, because it shrugs off scuffs and wipes clean. ## Caring for your bag

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use, or a slightly damp one on monogram canvas, and blot spills right away. Keep it out of a hot parked car in summer, where a 35-degree day can soften coatings and glue, and keep light colours away from dark denim. Store it empty and stuffed with paper in its dust bag. Never use household cleaners or wet wipes.

## Questions and answers

### Is a monogram bag good for everyday use?

Yes, it is one of the most practical choices. The coated monogram canvas is light, resists light rain and scuffs and wipes clean with a slightly damp cloth. It keeps its look through daily use better than many smooth finishes, which is why it suits work, university and travel.

### Which bag fits a laptop?

A large tote, a large shoulder bag or a backpack. Check the inside width on the product page: a 13-inch laptop usually needs at least 33 centimetres, a 14-inch about 35. If you are unsure, send the product link and your laptop model to our customer service on WhatsApp.

### Will a beige bag get dirty quickly?

It shows marks sooner than black or navy, but it stays clean with a few habits: keep it away from dark denim, keep pens and make-up in an inner pouch, and wipe it gently after use. Store it in its dust bag, out of the sun, so it keeps its colour.

### Can I wear a handbag as a crossbody?

If the bag has a detachable long strap or rings for one, yes. The product page and the photos show the straps and how the bag is worn. Bags with only a top handle are made to be carried by hand or on the forearm.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
