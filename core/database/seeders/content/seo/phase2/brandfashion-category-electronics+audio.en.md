Seven products sounds like a small page, but they cover three quite different ways of listening. This guide helps you decide between them by asking 3 questions: where you listen, how long you listen, and whether calls matter as much as music.

## Question 1: where do you listen?

On the metro, in a microbus or on a plane, steady noise is the problem, and active noise cancelling solves it. At the gym, small earbuds that stay put matter more than isolation. At a desk or a laptop, a wired earphone is simple and never runs flat. The answer to this question usually points to 1 of the 3 types on the page.

## Question 2: how long do you listen?

For an hour or two at a time, earbuds are the most comfortable. For a 5-hour flight or a long study session, over-ear headphones spread the pressure around the ear instead of inside it, and their battery lasts longer. Wired earphones have no battery limit at all, which is why many people keep a pair as a spare.

## Question 3: do calls matter?

ENC, environmental noise cancellation, cleans up your voice for the person on the other end by filtering wind and traffic around the microphone. The earbuds with ANC and ENC combine both: quiet for you, clear for them. If you take many calls on the street or in a busy office, that combination is the one to choose.

## Fit and comfort

In-ear models come with silicone tips in 3 sizes; the right one seals gently, improves the bass and reduces outside noise. If an earbud slips out, try a bigger tip. Half in-ear earphones fit most ears without tips. For over-ear headphones, adjust the band so the cushions sit evenly and do not press on the glasses arms if you wear them.

## Phones without a headphone jack

Many recent phones have only a Type-C or Lightning port. With those, wireless earbuds or headphones are the simplest choice. Wired 3.5 mm earphones still work with a small Type-C or Lightning adapter, and they always work with laptops, many tablets and older phones.

## Battery tips

Charge earbuds and their case with a 5 to 20 W charger; a stronger charger is safe too. Turning ANC off in a quiet room extends playback noticeably. If earbuds are not used for weeks, charge them every month or so, because a fully empty battery ages faster. The [chargers page](/subtypes/chargers) has suitable cables.

## Hygiene and care

Clean the tips and the mesh with a dry soft brush every 7 days; earwax blocks sound and makes earbuds seem quieter. Wipe headphone cushions with a slightly damp cloth. Keep audio products away from water unless the product page states a rating, and do not leave them in a hot car.

## Listening safely

A simple rule is 60 percent volume for no more than 60 minutes, then a short break. Noise cancelling helps, because you do not need to turn the volume up to drown out the metro. On the street, keep the volume low or use 1 earbud so you can hear traffic around you.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between ANC and ENC?

ANC, active noise cancelling, reduces the noise you hear, using microphones and an opposite sound wave. ENC, environmental noise cancellation, reduces the noise the other person hears when you are on a call, by filtering the sound around your microphone. Some earbuds on this page offer both at once.

### Are half in-ear earphones comfortable?

Many people find them more comfortable than in-ear models, because they rest at the entrance of the ear instead of sealing the canal. They let some outside sound in, which is useful on the street but less suited to noisy transport. They suit long wear at a desk or a laptop.

### Do wireless earbuds work with iPhone?

Yes. They connect by Bluetooth to iPhones, Android phones, tablets and laptops. Open the charging case, put the earbuds in pairing mode as the leaflet explains, and select them in the Bluetooth settings. After the first time, they reconnect automatically when you open the case.
