Armani Exchange is the street-style line of the Armani group, created in 1991 to bring the Milan house's design to a younger, city audience at more accessible prices. Where Giorgio Armani is quiet tailoring, A|X is denim, sneakers and bold graphics. Its bags follow that idea: practical shapes, mostly black, and a logo that is part of the design rather than hidden. This guide explains the shapes in our range, who each suits, and how to keep a bag looking new.

## Totes for work and university

The **tote** is the largest group of women's Armani Exchange bags in our shop. A tote has two handles, a wide opening and a roomy inside, so it carries a tablet, a notebook, a water bottle and a small pouch without effort. Most of ours are black with an embossed or printed A|X logo, and one is off-white for a lighter summer look. If you carry a laptop every day, check the width on the product page against your laptop before ordering; a tote with a zip top is the safer choice in crowded places.

## Chain bags and shoulder bags

**Chain bags** hang from a metal chain strap, sometimes woven with leather, and they are the dressiest shapes in the range: small enough for an evening, with a flap and a logo clasp. The **crescent** shoulder bag has a curved, modern silhouette that sits neatly under the arm. These suit dinners, weddings and evenings out, and they pair well with silver jewellery and a steel watch. A monogram version in black and silver or white and grey adds pattern without colour. A chain strap can often be worn on the shoulder or doubled and carried by hand, giving you two looks from one bag.

## Crossbody and camera bags

A **crossbody bag** is worn across the body on a long strap, keeping your hands free and your phone, keys and cards in front of you. The **camera bag** is a compact, boxy crossbody with a logo plaque, named for its shape rather than its use. Both are the most practical everyday bags for errands, shopping and travel, and both work with jeans and trainers, which is exactly the Armani Exchange look. Choose black for the most versatile option; it goes with every colour and hides daily wear better than lighter shades.

## The A|X logo and the finishes

Armani Exchange uses its logo in three ways, and the product title tells you which: **embossed**, pressed into the surface for a tone-on-tone look; **printed**, often as a monogram pattern across the whole bag; and **plaque** or **charm**, a metal piece on the front or the strap. Embossed logos are the most discreet; printed monograms the most recognisable; metal plaques the dressiest. Silver-tone hardware is the norm across the range, which suits steel watches and silver jewellery.

## Keeping a bag looking new

Most Armani Exchange bags are made of coated or synthetic materials that resist light rain and are easy to clean. Wipe them with a slightly damp cloth, never with household cleaners or alcohol, which can strip the finish and the print. Keep them out of a hot car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside of a parked car gets far hotter, and heat can soften the coating and mark it. Store a bag stuffed with paper so it keeps its shape, and keep light colours such as off-white away from denim, which can transfer its dye when it rubs against the bag.

## Questions and answers

### Is Armani Exchange the same as Emporio Armani?

Both belong to the Armani group, but they are different lines. Emporio Armani is the more refined, fashion-led line; Armani Exchange is the casual, street-style one, with bolder logos and denim-inspired designs. A|X bags are usually more practical and more logo-forward, and they are designed for everyday city life.

### Will a tote fit my laptop?

Check the width and height on the product page against your laptop's measurements, adding a little room for the handles to close comfortably. If you are unsure, send the product link and your laptop size to our customer service on WhatsApp.

### How do I clean an Armani Exchange bag?

For everyday dust, a soft dry cloth is enough. For marks, use a cloth that is only slightly damp and dry the bag afterwards. Do not use household cleaners, alcohol or wet wipes with chemicals, which can damage the coating and fade the logo print. Let the bag dry at room temperature, out of direct sun.
