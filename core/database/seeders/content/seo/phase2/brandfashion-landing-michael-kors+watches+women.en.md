Michael Kors gives almost every women's watch a line name, and the name tells you most of what you need to know before you look at the colour. This guide groups the lines we have in stock by their character: everyday classics, crystal watches, chronographs, sporty models, small and delicate cases, and unusual dials. Find the group that fits you, then compare the lines inside it.

## The everyday classics: Runway and Runway Slim

**Runway** is the brand's signature round watch: a clean steel case, a simple dial with baton markers and a link bracelet in gold, rose gold or silver. Runway Slim keeps the same look in a thinner case that sits flatter and slides under a sleeve. Together they are the starting point for a woman who wants one watch for every day.

## Crystal watches: Darci and Portia

**Darci** frames the dial with a full ring of crystals, the line's signature evening look. Portia is more delicate: crystal accents on a slim case, with dials in gold, champagne, rose gold or a black dial with a star pattern. Choose Darci for a bold sparkle and Portia when you prefer a finer, jewellery-like shine.

## More crystal: Colette, Maren and Lennox

**Colette** sets crystals across the dial, in one version in several colours on a gold dial, which makes it a playful party watch. Maren pairs a gold dial with crystals for a warm, rich look, and Lennox adds crystals around a rose gold dial with an MK logo. All three suit occasions and look best with simple clothes.

## The chronographs: Parker, Bradshaw and Ritz

A chronograph adds a stopwatch and three sub-dials. **Parker** combines it with a crystal-set bezel, glamorous and sporty at once. Bradshaw is the bolder steel chronograph, including a Mini version for a smaller wrist. Ritz is a classic chronograph in silver. If you like a busy, dressy dial, start with these three.

## More chronographs: Brinkley, Sofie, Slater and Everest

**Brinkley** comes with a rose gold dial or a blue dial on a two-tone bracelet with crystals. Sofie offers a crystal dial or a chronograph on a steel and acetate bracelet, Slater stays in rose gold, and Everest pairs a black dial with a gold bracelet and crystals. Each gives the chronograph look a slightly different mood.

## Sporty and colourful: Camille

**Camille** sets a crystal dial in a gold or rose gold case on a coloured rubber strap: black, white, blue or pink. It is the most relaxed line here, comfortable in the heat and easy to rinse after the beach or the gym. The colour of the strap makes it fun to match with trainers, a sports bag or a summer dress.

## Small and delicate: Pyper and Mini Emery

**Pyper** is a small, fine watch with a white dial, in rose gold steel with crystals, as a Mini Pyper on a gold mesh bracelet, or as a set with a brown strap and a matching rose gold bracelet. Mini Emery is another compact model. These suit a slim wrist and a woman who prefers a watch that reads as jewellery.

## Unusual dials: Jaryn and Portia's star

**Jaryn** stands out with a heart-shaped motif on a rose gold dial, or as a black dial on a black metal bangle that slips on like a bracelet. Portia's black star dial is another original face. Choose one of these when you want a watch that people notice for its design rather than its sparkle.

## Leather and mother-of-pearl: Layton

**Layton** shows the softer side of the brand: a white dial on a beige leather strap, a pink dial on rose gold steel, and a white mother-of-pearl dial on gold. Mother-of-pearl changes colour gently with the light, from white to pale pink. Layton suits a woman who likes a delicate, feminine watch without crystals.

## Comparing two lines

When two lines look similar, compare three things on the product pages: the **case size**, the bracelet and the dial. A Darci and a Portia may share a metal tone, but Darci's crystal ring makes it look larger. A Runway and a Runway Slim may share a dial, but Slim is thinner. The photos and the case size settle it quickly.

## Gold, rose gold, silver or two-tone

**Rose gold** suits warm skin tones and rose gold jewellery; yellow gold looks rich and pairs with gold rings; silver goes with white gold and a cooler wardrobe; two-tone bridges both if you wear mixed jewellery. Whatever the line, choose the metal you wear most, so the watch works with your rings and bracelets every day.

## Sizes and wrists

Most women's models here are 38 to 39 mm, with smaller cases of 36 to 37 mm in the Mini and delicate lines and larger chronographs at 40 to 43 mm. On a wrist below about **15** cm, choose the smaller cases; on an average wrist, 38 to 39 mm looks balanced; large chronographs suit a wider wrist.

## Water and everyday wear

Check the rating on each product page. 50 m (**5 ATM**), the most common here, allows a short pool swim; 30 m (3 ATM) means splashes and rain only; 100 m (10 ATM) or more is fine for the sea. Leather straps should stay dry, and crystals keep their sparkle when you wipe them after perfume and hand cream.

## Questions and answers

### Which Michael Kors line has crystals around the dial?

Darci, with a full ring of crystals framing the dial. Parker sets crystals on the bezel of a chronograph, Portia adds finer crystal accents, and Colette, Maren and Lennox place crystals on or around the dial. The product photos show the crystals in close-up for each model.

### What is the difference between Runway and Runway Slim?

Both share the Runway name and its clean round design with baton markers. Runway Slim uses a thinner case, so it sits flatter on the wrist and slides under a fitted sleeve more easily. The dials and metal tones are similar, so the choice comes down to how slim you want the watch to be.

### Which line suits the beach or the gym?

Camille, with a crystal dial in a gold or rose gold case on a coloured rubber strap that copes with sweat and rinses clean. Check the water rating on the product page before swimming: 50 m allows a short pool swim, while 100 m or more is the rating for the sea.

### Which watch suits a slim wrist?

The small, delicate lines: Pyper, Mini Pyper, Mini Emery and Bradshaw Mini, or a Portia with its slim case. On a wrist below about 15 cm, a case of 36 to 37 mm keeps the watch in proportion. The case size is on every product page, so you can compare before you order.
