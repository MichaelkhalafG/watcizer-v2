A man's bag used to be a briefcase or nothing. Today a phone, a power bank, earphones, keys and a wallet add up to more than any pocket should carry, and a bag that sits close to the body keeps trousers and jackets in shape. This page brings together every men's Tommy Hilfiger bag at Brand Fashion, and the guide below sorts them by how you carry them, what they hold and when they make sense.

## Three ways to carry a men's bag

There are three main ways, and each suits a different day. **Across the chest**, a small crossbody or chest bag sits in front of you, the most secure position in crowds and on public transport. **At the hip**, the same bag on a longer strap is more relaxed and easier to open while walking. **On the shoulder**, a messenger or a larger crossbody bag carries more weight comfortably. Most straps on this page are adjustable, so one bag can move between these positions.

## Small crossbody and chest bags

The smallest bags here are made for the essentials — 5 things: a phone, a card holder, keys, earphones and sunglasses. They are light, flat and quick to open, and they suit errands, weekends, cafés and evenings out. Choose a model with an outer pocket for your phone if you reach for it often, and a zipped inner pocket for cash and keys. Black hides daily marks best; navy adds the brand's colour without being loud.

## Messenger and laptop bags

For work and university, a **messenger** bag with a flap or a zipped laptop bag is the practical choice. It takes a tablet or a laptop, a notebook, chargers and documents, and it looks smarter than a backpack in an office. Before you order, check the inside width on the product page against your device: a 13-inch laptop usually needs about 33 centimetres, a 15-inch about 38.

## Hand pouches and clutches

A **hand pouch** is a flat bag carried in the hand or under the arm, often with a wrist strap. It holds a passport, a phone, cards and cash, and it is popular for weddings, evenings and travel days, when a strap across a shirt is not wanted. It also slides into a larger bag to keep small things organised. Several pouches in our range come in gift packaging, which makes them easy presents.

## Backpacks for long days

A **backpack** spreads the weight over both shoulders, so it is the most comfortable choice for long commutes, students and anyone who walks a lot. The Tommy Hilfiger backpacks here keep a clean, structured look with a small logo, smart enough for many workplaces. Look for a padded laptop sleeve, a front pocket for things you need quickly and wide shoulder straps that stay comfortable when the bag is full all day.

## Travel pouches

Travel pouches and wash bags keep toiletries, chargers or documents together inside a suitcase or a gym bag. They have wipeable linings and zipped tops, and they make thoughtful small gifts for someone who travels for work. A pouch of about 25 centimetres holds a full set of toiletries for a week away, and a smaller one keeps cables and chargers tidy in a backpack or a carry-on, so nothing tangles at the bottom of the bag.

## Materials and colours

You will find smooth and textured **leather**, **coated canvas** with the monogram, and tough **nylon** or fabric. Leather looks the smartest and softens with use; coated canvas and nylon are lighter, shrug off light rain and wipe clean easily, which suits daily use in dust and heat. Black is the most common colour on this page, followed by navy, with brown and grey among the rest.

## Caring for your bag

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use and blot spills straight away. Keep it out of a hot parked car in summer, where a 35-degree day can soften coatings and glue. Empty it regularly so it keeps its shape, and store it stuffed with paper. Do not use household cleaners or wet wipes on leather or coated canvas.

## Questions and answers

### Which is better, a crossbody or a chest bag?

They are often the same bag worn differently. Worn high across the chest, it is the most secure in crowds; worn lower at the hip, it is more relaxed. If you take public transport often, keep it high and in front; if you drive, the hip position is perfectly practical.

### Will my laptop fit?

Check the inside width and height on the product page against your laptop. A 13-inch laptop usually needs about 33 centimetres of inside width and a 15-inch about 38. If you are unsure, send our customer service the product link and your laptop model on WhatsApp before you order.

### Is a hand pouch practical for every day?

It is ideal for light days, evenings and travel, when you carry a phone, a wallet and a passport. For a full working day with chargers and documents, a messenger or a backpack is more practical. Many men use a pouch inside a larger bag to keep small items together.

### How do I clean a nylon or canvas bag?

Wipe it with a slightly damp cloth and let it dry at room temperature. Do not put it in a washing machine or use strong cleaners, which can damage the coating and the logo. For leather details, use a dry cloth only.

### Can I return a bag?
