Lacoste was founded in France in 1933 by René Lacoste, a tennis champion nicknamed "the Crocodile", who put the animal on the chest of a short-sleeved tennis shirt and created one of the first visible logos in fashion. Nearly a century later, the crocodile still stands for relaxed, sporty elegance. The accessories we carry bring that spirit to small, everyday pieces, and this guide explains how to choose and wear them.

## Steel bracelets: Metropole and Arthor

The men's bracelets in our Lacoste range are made of stainless steel, so they do not rust, tarnish or react to sweat. **Metropole** bracelets use flat links, some embossed with the **Petit Piqué** pattern borrowed from the texture of the brand's famous polo shirt, and some with an "L" detail; they come in silver steel and in black. **Arthor** is a sleeker, adjustable design that suits a slimmer wrist. A steel bracelet is the easiest piece of men's jewellery to wear: it looks right with a T-shirt, a shirt or a suit.

## Wearing a bracelet with a watch

Because we are a watch shop, the most common question we hear is how to wear a bracelet beside a watch. The simple rule is to match the metal and the mood. A silver steel Lacoste bracelet goes with a steel watch; a black bracelet goes with a black-dial or black-strap watch. Wear the bracelet on the other wrist if you want each piece to stand out, or on the same wrist, just above the watch, for a stacked look, leaving a little space so the steel does not scratch the watch case. A sporty chronograph and a Metropole bracelet make a natural weekend pair.

## Wallets with the crocodile

Our Lacoste wallets are men's leather wallets in black, with the crocodile logo in metal, embossed or printed in white. A **bifold** wallet holds cards on both sides and notes in a long pocket, and it fits a trouser pocket; a **vertical** wallet is narrower and slimmer, made for a jacket's inside pocket or a light everyday load. Textured finishes, such as the crosshatch pattern, hide small scratches better than smooth leather. Count your cards before choosing: a slim wallet stays slim only if you carry 6 to 8 cards, not 15.

## The leather belt

The Lacoste belt in our range is black leather with a silver-tone pin buckle and a discreet crocodile. A pin buckle is the most classic and the easiest to adjust: it fits through the holes like any traditional belt, so you can loosen or tighten it in a second. Match it with black shoes and a silver-tone watch case for a coordinated look. Choose the length so the buckle closes on the middle hole, which leaves 2 holes either side for adjustment. If the belt is too long, a leather shop can usually shorten it at the buckle end.

## Caring for steel and leather

Steel bracelets need very little care: rinse them with fresh water after the sea or a sweaty day, dry them with a soft cloth and store them in their pouch so they do not scratch other jewellery. Leather wallets and belts last longest when they are kept dry. Do not leave a wallet in a back pocket all day in the heat, empty it regularly so it keeps its shape, and wipe a belt with a dry cloth after a hot day. Avoid household cleaners and perfume on both steel and leather. Every 2 or 3 weeks, a dry microfibre cloth brings back the shine of a steel bracelet.

## The sporty Lacoste look, every day

Lacoste was born on a tennis court, and its accessories still suit a relaxed, sporty way of dressing. A classic combination is a polo shirt, chinos and white trainers, finished with a steel Metropole bracelet and a watch on a steel bracelet or a rubber strap. For the office, swap the polo for a shirt and add the black leather belt with its pin buckle. The **Petit Piqué** texture on some bracelets echoes the fabric of the brand's polo, a subtle nod that works without a large logo. Keep colours simple — navy, white, grey and black — and let the crocodile be the only branding you wear that day.

If you like to mix sporty and formal, a steel bracelet with a watch on a brown leather strap and a white shirt is a balanced look that works for the office and a relaxed evening alike.

## Questions and answers

### Will a stainless steel bracelet change colour?

No. Stainless steel does not rust or tarnish with normal wear, and it does not turn your skin green. Black versions are coated, and the coating can show wear at the edges after a long time, especially if the bracelet rubs against a desk or a watch. Rinse and dry the bracelet now and then to keep it looking new.

### How do I size a Lacoste bracelet?

Link bracelets can be adjusted by removing links, which any jeweller or watch shop can do in a few minutes; adjustable designs such as Arthor can be set by hand. Measure your wrist with a tape and compare it with the length on the product page, leaving about one finger's width of space for comfort.

### What is the Petit Piqué pattern?

It is a small, raised texture inspired by the knitted fabric of the classic Lacoste polo shirt. On the Metropole bracelets it is pressed into the steel links, so the bracelet has a subtle pattern that catches the light. It is a detail that Lacoste fans recognise immediately, without needing a large logo.
