Hugo Boss designs its women's watches with the same idea as its tailoring: a strong, clean line and one well-placed detail. On most of these watches that detail is a bezel set with crystals or a dial that shimmers with a sunray finish. This guide helps you choose among them by size, metal, bracelet and occasion, then covers care, stock and ordering.

## Sizes from 32 to 38 mm

The women's Hugo Boss range here runs from **32** to 38 mm, with 34 mm the most common. 32 mm reads as a classic, delicate dress watch; 34 to 35 mm is balanced on most wrists of 14 to 16 centimetres; 36 to 38 mm looks bold and modern. Compare the case size with a watch you already own before choosing.

## The detail: crystals on the bezel

Many models carry crystal-style accents around the **bezel**, sometimes beside the BOSS lettering. The crystals frame the dial rather than cover it, so the watch stays easy to read while it sparkles. This is the design feature that ties the range together, from the Breath to the Signature and the Infinity.

## The dial: sunray finishes

Most dials are **sunray** finished, brushed with fine lines that radiate from the centre. Under a lamp or in sunlight, a band of light sweeps across the dial as you move. Green, silver, grey and black dials all take on a deeper colour in the shade and a brighter one in the light.

## Choosing the metal

**Silver** steel is the everyday choice and goes with white gold and silver jewellery. **Gold** tone suits warm skin and yellow-gold rings. **Two-tone** steel with a rose-gold bezel bridges both. **Gunmetal**, a dark grey steel, is the understated modern option, and **black** steel is the boldest look in the range.

## Green dials: the Breath

The **Breath** is the line to look at if you want colour. We carry it with an emerald green dial in gold tone and with a light green dial in silver, both with crystal accents on the bezel. Green is a fresh alternative to black and white dials: it adds colour while staying elegant.

## The classic silver: Signature

The **Signature** keeps everything silver: dial, case, crystal-accented bezel and a bracelet with brushed and polished links. It is the watch that suits every outfit and every age, which makes it the most practical choice for daily wear and the easiest gift.

## The darker side: Allusion and Infinity

For a modern, darker look, the **Allusion** pairs a grey dial and rose-gold hands with a gunmetal case, and the **Infinity** combines a black dial and crystal bezel with a black mesh bracelet. Both suit black outfits, evening wear and anyone who prefers a cool, contemporary style over shine.

## Link bracelets and mesh

Most models close with a **folding clasp** on a steel link bracelet, sized by removing links at any watch shop. A **mesh** bracelet, as on the Infinity, is woven from fine steel and adjusts through its clasp instead. Mesh sits flatter and feels lighter, while links look more structured and jewellery-like.

## Water: what the rating means

The specifications list water resistance in metres or ATM: **3 ATM** equals 30 metres and 5 ATM equals 50 metres. At 30 metres the watch is for splashes, such as rain and washing your hands. At 50 metres a short swim in a pool is fine, but not the sea. Take the watch off before a hot shower.

## By occasion

For the office, the silver Signature or a two-tone model at 34 mm. For weddings and evenings, the gold-tone Breath with its emerald dial. For a modern, all-black look, the Infinity. For weekends and travel, a silver Breath with a light green dial, fresh and easy to match with casual clothes.

## Care for crystals and plating

Crystals and gold or rose-gold plating both dislike chemicals. Put the watch on after perfume and creams, wipe it with a soft, dry **cloth** after a hot day and keep it in its box rather than with harder jewellery. Take it off for sport and housework, where a knock can loosen a crystal.

## Battery and accuracy

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside, and have the date and time set again after the change.

## Questions and answers

### Which women's Hugo Boss size should I choose?

For most women, 34 mm is the balanced choice: easy to read, elegant and comfortable under a sleeve. Choose 32 mm for a delicate, classic look on a slim wrist, and 36 to 38 mm if you like a bold, modern watch. Measuring your wrist helps; about 14 to 16 centimetres suits 34 mm well.

### Are the bezel crystals real stones?

They are crystal-style accents, cut and set to catch the light; they are not diamonds, which keeps the watches at a fashion price. They are fixed firmly, but a hard knock can loosen one, so remove the watch for sport, housework and anything that might strike the wrist.

### Can I wear a women's Hugo Boss watch in the pool?

Only if its page lists 50 metres or 5 ATM, and then for a short swim. A 30-metre or 3 ATM watch is for splashes only. Never wear any of them in the sea, and rinse the watch with fresh water after the pool, then dry it with a soft cloth.

### Does a women's Hugo Boss watch have a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered.
