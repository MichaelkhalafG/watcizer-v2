## Reading a Tommy Hilfiger title

A typical title gives you the line, the dial colour and the strap. Line names such as **TH85**, **Stewart**, **Bryan**, **Lars**, **Baker**, **Tyson**, **Weston**, **Decker** and **Kane** tell you the style; many titles also use the word **Classic** for the brand's everyday three-hand and multi-dial designs. Then comes the dial colour, and often the strap material. Searching a line name in the box above shows all its versions together, which is the fastest way to compare colours of the same watch.

## Leather or steel: almost an even split

On this page, steel bracelets and leather straps are almost equally common, with rubber and silicone after them. **Steel** is durable, easy to clean and right for every day; it needs sizing, which takes a few minutes in our stores. **Leather** is warmer and dressier, and it ages with character, but it prefers dry days and a rest after a hot one. If you wear one watch every day in summer, choose steel; if the watch is mainly for work and evenings, leather looks more refined.

## Rubber and silicone for heat and sport

Rubber and silicone straps are the comfortable choice for Egyptian summers, the gym, the beach-side café and travel. They are light, they do not absorb sweat and they rinse clean under the tap. They fasten with a buckle and a row of holes, so they fit most wrists straight from the box, which also makes them a safe choice for a gift. Sporty lines such as Stewart and Tyson often come on rubber.

## Sizes from 40 to 50 mm

The most common case size on this page is 44 millimetres, followed by 46 mm, with smaller 40 to 43 mm models and a few large 48 and 50 mm pieces. A simple wrist guide helps: under about 16.5 centimetres, choose 40 to 42 mm; between 16.5 and 18.5 centimetres, 44 mm is the natural size; above that, 46 mm and up looks balanced. Measure your wrist with a tape, and remember that a leather strap makes a watch look slightly smaller than a steel bracelet does.

## Dial colours and what they say

**Black** is the most common dial here and the most versatile, as comfortable with a T-shirt as with a suit. **Blue**, the brand's own navy, is the most Tommy Hilfiger choice and pairs naturally with denim and white shirts. **Silver** and **white** dials look classic and formal, ideal for the office. Grey, green and red accents are the ones to pick if you want a second watch that stands out from the first.

## Checking water resistance

Most watches on this page list 50 metres of water resistance on the product page. That means rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but the sea and diving are not, and the pushers of a chronograph should never be pressed under water. A few models list higher ratings; if you plan to swim often, choose one of those and rinse it with fresh water afterwards.

## Multi-dial, chronograph and three-hand dials

Three small sub-dials on a Tommy Hilfiger dial can mean two different things. On a **chronograph**, they count the minutes and seconds of a stopwatch started and stopped by the 2 pushers beside the crown. On a **multi-function** watch, the same sub-dials show the day, the date and a 24-hour scale instead, and there is no stopwatch. A **three-hand** watch keeps only hours, minutes and seconds, which makes it the thinnest and the easiest to read at a glance. The product page names the functions of each model, so check it if a working stopwatch matters to you.

## Looking after a leather strap

Leather is one of the most common straps on this page, and a little care makes it last for years. Do not wear it in the shower, the pool or the sea, because water makes leather swell and then crack as it dries. In summer, rotate it with a steel or rubber watch so sweat can dry out, and wipe the inside of the strap with a dry cloth. Keep perfume and creams off it. When a strap finally wears out, it can be replaced at any watch shop; the strap width, usually 20 or 22 millimetres on men's models, is in the specifications on the product page.

To keep a leather strap beautiful for longer, store the watch in its box away from direct sun, because strong light fades the colour of leather over time.

## Questions and answers

### What does "Classic" mean in a Tommy Hilfiger title?

It is the word the brand and our titles use for its core everyday men's watches: clean three-hand designs and multi-dial models in steel, leather or rubber. A "Classic" watch is a versatile, everyday design rather than a specific line, and the rest of the title tells you its colour and strap.

### Should my first Tommy Hilfiger watch be on leather or steel?

For one watch worn every day, steel: it handles heat, sweat and knocks better and can be cleaned in seconds. For a watch kept mostly for work and evenings, leather looks dressier. A sensible order is to start with steel and add a leather-strap model later.

### Is 46 mm too big for me?

It depends on your wrist. Above about 18.5 centimetres, 46 mm looks balanced; on a slimmer wrist it can look oversized, and a 44 mm model is the better fit. Compare the case size with a watch you already own before you order.

### Do all these watches have a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains what is covered.

### Can I exchange a watch for another colour?
