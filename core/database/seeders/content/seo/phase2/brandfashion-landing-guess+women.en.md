Guess makes it easy to dress a whole look from one brand: a watch, a bag and a wallet that share the same gold tones, logo and spirit. This page gathers everything we carry for women, and the guide below is organised around the year in Egypt, from the university term to summer, the wedding season and Eid, so you can see which pieces suit each moment, and which make the best gifts.

## Watches, bags and wallets on one page

Watches and bags make up almost all of this page, in roughly equal numbers, with a smaller group of **wallets** and a few accessories. The watches are quartz, mostly 34 to 40 mm; the bags range from mini bags to large totes. The [women's Guess watches](/brand/guess/watches/women), [Guess bags](/brand/guess/bags) and [women's Guess wallets](/brand/guess/wallets/women) pages explain each family in depth.

## Back to university and work

A new term or a new job calls for pieces that carry a full day. A **tote** or a structured satchel such as Cordelia holds a notebook, a water bottle and a long wallet. Pair it with a 36 mm steel watch with a white or silver dial, easy to read in a lecture or a meeting, and a black zip-around wallet that hides marks.

## Summer days

In the heat, light pieces win. A watch on white or blue **silicone**, such as Athena or Princess, is cool on the wrist and rinses clean after the beach café. A small crossbody bag keeps your hands free on holiday, and a pink or white wallet adds a cheerful colour that is easy to find in the bag.

## The wedding season

For weddings and engagements, let one piece sparkle. A crystal watch such as **Afterglow**, Crush or Bedazzle pairs well with a mini bag or a top-handle bag such as Amara, which holds only a phone, a card holder and keys. Leave the long wallet at home; a quilted wallet can stay in the car for the drive back.

## Ramadan and Eid

Iftar invitations, family visits and Eid outings mix formal and relaxed moments. A **rose-gold** steel watch goes with both, and a brown 4G shoulder bag looks warm and classic beside traditional or modern clothes. Eid is also a season of gifts, and a Guess wallet is an easy present for a sister or a cousin.

## Graduation and new beginnings

A graduation, a first job or a move to a new city deserves a gift that lasts. A classic **36 mm** steel watch in her metal tone is the one she will wear every working day for years, and a black satchel or tote is the bag she will carry to her first interviews. Add a matching wallet, and the set feels complete without being showy.

## Winter

Cooler months suit richer textures. A tweed or bouclé tote such as **Cessily**, a latte monogram bag or a quilted shoulder bag looks right with coats and knitwear. On the wrist, a gold watch on leather or a mesh bracelet sits comfortably under a sleeve and does not catch on wool.

## One metal tone across the pieces

The simplest rule for a Guess look is one metal tone. Gold-tone buckles and logos on the bag and the wallet go with a **gold** or rose-gold watch; silver details go with a steel watch. Colours of the bag and the watch strap can differ freely, as long as the metal matches.

## Logo or no logo

Many Guess bags and wallets carry the **4G** monogram, the brand's interlocking G letters. It looks best on one piece at a time: a monogram bag with a plain wallet and a simple watch reads as confident, while three logo pieces together can feel busy. If you prefer a quiet look, choose a plain black bag with a small metal logo.

## Gifts by person

For a student, a rose-gold watch on silicone or a pink quilted wallet. For a sister or a friend, a crossbody bag in her favourite colour. For a fiancée or a wife, a crystal watch in the metal tone of her jewellery. For a mother, a classic 36 mm steel watch or a structured satchel. A wallet is always safe, since there is no size involved.

## Caring for a Guess set

Keep the watch out of the outer pockets of the bag, where keys and coins scratch the glass and crystals. Store bags stuffed with paper in their **dust bags**, wallets in their boxes and the watch in its case. Wipe everything with a soft cloth, never alcohol, and put perfume on before the watch, not after.

## Questions and answers

### Can I order a Guess bag and a watch together?

Yes. Add both to the basket and check out once; we confirm the whole order with you before it ships. With cash on delivery you can inspect every piece before you pay, and if one is not right you can return it within 14 days and keep the others.

### Which Guess piece is best for Eid gifts?

A wallet is the easiest, because there is no size to choose: a quilted pink or white one for a young woman, a black monogram or pebbled one for a mother or an aunt. For a bigger gift, a watch in her usual metal tone or a crossbody bag in her favourite colour.

### Do the watch and the bag need to match exactly?

No. Matching the metal tone is enough: gold-tone hardware on the bag with a gold or rose-gold watch, silver with steel. The colours of the bag and the strap can differ. A black bag goes with every watch, which makes it the easiest starting point.

### Why are some Guess pieces out of stock?

Each design arrives in small numbers, and a colour can sell out between deliveries. The product page always shows whether a piece is in stock. If it is not, ask us on WhatsApp whether it is expected again, or choose a similar piece that is available now.

### Can I return part of an order?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange any piece, and we pay the return shipping. It must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a watch bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup.
