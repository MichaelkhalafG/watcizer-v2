## Totes: room for the day

A **tote** has two long handles that sit on the shoulder and an interior large enough for a notebook, a water bottle, a wallet and a scarf. Choose one for work, university or long days out; the black monogram tote and the colour-block totes are the in-stock options.

## Crossbody and camera bags

A **crossbody** bag rides across the body on a long strap, keeping your hands free and the bag in sight, which makes it the safest choice in crowds. A camera bag is a compact, boxy crossbody for a phone, cards and keys. Both suit errands, travel and evenings when you carry little.

## Shoulder bags and backpacks

A **shoulder** bag sits under the arm and opens quickly, ideal for daily errands and the office. A backpack spreads the weight over both shoulders for university or a day of walking. Calvin Klein's colour-block backpack in vanilla, caramel and brown looks softer and more feminine than a sports backpack.

## Choosing the size

Think about the largest thing you carry every day. A phone and cards need only a crossbody or camera bag. A long wallet, sunglasses and a power bank need a shoulder bag. A notebook, a **tablet** or a water bottle need a tote or a backpack. Each product page lists the measurements, so compare them with a bag you already own.

## Choosing the colour

**Black** is the most practical, hides marks and suits a city look. Vanilla and caramel are warm and soft for spring and summer. Brown, khaki and white are fresh with denim. If you are buying one bag for everything, black or the black and brown block is the safest choice.

## Materials

Many Calvin Klein bags are made of synthetic leather, often **polyurethane** or PU, which is lighter than leather, holds its colour and wipes clean easily. Some styles are listed as leather. The product page names the material of each bag, so check it if a particular material matters to you.

## Care

Wipe synthetic leather with a soft, slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol or strong cleaners, and dry it in the shade. Stuff the bag with paper to keep its shape when you store it, keep it in its **dust bag**, and keep light colours away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.
