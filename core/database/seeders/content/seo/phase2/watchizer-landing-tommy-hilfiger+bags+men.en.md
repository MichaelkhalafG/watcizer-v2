Men's bags have become an everyday accessory in Egypt, and for good reason: a phone, a wallet, keys, earphones and a power bank no longer fit comfortably in trouser pockets, and a good bag keeps them together without spoiling the shape of your clothes. Tommy Hilfiger's men's bags do this in the brand's clean, mostly black style. This guide explains the types on this page and which one suits which day.

## The crossbody bag: the everyday choice

The **crossbody bag** is the largest group on this page. It is a small, flat bag on a long strap worn across the chest or the hip, sized for the things you carry every day: phone, wallet, keys and sunglasses. It keeps everything in front of you, which is safer in crowded streets and malls, and it leaves both hands free. Vertical crossbody bags, taller than they are wide, are the slimmest; the **Signature Stripe** versions add the brand's red, white and navy stripe as a detail. If you only buy one men's bag, this is the one.

## Hand bags and clutches

A men's **hand bag** or **clutch** is a flat pouch carried in the hand or under the arm, often with a wrist strap. It holds a phone, a passport, cards and cash, and it suits evenings, weddings and travel days when you do not want a strap across your shirt, and it slips easily into a car's glovebox or a larger bag. Many of ours come in a **gift box**, in black leather or textured fabric, which makes them one of the easiest ready-made presents in the shop. Some carry a co-branded edition logo, shown in the photos.

## Messenger, reporter and briefcase

For work, a larger bag makes sense. A **messenger** or **reporter** bag has a flap and a longer strap and takes a tablet, a notebook and documents; it suits university and offices where a briefcase would feel too formal. The **briefcase** is the most formal shape, with a top handle and room for a laptop and files. Before you order, compare the width on the product page with your laptop or tablet: a 13-inch device usually needs about 33 centimetres of inside width.

## Backpack and wash bags

The **backpack** is the most comfortable way to carry weight every day, with the load on both shoulders; it is the natural choice for students and long commutes. **Wash bags** are travel pouches for toiletries, with a wipeable lining, and they make a practical gift for someone who travels often. Both keep the brand's black, low-key look. A wash bag is also handy in a gym bag, keeping toiletries away from clean clothes.

## Leather, pebbled leather and fabric

Smooth **leather** looks the smartest and develops a soft sheen with use. **Pebbled** leather has a small grain that hides scratches, which makes it the toughest choice for a bag used daily. Textured **fabric** and coated materials are lighter and easier to wipe clean after a dusty day. Black is by far the most common colour here, because it goes with everything and hides wear; navy, blue and olive green are the options if you want a little colour.

## Choosing by the shape of your week

Think about a normal week rather than one day. If you drive to an office and carry a laptop, a messenger bag or the briefcase is worth it, with a crossbody bag for the weekend. If you take the metro or a microbus, a crossbody bag worn in front, or a backpack, keeps your things secure in the crowd. If you work from cafés and coworking spaces, a backpack with a padded section for a laptop is the most comfortable. And if most of your days are short errands, a vertical crossbody bag is all you need. A practical pair is 2 bags: a small crossbody for every day and a larger one for work or travel.

## Questions and answers

### Which men's bag is best for every day?

A crossbody bag. It carries a phone, a wallet, keys and sunglasses, sits flat against the body, keeps your things in front of you and leaves your hands free. Choose black for the most versatile look, and check the measurements on the product page if you also carry a power bank or a small tablet.

### Is a men's hand bag a good gift?

Yes, it is one of the easiest gifts on this page. Many come in a gift box, ready to give, and a black leather clutch suits almost any man, from a student to a father. It is practical for evenings, weddings and travel, when a strap across the shirt is not wanted.

### Will my laptop fit in a messenger bag?

Check the inside width on the product page against your laptop. A 13-inch laptop usually needs about 33 centimetres of inside width and a 14-inch about 35. If you are unsure, send our customer service the product link and your laptop model on WhatsApp before you order.

### How do I clean a leather bag?

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use and blot any spill immediately rather than rubbing it. Do not use household cleaners or wet wipes, which can mark leather. Keep the bag out of a hot parked car in summer and store it stuffed with paper so it keeps its shape.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
