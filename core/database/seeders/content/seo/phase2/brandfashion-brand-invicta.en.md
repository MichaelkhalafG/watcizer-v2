Invicta's style is generous: big cases, strong colours and plenty of detail. This page explains the 2 lines we carry, who each suits, how to live with a large automatic or a small quartz watch, and how to get a model that is between deliveries.

## Coalition Forces for men

Coalition Forces is Invicta's military-inspired line. The models here are black, with a 48 mm case, a bold bezel and large luminous hands designed to be read in a second. They run on automatic movements, so they wind themselves as you move. They suit men who like a big, rugged watch with presence on the wrist.

## Angel for women

Angel is the women's line, with a 34 mm case on a stainless steel bracelet in rose gold or silver tones. The dials are bright and feminine, and the quartz movement keeps excellent time with no winding. Angel watches work as everyday jewellery and dress up an office outfit or an evening dress.

## 48 mm: wearing a big watch

A 48 mm case is large, and that is the point of Coalition Forces. It looks best on wrists of about 18 cm or more and with casual or sporty clothes. Because an automatic case is thick, it sits high on the wrist, so it is better with short sleeves or a loose cuff than under a fitted shirt.

## 34 mm: a classic women's size

34 mm is a balanced women's size: visible and easy to read, without overwhelming a slim wrist. With a steel bracelet it is light and comfortable all day. Our stores in Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis can shorten the bracelet so it fits on the first day.

## Automatic and quartz

The Coalition Forces automatics need no battery; wear them daily and they run. Left off for a day or 2 they stop, and a few turns of the crown restart them. Angel quartz watches need only a battery every 2 to 3 years. Choose by habit: automatic for daily wearers who enjoy the mechanism and its sweeping seconds hand, quartz for grab-and-go ease.

## Water resistance

Both lines on this page are rated 100 m, which covers swimming in the pool or the sea. Push the crown fully in before you swim, avoid pressing anything under water, and rinse the watch with fresh water after salt water or chlorine. Avoid hot showers and saunas, which age the seals faster than cold water does.

## Colours

Coalition Forces comes in black, which keeps a large case looking sharp rather than loud. Angel comes in rose gold and silver: rose gold is warm and flatters most skin tones, silver is cool and matches white gold and steel jewellery. Pick the tone that matches the rings and bracelets you already wear.

## Caring for a large automatic

Keep it away from strong magnets such as speakers and magnetic phone cases, which can make it run fast. Avoid hard knocks during sport. Wipe the case and bracelet after wearing. Have it serviced every 4 to 5 years so the movement stays accurate and the 100 m rating holds.

## Caring for a quartz steel watch

Wipe the bracelet with a soft cloth after wearing, and wash it gently with mild soap and a soft brush every month or so. Put perfume on before the watch and let it dry. Replace the battery every 2 to 3 years and ask for a seal check at the same time.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 48 mm watch too big?

It is big by design. A 48 mm Coalition Forces suits wrists of about 18 cm or more and casual outfits. If your wrist is smaller, try a similar style around 42 to 44 mm on the automatic page, or visit our stores to compare sizes on the wrist before you buy.

### Can I swim with an Invicta?

Both the Coalition Forces and the Angel models on this page are rated 100 m, which covers swimming. Push the crown in before swimming, do not press anything under water, and rinse the watch with fresh water after the sea or the pool. Avoid hot water and saunas.
