The right wallet depends less on the logo than on how you pay, where you carry it and how much you keep inside. This page covers every Calvin Klein wallet we list, for men and for women, so the guide below is organised by wallet shape: what each one holds, which pocket or bag it suits and who it is best for.

## The bifold

The **bifold** folds once down the middle. It holds a row of cards on each side and banknotes in the full-length section, and many models add a coin pocket. It is the classic men's wallet for a trouser pocket and the shape used in most of our Calvin Klein gift sets.

## The trifold

A **trifold** folds twice into a compact square, with more card slots than a bifold in the same footprint. It suits anyone who carries many cards but wants a short wallet. One trifold we list has an **RFID** lining, which blocks the signal of contactless cards so a reader cannot scan them through the wallet.

## The vertical wallet

A **vertical** wallet stands upright rather than opening sideways. It is slim and tall, so it slides neatly into the inside pocket of a jacket, and it looks tidy when you take it out at a meeting or a restaurant. Calvin Klein's vertical wallets carry an embossed CK monogram and a small silver logo.

## The card holder

A **card holder** is the slimmest option: a few slots for cards and folded notes, without a coin pocket. It fits a front trouser pocket or a shirt pocket and suits days when you pay mostly by card. Some of our gift sets pair a belt with a card holder instead of a full wallet.

## The long zip-around

The women's **zip-around** wallets close with a zip on three sides, so nothing falls out, and open wide to show cards, notes and coins at once. The black **Re-Lock** has a long profile, square quilting and a raised CK metal logo; the beige quilted model is more compact, with polished CK hardware.

## The phone crossbody wallet

The black **phone crossbody** combines a phone pouch and a card wallet in one small vertical bag on a strap. It carries a phone, a few cards and some notes, so you can go out without a handbag. It suits walks, markets and concerts, when keeping both hands free matters.

## Gift sets

Many men's wallets come as **gift sets** with a matching belt, both in black with the same CK logo or monogram. A set is the easiest complete present for a birthday, a graduation, Eid or a wedding, because the pieces already match each other and almost any outfit.

## Logos and finishes

Calvin Klein uses its logo in several ways on wallets: a large **embossed** CK, an all-over monogram, a vertical logo, a metal plate on a crosshatch texture or metal CK hardware on quilting. The smaller and quieter the logo, the more formal the wallet looks; the monogram is the most casual.

## Matching a wallet to how you pay

If you pay mostly in **cash**, choose a bifold with a coin pocket or a long zip-around, which keep notes flat and change separate. If you pay mostly by card, a card holder or a slim vertical wallet is enough. If you carry many cards, a trifold holds the most in the smallest space.

## Matching a wallet to where you carry it

For a trouser pocket, a **bifold** or a card holder. For a jacket's inside pocket, a vertical wallet. For a large handbag, a long zip-around; for a small bag, a compact zip-around or the phone crossbody. A wallet that suits its pocket lasts longer and keeps its shape.

## Materials

The product page names the material of each wallet. Many are described as **leather-look** or synthetic, light and easy to wipe clean; some men's wallets are listed as leather. Whatever the material, keep the wallet away from water and long hours of heat, which can mark the surface and loosen the stitching.

## Caring for a wallet

Do not overfill it: about **10** cards and a few notes is plenty, and receipts are best cleared out weekly. Close zips slowly along their line, wipe the wallet with a soft, dry cloth, and keep light colours such as beige away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### Which Calvin Klein wallet holds the most cards?

For men, a trifold, which folds twice to give more card slots in a compact size. For women, the long Re-Lock zip-around, which has rows of slots on both sides. The product photos show the inside of each wallet, so you can count the slots before you order.

### What is an RFID wallet?

An RFID wallet has a lining that blocks the radio signal used by contactless bank cards, so a nearby card reader cannot read your cards through the wallet. It adds a layer of protection in crowded places. The product page states clearly whether a wallet has this lining.

### Are the women's wallets big enough for a phone?

Usually not; the zip-around wallets are designed for cards, notes and coins. If you want to carry a phone and cards together without a handbag, the phone crossbody with its card wallet is made for exactly that. Compare its measurements with your phone before ordering.

### Does a Calvin Klein wallet come in a gift box?

### Can I return a wallet?
