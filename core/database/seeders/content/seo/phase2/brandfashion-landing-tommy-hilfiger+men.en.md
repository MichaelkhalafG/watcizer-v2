Most of the men's Tommy Hilfiger range at Brand Fashion is watches, and what completes them is everything around them: a wallet, a belt and a bag in the same colours and the same clean American style. Rather than repeat the detail of each category, this page shows how the pieces work together, with five ready-made sets for everyday situations, and links to the pages where each category is explained in depth.

## The office set

For a working week, start with a three-hand watch or a calm chronograph — a 44 mm **Bryan** or **Weston** is ideal — on a black leather strap or a steel bracelet, with a black or blue dial. Add a black leather bifold wallet and a black belt with a simple buckle, or a reversible belt that also covers brown shoes. A messenger bag or a slim laptop bag completes the look. Keep the metal of the watch case and the belt buckle in the same tone, silver with silver, and the whole outfit reads as one decision.

## The weekend set

For weekends, cafés and errands, choose a sporty chronograph such as a **Stewart** or **Tyson** on a rubber or silicone strap, in navy, black or with a red accent. Pair it with a small crossbody bag worn across the chest for your phone, keys and cards, and a slim card case instead of a full wallet. Navy is the most Tommy Hilfiger colour of all, and a navy watch strap with a navy crossbody bag looks deliberate without trying too hard.

## The travel set

At airports and in new cities you want security and order. A steel-bracelet watch that you never need to take off, a crossbody bag that keeps your passport, boarding pass and phone in front of you, a backpack for the rest, and a travel pouch for chargers or toiletries inside it. A watch rated at 50 metres or more handles a hotel pool; rinse it with fresh water afterwards. This combination keeps your hands free and your valuables out of reach of others.

## The evening set

For weddings, dinners and events, a dressier watch matters most: a TH85 on its integrated steel bracelet, or a slim three-hand watch on black leather. Instead of a crossbody strap across a shirt, carry a leather hand pouch for your phone and cards, and wear a polished leather belt. Keep colours dark and simple, and let the watch be the one piece that catches the light.

## The gift set

If you are buying for someone else, the safest combination is a watch plus a boxed wallet-and-belt set in the same colour family. A blue-dial watch on steel with a black set suits almost any man; a watch on brown leather with a brown set feels warmer and more classic. Belts with an automatic buckle adjust to any waist, which removes the risk of guessing sizes. Keep the invoices, so each piece can be exchanged within 14 days if needed.

## Sizes at a glance

Three measurements decide whether a set fits. For the **watch**, most men's models here are 44 mm, which suits a wrist of about 16.5 to 18.5 centimetres; smaller wrists suit 40 to 42 mm. For the **belt**, measure a belt you already wear from the buckle to the hole you use most and compare it with the product page; automatic-buckle belts adjust to any waist and can be trimmed. For the **wallet**, count your cards: up to 5 suit a card case, 6 to 10 a standard bifold. With those three numbers, you can order a whole set with confidence.

## Caring for a matching set

The pieces of a set age at different speeds, so care for each the right way. Rinse a steel watch bracelet after the sea and dry it; give a leather strap and a leather belt a rest after a hot day. Empty the wallet of old receipts so it keeps its shape, and do not carry it in a back pocket all day in summer. Store bags stuffed with paper, out of the sun, and keep the watch in its box when you travel with all 4 pieces. Kept this way, a set still matches after years of use, instead of one piece looking much older than the others.

If one part of the set wears out before the others, such as the belt, you can replace it alone with a piece in the same colour, and the set matches again without buying everything new.

## Choosing your colour family

The range moves in three colour families. **Black** is the most formal and the easiest to match. **Brown**, from tan to chocolate, is warmer and pairs with brown shoes and leather watch straps. **Navy**, with touches of red and white, is the brand's own signature. Choose one family per outfit: mixing black and brown leather in the same look is the most common mistake to avoid.

## Questions and answers

### Can I order a watch, a wallet and a belt together?

Yes, add them to the cart together and they arrive in one delivery after one confirmation call. With cash on delivery you can inspect every piece in front of the courier before you pay. Each item keeps its own return and exchange right for 14 days.

### Should my belt match my watch strap?

It looks best when it does. A brown leather strap with a brown belt, or a black strap or steel bracelet with a black belt, keeps the outfit consistent. Matching the metal of the watch case and the belt buckle helps too. If you own both brown and black shoes, a reversible belt covers both.

### What is the safest gift from this page?

A watch with a blue or black dial on a steel bracelet, plus a boxed black wallet-and-belt set with an automatic buckle. The bracelet can be shortened after delivery and the belt adjusts to any waist, so there is no size to guess, and the colours suit almost any wardrobe.

### Do the watches come with a warranty?

Each watch's product page states whether it has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I exchange one piece of a set?

Yes. Each item can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. It must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a belt or bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp.
