Alba is one of the brands of Seiko, the Japanese watchmaker, and it carries the group's approach to watches into a more affordable range: reliable movements, solid stainless steel cases and designs that stay in style for years. That makes Alba a sensible choice for a first good watch, a graduation present or a watch for every working day. This guide looks at the men's and women's ranges separately, then at what to check before you order.

## The men's range: chronographs, automatics and clean dials

The men's Alba watches we list cluster around 42 and 43 millimetres, a balanced modern size, with some 40, 41 and 44 mm models. The **Active** line brings the sporty pieces: chronographs with three sub-dials, bold indexes and steel bracelets. The **Standard** and **Prestige** models are calmer, with clean three-hand dials that look right with a shirt and tie. Around 20 men's models are **automatic**, powered by a self-winding movement that needs no battery and shows a little of Japanese mechanical watchmaking at a reasonable price. The [men's Alba watches page](/brand/alba/men) gathers the whole range.

## The women's range: petite and precise

Alba's women's watches are notably small: the most common sizes in our range are 30 to 32 millimetres, with some at 28 and 29 mm. That is a true classic ladies' size, closer to a bracelet than to a sports watch, and it suits a slim wrist and a refined style. Most come on stainless steel bracelets in silver, gold and two-tone, with white, silver, blue and mother-of-pearl dials; a group of models uses leather straps. The **Fashion** line in the titles is the one to look at for the most decorative designs. The [women's Alba watches page](/brand/alba/watches/women) lists them.

## Japanese quartz: why it matters

Most Alba watches use Japanese quartz movements from the Seiko group. In daily life that means three things: the watch keeps time to within a few seconds a month, the battery usually lasts 2 to 3 years, and the movement is simple and robust enough to be serviced for many years. For a watch you want to wear every day without thinking about it, that is exactly what you need. When the battery runs low, the second hand often starts jumping in 2-second steps; that is your signal to change it before it stops.

## Water resistance and the glass

Alba models vary in water resistance, and the product page states the value of each. A simple rule covers most cases: 30 metres means rain and splashes, 50 metres covers washing and a short swim in a pool, and 100 metres, which several men's models offer, is safer for the sea. Some models have sapphire glass, which resists scratches extremely well; others use mineral glass, which is tougher against knocks. The specifications on the product page tell you which one a model has.

## Steel or leather

A steel bracelet is the most common choice in our Alba range and the most practical in Egypt's heat: it does not absorb sweat, it can be rinsed and it lasts for years. It needs sizing, which any watch shop or our stores can do in a few minutes. Leather straps, on about 1 in 5 models, look warmer and more formal; they suit an office watch and cooler months, and they should be kept away from water and perfume. A petite women's watch on a fine steel bracelet is the most elegant combination in the range.

## Choosing a dial and a finish

Alba keeps its colours classic, which makes choosing easier. For men, a black or blue dial on a silver steel bracelet is the most versatile; a white or silver dial looks the most formal; a two-tone gold and silver bracelet suits someone who likes a traditional, dressy look. For women, a white or mother-of-pearl dial on a silver bracelet is timeless, gold-tone looks warm and festive, and two-tone is the safest choice if she mixes gold and silver jewellery. Because the cases are small and the designs restrained, an Alba rarely clashes with an outfit, which makes it easy to wear every day for years. ## Questions and answers

### Is Alba a good brand?

Alba is a brand of the Seiko group, one of the most respected Japanese watchmakers, and its watches use the group's movements. That gives you dependable Japanese quality at a price below the Seiko name itself. It is not a fashion brand: you buy Alba for a reliable, well-made watch with a classic design that will not look dated next year.

### Are Alba women's watches too small?

They are smaller than most fashion watches, by design: 30 to 32 mm is the classic ladies' size. On a slim wrist it looks refined and elegant. If you prefer a bigger, modern look, a 36 to 38 mm watch from another brand in our range, or an Alba men's model on a small wrist, may suit you better.

### How long does an Alba automatic run when I take it off?

Usually around a day and a half. After that it stops, which is normal for an automatic. Wind the crown a few times or wear it for a while, then set the time and date. If you wear it daily, it keeps itself wound; if you rotate watches, expect to set it each time you put it on.

### What warranty comes with an Alba watch?

Each product page states the warranty length and who provides it, and the card in the box carries the same information. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and the invoice together. A broken glass, damage from a fall and water that entered because the case was opened are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I exchange an Alba watch?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
