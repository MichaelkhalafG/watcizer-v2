The most distinctive watch here has an **oval** case of 26 by 34 mm in gold-tone steel, a warm grey sunray dial and gold Roman numerals. It shows only the hours and minutes, and it sits on a fine multilink bracelet that looks more like jewellery than a strap. ## Sinfonia: mother of pearl

The **Sinfonia** pairs a white mother-of-pearl dial with a silver-tone steel bracelet. It is the most feminine watch in our selection and suits pearls, silver jewellery and light colours.

## Gianni T-Bar: two-tone

The **Gianni T-Bar** has a white dial on a steel bracelet in silver and gold. Two-tone steel is the most practical choice when you wear both gold and silver jewellery, because it matches either. The two-tone bracelet gives it a structured, polished look that works from morning meetings to dinner.

## Gianni in silver

The second **Gianni** is all silver, with a white dial. It is the quietest piece on the page and the easiest to wear every day: silver steel goes with everything, the white dial is clear to read, and the clean design never looks out of place, whether with a suit, a dress or jeans.

## Why 34 mm

All four watches measure **34** millimetres. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, that size looks elegant and balanced, closer to classic dress watches than to oversized fashion pieces. It slips under a fitted sleeve easily, and it lets a bracelet or a ring share the wrist without crowding it.

## Two hands or three

The oval watch has **2** hands, for the hours and minutes, with no seconds hand. A two-hand dial looks calmer and more jewellery-like, and it is perfectly practical for a dress watch. On the other models, the photos show whether a seconds hand is added, which some people like to see moving. Neither is more accurate; it is a question of style.

## Sunray and mother-of-pearl dials

A **sunray** dial is brushed in fine lines that radiate from the centre, so it catches light in a sweeping band as the wrist turns. A **mother-of-pearl** dial is cut from the inner layer of a shell and shimmers with soft colours. Both look richer than a flat painted dial and are a hallmark of dressy women's watches.

Let the metal of the watch follow your everyday jewellery. ## Water and care

These watches list a **30-metre** water rating: rain and washing your hands are fine, but swimming, the shower and the sea are not. Wipe a steel bracelet with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day, put the watch on after perfume and hand cream, and keep it in its box rather than loose with other jewellery.

## Battery and sizing

All four watches run on **quartz** movements, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows. The steel bracelets need sizing, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty.

## Questions and answers

### Is a two-hand watch practical?

Yes. A two-hand watch shows the hours and minutes clearly, which is all most people check during the day. It simply has no seconds hand, which gives the dial a calmer, more elegant look. It is just as accurate as a three-hand quartz watch and runs on the same kind of battery.

### What is a mother-of-pearl dial?

It is a dial cut from nacre, the shiny inner layer of a shell. Each piece has its own pattern of soft pink, blue and green reflections, so every dial is slightly different. It needs no special care beyond the watch itself, as it sits safely under the glass.

### Does a 34 mm watch look small?

On most women's wrists it looks elegant rather than small, in the tradition of classic dress watches. If you prefer a large, modern look, a 34 mm watch will feel discreet; if you like jewellery-style watches that sit neatly under a sleeve, it is the ideal size.
