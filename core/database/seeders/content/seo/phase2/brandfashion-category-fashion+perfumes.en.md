Choosing a perfume online is easier when you know what the words on the bottle mean. This guide explains strengths, scent families and seasons, then gives a simple way to choose a fragrance for yourself or as a gift.

## Reading the label: strength

Eau de toilette is the lightest common strength: fresh at first and lasting a few hours, ideal for daytime and hot weather. Eau de parfum is richer and lasts longer, often through a working day. Parfum, or extrait, is the most concentrated and needs only 1 or 2 sprays. The strength is in every product title on this page.

## Scent families

Most fragrances belong to about 5 families. Fresh scents feel clean, with citrus, water or green notes. Woody scents feel warm and calm, with cedar, sandalwood or vetiver. Spicy scents add pepper, cardamom or cinnamon. Amber and oriental scents are sweet and deep, with vanilla and resins. Floral scents are built on flowers such as rose or jasmine. Knowing which family you like narrows the choice.

## Top, heart and base

A perfume changes on the skin. The top notes are what you smell in the first 10 to 15 minutes, often citrus or spice. The heart appears after that and lasts a few hours. The base, woods, musk, amber or vanilla, stays longest. Judge a perfume by how it smells after an hour, not in the first minute.

## Choosing for the Egyptian climate

Heat makes a perfume project more and fade faster. From May to September, fresh eau de toilette and light eau de parfum feel comfortable in the day. From November to March, woody, spicy and amber scents come into their own. For Ramadan nights and winter weddings, a deeper parfum lasts through a long evening.

## How to apply it

Spray on clean, moisturised skin at the pulse points: the neck, behind the ears and the inner wrists. 2 to 4 sprays are enough for most eau de parfum. Do not rub the wrists together, which breaks the top notes. Spray after dressing for a lighter effect, or a little on clothes, avoiding silk and light colours.

## Keeping a bottle at its best

Light, heat and air age a perfume. Keep the bottle in its box, in a cupboard, away from the bathroom's heat and humidity and from a sunny window. Close the cap after each use. A well-kept bottle stays true for 2 to 3 years or more after opening.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between eau de toilette and eau de parfum?

Eau de parfum contains more fragrance oil than eau de toilette, so it smells richer and lasts longer on the skin, often through a working day. Eau de toilette is lighter and fresher, which suits daytime and hot weather. Parfum is the strongest of the 3 and needs only 1 or 2 sprays.

### How do I make a perfume last longer?

Apply it to clean, moisturised skin at the pulse points, the neck and the inner wrists, without rubbing. Unscented lotion underneath helps the skin hold the scent. A light spray on clothes also lasts longer than on skin. In the heat, choose an eau de parfum rather than an eau de toilette.
