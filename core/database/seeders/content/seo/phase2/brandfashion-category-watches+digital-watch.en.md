A digital watch is the most practical watch you can own: it wakes you up, times your workout, lights up in the dark and shows the date without thinking. This page explains the 2 kinds of digital watch on offer, how to set and use them, and which brand suits which wearer.

## Fully digital or analog-digital

A fully digital watch shows everything as numbers on a screen. An analog-digital watch, often called ana-digi, has traditional hands plus a small screen for the extra functions. Most models here are analog-digital, which gives you the look of a classic watch with the convenience of a digital one. The G-Shock combines both in a tough resin case.

## Functions you can expect

Depending on the model: the date and day, 1 or more daily alarms, an hourly chime, a stopwatch, a countdown timer, a second time zone and a backlight. Some screens show 12 or 24-hour time. The product description lists every function, and the leaflet in the box explains the buttons.

## Setting the watch

Digital functions are set with the buttons. Usually 1 button changes mode, 1 held for a few seconds enters setting mode, and the others change the digits. On analog-digital models, the hands are set separately with the crown. If you prefer, our team sets both for you when you collect the watch in our stores in Arkadia Mall or Heliopolis.

## Sizes and fit

Cases range from 39 to 48.5 mm. A digital screen needs room to stay readable, so most models are 44 mm or more. If your wrist is under 17 cm, look at the 39 to 42 mm models, or try a watch in our stores. Mesh bracelets adjust with a sliding clasp; link bracelets are shortened in our stores.

## Water resistance

Fully digital sports models from SKMEI and the G-Shock are generally the most water resistant, and some are suitable for swimming. Analog-digital fashion models usually resist splashes only. Always check the rating on the product page, never press the buttons under water, and dry the watch after contact with water.

## Battery

One battery powers both the hands and the screen. Frequent use of the backlight and alarms shortens its life, so expect about 1 to 3 years depending on the model and on how you use it. A fading screen is the first sign of a low battery. Our stores change batteries and check the seals at the same time.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between digital and analog-digital?

A digital watch shows the time and every function as numbers on a screen. An analog-digital watch has traditional hands for the time and a small screen for the date, alarms, stopwatch and backlight. Analog-digital looks more classic; fully digital is usually lighter and more water resistant.

### Can I swim with a digital watch?

Some models can, especially fully digital sports watches and the G-Shock, but always check the rating on the product page first. Most analog-digital fashion models resist splashes only. Never press the buttons under water, because that is how water reaches the electronics inside.

### How do I turn off the hourly beep?

On most models, enter the alarm mode with the mode button, then press the adjust button to switch the hourly chime off; a small symbol on the screen disappears. The leaflet in the box shows the exact steps for each watch. Our team can also do it for you on WhatsApp video or in our stores.

### How long does the battery last?

Usually 1 to 3 years, depending on the model and how often you use the backlight and alarms. A faint screen is the first sign of a low battery. Bring the watch to one of our stores for a replacement, and the team will also check the seals.
