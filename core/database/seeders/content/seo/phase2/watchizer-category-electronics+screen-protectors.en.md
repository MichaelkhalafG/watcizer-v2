A phone screen is the most expensive part to replace and the easiest to scratch. A good protector costs a fraction of a repair and takes the damage instead. This guide explains how tempered glass works, how to pick the right protector for your iPhone, and how to fit it without bubbles.

## How tempered glass protects

Tempered glass is heat-treated so it is much harder than plastic film. It resists scratches from keys and coins, and when the phone falls, the protector absorbs part of the impact and can crack instead of the screen beneath. A protector of about 0.3 mm keeps the feel and clarity of the original display, and an oleophobic coating reduces fingerprints.

## Choosing your exact model

## Clear, privacy and other finishes

A clear protector keeps colours exactly as the screen shows them. A privacy protector darkens the screen from an angle of about 30 degrees to the side, so people next to you on the metro cannot read it, while you still see it straight on. A matte finish reduces glare and fingerprints but softens the image slightly. Choose clear for photos and video, privacy for work and banking.

## Fitting it without bubbles

Fit the protector in a bathroom after a hot shower, when there is little dust in the air. Clean the screen with the wet wipe, dry it with the cloth, and lift remaining dust with the sticker. Align the protector using the camera cut-out or the installation frame, lower it, and press from the centre outwards. Small bubbles usually disappear within 24 hours.

## Cases and protectors together

Most protectors are made slightly smaller than the screen so that they work with a case. If you use a thick case with raised edges, check that the protector is described as case-friendly, otherwise the case can lift its edges. The 2 together give the best protection: the case for the corners, the protector for the face.

## When to replace a protector

Replace the protector when it cracks, when its edges lift, or when scratches start to bother you. A cracked protector has done its job; keep using it and the sharp edges can cut a finger, so change it soon. Many customers keep a second protector at home, since 1 drop can crack the first, ready for the day the first one breaks.

## Caring for your screen

Wipe the protector with a soft dry cloth or a lightly damp one; avoid alcohol and strong cleaners, which remove the oleophobic coating. Keep the phone away from sand and grit at the beach, which can scratch even tempered glass. Keep it in a separate pocket from keys and coins. A clean protector stays clear for 12 months or more.

## Questions and answers

### Will this protector fit my iPhone?

Only the protector cut for your exact model will fit. Check your model in Settings, under General and About, then choose the product whose title names it, for example the iPhone 17 Pro or the 17 Pro Max. The 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max all have different sizes.

### Does a protector affect the touch screen?

No. A tempered glass protector of about 0.3 mm keeps the touch response, the clarity and Face ID working as before. If touch feels uneven, a bubble or a speck of dust is usually trapped under one area; lift that corner gently with the sticker, remove the dust and press it down again.

### What is a privacy protector?

A privacy protector darkens the screen when it is seen from the side, so people next to you cannot read it, while it stays clear when you look straight at it. It suits work, banking and crowded transport. The screen looks slightly darker, so you may raise the brightness a little.
