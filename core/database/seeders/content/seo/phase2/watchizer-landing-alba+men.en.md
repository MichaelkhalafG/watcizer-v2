Alba is the sensible choice in a men's watch: Japanese engineering, honest materials and designs that stay in style. It does not try to impress with a big logo; it earns its place by keeping good time for years. This guide explains the four collections in our men's range, the difference between the quartz and automatic models, and how to choose a strap and a dial.

## Active: the everyday collection

**Active** makes up most of our men's Alba range. It covers sporty and everyday watches: three-hand models with a date, chronographs and automatics, in steel cases with dials in black, blue, grey, brown and silver-white. If you want one watch for work, weekends and travel, start with an Active on a steel bracelet.

## The Active automatics

Four Active models are **automatic**: mechanical watches that wind themselves with the movement of your wrist and need no battery. We carry them with black, blue, dark blue and dark grey dials, three on steel bracelets and one on black silicone. The seconds hand sweeps smoothly rather than ticking, a small pleasure for anyone who likes mechanical watches.

## Chronographs

An Alba **chronograph** adds a stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown, with sub-dials that count the elapsed time. We carry an Active chronograph with a black dial on steel, a **Prestige** chronograph with a grey dial on grey leather and a **Flagship Signa** chronograph with a silver dial on steel. They suit men who like a detailed, technical dial.

## Prestige: the dressier line

**Prestige** is the dressier side of Alba, with refined dials and leather straps. Our Prestige models pair grey or black dials with grey leather. They look calm and grown-up with a shirt and trousers, and they are a good choice for an office where a sports watch would feel out of place.

## Standard: simple and classic

**Standard** keeps things simple: a clean dial, a steel or leather strap and nothing more. We carry a black-dial Standard on leather and a gold-dial model on a silver steel bracelet. These are honest, easy-to-read watches, and a gold dial on silver steel is a warm, slightly vintage look.

## Quartz or automatic

Most of our men's Albas run on Japanese **quartz**: accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. The automatics are less precise, gaining or losing a few seconds a day, but they never need a battery. If you want zero fuss, choose quartz; if you enjoy mechanics and wear the watch daily, choose automatic.

## Living with an automatic

If you leave an automatic Alba off for a day or two, it may stop. Give the crown a few turns to wind it, then set the time and the date. Avoid setting the date between about 9 in the evening and 3 in the morning, when the date mechanism is engaged. Wearing it every day keeps it running on its own.

**Steel** bracelets are the most common here: durable, easy to clean and right for every season. **Leather**, on the Prestige and some Active and Standard models, looks warmer and dressier. ## Dial colours

**Black** and **blue** are the easiest dials to read and to match. **Grey** looks modern and technical, **brown** warm and earthy, and **silver-white** classic and bright. For one watch for everything, a black or blue Active on steel; for a dressier second watch, a grey Prestige on leather.

## Reading the specifications

Each product page lists the case size, the glass, the water rating and the movement. Check the **case size** against a watch you already own, and the water rating against how you will use it: 30 metres means splashes only, 50 metres a short swim in a pool, and 100 metres or more the sea.

## Bracelet sizing

## Questions and answers

### Is Alba a good watch brand?

Alba is the Japanese brand of the Seiko group and is known for reliable, well-made watches at a sensible price. It is not a luxury brand; it is a dependable one. For a watch you want to wear daily, keep accurate and rarely think about, it is a very sound choice.

### Does an Alba automatic need a battery?

No. An automatic winds itself from the movement of your wrist while you wear it. If it stops after a day or two off the wrist, wind it with a few turns of the crown, set the time and wear it. The quartz Albas do need a battery, usually every 2 to 3 years.

### Which men's Alba is best for the office?

A Prestige on leather or an Active three-hand model on steel, with a black, blue or grey dial. Both are calm enough for an office, slip under a shirt cuff and are easy to read in meetings. Save the chronographs and the nylon-strap model for weekends.
