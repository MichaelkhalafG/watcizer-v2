Seiko is one of the great names of Japanese watchmaking, and the 3 families on this page show 3 sides of it: a refined line, a sporty automatic and a practical chronograph. This guide explains each, then covers size, straps and how to buy a model that is between deliveries.

## The Astron line

Astron models, about 17 of them, are the most refined Seiko watches on this page, mostly on stainless steel bracelets with black or blue dials, and a few on black rubber. They have a substantial, modern case and a clean, technical dial. They suit a professional who wants a watch that looks serious at a meeting and holds up on a trip.

## The 5 Sports line

5 Sports is Seiko's automatic family: a self-winding mechanical movement in a sporty case, with a display back on many models. The page offers it on steel bracelets in black, blue and orange, and on leather, canvas and fabric straps in brown, green, gold and grey. It is the most varied family here and a favourite first automatic for men who like to change straps.

## The chronographs

Seiko's quartz chronographs, more than 20 models, add a stopwatch with 2 or 3 sub-dials to a precise quartz movement. They come on steel bracelets with black, white or grey dials, on brown or black leather, on black or grey nylon, and on rubber in an oily green tone. A titanium solar chronograph is the lightest piece on the page and charges from light rather than a battery.

## Quartz, automatic or solar

Quartz is the most accurate and the easiest to live with: a battery every few years and nothing else. Automatic, in the 5 Sports line, winds itself as you move and needs a service every 4 to 5 years instead of a battery. Solar quartz charges from light, so a few hours near a window keeps it running. Each product page states the movement.

## Sizes

Cases range from about 36 to 47 mm. The 5 Sports models are typically around 40 to 42 mm, a size that suits most wrists. Chronographs and Astron models are larger, closer to 42 to 47 mm. If your wrist is under 17 cm, start with a 5 Sports or a smaller chronograph, and check the case size on the product page.

## Straps and bracelets

Steel, on about half of the 56 watches, is the most versatile: smart for work and durable. Leather adds warmth and suits a classic look. Nylon, canvas and fabric are light, breathable and casual, ideal for summer and travel. Rubber is the sport choice. Titanium, on the solar chronograph, is lighter than steel and comfortable all day.

## Water resistance

Water resistance on this page ranges from splash protection to 100 m. The 5 Sports and many chronographs manage 100 m, enough for regular swimming; others are best kept to rain and hand washing. Check the rating on each product page, and keep the crown pushed in before you go in the water.

## Caring for a Seiko

Wipe the case and bracelet with a soft cloth after wearing. Keep leather away from water, and rinse rubber and nylon after sport. Keep automatics away from strong magnets. Replace a quartz battery every 2 to 3 years, and have an automatic serviced every 4 to 5 years so it stays accurate and water resistant.

## Questions and answers

### Is the 5 Sports automatic?

Yes. The 5 Sports models on this page are automatic: a mechanical movement winds itself as you move, so there is no battery. If you leave the watch off for a day or 2 it stops; wind it with the crown, set the time and wear it again.

### Why are Seiko watches out of stock?

Seiko arrives in batches and popular models sell out between deliveries. Leave your e-mail in the form on the watch's page and we write to you as soon as it returns. You can also ask our team on WhatsApp about the next delivery or a similar model in stock.

### Which Seiko suits a slim wrist?

Look at the 5 Sports models around 40 to 42 mm and the smaller chronographs. A leather, nylon or fabric strap sits lighter on a slim wrist than a steel bracelet. Check the case size on each product page, or ask our team for help comparing models.

### How does a solar watch work?

A solar quartz watch has a light-sensitive cell under the dial that charges a rechargeable battery. Normal daylight and indoor light keep it running, and a full charge lasts for months in the dark. Leave it near a window now and then, never on a hot dashboard.
