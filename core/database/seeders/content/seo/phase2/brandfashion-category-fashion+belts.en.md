A belt is worn every day and noticed rarely, until it is the wrong one. This guide is organised around 4 decisions: the look you want, the buckle, the size and whether a gift set makes more sense than a belt alone.

## Decision 1: the look

A smart belt is slim, about 3 cm wide, in smooth black or dark brown leather with a discreet buckle. A casual belt is wider, 3.5 to 4 cm, often with a larger logo buckle or a textured finish, and goes with jeans and chinos. Tommy Hilfiger leans casual and preppy; Calvin Klein leans minimal and smart. Choose by the trousers you wear most.

## Decision 2: the buckle

Pin buckles with holes are the classic choice and the easiest to adjust in a store. Automatic buckles grip a smooth strap with a ratchet, so you can tighten them a few millimetres at a time; they look clean with suits. Reversible buckles turn to show the black or the brown side. Logo plates from Calvin Klein or Tommy Hilfiger are the most recognisable of the 4 buckle types.

## Decision 3: the size

Measure a belt you already wear, from the buckle's pin to the hole you use, or take your trouser waist and add about 5 cm. A waist of 86 cm usually needs a belt of 90 to 95 cm. Many belts here, especially automatic ones, can be cut to length, which our stores in Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis do for you.

## Decision 4: belt alone or a set

Roughly 1 in 3 items on this page is a gift set with a belt and a wallet in the same box, colour and logo family. For a gift, a set solves the matching question and arrives ready to give. For yourself, a belt alone lets you pick the exact width and buckle you want and add a wallet from the [wallets page](/subtypes/wallets) later.

## Matching the belt to the rest

Match the belt to your shoes: black with black, brown with brown. Match the buckle metal to your watch case where you can: silver with steel, gold with gold. A reversible belt covers both colours on trips, 2 belts in 1. With a suit, keep the buckle small; with jeans, a logo buckle is welcome.

## Colours

Black is the colour of most belts here, followed by navy, brown and red details. Silver is the most common buckle. If you own only 1 belt, choose black leather with a silver buckle. A second brown belt covers casual brown shoes.

## Caring for a leather belt

Rotate 2 belts if you can, so each rests a day and keeps its shape. Hang a belt by its buckle or roll it loosely; never fold it sharply. Keep it out of rain and heat. A leather conditioner every 6 months stops the surface drying and cracking around the holes.

## Questions and answers

### How do I choose my belt size?

Add about 5 cm to your trouser waist size, so a 86 cm waist usually needs a belt of 90 to 95 cm. Or measure your current belt from the pin to the hole you use. Many belts here can be shortened, and our stores in Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis do it for you on the spot.

### What is in a belt and wallet gift set?

A leather belt and a matching wallet from the same brand, usually in the same colour and logo pattern, presented in a gift box. The title and the photos show both pieces. Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein sets are the most popular gifts on this page for Eid and birthdays.

### Can an automatic buckle belt be shortened?

Yes. Most automatic belts are made to be cut to length at the strap end, and the buckle clamps back on. Measure carefully before cutting, or let our stores do it. Once cut, the belt cannot be returned, so confirm the size first by wearing it around the house.

### Can I exchange a belt?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery if it is unused, uncut and in its packaging, and we pay the return shipping. Before exchanging for size, ask our team on WhatsApp: shortening is often quicker than sending the belt back and waiting for another.
