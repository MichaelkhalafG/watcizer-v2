A Coach wallet is a small way to carry the brand every day, and our range shows its playful side as well as its classic one. There are wallets of every size, from a card holder that slips into a pocket to a phone wallet that holds everything, and many carry cheerful prints on the Signature canvas. This guide explains the sizes, the prints and how to choose one for yourself or as a gift.

## Signature canvas

Most of these wallets are made of **Signature canvas**, Coach's coated canvas printed with the interlocking C pattern and trimmed with leather. It is durable, light and easy to wipe clean, which matters for a wallet that is opened many times a day. One small zip-around combines brown leather with the Signature canvas.

## The playful prints

Several wallets add a print on top of the Signature pattern: **floral** sprays, a floral heart, a floral leaf, cherries and strawberries. The prints make a wallet feel personal and cheerful, and they are easy to spot in a busy bag. They suit anyone who likes a touch of fun in otherwise classic accessories.

## Snap wallets

A **snap** wallet closes with a press stud rather than a zip. It is compact, folds in half or in thirds and opens with one hand at a till. We carry a floral print snap wallet and a Signature snap wallet, both sized for a small or medium handbag.

## The medium corner zip wallet

The **medium corner zip** wallet has a zip around one corner and holds cards, notes and coins in a size between a card case and a long wallet. It is the practical everyday choice for a medium shoulder bag, and it comes in a Signature canvas version and a floral leaf version.

## The small zip-around

The **small zip-around** wallet closes on three sides, so nothing falls out, and combines brown leather with Signature canvas. It is compact enough for a crossbody bag and still holds cards, folded notes and coins, a good choice for anyone who wants security in a small size.

## The phone wallet

The **phone wallet** in black and brown Signature canvas holds a phone together with cards and notes, so it can be carried on its own for a short outing. It is ideal for days when you do not want to take a bag at all, such as a walk, a quick errand or a visit to a café.

## Zip card cases and a card holder

For the lightest option, two **zip card cases**, one with a cherry print in pink and one with a floral print in white, hold a few cards, folded notes and coins behind a small zip. A beige **card holder** with a strawberry print holds cards only. They slip into a pocket or a mini bag.

## The men's bifold

The men's **Signature canvas bifold** in black and grey folds once and fits a trouser pocket, with card slots and a section for notes. Its monochrome Signature pattern looks discreet and modern, and it pairs naturally with a black or grey watch strap and a dark leather belt.

## Choosing the size

Match the wallet to the bag. A **card case** or card holder suits a mini bag or a pocket. A snap wallet or a small zip-around suits a crossbody bag. The medium corner zip wallet suits a shoulder bag, and the phone wallet replaces the bag altogether. Keep cards to about 10 for the smaller sizes.

## Choosing a colour or a print

**Black** and brown Signature are the classic, discreet choices. **Burgundy** and pink feel warm and feminine. White and beige are fresh and easy to find in a dark bag. The floral, cherry and strawberry prints are the cheerful choices and make a wallet stand out as a gift.

## Caring for Signature canvas

Wipe Signature canvas with a slightly damp cloth and dry it in the shade; use a dry cloth on the leather trims. Avoid alcohol and strong cleaners, which can dull the print, and keep light wallets away from dark denim and **newspaper** ink, which can transfer colour.

## Questions and answers

### Which Coach wallet holds a phone?

The phone wallet in black and brown Signature canvas is designed to hold a phone together with cards and notes, so you can carry it on its own. Phone sizes vary, so compare your phone with the measurements on the product page, or ask us on WhatsApp before you order.

### What is the difference between a card case and a card holder?

A zip card case closes with a small zip and holds a few cards, folded notes and coins. A card holder has open slots for cards only and is the slimmest option. Both fit a pocket or a mini bag; the card case is better if you carry some cash and change.

### Is Signature canvas durable?

Yes. Coach's coated Signature canvas is made to withstand daily use: it resists light rain, keeps its colour and wipes clean easily. Its leather trims prefer a dry cloth. Keep the wallet away from long hours of heat, such as a hot car, which can mark the coating.
