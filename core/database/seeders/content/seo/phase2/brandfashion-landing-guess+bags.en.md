With more than two hundred Guess bags on one page, the quickest way to find yours is to decide on the shape first, then the line and the colour. This guide sorts the range by shape, explains what each line is known for, and ends with practical advice on size, care and ordering.

## Shoulder bags: the everyday shape

The **shoulder bag** is the most common shape on this page, and many titles call it an elegant everyday bag. It sits under the arm on a medium strap, opens quickly and holds a wallet, a phone, sunglasses and keys. Most come in black, latte or light brown, often with the 4G logo printed across the front.

## Crossbody and camera bags

A **crossbody** bag rides on a long strap across the body, so your hands stay free and the bag stays in sight on the metro or in a crowded market. The **Noelle Camera** crossbody is a compact, boxy version with a zip around the top. **Giully** adds quilting to a crossbody shape, and some models, such as the Easy crossbody, add 2 outer pockets for a phone and cards.

## Satchels: structured and smart

A **satchel** has a flat base, a structured body and a top handle, often with a detachable strap as well. **Cordelia** is a satchel we carry in a 4G logo print and in a saffiano-style finish, a fine cross-hatched texture that resists scratches. The Girlfriend and Geneva Elite satchels follow the same idea. Satchels look the most polished for work.

## Hobo and convertible bags

A **hobo** bag is soft and slouchy, curving into a half-moon on the shoulder. **Zamira** comes as a hobo in a latte monogram and as a convertible Quattro G handbag that changes from a top-handle bag to a shoulder bag. Convertible designs are useful when one bag has to cover a working day and an evening.

## Totes for the long day

A **tote** has two handles and an open or zipped top, with room for a notebook, a water bottle and a scarf. **Noelle Elite** is a black shopper tote, Noelle II a grey 4G tote, and **Centre Stage Society** a structured tote for the office. **Cessily** brings a bouclé or tweed texture for winter.

## Top-handle and mini bags

**Amara** is a small top-handle bag with a flap, elegant in the hand or on a short strap for weddings and dinners. **Kamina** mini bags hold a phone, a card holder and keys, and nothing more. Choose a mini bag for evenings out; for a day in town, a shoulder or crossbody bag carries more without weighing you down.

## Multi-piece designs: Izzy, Kuba and Noelle 3-in-1

Some Guess bags come as a set or with several compartments. **Izzy** is a 2-in-1 design with a double pouch that works as a crossbody bag or a handbag. **Kuba** has 3 compartments under one flap, which keeps a phone, a wallet and makeup apart. The **Noelle 3-in-1** set, in brown 4G, pairs a shoulder bag with smaller matching pieces, as its photos show.

## Black, brown, latte and pink

Black is the most common colour by far and the easiest to match with every outfit. **Brown** and **latte** 4G prints are the classic Guess look and hide marks almost as well as black. **Pink**, white and nude bring a lighter feel for summer and weddings, and grey is a soft alternative to black.

## Choosing the size

Think about the largest thing you carry every day. If it is a phone and a card holder, a **mini** or **camera** bag is enough. If it is a long wallet, sunglasses and a power bank, choose a shoulder or crossbody bag. If it is a notebook, a tablet or a water bottle, choose a tote or a satchel. Each product page lists the measurements, so compare them with a bag you already own.

## Looking after a Guess bag

Most Guess bags use coated or faux-leather materials, and the product page names the material of each. Wipe the surface with a soft, slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol or strong cleaners, and dry it in the shade. Stuff the bag with paper when you store it, keep it in its **dust bag**, and keep light colours away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### Which Guess bag is best for work?

A satchel or a tote. A structured satchel such as Cordelia keeps its shape, looks smart in meetings and holds papers flat, while a tote such as Noelle Elite carries a notebook, a water bottle and a long wallet. Both look professional in black, brown or latte.

### What is a saffiano finish?

Saffiano is a fine cross-hatched texture pressed into the surface of a bag. It looks crisp, keeps its colour and resists small scratches better than a smooth finish, which makes it practical for a bag used every day. The product page names the finish of each model where it applies.

### What does a 3-in-1 or 2-in-1 bag include?

It means the bag can be carried in more than one way, or comes with extra pieces. The Noelle 3-in-1 set pairs a shoulder bag with matching smaller pieces, and Izzy's double pouch works as a crossbody bag or a handbag. The product photos show every piece included, so check them before ordering.

### Why is a bag shown as out of stock?

Guess bags arrive in limited quantities, and a colour can sell out between deliveries. The product page always shows whether a bag is in stock. If the one you want is not, ask us on WhatsApp whether it is expected again, or choose a similar shape that is in stock now.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
