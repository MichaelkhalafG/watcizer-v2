Calvin Klein suits women who like a calm, modern look that does not change with every season. Its bags and wallets for women use the same few colours and a restrained logo, so a piece bought today still matches one bought next year. This guide walks through the occasions of a typical year and the pieces that suit each one, then covers materials, care, gifts and ordering.

## What the range includes

Most of this page is **bags**: totes, shoulder bags, an embossed crossbody, a chain shoulder bag, a flap bag and a backpack. Then come **wallets**, quilted zip-arounds in black and beige and a phone crossbody wallet. Unisex slippers and a women's fragrance join the range when they are in stock.

## For university

Lectures, libraries and long commutes call for a bag that carries a lot without weighing you down. The colour-block **backpack** in vanilla, caramel and brown spreads the weight and still looks polished, and the compact beige wallet inside keeps cards and cash in order between classes.

## For the office

A working day needs structure. A **tote**, in black with the CK monogram or in brown, khaki and white, holds a notebook, a water bottle and a long wallet, and looks smart in a meeting. Pair it with the black Re-Lock zip-around, which keeps notes flat and cards easy to reach at lunch.

## For errands and weekends

On weekends, travel light. The embossed **crossbody** bag in vanilla, khaki and caramel keeps your hands free in a market or a mall, and the phone crossbody wallet can replace a bag altogether for a short walk. Both suit denim, linen and trainers.

## For weddings and evenings

For engagements, weddings and dinners, choose a bag with a little shine: the Calvin Klein Jeans shoulder bag on a **silver chain** or the Minimal Monogram flap bag with its silver-tone CK clasp. Carry only a card holder or a slim wallet inside, and let the bag finish the outfit.

## For travel

On a trip, the backpack or a tote carries the day, the toiletry bag keeps cosmetics together in the suitcase, and a **zip-around** wallet keeps cards and cash secure. Black pieces are the most practical for travel, as they hide marks from airports, taxis and long days out.

## For gifts through the year

Birthdays, graduations, Mother's Day and **Eid** all call for gifts that are easy to get right. A wallet needs no size, a crossbody bag suits almost anyone, and a bag with a matching wallet makes a complete present. Neutral colours are the safest choice when you do not know her wardrobe.

## Colour families

Calvin Klein's women's pieces fall into two families: **warm neutrals**, vanilla, caramel, khaki, brown and white, and black with the CK monogram. Staying in one family means every new piece matches the last; mixing them, a black bag with a beige wallet for example, adds a pleasant contrast.

## Calvin Klein and Calvin Klein Jeans

**Calvin Klein** is the main line: clean colour blocks and the classic monogram tote. **Calvin Klein Jeans** is the casual, urban line: black bags with bolder lettering, a silver CK clasp or a chain strap. Pieces from both lines combine easily, since they share the same black and neutral palette.

## Materials

Many bags and wallets are made of synthetic leather, often 100% **polyurethane**, or PU, as some product pages state. It is light, colourfast and easy to wipe clean. Some styles are listed as leather. The product page names the material of each piece, so check it if it matters to you.

## Care for bags and wallets

Wipe synthetic leather with a soft, slightly damp cloth, never alcohol, and dry it in the shade. Store bags stuffed with paper in their dust bags and wallets in their **boxes**, keep light colours away from dark denim, and do not hang a heavy bag by its handles for weeks.
