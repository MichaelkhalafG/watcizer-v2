Two women's Michael Kors watches can share a case and still look completely different, because the dial and the bracelet decide the character. This guide sorts our 150 women's models by those two choices: first the face, from plain dials to crystals, mother-of-pearl and chronographs; then what holds it, from link bracelets to mesh, leather and rubber. Finish with the size, and you have your watch.

## Plain dials

A plain dial with slim hands and simple markers is the most versatile face: easy to read and calm enough for every day. The **Runway Slim** models are the clearest example, with clean dials in silver, gold, rose gold, blue, purple or turquoise on a slim steel case. If you want one watch to wear to work every day, start here.

## Crystal dials and bezels

**Darci** surrounds the dial with a ring of crystals, and several other models set crystals on the dial itself or at the hour markers. Crystals catch the light at every movement of the wrist, which makes these watches natural for evenings, engagements and weddings. On a rose gold or silver Darci, the sparkle stays elegant rather than loud.

## Mother-of-pearl

A few models use a **mother-of-pearl** dial, such as the white pearl Darci in rose gold and a gold model with a pearl face. Mother-of-pearl shimmers softly and changes colour with the light, from white to faint pink and grey. It is a quieter alternative to crystals for a woman who likes subtle detail.

## Chronograph dials

A chronograph adds three small sub-dials and a stopwatch, which gives a watch a busier, sportier face. **Parker** combines the chronograph with a crystal-set bezel, Bradshaw keeps it bold and steel, and the Runway Twist pairs it with a brown tortoise-effect bracelet. Choose a chronograph if you like a watch that reads as a statement piece.

## Coloured dials

Beyond white, silver and gold, the range includes dials in **pink**, purple, red, blue and turquoise. A coloured dial on a silver bracelet looks fresh and young; on gold or rose gold, it looks richer. Purple models come both with a purple dial on silver and with an all-purple steel case and bracelet, for a woman who loves the colour.

## Link bracelets

Most models, 126 of the 150, sit on a **stainless steel** link bracelet in gold, rose gold, silver or two-tone. A link bracelet is the most durable and the easiest to keep clean, and it looks equally right with a blouse or a knit. It is sized by removing links, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty.

## Mesh bracelets

A **mesh** bracelet is a fine woven band, as on several Runway Slim, Darci and Mini Pyper models. It sits flat and supple on the wrist, feels lighter than links and gives a slim, jewellery-like line. Mesh bracelets usually adjust with a sliding clasp, which makes them easy to fit to a slim wrist.

## Leather straps

Sixteen models are on **leather**, such as Layton and Bryn in beige, Averi in white and a red leather model with crystals. Leather feels warm and softer on the wrist, and light colours look especially pretty in spring and summer. Leather does not like water or perfume, so keep it for dry days and put perfume on before the watch.

## Rubber straps

A few models, mostly chronographs, come on a **rubber** strap. Rubber is the practical choice for sport, the beach and hot days, because it shrugs off sweat and rinses clean under the tap. Check the water rating on the product page: 50 m (5 ATM) allows a short pool swim, 30 m means splashes only and 100 m or more is fine for the sea.

## Small cases for slim wrists

If your wrist is slim, below about 15 cm, look at the **Mini** models: Mini Pyper, Mini Emery and Bradshaw Mini, and the smallest cases from 33 mm. A small case keeps the watch in proportion and slides under a fitted sleeve. On an average wrist, the most common sizes here, 38 and 39 mm, look balanced without dominating.

## Larger cases

Some women's chronographs and statement models reach 40 to 43 mm. A larger case reads boldly and suits a wide wrist or a woman who likes an oversized look. If you are between sizes, remember that a chronograph with a crystal **bezel** looks larger than a plain watch of the same diameter.

## Wearing a watch with bracelets

A watch shares the wrist well with one or two fine bracelets in the same **metal** tone: a rose gold watch with rose gold bangles, a silver one with silver. Wear the bracelets on the side away from the watch's crown, or on the other wrist, so they do not scratch the case or the crystals.

## One for the day, one for the evening

If you are building a small watch wardrobe, pair a plain-dial **Runway** or a leather model for everyday wear with a Darci or another crystal-set watch for evenings. The two together cover the office, the weekend and every occasion, and they can share the same metal tone so your jewellery works with both.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between Runway and Runway Slim?

Both carry the Runway name and its clean, round design. Runway Slim uses a thinner case and a simpler dial, often on a steel or mesh bracelet, so it sits flatter on the wrist and slides under a sleeve. Runway Twist adds a chronograph with a tortoise-effect bracelet for a bolder look.

### Which model has crystals around the dial?

Darci is the model known for its ring of crystals around the dial, and several Darci versions add crystal markers or a pink-crystal accent. Parker combines a crystal-set bezel with a chronograph. Other models set crystals on the dial itself; each product page shows the crystals in close-up photos.

### Which size suits a slim wrist?

On a wrist below about 15 cm, choose a Mini model or a case from 33 to 36 mm, such as Mini Pyper, Mini Emery or Bradshaw Mini. On an average wrist, 38 to 39 mm, the most common sizes here, look balanced. Large chronographs from 40 mm suit a wider wrist or an oversized style.

### Can the bracelet be sized?

Yes. Steel link bracelets are sized by removing links, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty. Mesh bracelets usually adjust with a sliding clasp, and leather and rubber straps adjust with their buckle holes.
