An Armani Exchange bag is about attitude as much as practicality: a clean black shape, a confident logo and silver details that catch the light. Before you choose, it helps to look at four things on each product page: how visible the logo is, how the bag is carried, what it holds and what it is made of. This guide explains each, using the bags we have in stock.

## How visible is the logo

Our Armani Exchange bags cover the whole range of branding. The most **discreet** use a tonal A|X plaque or embossed lettering in the same colour as the bag. A silver-tone AX logo or a metal charm adds a small highlight. The **boldest** carry an all-over geometric print or monogram. Pick the one that suits your wardrobe.

## How it is carried

Totes have two long **handles** for the shoulder and, on most, a detachable strap for carrying across the body. The crescent, the Liz and the camera bag have adjustable shoulder or crossbody straps. The monogram chain bag hangs from a silver chain. The backpack has padded straps and a top handle.

## What it holds

The **camera** bag and the small Liz shoulder bag carry a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick. The crescent bag adds a wallet. The totes and the satchel hold a notebook, a water bottle and the day's essentials, and the satchel has three inner sections. The backpack carries the most and suits a full day.

## What it is made of

Armani Exchange bags use **coated** and grained finishes that are light, keep their colour and wipe clean easily. Some have a refined grained texture, others a smooth or embossed surface. The product page describes the finish and the material of each bag, so check it if a particular material matters to you.

## The totes

We carry four **totes**: a structured black tote with a tonal A|X plaque, a grained black tote with a detachable metal logo charm, a black and ash grey tote with a geometric logo print, and an off-white tote with embossed logos and a silver-tone charm. All suit a working day or a long day out.

## The satchel

The structured black **satchel** has rounded top handles, a silver-tone AX logo and three inner sections. It keeps its shape, stands on a desk and looks the most professional of all our Armani Exchange bags, which makes it the natural choice for an office or a formal appointment.

## The crescent and the Liz

The **crescent** shoulder bag has a curved, modern shape and a silver-tone A|X buckle; the small **Liz** shoulder bag has a shiny finish and a compact size. Both sit neatly under the arm and move easily from day to evening, carrying only what you need.

## The camera bag

The **camera** bag in black and dark grey is a compact, boxy crossbody with an all-over logo print and a bold tonal AX plaque. Worn across the body, it keeps your hands free and your essentials in front of you, ideal for markets, concerts and travel days.

## The chain bag and the backpack

The white and grey **monogram** chain bag, with a silver-tone AX clasp and a silver chain, is the evening piece of the range. The black embossed-logo **backpack**, with padded straps, is the practical one, suited to university, commuting and travel, and to women and men alike.

## Silver hardware

The clasps, buckles, charms and chain are mostly **silver-tone**, cool and modern. They match a steel watch and silver or white-gold jewellery. If you mostly wear gold, a tonal or embossed logo bag keeps the metal discreet, so it does not compete with your jewellery.

## Black and the lighter options

**Black** is the backbone of the range and goes with everything. The **off-white** tote and the white and grey chain bag lighten the look for spring and summer, and the ash grey and dark grey prints add pattern while staying neutral. Light bags show marks sooner, so wipe them now and then.

## Care

Wipe the surface with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry it in the shade; avoid alcohol and strong cleaners. Wipe metal logos and the **chain** with a dry cloth to keep their shine. Store the bag stuffed with paper in its dust bag, away from dark denim, which can transfer dye to light bags.

## Choosing one as a gift

For a student, the camera bag or the backpack. For a colleague, the satchel or a structured tote. For a friend who loves to go out, the monogram chain bag or the Liz. For a minimalist, the crescent bag with its tonal finish. Keep the invoice, so the bag can be exchanged within 14 days.

## Questions and answers

### Which Armani Exchange bag has the quietest logo?

The structured black tote with the tonal A|X plaque, and bags with embossed lettering in the same colour as the bag. Both show the brand without contrast or shine. Bags with a silver-tone logo, a charm or an all-over print are more visible, so choose them when you want the logo to stand out.

### Can the totes be worn across the body?

Most of our Armani Exchange totes come with a detachable shoulder strap, so you can carry them by the handles or on the strap. The product page lists the straps included with each tote. For true hands-free carrying, the camera bag and the backpack are the better choices.

### Is the backpack suitable for men?

Yes. The black embossed-logo backpack has a clean, unisex design with padded straps and a top handle. It suits university, commuting and travel for women and men alike, and its black colour goes with any outfit, from jeans to a smart jacket.

### Can I see the inside of a bag before ordering?

The product photos show the outside and the main details, and the description lists the inner sections where available. If you need more, ask us on WhatsApp. With cash on delivery you can also open the parcel and check the bag in front of the courier before you pay.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
