A wallet is the accessory you open many times a day, so it is worth choosing one that is easy to use and still looks good on a café table. The Guess wallets we carry follow one practical shape, the zip-around, and differ in texture, colour and details. This guide explains those differences, what fits inside and how to keep the wallet looking new.

## The zip-around shape

A **zip-around** wallet closes with one zip that runs along three sides. When it is open, it folds out like a small book, with card slots on both sides and a long note compartment in the middle; on many models a zipped coin pocket sits in the centre. When it is closed, nothing can slip out, even at the bottom of a full bag. That makes it the most secure everyday wallet for women.

## Quilted: soft and dressy

Four of our Guess wallets are **quilted**, with stitched diamonds or lines that give the surface a padded, slightly glossy look. Quilting is the dressiest finish here, and it pairs naturally with a quilted bag. We carry it in black, white and pink, with gold-tone hardware on some models and gold studs on one.

## The 4G monogram

The **monogram** wallets carry the 4G pattern, four interlocking G letters repeated across the surface. One black model adds a gold metal logo, and one brown model is a double-zip wristlet. If you want everyone to recognise the wallet as Guess at a glance, the monogram is the one to choose.

## Pebbled: the hard-wearing choice

**Pebbled** texture is a fine, grained surface that hides small scratches and fingerprints. Our pebbled Guess wallet is black and plain, with no pattern, which makes it the most understated wallet on the page and the one that ages most gracefully with daily use.

## The double-zip wristlet

A **wristlet** has a short strap that loops around the wrist, so it can be carried on its own like a mini clutch. Our brown monogram wristlet has 2 zipped compartments, one for cards and one for notes or a phone, depending on its size. It is ideal for a short outing when you do not want to carry a bag at all.

## Colours: black, white, pink and brown

**Black** is the most practical colour: it matches every bag and hides marks. **White** looks fresh and elegant but shows stains sooner, so it suits a careful owner or a gift for summer. **Pink** is the cheerful choice, and **brown** monogram looks warm and classic. If the wallet will live inside a black bag, a pink or white one is also easier to find.

## What fits inside

A zip-around wallet holds a typical set of bank cards, an ID card, a few loyalty cards, folded notes and coins. Most phones do not fit in a standard zip-around wallet, so check the measurements on the product page if you want one that does. Keep it to about **10** cards, since an overfilled wallet strains the zip and stretches the card slots. Move receipts out every week or so; they add bulk faster than anything else.

## Wallet and bag size

A long zip-around wallet needs a bag with room for it: a **shoulder bag**, a **tote** or a large crossbody bag. For a mini bag, choose the wristlet or a small card holder instead. If you are buying both at once, look at the bag's measurements and make sure the wallet's length fits inside with room for your phone and keys.

## Matching the hardware

Gold-tone details on a wallet look best with gold-tone buckles on the bag and with a gold or rose-gold watch. If your bag and watch are silver, a plain wallet without metal details is the safer match. The [Guess brand page](/brand/guess) shows the bags and watches we carry next to the wallets.

## Looking after a Guess wallet

Wipe the wallet with a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol or a strong cleaner. Open and close the **zip** gently, pulling along its line rather than at an angle, and never force it over a bulging stack of cards. Keep white and pink wallets away from dark jeans and newspaper ink, which can transfer colour, and store the wallet in its box when you are not using it. If the zip ever starts to stick, rub the teeth lightly with a dry candle or a pencil lead rather than pulling harder.
