Giorgio Armani founded his fashion house in Milan in 1975 and launched Emporio Armani a few years later as its younger, more accessible line. The watches follow the house style: understated, well proportioned and never shouting, with the eagle logo kept small. They are produced under licence by one of the large watch groups and run on quartz or automatic movements. This guide explains the lines we carry and how to choose between them.

## The lines in our range

The titles name the line, and the line tells you the kind of watch. **Sea Explorer** is the diver: a steel case, a rotating bezel, luminous markers and 200 metres of water resistance, the most of any watch in our Emporio Armani range. **World Explorer** is the traveller's watch, with a clean sporty dial. **Racer** is the chronograph inspired by motorsport, with sub-dials and a tachymeter. **Gino** and **Carlo** are the dressier pieces, slimmer and calmer, made for a suit or a smart shirt. For women, **Myea** offers slim cases and refined dials.

If you know the line you like, search for it by name: most come in two or three dial colours, and the [men's Emporio Armani watches page](/brand/emporio-armani/men) shows them with filters for strap and colour.

## The 43 mm case and how it wears

Half of the Emporio Armani watches we list measure 43 millimetres across, and most of the rest sit between 42 and 44 mm. That is a modern men's size: present on the wrist without looking oversized. The cases are well shaped, with curved lugs that follow the wrist, so a 43 mm Emporio Armani often wears smaller than a flat-lugged watch of the same width. If you have a slim wrist, choose a model on leather, which sits lower than a steel bracelet and looks lighter and smaller.

## Automatic or quartz

Most of our Emporio Armani watches are quartz, which means a battery, excellent accuracy and no need to wind. 7 are automatic, and those are the ones to look at if you enjoy mechanical watchmaking: they wind themselves as you wear them, often show the movement through an open dial or a glass back, and run for around a day and a half after you take them off. An automatic is less precise than quartz — a few seconds a day — which is normal for a mechanical movement and not a fault.

## Steel, leather and the finishing

Steel bracelets are the most common choice in our range, often with brushed and polished surfaces side by side, which is where you see the care in an Emporio Armani case. Leather straps give the dressier models a softer look. A steel bracelet needs sizing, and we do it free of charge in our stores while the watch is under [warranty](/warranty); a leather strap fits most wrists from the box.

Two-tone and gold-tone versions exist for some lines. They look striking with a dark suit but show wear on the clasp sooner than plain steel, so they suit someone who rotates watches rather than wearing one every day.

## Water resistance: from 30 to 200 metres

Emporio Armani is a brand where the water rating really varies between models, so read it on the product page. Many dress and fashion models are rated at 30 or 50 metres: rain and splashes are fine, a short pool swim only at 50. The **Sea Explorer** divers are rated at 200 metres and are made for swimming and the sea, with a screw-down crown that must be screwed in fully before you go into the water. Whatever the rating, rinse the watch with fresh water after the sea and never press chronograph pushers under water.

## Questions and answers

### Can I swim with an Emporio Armani watch?

It depends on the model. The Sea Explorer divers are rated at 200 metres and are made for swimming and the sea, as long as the crown is screwed down. Models rated at 50 metres are fine for a short swim in a pool, and those at 30 metres only for rain and splashes. The rating is on every product page, so check it before you go into the water.

### What is the difference between Gino and Racer?

Gino is the dress watch: a slimmer case, a calm dial and usually a leather strap or a fine bracelet, made to sit under a shirt cuff. Racer is the sporty chronograph, with sub-dials, a tachymeter scale and a bolder look for casual wear. Owning one of each covers both; if you can only choose one, pick the one that suits the clothes you wear most days.

### How long does an automatic Emporio Armani run off the wrist?

Usually around a day and a half. After that it stops, which is normal for an automatic. To restart it, wind the crown about 20 times or wear it for a while, then set the time. If you rotate several watches, a watch winder keeps an automatic running, but it is not needed for a watch you wear most days.

### What does the warranty cover?

The product page and the card in the box state the warranty length and who provides it. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. It does not cover a broken glass, damage from a fall, or water that entered because the crown was not pushed or screwed in. Keep the card with your invoice. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details and how to claim.

### Is the bracelet adjustable?

Yes. Steel bracelets have removable links, and some have a fine-adjust position in the clasp for the last few millimetres. We size them for free in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty; bring the watch with its box and invoice. Keep the removed links in the box in case you need them later.
