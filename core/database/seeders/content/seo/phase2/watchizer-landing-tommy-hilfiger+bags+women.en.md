Tommy Hilfiger's women's bags follow the same idea as its clothes: simple shapes, a confident colour palette and a small flag or monogram that tells you the brand without shouting. That makes them easy to wear with jeans, office clothes and abayas alike. This page gathers every women's bag we carry, and the guide below helps you choose a shape, a colour and a size, and look after the bag once it is yours.

## Black or navy: the two colours of this page

Almost every bag here is **black** or **navy blue**. Black is the safest colour of all: it matches every outfit and shoe, hides daily marks and looks right from morning to night. Navy is the brand's signature colour, a little softer than black and very elegant with white, beige, grey and denim; it also pairs naturally with a Tommy Hilfiger watch on a navy dial or strap. If you already own a black bag, navy is the most useful second one, because it changes the look without making matching any harder.

## Handbags and shoulder bags

The **handbag**, carried by its top handle in the hand or on the forearm, is the most polished shape and suits the office and meetings. The **shoulder bag** sits under the arm on a medium strap, leaves your hands free and is the most practical for a full day out. Many of our models combine both, with a top handle and a detachable strap. A medium shoulder bag holds a wallet, a phone, keys, sunglasses and a small make-up pouch, which covers most days.

## Crossbody bags

A **crossbody bag** is the compact option, worn on a long strap across the body. It keeps your phone and cards in front of you, which is the safest way to carry a bag on public transport, in a busy mall or in a crowded market, and it leaves both hands free for shopping or for children. It is also the best travel bag for passports and boarding passes. If you choose one bag for errands and weekends, choose this one.

## Backpacks

The women's **backpacks** on this page are the practical choice for university, long days and travel. They spread the weight across both shoulders, hold a tablet or notebook comfortably and keep a clean, structured look that is smarter than a sports backpack. Black leather or leather-look versions look polished enough for an office that allows a backpack.

## Choosing the size

Empty your current bag and look at what you really carry. A phone, a card holder and keys — 3 small items — fit any crossbody bag. A full wallet and sunglasses need a medium shoulder bag. A tablet, a notebook or a water bottle need a large shoulder bag, a handbag with a wide base or a backpack. Each product page lists the width, height and depth in centimetres; measure your current bag and compare, rather than judging from photos.

## Closures and inside pockets

How a bag closes matters as much as its size. A **zip** top keeps everything in and is the safest choice in crowds; a **flap** with a magnetic snap is quicker to open but less secure; an open tote is the fastest and the least protected. Inside, look for at least one zipped pocket for keys or cash and a slip pocket for a phone, so you are not digging through the whole bag at the till. The product photos show most bags open, so you can see their layout before you decide.

## Caring for your bag

Wipe the bag with a soft, dry cloth after use and blot any spill right away. Keep it out of a hot parked car in summer, where a 35-degree day can soften coatings and glue, and away from dark denim if it is a light-lined or coated piece. Store it empty, stuffed with paper and in its dust bag, so it keeps its shape. Never use household cleaners or wet wipes.

## Questions and answers

### Which bag is best for work?

A medium or large shoulder bag or a handbag with a detachable strap, in black or navy. It should hold a wallet, a phone, a charger and a notebook; if you carry a tablet, check the width on the product page. A structured shape keeps its form when full and looks smarter in meetings.

### Is navy easier to match than black?

Black is the easiest to match with everything, but navy is almost as easy and looks softer, especially with white, beige, grey and denim. If you mostly wear dark colours, black is the better choice; if your wardrobe is lighter, navy often looks more elegant.

### Can I wear a crossbody bag on the shoulder?

Usually yes. Most crossbody straps are adjustable: shorten the strap to wear the bag on the shoulder, lengthen it to wear it across the body. The product page shows the strap and its length, and the photos show how the bag sits.

### How do I know the bag is the right size?

Each product page lists the width, height and depth in centimetres, and the photos show the bag from several sides. Measure a bag you already use and compare, and make sure your largest item fits within the stated width. If you are unsure, send the product link to our customer service on WhatsApp.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
