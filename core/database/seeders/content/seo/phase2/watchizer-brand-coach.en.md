Coach began in 1941 as a small family workshop in Manhattan, making leather goods by hand, and it became one of the best-known American names in handbags. Its approach has always been practical luxury: bags made to be used every day, in leather that softens and improves with age. This guide explains the two materials you will see in our Coach range, the shapes, the colours and how to look after a Coach bag in Egypt's heat and dust.

## Leather or Signature canvas

Most Coach bags in our shop come in one of two materials. **Smooth or pebbled leather** is the brand's heritage: it feels soft, ages well and develops a gentle sheen with use. **Signature canvas** is the coated fabric printed with Coach's interlocking "C" pattern, often trimmed with leather; it is lighter, more resistant to scratches and rain, and very easy to wipe clean, which makes it a practical everyday choice. If you want a bag that looks better after five years, choose leather; if you want the most carefree bag for daily use, choose Signature canvas.

## Shoulder bags, crossbody bags and totes

**Shoulder bags** are the largest group in our Coach range, and they suit most days: big enough for a wallet, a phone, keys, sunglasses and a small pouch. **Crossbody bags** have a long strap worn across the body; they keep your hands free and your things in front of you, which is the safest way to carry a bag in a crowded street or a mall. **Totes** are the roomiest, open-topped or zipped, and work for the office or university with a tablet or a water bottle. Many Coach bags have a detachable strap, so one bag can be carried both ways. The [Coach bags page](/brand/coach/bags) lists every shape with its measurements in centimetres.

## The colours and the hardware

Black and brown lead the range, followed by 4 more neutrals — khaki, chalk, tan and walnut — with a few lighter and seasonal shades such as pink, ivory and floral prints. Neutral colours are the easiest to match and show wear least; chalk and ivory look fresh in summer but need more care to stay clean. The metal details — clasps, rings and the Coach plaque — are mostly gold-tone or brass, which looks warm and pairs well with gold jewellery and watches. If you mostly wear silver, choose a darker leather where the hardware stands out less.

## Caring for a Coach bag in Egypt

Heat, sun and dust are what age a bag fastest here. Keep it out of a parked car in summer, because the heat can soften the glue and the edge paint. Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth after a dusty day, and Signature canvas with a slightly damp cloth. Do not use household cleaners or alcohol on either. When you store a bag, empty it, stuff it with paper so it keeps its shape, and keep it in its dust bag away from direct sun, which fades the lighter colours. A leather bag that gets caught in the rain should be dried at room temperature, never with a hair dryer.

## Questions and answers

### Is Signature canvas real leather?

No, Signature canvas is a coated fabric printed with the Coach "C" pattern, usually trimmed with real leather on the handles and edges. It is lighter than leather, resists scratches and light rain better, and is easier to clean. Many people choose it precisely for that reason, as a bag they can use every day without worrying about it.

### How do I know the bag is big enough for my things?

Each product page lists the width, height and depth in centimetres, and the photos show the bag from several sides and often open. Measure a bag you already own and compare, or check that your largest item, such as a tablet, fits within the stated width. If you have a question about a specific bag, send our customer service the product link on WhatsApp.

### Can a Coach bag be cleaned if it gets stained?

Fresh marks on Signature canvas usually come off with a slightly damp cloth. On leather, blot liquid immediately with a dry cloth and let it dry naturally; do not rub, and do not use household cleaners, which can strip the colour. For stubborn stains on leather, a leather-care specialist is the safest option.
