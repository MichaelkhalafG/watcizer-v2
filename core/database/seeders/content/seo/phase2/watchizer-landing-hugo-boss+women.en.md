A women's Hugo Boss watch carries the German fashion house's precise, modern style into a smaller, more delicate form. The designs are clean, with three hands and a single numeral at 12, and the sparkle comes from a bezel set with crystals rather than from a busy dial. This guide introduces each model we carry and helps you choose a colour, a size and an occasion.

## Breath in emerald and gold

The **Breath** is the model we carry most of. Its gold-tone version pairs an emerald green sunray dial with a gold-tone case and bracelet, and a bezel decorated with crystals and the BOSS lettering. Green and gold together look rich and warm, a striking choice for evenings and for anyone who already wears gold jewellery.

## Breath in light green and silver

The second **Breath** has a light green sunray dial in a silver-tone steel case, with the same BOSS-lettered bezel and crystal accents and a multilink bracelet. The soft green feels fresh and modern, and silver keeps it easy to wear every day. It suits spring and summer outfits and white or silver jewellery.

## Two-tone with a rose-gold bezel

One model combines a silver sunray dial with a **rose gold-tone** bezel set with crystals, rose-gold hands and markers, an Arabic numeral at 12 and a silver steel bracelet with a folding clasp. Two-tone steel matches both silver and rose-gold jewellery, which makes it the most versatile watch on the page.

## Signature

The **Signature** is all silver: a silver sunray dial, a polished bezel with crystal accents and a bracelet of sculpted links with brushed and polished finishes. It is the classic choice, as at home in an office as at a dinner, and the safest gift when you do not know which metal someone wears.

## Allusion in gunmetal

The **Allusion** takes a darker route: a grey sunray dial with rose gold-tone markers and hands, in a gunmetal case with a geometric link bracelet. Gunmetal is a dark grey steel that looks modern and understated, ideal for someone who finds silver too bright and gold too dressy. At 36 mm, it is the largest women's model here.

## Infinity on black mesh

The **Infinity** has a black dial, a crystal-set bezel and a black stainless steel **mesh** bracelet. Black mesh looks sleek and contemporary and sits flat and supple on the wrist. It is the boldest women's Hugo Boss we carry, a good match for black outfits and evening wear.

## What a sunray dial does

Every watch on this page has a **sunray** dial, brushed with fine lines that radiate from the centre. As the wrist turns, a band of light sweeps across the dial, and the colour seems to change from bright to deep. It is why the green Breath dials look so different in daylight and under evening lights.

## The size: 34 to 36 mm

Most women's Hugo Boss watches here measure **34** mm, and the Allusion 36 mm. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 34 mm looks elegant and balanced, large enough to read at a glance yet discreet under a sleeve. If you prefer a slightly bolder look, choose the 36 mm Allusion.

## Water: 30 or 50 metres

Most models list **50 metres**: rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but not the sea. A few list 30 metres, which means splashes only. Whatever the rating, take the watch off before a hot shower, when steam and soap can reach the seals, and never wear it in the sea.

## Bracelets and sizing

The Infinity's **mesh** bracelet is adjusted differently, through its clasp, and the photos show how. Most of the link bracelets close with a folding clasp that stays secure on the wrist.

## Caring for crystals and plating

Crystal-set bezels sparkle most when they are clean. Wipe the watch with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day and keep it away from perfume, creams and cleaning products, which dull both the crystals and the gold or rose-gold plating. Store it in its **box**, away from harder jewellery that can scratch it.

## Battery and accuracy

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside. If the date or time needs setting after a battery change, our stores in Cairo do it for you.

## Questions and answers

### Are the crystals on a Hugo Boss bezel diamonds?

No. They are crystal-style accents, cut and set to catch the light, which keeps these watches at a fashion price. They are fixed firmly, but a hard knock can loosen one, so take the watch off for sport, housework and anything that might strike the wrist.

### Which women's Hugo Boss watch is best for every day?

The silver Signature or the light green Breath. Silver steel goes with every outfit and every kind of jewellery, and a 34 mm case is comfortable all day under a sleeve. For a warmer look that still matches silver, the two-tone model with a rose-gold bezel is the alternative.

### Will the gold or rose-gold colour fade?

The gold and rose-gold tones are plating over stainless steel. With normal care they last a long time; friction and chemicals such as perfume, creams, sea water and cleaning products are what wear them. Put the watch on after applying them and wipe it after a hot day.

### What warranty comes with a Hugo Boss watch?
