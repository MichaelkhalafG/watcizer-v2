Joyroom's strength is breadth: almost every accessory a phone owner needs, built on the same charging standards so the pieces work together. This page groups the brand's range by the problem each product solves, then gives tips on choosing and checking them.

## Problem: a phone that charges slowly

The fix is usually a better charger and cable pair. A Joyroom 20 W charger with PD, or the super slim 20 W model with a USB-A and a Type-C port, fast charges an iPhone or most Android phones. Pair it with a Crystal-Clear Type-C cable or a StarFlight Lightning cable rated 3 A or more. The [chargers page](/subtypes/chargers) covers every model.

## Problem: too many chargers in the bag

A Joyroom GaN charger of 45, 65 or 67 W replaces the laptop brick and the phone charger at once. One version has a retractable cable and a foldable plug; another ships with a 100 W Type-C to Type-C cable of 1.2 m. With 2 Type-C ports and 1 USB-A port, a single charger covers a laptop, a phone and earbuds.

## Problem: a battery that dies by evening

Joyroom power banks of 10 or 20 thousand mAh charge at 22.5 W and show the remaining charge on a display. One 10 thousand mAh model carries its own cables and 3 ports, so you never forget a cable. The [power banks page](/subtypes/power-banks) also lists magnetic and larger models from other makers.

## Problem: noise on the commute

Joyroom's hybrid ANC headphones and its ANC earbuds, including the third-generation T03S Pro, cut the steady noise of the metro and traffic. The T03S Pro Max focuses on comfort and simple controls. For a laptop or an older phone, the metal in-ear earphone with a 3.5 mm plug needs no charging. The [audio page](/subtypes/audio) explains fit and battery.

## Problem: the phone slides around the car

Joyroom car holders mount on the air vent, the dashboard or the windshield, and the ZS259 family includes a flexible-arm version. They hold phones of about 4.7 to 6.9 inches. The [phone holders page](/subtypes/phone-holders) helps you choose by car layout and phone size.

## Problem: an Apple Watch with its own cable

The Joyroom 3-in-1 charger combines a magnetic Apple Watch puck with a Lightning and a Type-C connector on 1 USB-A cable of 1.2 m. It replaces 3 cables on a bedside table or in a travel pouch. Plug it into a charger of 20 W or more so all 3 devices charge at a good speed.

## Problem: a new screen to protect

Joyroom tempered glass protectors are cut for the iPhone 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max and for earlier models, with clear and privacy versions. The slim MagSafe card holder snaps to the back of an iPhone 12 or later. The [screen protectors page](/subtypes/screen-protectors) explains how to fit glass without bubbles.

## Standards that tie it together

Joyroom chargers, cables and power banks follow USB Power Delivery for iPhones and many Android phones and Quick Charge 3.0 for many Samsung, Xiaomi and Oppo models. GaN keeps chargers small and cool, and ANC cancels steady noise. Knowing these 4 terms makes the product titles easy to read and compare.

## Buying a set, not a single piece

Most customers end up with a set: a charger, 2 cables, a power bank and a protector. Buying them from 1 brand avoids speed mismatches, such as a 65 W charger slowed by a 30 W cable. A good starting set is a 65 W GaN charger, a 100 W Type-C cable, a Lightning or Type-C phone cable and a 10 thousand mAh power bank.

## Questions and answers

### Is a Joyroom 65 W charger safe for my phone?

Yes. Your phone negotiates the power it accepts through PD or QC, so a 65 W charger simply delivers less to a phone and more to a laptop. Use a cable rated for fast charging. One charger can then cover a laptop, a tablet and a phone.

### Which Joyroom earbuds cancel noise?

The third-generation T03S Pro with ANC, and the over-ear headphones with hybrid ANC, both cancel steady noise such as engines and traffic. The T03S Pro Max focuses on comfort without ANC. The product titles state ANC where a model has it.
