Lacoste's style is sporty and clean, recognisable by the small crocodile. This page gathers everything Lacoste at Brand Fashion: watches, wallets, a belt and bracelets. The guide below explains the watch sizes and straps, the leather goods, and how to build a coordinated set from 1 brand.

## The watches

The Lacoste range here counts more than 60 quartz watches, about 3 in 4 of them for men. Cases run from 38 to 44 mm, with dials in black, blue and green and the crocodile on the dial or the crown. They are light, easy to read and comfortable for daily wear, which suits the brand's sporty spirit.

## The Boston line

Several watches on this page belong to the Boston line, including a men's model with a green dial. Boston watches have a clean round case and a sporty strap or bracelet, and they work with a polo shirt at the weekend as well as with a shirt at the office. The product titles name the line where it applies.

## Straps: silicone, steel, rubber or leather

Silicone is a Lacoste signature: soft, light and available in strong colours, perfect for summer and sport. Steel bracelets, on about 33 watches, look smarter for work and evenings. Rubber and leather straps, on a smaller number, add a sporty or a classic touch. Silicone and rubber can be rinsed with water after a hot day.

## Sizes for men and women

Men's Lacoste watches mostly measure 42 to 44 mm, a comfortable sporty size on most wrists. Women's models are smaller, around 36 to 38 mm. There are also about 5 unisex models for couples or for anyone who likes a middle size. Check the case size on each product page before you order.

## Wallets with the crocodile

Lacoste's 4 men's wallets come in black leather: bifolds with a silver-tone crocodile, a vertical wallet, a crosshatch-textured bifold with a metal logo, and a bifold with an oversized white logo for a bolder look. They are slim enough for a front pocket and suit both casual and smart outfits.

## Care for watches and leather

Rinse silicone straps with water after sport and let them dry. Wipe steel bracelets with a soft cloth. Keep leather wallets and the belt away from rain and heat, and do not overfill the wallet. Replace a quartz watch's battery every 2 to 3 years and have the seals checked at the same time.

## Water and sport

Lacoste watches are made for everyday activity: they resist rain, splashes and hand washing, and many are rated 30 to 50 m. Check the rating on each product page before swimming, and never press the crown under water. For the pool or the sea, a watch rated 100 m from the [sport watches page](/subtypes/sport) is the safer choice.

### Are Lacoste watches in stock?

Lacoste watches arrive in batches, and at times most are between deliveries while the wallets, the belt and the bracelets remain in stock. Switch on the in-stock filter to see what ships today, and leave your e-mail on a watch's page so we can write to you as soon as it returns.

### Which Lacoste watch suits a small wrist?

Look at the women's and unisex models of about 36 to 38 mm, or the smallest men's cases near 38 to 40 mm. A silicone strap sits comfortably on a slim wrist. Check the case size on each product page, and ask our team on WhatsApp if you would like help comparing models.

### Can I swim with a Lacoste watch?

Most Lacoste watches resist rain and splashes and many are rated 30 to 50 m, which covers hand washing and a shower. Check each product page before swimming, and avoid hot water and saunas. For regular swimming, a watch rated 100 m or more is the better choice.

### Can I exchange a Lacoste item?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it if it is unused and in its packaging, and we pay the return shipping. Watch bracelets must not have been shortened. Send your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup and the exchange.
