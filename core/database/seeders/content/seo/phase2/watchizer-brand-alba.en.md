Alba belongs to Seiko, one of the best-known Japanese watchmakers, and sits beside brands such as Lorus and Pulsar as one of the group's more affordable names. That family connection is the point: an Alba gives you Japanese quartz or automatic movements and solid steel construction at a price below the Seiko name itself. ## The collections in the titles

Alba titles usually name the collection, and it is the fastest way to understand the watch. **Fashion** is the largest group in our range and is mostly women's: slim steel cases, link or mesh bracelets and soft dial colours. **Active** is the sporty men's line, with chronographs, bold markers and, on many models, luminous hands for reading the time in the dark. **Standard** covers simple three-hand watches for men and women, and **Prestige** and **Signa** are the dressier pieces, with finer finishing and more classic faces.

If you like a design but want another colour, search the collection name with the colour you want; most models come in two or three dial variations. The [women's Alba watches page](/brand/alba/women) and the [men's Alba watches page](/brand/alba/men) show each range with its filters.

## Women's Alba watches

About 7 in 10 of our Alba watches are women's models. They are designed to be worn every day: stainless steel cases, steel bracelets with a folding clasp, and dials in silver, white, light blue, pink and **mother-of-pearl**, a natural shell surface that shifts colour as it catches the light. Mother-of-pearl dials are never exactly alike, so the photos of your watch and the one you receive can differ slightly in the pattern; that is the nature of the material, not a fault.

A steel bracelet needs sizing, and we do it for free in our stores while the watch is under warranty. If you prefer a softer feel, a few Alba women's models come on leather, which is lighter and warmer on the skin in winter. In summer, a steel bracelet is the most comfortable, because it does not absorb sweat and washes easily.

## Men's Alba watches

The men's range is led by the **Active** chronographs: steel cases, black or dark grey dials, a stopwatch with sub-dials and, often, luminous hands. Beside them are 4 automatic models, which wind themselves as you wear them and need no battery, and a few simple Standard pieces for the office. The men's Alba models we list use steel bracelets or leather, with one or two on nylon or silicone for a more casual look.

Japanese quartz is the main reason Alba watches last. A quartz movement keeps accurate time to within seconds a month, and a battery usually lasts 2 to 3 years. When the second hand starts jumping in 2-second or 4-second steps, the battery is getting low: change it before it stops.

## Reading the specifications before you order

Each Alba product page lists the details that decide how the watch will feel and last, and three of them are worth a minute. The movement tells you whether it needs a battery (quartz) or winds itself as you wear it (automatic). The glass tells you how well it resists scratches: sapphire is the hardest and almost never scratches in daily use, while mineral glass is tougher against knocks but marks more easily. The water resistance, in metres, tells you what the watch survives: 30 metres means splashes and rain, 50 metres means washing and a short swim in a pool, and 100 metres is safer for the sea. The clasp matters too: a folding clasp on a steel bracelet opens with 1 press and is the easiest to put on with one hand.

If a detail you need is missing from the product page, send the watch's link to our customer service on WhatsApp and we answer with the specification before you order, so you never find out after delivery that the watch does not suit how you will use it.

## Why choose Alba over a fashion brand

Fashion-brand watches are bought for the name and the design. Alba is bought for the watch itself, with movements from the Seiko group, steel cases and a sober design that does not date after a season. If you want a watch to wear every day for many years, that matters more than a logo, and it is why Alba is a common choice for a graduation, a new job or a parent's gift. If you want a bold statement, the fashion brands in our range give you more colour and size; if you want something that simply works and looks right with everything, Alba is the better fit.

## Caring for an Alba watch

Most Alba watches have a steel case and a mineral or sapphire glass; the product page names the glass. Steel can be wiped clean with a soft, damp cloth, and the bracelet links with a soft toothbrush and a little water. Keep perfume and creams off mother-of-pearl dials and leather straps. Do not open the case back yourself to change the battery; a watch opened without the right tools can lose its water resistance, and the [warranty](/warranty) no longer covers it.

## Questions and answers

### Is Alba really made by Seiko?

Alba is one of the brands of the Seiko group, the Japanese watchmaker, alongside other affordable names such as Lorus and Pulsar. The watches use movements from the group and are sold at a lower price than watches carrying the Seiko name itself. That makes Alba a good way to own a Japanese watch on a moderate budget.

### Will the mother-of-pearl dial look like the photo?

Very close, but not identical. Mother-of-pearl is a natural material taken from the inside of shells, so every dial has its own pattern and play of colour. Our photos show the model, not your individual piece. If you open your order on delivery and the dial is not to your liking, you can refuse it with no delivery fee.

### How long does the battery last in an Alba quartz watch?

Usually 2 to 3 years, and longer on simple three-hand models than on chronographs that are used often. A second hand that jumps several seconds at a time is the signal to change it. While the watch is under warranty we change the battery for free in our stores; bring the warranty card and invoice.

### What warranty does an Alba watch have?

The warranty length and who provides it are written on each product page and on the card in the box. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. It does not cover a cracked glass, damage from a fall, or water that entered because the case was opened. The [warranty page](/warranty) gives the full details and how to claim it.

### Can I return an Alba watch?
