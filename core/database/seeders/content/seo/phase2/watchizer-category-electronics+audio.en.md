Good sound turns a commute, a workout or a long flight into time you enjoy. This guide explains the 3 kinds of listening devices on this page, what noise cancelling does, and how to choose between wireless and wired for the way you listen.

## The 3 types on this page

Over-ear headphones cover the ears completely, give the fullest sound and the strongest noise cancelling, and are the most comfortable for hours. True wireless earbuds are the smallest and come in a charging case. A wired in-ear earphone plugs into a 3.5 mm jack and needs no battery. Each has its place, and many people own 2 of the 3.

## Active noise cancelling

ANC uses small microphones to listen to the noise around you, then plays an opposite sound wave that cancels it. It can reduce steady noise by many decibels and works best on sounds such as an aeroplane engine, a metro, an air conditioner or traffic. Hybrid ANC uses 2 sets of microphones, outside and inside the ear cup, for stronger reduction. Voices and sudden sounds are reduced less, which keeps you aware of your surroundings.

## Over-ear wireless headphones

The over-ear model combines hybrid ANC with Bluetooth and a rechargeable battery that lasts many hours between charges. The cushions seal around the ear for comfort and passive isolation. They suit office work, study, travel and long evenings with a film. Turn ANC off in a quiet room to make the battery last even longer.

## True wireless earbuds

The earbuds connect by Bluetooth and recharge in their case, which itself recharges by cable. The Pro Max version focuses on comfort and simple controls, while the third-generation ANC version adds noise cancelling for the metro and the street. Touch controls let you play, pause and answer calls without taking out your phone. They are the most practical choice for the gym and daily trips.

## Wired earphones

The metal in-ear earphone plugs into a 3.5 mm jack and has a 1.2 m cable. There is no battery to charge and no pairing, so it simply works, with no delay between picture and sound, which is useful for video and games. It suits laptops, older phones and anyone who wants a reliable spare pair. For a phone with only Type-C, use a Type-C to 3.5 mm adapter.

## Choosing the right fit

Earbuds usually come with 3 sizes of silicone tips. The right tip seals the ear canal gently: bass improves and outside noise drops. If an earbud falls out, try the next size up. For over-ear headphones, adjust the headband so the cushions sit evenly around the ear. A good fit makes more difference to sound than the price.

## Bluetooth and calls

All wireless models connect to Android phones, iPhones, tablets and laptops through Bluetooth. Keep the phone within about 10 m for a stable signal. Built-in microphones handle calls, and ANC models often reduce background noise for the person you are talking to. To pair with a new device, put the earbuds in pairing mode as the leaflet explains.

## Battery and charging

Wireless earbuds give several hours of listening per charge, and the case adds more charges on the go. Headphones last longer, often a full day. Charge them with a 5 to 20 W charger and a Type-C cable; a higher-watt charger is safe. The [chargers page](/subtypes/chargers) has cables and compact chargers that suit them.

## Caring for your earphones

Clean the tips and the mesh with a dry, soft brush every 7 days, since wax blocks sound. Do not share earbuds, for hygiene. Keep them away from water and sweat-soaked bags unless the product page states a water rating. Store earbuds in their case and headphones in their pouch, away from heat.

## Questions and answers

### Do these earbuds work with an iPhone?

Yes. All the wireless models connect by Bluetooth to iPhones, Android phones, tablets and laptops. Open the Bluetooth settings, put the earbuds in pairing mode as the leaflet explains, and select them. The wired earphone needs a 3.5 mm jack, or a Lightning or Type-C adapter on phones without one.

### What does ANC mean?

ANC means active noise cancelling. Microphones pick up the noise around you, and the earphones play an opposite wave that cancels it out. It works best on steady sounds such as engines, air conditioners and traffic. Hybrid ANC uses microphones inside and outside the ear cup for a stronger effect.

### How long does the battery last?

It depends on the model and the volume. Earbuds usually play for several hours per charge, and the case adds more charges. Over-ear headphones often last a full day or more. Turning ANC off and keeping the volume moderate extends battery life; the product description lists the figures for each model.
