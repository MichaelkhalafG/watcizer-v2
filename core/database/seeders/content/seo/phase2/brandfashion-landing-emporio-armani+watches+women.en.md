A women's Emporio Armani watch is chosen for elegance rather than size. The cases are small and finely finished, the dials are calm, and the bracelets are designed to sit like jewellery. Because stock of each model is limited, this guide describes the families you will meet on the page and what sets them apart, so you can recognise the style you like even when a particular colour is out of stock.

## A range built on 32 mm

The most common case size here is **32** mm, followed by 30 and 34 mm, with a few smaller models of 26 to 28 mm. These are classic proportions for a women's dress watch. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 32 mm looks graceful; 34 mm reads a little more modern; 30 mm and below feel like a piece of jewellery.

## The Gianni T-Bar family

**Gianni T-Bar** is one of the names you will see most often in our titles. Its bracelets and straps take their name from the T-shaped bar that links the case to the band, a detail borrowed from classic jewellery. We have carried it on silver steel, on two-tone silver and gold steel, and on black leather, with clean white dials.

## The plain Gianni

Next to the T-Bar sits the plain **Gianni**, a silver steel watch with a white dial. It is the most understated model in the range and the one that goes with everything, from a work blouse to an evening dress. If you want one Emporio Armani to wear every day, start here.

## Sinfonia and mother of pearl

The **Sinfonia** pairs a mother-of-pearl dial with a silver-tone bracelet. Nacre, the inner layer of a shell, gives each dial its own play of pink, green and blue light, so no two are quite alike. It is the most romantic watch on the page and a natural partner for pearl earrings.

## The oval two-hand watch

One model breaks from the round case: a gold-tone **oval** watch of 26 by 34 mm, with a grey sunray dial, gold Roman numerals and a fine multilink bracelet. It shows only hours and minutes, which keeps the dial serene. Wear it as you would a bracelet, alone on the wrist.

## Gino

The **Gino** appears in both the women's and men's listings: a dress watch in silver steel with a mother-of-pearl dial, or in gold tone on black or brown leather. Because it is listed for both women and men, it is a good choice for a couple who want the same design.

## Sport and automatic models

A few women's models step away from the dress style. A **sport chronograph** on hot-pink silicone brings colour and a stopwatch, and a small group of **automatic** watches, about 5 in the range, wind themselves as you wear them. These are rarer here, so check their stock on the product page.

## Steel, leather or silicone

**Steel** bracelets make up most of the range: bright, durable and easy to wipe clean. They are sized by removing links. **Leather** straps, as on some T-Bar models, look softer and warmer for the office. **Silicone** appears only on the sporty models and suits summer days and travel.

## Water: 30 or 50 metres

Women's Emporio Armani watches list either **30 metres** (3 ATM) or 50 metres (5 ATM). At 30 metres the watch is for splashes: rain and washing your hands. At 50 metres a short swim in a pool is fine, but not the sea. Either way, take the watch off before a shower, when soap and steam can reach the seals.

## Choosing the metal

**Silver** steel suits cool skin tones and white-gold or silver jewellery. **Gold** tone flatters warm skin tones and yellow-gold rings. **Two-tone** steel, as on the Gianni T-Bar, matches both, which makes it the safest choice for a gift when you do not know which metal she wears.

## Dial colours and finishes

Most dials here are **white** or silver, the classic choice for a dress watch. **Mother-of-pearl** adds shimmer, a **sunray** finish catches light in a sweeping band, and a few models have black dials for a sharper look. A plain white dial is the easiest to read and to match with any outfit.

## Questions and answers

### What does T-Bar mean on a Gianni watch?

It refers to the T-shaped bar that joins the case to the bracelet or strap, a design taken from classic jewellery. It gives the watch a more decorative, bracelet-like look than standard lugs. The T-Bar appears on steel, two-tone and leather versions of the Gianni.

### Is 32 mm the right size for me?

For most women, yes. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 32 mm is the classic dress-watch size: elegant, easy to read and discreet under a sleeve. If you prefer a slightly more modern look, choose a 34 mm model; for a jewellery look, 30 mm or smaller.

### Can I shower with an Emporio Armani watch?

No. Even a 50-metre watch should come off before a shower or a bath, because hot water, steam and soap can weaken the seals over time. Rain and washing your hands are fine. If water ever fogs the glass, stop wearing the watch and have it checked.

Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered.
