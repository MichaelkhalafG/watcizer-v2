Coach started in a Manhattan loft in 1941, where a family of craftsmen cut and stitched leather goods by hand. That leather heritage is still at the heart of the brand, alongside the Signature canvas printed with its interlocking C pattern. With a range as large as ours, the easiest way to choose is by how you will use the bag, so this guide walks through size, closure, strap and material, then colour and care.

## Start with the size

Think about what you carry on an ordinary day. A **small** bag holds a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick, and suits evenings and errands. A **medium** shoulder bag, the most common size in our Coach range, adds room for a full wallet, sunglasses and a small pouch, which is enough for most working days. A **large** tote or shoulder bag takes a tablet, a notebook and a water bottle, for university, the office or travel. Every product page gives the width, height and depth in centimetres; measure the bag you use now and compare, rather than guessing from the photos.

## Then the closure

How a bag closes matters for both safety and convenience. A **zip** top is the most secure in crowds and on public transport, because nothing falls out and nothing can be reached in. A **snap** or magnetic flap is quicker to open with one hand but less secure, so it suits a car, an office or an evening. An open tote is the fastest to use and the least secure. If you often carry your bag in busy places, choose a zip; if you mostly drive, a flap or snap is perfectly practical.

## The strap and how you carry it

Most of our Coach bags are **shoulder bags**, carried on a short or medium strap under the arm. Many have a second, longer strap that can be clipped on so the bag can also be worn **crossbody**, across the body with your hands free; check the product photos for the D-rings where it attaches. Handbags with top handles look the most formal and suit meetings and events. If you want one bag to do everything, choose a shoulder bag with a detachable crossbody strap.

## Leather or Signature canvas

Coach leather comes smooth, for a polished look, or pebbled, with a small grain that hides scratches and softens quickly. **Signature canvas** is a coated fabric printed with the C pattern and usually trimmed with leather; it is lighter, resists rain and marks better, and wipes clean easily, which makes it a practical choice for daily use and travel. As a rule of thumb, leather for elegance and ageing well, canvas for low maintenance and the most recognisable Coach look.

## Colours, prints and hardware

Neutral colours lead the range: black, brown, khaki, tan, chalk and walnut, all easy to match. Floral and seasonal prints add a playful touch and work best as a second bag. Hardware is usually gold-tone or brass, warm metals that pair naturally with gold jewellery and watches. ## Caring for a Coach bag

Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth and canvas with a slightly damp one; never use household cleaners, alcohol or wet wipes. Keep the bag out of a hot parked car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside gets far hotter, and heat can soften the glue and the edge paint. Store it empty and stuffed with paper, in its dust bag and out of direct sun, which fades chalk, tan and other light colours. If leather gets wet, let it dry slowly at room temperature, away from heaters and hair dryers.

## Questions and answers

### Which Coach bag is best for work?

A medium or large shoulder bag or a tote in black, brown or khaki, with a zip closure. It should fit a wallet, a phone, a charger and a notebook, and a tablet if you carry one; check the width on the product page. Leather looks the most polished for the office, while Signature canvas is lighter for long days.

### Can every shoulder bag be worn crossbody?

Not every one. Bags with a detachable long strap, or with D-rings ready for one, can be worn crossbody; bags with a single fixed short strap are made for the shoulder only. The product photos show the straps and the rings, and the product page lists the strap length.

### How do I keep a light-coloured bag clean?

Keep it away from dark denim, which can transfer dye, and keep pens and make-up in a pouch inside the bag. Wipe it gently after use and store it in its dust bag. Signature canvas in light shades is easier to keep clean than light smooth leather, so it is the better choice if you want a pale bag for every day.

### Are the bags sent in their dust bag?

The product page shows what comes with each bag. When a dust bag is included, keep it: it is the best way to store the bag between uses and when you travel. Keep the tags on until you are sure about the bag, because a return or exchange needs them attached.

### Can I return or exchange a Coach bag?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund is made by the payment method you used once the bag reaches us.
