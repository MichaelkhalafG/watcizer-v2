Calvin Klein bags are about calm, confident design: clean shapes, a restrained logo and colours that work with everything you own. Our women's range follows two moods, warm neutral colour blocks for everyday wear and black monogram pieces with a sharper, city look. This guide explains both, then helps you pick a shape, a colour and a bag for each part of your day.

## Neutral colour blocks

Several bags combine three soft tones in one design: **vanilla, caramel and brown**, **brown, khaki and white**, or black with brown. Colour blocking makes a plain shape look designed, and neutral tones go with beige, white, denim, black and almost every colour you wear. These are the bags to choose if you want one that matches your whole wardrobe.

## Totes for a full day

The **tote** bags have two long handles that sit comfortably on the shoulder and roomy interiors for a notebook, a water bottle, a wallet and a scarf. We carry a black and brown tote, a brown, khaki and white tote, and a black tote covered in the **CK monogram**, which is the most recognisable Calvin Klein design on the page.

## Shoulder bags for every day

The **shoulder** bags are smaller than the totes and quicker to open, with room for a phone, a wallet, sunglasses and keys. They come in the vanilla, caramel and brown colour block, in brown, khaki and white, and in a brown printed design with a secure zip closure, a practical detail for crowded places.

## The crossbody bag and the backpack

The embossed **crossbody** bag in vanilla, khaki and caramel rides across the body, keeping your hands free and the bag in sight, which makes it the safest for markets and public transport. The **backpack** in vanilla, caramel and brown spreads the weight over both shoulders, ideal for university or a day of walking.

## A toiletry bag

The black Calvin Klein Jeans **toiletry bag** keeps makeup, skincare and small essentials together inside a larger bag or a suitcase. It is a useful travel companion and a thoughtful small gift on its own or alongside a bag.

## Closures and pockets

Check how a bag closes before you choose. A **zip** keeps everything inside and is the safest in crowds; a flap with a metal clasp looks smart and opens quickly; an open tote is the easiest to reach into but best kept on your shoulder in front of you. Inner pockets keep a phone and keys apart from the rest, and the product photos show the inside where available.

## Synthetic leather

Many of these bags are made of **synthetic leather**; some product pages describe it as 100% polyurethane, often called PU. It is lighter than leather, keeps its colour well and wipes clean with a damp cloth. The product page names the material of each bag.

## Choosing a colour

Black is the most practical and suits a city look. The **vanilla and caramel** blocks are warm and soft, lovely in spring and summer. **Brown, khaki and white** is fresh and easy with denim. Black and brown together bridge both moods. For one bag for everything, choose black or the black and brown tote.

## One bag for each part of the day

For work or university, a tote or the backpack. For errands and travel, the crossbody bag. For dinners and evenings, the silver chain shoulder bag or the Minimal Monogram flap bag. If you are starting with one bag, a neutral colour-block shoulder bag is the most versatile choice.

## Looking after a Calvin Klein bag

Wipe synthetic leather with a soft, slightly damp **cloth**, never with alcohol or strong cleaners, and dry it in the shade. Stuff the bag with paper when you store it so it keeps its shape, keep it in its dust bag, and keep light vanilla and white bags away from dark jeans, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between Calvin Klein and Calvin Klein Jeans bags?

Calvin Klein is the main line, with the most minimal shapes and quiet branding, such as the colour-block totes and the CK monogram tote. Calvin Klein Jeans is the more casual line, with bolder lettering, monograms and details such as a silver CK clasp or a chain strap. Both are easy to match.

### What is PU synthetic leather?

PU, or polyurethane, is a coated material made to look and feel like leather. It is lighter than leather, holds its colour well, resists light rain and wipes clean easily. It prefers shade and a dry cloth; long hours in strong sun or heat can mark the surface.

### Which Calvin Klein bag is best for every day?

A neutral colour-block shoulder bag or the black and brown tote. Both hold the essentials, match almost every outfit and hide small marks. If you carry a laptop or many things, choose the backpack; if you want your hands free, the crossbody bag.
