The neckline decides the length and the size of pendant that look best. A round neck or a V neck frames a pendant just below the collarbone, where most of these chains, about 45 cm, fall. ## Gold-tone, explained

These necklaces are fashion jewellery with a gold-tone finish, as each title says. That keeps them light, often under 20 g, and affordable while giving the warm look of gold. The finish lasts much longer when it is kept away from water, perfume and humidity, which the care tips below explain.

## Care in 4 habits

1: put the necklace on after perfume, lotion and hairspray. 2: take it off before showering, swimming and sport. 3: wipe it with a dry soft cloth in the evening. 4: store it alone in its pouch so the chain does not tangle or scratch other pieces. These habits keep the shine for many months.

### Are these necklaces real gold?

No. They are fashion necklaces with a gold-tone finish, as their titles state, which keeps them light and affordable. With simple care, keeping them away from water, perfume and humidity and storing them in their pouch, the finish stays bright for a long time.

### Which pendant suits a gift if I do not know her taste?

The two-tone geometric pendant is the safest, because it matches gold and silver watches and jewellery. A heart is a classic choice for close family. If you know her name, an initial pendant is the most personal. Every piece can be exchanged within 14 days if unused.

### What is the petit piqué pattern on the Lacoste bracelet?

Petit piqué is the fine textured weave of Lacoste's famous polo shirt. On the Metropole link bracelet it is reproduced in steel, so the bracelet carries the brand's signature without a large logo. It suits a steel watch and a smart casual style.
