A belt is a small piece that finishes an outfit. The right one holds its shape, matches your shoes and suits the trousers you wear most. This guide explains the types on this page, how to choose your size, and why the belt and wallet sets are among the most popular gifts in the shop.

## Types of belts on this page

There are 3 main types. A classic pin-buckle belt has holes and a metal prong, the most traditional style. An automatic buckle belt has a smooth strap that slides through a ratchet buckle, so it adjusts in small steps without holes. A reversible belt is black on one side and brown on the other, with a buckle that turns. Each product title tells you which one it is.

## Finding your size

Belt sizes usually refer to the length from the buckle to the middle hole, in centimetres. Take your trouser waist size and add about 5 cm: a 34 inch or 86 cm waist usually needs a belt of 90 to 95 cm. Automatic buckle belts and many leather belts can be cut to length at home or in our stores, which removes most of the guesswork.

## Width and occasion

A belt of about 3 cm wide is the formal standard, slim enough for suit trousers. A width of 3.5 to 4 cm is the casual standard and suits jeans and chinos with wider loops. Most belts here sit between 3 and 4 cm, and the product page states the width when it matters. If you want 1 belt for both, choose 3.5 cm in black.

## Matching the belt

The rule is simple: match the belt to your shoes. Black belt with black shoes, brown belt with brown shoes. Match the buckle metal to your watch if you can: a silver buckle with a steel watch, a gold buckle with a gold watch. Reversible belts give you both black and brown in 1 belt, which is perfect for travel.

## Caring for a leather belt

Do not wear the same belt every day; giving it a day off lets the leather recover its shape. Roll it loosely or hang it by the buckle rather than folding it. Keep it away from rain and heat. Wipe it with a dry cloth, and use a leather conditioner every 6 months if the surface starts to look dry.

## Questions and answers

### How do I choose the right belt size?

Add about 5 cm to your trouser waist size. For example, a waist of 86 cm usually needs a belt of 90 to 95 cm. Many belts on this page, especially automatic buckle belts, can be cut to length, and our stores can shorten one for you so that it fits perfectly on the first day.

### What is an automatic buckle belt?

It is a belt with a smooth strap and no holes. The buckle grips the strap with a small ratchet, so you can tighten or loosen it in steps of a few millimetres. It looks cleaner than a pin-buckle belt and gives a more precise fit, which is why it is popular with suit trousers.
