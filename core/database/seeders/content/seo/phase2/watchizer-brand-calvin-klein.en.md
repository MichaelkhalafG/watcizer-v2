Calvin Klein founded his company in New York in 1968 and made minimalism a brand: clean lines, neutral colours and a logo that became famous by being simple. **Calvin Klein Jeans**, the younger line, brings the same look to casual wear with bolder logos and monogram prints. The accessories we carry come from both lines, and this guide explains how to choose a wallet, a belt, a set or a bag, and how to look after leather in Egypt's climate.

## Wallets: bifold or vertical

A **bifold** wallet folds once across the middle and holds cards in slots on both sides, with a long section for notes; it is the most common format and fits a trouser pocket. A **vertical** wallet opens upwards, is narrower and slimmer, and suits a jacket's inside pocket or someone who carries few cards. Many Calvin Klein wallets in our range use an embossed or printed CK monogram, a subtle pattern pressed into the leather, or a smooth finish with a small metal logo. Count how many cards you carry before choosing: a slim wallet stuffed with twelve cards loses its shape quickly.

## Matching the set with your watch

A wallet-and-belt set is the easiest way to finish an outfit, and it works best when it matches your watch. With a black leather strap or a black-dial steel watch, choose a black set; with a brown leather strap, a brown belt keeps the look consistent. Silver-tone buckles go with steel watch cases, and if your watch has a gold-tone case, look for a plain leather set rather than a bright silver buckle. A minimal CK set suits the dress watches in our range, while a monogram set from Calvin Klein Jeans pairs well with a sporty chronograph and jeans. The [Calvin Klein wallets page](/brand/calvin-klein/wallets) shows the wallets on their own if you only need one piece.

If you are buying the set as a gift for someone whose watch you do not know, black is the choice that cannot go wrong: it suits almost any watch, and works with a suit and with jeans alike.

## Looking after leather in the heat

Leather lasts for years if you let it breathe. Do not keep a wallet in a back pocket all day in summer; sweat and pressure are what wear it fastest. Empty it regularly so it keeps its shape. Wipe a belt with a dry cloth after a hot day, and alternate 2 belts if you can, so each one has time to dry. Store bags stuffed with paper and away from direct sun, which fades lighter colours such as vanilla and caramel. Never use household cleaners on leather; a slightly damp cloth is enough.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between Calvin Klein and Calvin Klein Jeans?

Calvin Klein is the main line, with the cleanest, most minimal designs. Calvin Klein Jeans is the younger, more casual line, with bolder logos and monogram prints. Both are the same brand and the same quality of finish; the choice is about style. A plain black Calvin Klein wallet looks more formal; a Calvin Klein Jeans wallet with an all-over logo looks more casual.

### Is the wallet-and-belt set gift-wrapped?

The sets come in a gift box with the wallet and the belt together, ready to give as they arrive. The product page shows the box and the belt's length. If you would like a card or a message, tell our customer service when we call to confirm the order.

### How do I choose a belt size?

Measure a belt you already wear, from the buckle's pin to the hole you use most, and compare it with the length on the product page. A belt should reach the middle hole, leaving room to loosen or tighten it. Most leather belts can be shortened at the buckle end by a leather shop if they are too long.

### Can I return a wallet or a belt?

The item must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags attached, and a belt must not have been cut. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
