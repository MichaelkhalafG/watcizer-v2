Roberto Cavalli opened his fashion house in Florence in 1970 and became famous for animal prints, bold colours and unashamed glamour. Just Cavalli, the younger line he created in 1998, keeps that spirit with a lighter, more playful touch. Its watches are made as accessories first: they are meant to be worn like a bracelet and noticed like a ring. This guide explains the designs you will find in our range and how to choose and wear them.

## Snake and Serpente: the signature

The snake is Cavalli's most famous motif, and on the watches it appears in two ways. Some bracelets coil around the wrist like a serpent, with the case set in the snake's body; others use a snake head or scales as a detail on the case or the clasp. Titles with **Snake** or **Serpente** point to these models. They are the boldest pieces in the range and work best as the only jewellery on that arm, so the design can speak for itself.

## Septagon and the geometric cases

The **Septagon** line uses a seven-sided case, an unusual shape that stands out immediately on the wrist. Other lines play with geometry too, through square, octagonal or layered cases. These models look modern and architectural, and they pair well with minimal clothes: a plain shirt, a black dress, a simple blazer. If the snake designs feel too much for every day, a Septagon in silver steel is the most wearable way to own a Just Cavalli.

## Glam, Animalier and the decorated pieces

**Glam** models add crystals around the dial or the bezel and lean towards evening wear. **Animalier** brings the animal-print heritage of the brand onto the dial, in a refined form. Other names you may see, such as **DNA**, **Doppio** (Italian for double) or **Cerimonioso** (ceremonious), hint at each design's character; the photos on the product page show the details. These are statement watches for occasions, weddings and evenings out, and they make memorable gifts for someone who loves fashion.

## Why the cases are so small

Just Cavalli watches are among the smallest in our whole range: many measure 23 or 26 millimetres, and the larger ones 32 to 34 mm. That is intentional. A small dial keeps the watch in the world of jewellery, so the bracelet, the shape and the decoration are what people see first. On a slim wrist they look delicate and precious; if you prefer a watch you can read at a glance across a room, choose a 32 or 34 mm model.

## Steel bracelets and sizing

Every Just Cavalli watch in our range comes on a stainless steel bracelet, in silver, gold-tone, rose-gold or two-tone. Most are link bracelets that need sizing, or snake bracelets that wrap around the wrist; if a model is a bangle or cuff that slips over the hand, the product page says so. Link bracelets can be sized in our stores in a few minutes, usually by removing 2 to 4 links; a bangle or cuff has to be chosen by wrist size, so measure yours before you order.

## Matching it with your jewellery

Because a Just Cavalli is a piece of jewellery, plan what you wear with it. Match the metal: a gold-tone snake bracelet with gold earrings and rings, silver steel with white gold or silver pieces, rose gold with pearls and soft colours. Wear it on the wrist without other bracelets if it is a snake or a crystal model, so it does not compete for attention; a simple Septagon can be stacked with one or two fine bangles. Keep the rest of the outfit calm when the watch is bold, and let a quiet watch lift a plain outfit for an evening.

## A gift for someone who loves fashion

A Just Cavalli is a gift that says you paid attention to her style. For someone who loves bold fashion, choose a snake design in gold tone; for a minimalist, a silver Septagon; for an evening or an engagement, a Glam model with crystals. Small cases mean the size of the case is never the problem, but a link bracelet must fit, so a model with a bracelet that can be sized after delivery is the safest surprise. Keep the invoice so the watch can be exchanged within 14 days if needed.

If the gift is for a particular occasion, add a small card naming it; a watch like this becomes, over time, a memory tied to that day.

## Wearing and caring for a jewellery watch

Treat a Just Cavalli like a fine bracelet. Put it on after perfume, hand cream and hairspray, and take it off for the beach, the pool and the gym: the models we list are rated at 30 metres, enough for splashes but not for swimming. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after wearing it, and store it in its box, apart from other jewellery that can scratch the plating. Crystals are set to last, but a hard knock can loosen one, so be careful with doorframes and handbags.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 23 mm watch easy to read?

It is smaller than most watches, so you read it at a closer distance, the way you would look at a ring. For telling the time quickly in a meeting or at a glance, a 32 or 34 mm model is easier. A small Just Cavalli also works well as an evening or occasion watch next to a larger, simpler watch for daily use.

### How do I know my bracelet size for a bangle or cuff model?

Measure the widest part of your hand with your thumb tucked in, as if you were putting on a bangle, and compare it with the inner measurements on the product page. For snake and cuff styles, measure your wrist just above the wrist bone. If you are unsure, send our customer service the product link and your measurement on WhatsApp before ordering.

### Can I swim with a Just Cavalli watch?

No. The models we list are rated at 30 metres, which means splashes and washing your hands only. Water and chlorine also dull the plating and can affect the crystals. Take the watch off before swimming, the shower and the sea, and dry it if it gets wet.

### Does a Just Cavalli watch come with a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the warranty card and the invoice. Lost crystals after a knock, scratches and water damage are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I return or exchange it?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a link bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
