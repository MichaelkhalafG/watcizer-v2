Michael Kors launched his own label in New York in 1981, and the watches that carry his name have become one of the most familiar sights in a jewellery-style watch window. They are fashion watches in the best sense: designed to match a bracelet, a bag and an outfit, built on reliable quartz movements, and priced well below the Swiss brands they sometimes resemble. This guide explains how the range we carry is organised and what to look at before you choose.

## The women's lines, by name

Most Michael Kors titles in our shop carry the name of a line, and each line has its own personality. **Runway** is the brand's best-known shape: a round steel case, a simple dial with baton markers, and a link bracelet in gold, silver or rose gold. **Darci** adds a ring of crystals around the dial, which suits evenings and weddings. **Parker** is the chronograph for women, with three small sub-dials and often a crystal bezel, so it looks busier and more sporty. **Portia**, **Camille** and **Ritz** are smaller and slimmer, closer to a piece of jewellery than to a tool watch.

Knowing the line saves time. If you like a model but want it in another colour, search the line name together with the colour — "Darci rose gold", for example — and you will usually find the sister model. The [women's Michael Kors watches page](/brand/michael-kors/watches/women) lists them all, and the filters narrow them by strap and dial colour.

## Gold, rose gold or silver

The colour of the case and bracelet decides more about a Michael Kors watch than any other detail, so it is the best first filter to use. Rose-gold and gold-tone models together make up most of our range, in roughly equal numbers, and silver steel is the quieter third option.

The practical rule is to match the metal you already wear. If your rings and earrings are yellow gold, a gold-tone Runway or Darci will look like it belongs to the set. Rose gold suits warmer skin tones and pairs well with pink and nude colours. Silver is the easiest to wear with everything and the most likely to suit both office and evening. Two-tone bracelets, with silver and gold links side by side, are the compromise if you mix metals.

## Size: what 36 to 39 mm means on the wrist

Case sizes are printed on every product page, and the Michael Kors women's range we carry is concentrated between 36 and 39 millimetres, with 39 mm the single most common size. On a typical women's wrist, 36 to 38 mm looks classic and discreet; 39 mm and above looks modern and makes the watch a statement. The Parker chronographs run larger, and a few models reach 42 mm or more, which is close to a men's size.

If you are unsure, measure a watch you already own across the case, without the crown, and compare. A bracelet watch also needs the right length: the steel bracelets come with removable links, and we size them for free in our stores while the watch is under warranty.

## Water, crystals and everyday care

Most Michael Kors watches we carry list 50 metres of water resistance on the product page. In practice that means rain, splashes, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but the sea and diving are not, and you should never press the buttons of a chronograph under water. A hot shower or a sauna is the worst case, because steam can get past the seals.

The crystals set around many dials are glued in, so treat the watch as you would a piece of jewellery. Take it off before applying perfume, hand cream or sunscreen, which dull the stones and the gold-tone plating over time. Wipe the bracelet with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day; sweat and dust gather between the links, and a soft toothbrush with a little water cleans them without scratching. Keep the watch in its box when you are not wearing it, away from the other jewellery that could scratch it.

## Questions and answers

### Which Michael Kors line is best for a first watch?

Runway is the safest first choice. It has a clean dial, a steel bracelet and no crystals or sub-dials to distract, so it works at the office, at university and in the evening. Choose the colour that matches the jewellery you wear most. If you want a little more sparkle, Darci is the next step up, and Parker if you prefer a chronograph look.

### Can a 39 mm Michael Kors watch look too big?

On a very slim wrist it can, especially with a wide bracelet. Compare the case size on the product page with a watch you already own, and look at the photos taken on a wrist where we have them. The 36 to 38 mm models in the same lines look more classic. In our Cairo stores you can try both sizes side by side before choosing.

### Will the gold colour fade?

The gold and rose-gold finish is plating over stainless steel, and it lasts for years with normal care. What wears it down is friction and chemicals: perfume, creams, sea water and pool water. Take the watch off before you apply them, wipe it dry after a hot day and store it apart from other jewellery. Scratches on the clasp from desks and laptops are the most common sign of age.

### How do I get the bracelet sized?

Every steel bracelet has removable links. If you order online, you can have it sized at any watch shop, or bring it to one of our two stores in Cairo, where we size it for free while the watch is under warranty. Bring the watch, its box and the invoice. Removed links should be kept in the box in case you need them later.

### Who provides the warranty on a Michael Kors watch?

Each product page says how long the warranty is and who provides it. The card in the box and your invoice are what you need to use it, so keep them together. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects; damage from drops, water beyond the rating or opening the case is not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.
