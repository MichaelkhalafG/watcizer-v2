## How the men's watches are organised

Most titles in our Tommy Hilfiger range start with the word "Classic", which is how the brand groups its everyday three-hand and multi-dial models. Inside that group you will meet named lines: **Decker** is the dressier one, with a slim case and a leather strap that works with a suit; **Kane** and **Liam** are sportier, with bigger cases, chronograph-style sub-dials and silicone or rubber straps; **Connor** and **Aiden** sit in between, easy to wear every day. The line name is in the product title, so the [men's watches page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/men) and the search box let you go straight to the one you like.

Nearly all of them run on quartz movements. That means accurate time without winding, a battery that lasts a couple of years, and a price that stays reasonable for a branded watch — which is a large part of why Tommy Hilfiger is such a common first "real" watch and a common gift.

## What to compare between two watches

Three numbers on the product page tell you more than the photos. The case diameter decides how big the watch looks: 40 to 42 mm suits most wrists, 44 mm and above makes a statement. The thickness decides whether it slides under a shirt cuff. The water resistance tells you what it survives: 30 metres means splashes and rain, 50 metres means swimming in a pool, and 100 metres is safer for the beach and the sea.

Then look at the strap. Leather is the most formal and softens with wear, but it does not like water or sweat in August. Steel bracelets are the most durable and need to be sized to your wrist — we do that free of charge in our stores within the warranty. Silicone is the most comfortable in the heat and the easiest to clean, which is why the sporty Tommy Hilfiger models wear it.

## Wallets, belts and bags

The brand's leather goods follow the watches: the wallets we carry are men's bifolds and card holders, and the belts are men's, many of them reversible so one belt covers both black and brown shoes. Matching a wallet and a belt from the same line is the simplest way to put together a gift.

The bags are more evenly split. For men there are messenger bags, backpacks and reporter bags in the brand's monogram canvas or textured leather; for women, crossbody bags, totes and clutches. The [Tommy Hilfiger bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags) gathers them in one place.

## Choosing a Tommy Hilfiger watch as a gift

A watch is the classic gift from this brand, and the safest choice is a watch that suits more than one outfit. A silver or blue dial on a steel bracelet goes with work clothes and weekends; a black dial on a leather strap is the evening option. If you do not know the wrist size, a leather or silicone strap is easier to adjust than a bracelet. Each watch arrives with its box, so it is ready to give, and if the size or the colour is not right you have 14 days to exchange it.

## Looking after a quartz watch

A quartz watch needs very little: keep it away from strong magnets such as phone speakers, rinse a steel or silicone strap in fresh water after the sea, and do not press the buttons or pull the crown under water. When the second hand starts jumping every 4 seconds instead of every second, the battery is running out — bring it to one of our stores, where the change is free within the warranty. Keep the warranty card and the invoice together; you will need them for any service.
