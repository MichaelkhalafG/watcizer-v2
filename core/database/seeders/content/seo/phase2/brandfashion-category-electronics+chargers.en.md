## Choosing the cable

Pick the connector first: Type-C for Android phones, the iPhone 15 and later, tablets and laptops; Lightning or 8-pin for iPhones up to the 14. Then pick the power: 30 W for a phone, 60 W for a tablet or small laptop, 100 W for a large laptop. Auto power-off cables in the StarFlight series stop at 100 percent to spare the battery.

## Fast charging standards

PD, USB Power Delivery, is used by iPhones and many Android phones; QC, Quick Charge, by many Samsung, Xiaomi and Oppo models. A charger that supports both covers almost every phone. The phone always takes only the power it can handle, so a 67 W charger is safe for a 20 W phone.

## Safety at the socket

Use a charger with over-voltage and temperature protection, keep it uncovered while charging, and leave 1 or 2 cm of space around it. Replace a cable with a split jacket or a loose plug. During power cuts and returns, unplug chargers if your area has surges. Never charge on a bed or a sofa cushion.

## Charging habits that help the battery

Avoid running a phone to 0 percent; charging from around 20 to 80 percent is kinder to the battery. Keep the phone out of the sun while it charges, especially in summer. An auto power-off cable or the phone's own optimised charging setting stops it sitting at 100 percent all night.

## Questions and answers

### Will a 65 W charger work with my phone?

Yes. Your phone negotiates the power it needs through PD or QC, so a 65 W charger simply delivers less to a phone and more to a laptop. Use a cable rated for fast charging. The benefit is that 1 charger covers your laptop, tablet and phone.

### Which charger works in the UK and the Gulf?

The 65 W GaN model with EU and UK plugs works in Egypt and Europe with the round 2-pin plug, and in the UK and the Gulf countries that use the 3-pin UK socket. Other chargers here use the 2-pin plug and need a simple adapter abroad.

### Why does the car charger have a ring?

The pull ring lets you remove the charger from the car socket easily, since compact car chargers sit flush and are hard to grip. It also reduces strain on the socket. The 45 W model charges 2 phones at the same time with PD fast charging.
