A wallet is used dozens of times a day, so it should be slim enough for a pocket, strong enough to last and organised enough to find a card in a second. This guide explains the styles on this page, how to choose one, and which brand suits which taste.

## Wallet styles

There are 5 main styles here. A bifold folds once and holds cards and notes, the classic men's wallet. A trifold folds twice and holds more in a smaller footprint. A card holder is a slim sleeve for 4 to 8 cards and a few folded notes. A zip-around wallet closes on 3 sides and keeps coins safe. A phone wallet or wristlet holds a phone, cards and cash together.

## Choosing the right size

If you carry 4 to 6 cards and little cash, a card holder is the slimmest option and fits a front pocket. If you carry cash daily, a bifold with 6 to 8 card slots and a note compartment is the safe choice. Trifolds suit those who carry many receipts. For a handbag, a zip-around wallet keeps everything closed and organised.

## Leather and finishes

Smooth leather is classic and ages well. Textured or grained leather hides scratches better. Coated canvas, used by Coach, resists stains and water and stays light. Embossed logos are subtle; printed monograms are bolder. If you sit on your wallet in a back pocket, choose a slim style to avoid wear on the leather and strain on your back.

## Caring for a wallet

Do not overfill a wallet; extra cards stretch the slots and the leather does not recover. Keep it away from rain and wipe it with a dry cloth. Remove old receipts every week so the wallet keeps its shape. Store a spare wallet in its box, away from heat. With these habits a leather wallet lasts 3 years and often longer.

### Which wallet is the slimmest?

A card holder is the slimmest style. It holds about 4 to 8 cards and a few folded notes and fits easily in a front pocket. If you need more space for cash, choose a slim bifold rather than a trifold, and avoid carrying receipts so the wallet stays flat.
