A bag is the accessory people use most and think about least. The right one makes a day easier; the wrong one is too small, too heavy or always open in a crowd. Rather than sort the bags on this page by men's and women's, this guide sorts them by five steps — what you carry, how you travel, how secure it must be, how formal it should look and who it is for — so you can find the right Tommy Hilfiger bag whoever will carry it.

## Step 1: what you carry every day

Lay out your usual things. If they are a phone, a card holder and keys, any **crossbody bag** is enough, and it will stay light. If you add a full wallet, sunglasses and a charger, you need a medium **shoulder bag** or a **messenger** bag. If a tablet, a laptop, a notebook or a water bottle come too, look at the **backpacks**, the **briefcase** and the large **handbags**. The product pages give the width, height and depth in centimetres; compare them with your largest item before you order.

## Step 2: how you travel

On the metro, in a microbus or on foot in busy streets, carry your bag in front of you: a crossbody bag worn across the chest, or a backpack worn on 2 shoulders, is the safest. If you drive everywhere, a handbag or a shoulder bag with a flap is perfectly practical, because it moves from the car seat to the office desk. For flights, a crossbody bag keeps your passport and phone within reach while a backpack carries the rest.

## Step 3: how secure it must be

A **zip** top is the most secure closure; nothing falls out and nothing can be reached in. A **flap** with a magnetic snap opens faster with one hand but is easier to open by others. An open tote or a shopper is the quickest and the least secure. Inside, a zipped pocket for cash and keys makes any bag safer. Most product photos show the bag open, so you can see the closure and the pockets.

## Step 4: how formal it should look

The most formal shapes on this page are the **briefcase** for men and the structured **handbag** for women; both suit offices, meetings and events. Messenger bags and shoulder bags are smart-casual, right for university and relaxed workplaces. Crossbody bags, backpacks and wash bags are casual and practical. Smooth black leather always looks the most formal; textured fabric and pebbled leather look more relaxed.

## Step 5: who it is for

For a man who wants one everyday bag, a black crossbody bag; for a man who works with documents, a messenger bag or the briefcase. For a woman who needs one bag for everything, a medium shoulder bag in black or navy; for her weekends, a crossbody. For students of either sex, a backpack with room for books and a laptop. The [men's bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/men) and the [women's bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/women) narrow the list once you know.

## Looking after any of these bags

Whatever the shape, the same habits keep a bag looking new. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after use, and blot spills straight away instead of rubbing. Keep it out of a hot parked car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside of a car gets far hotter, and heat softens coatings and glue. Do not overload it, because a bag stretched beyond its shape loses it for good. Store it empty and stuffed with paper, ideally in a dust bag, and never use household cleaners, alcohol or wet wipes on leather or coated fabric.

## Questions and answers

### Are there unisex Tommy Hilfiger bags?

Several shapes work for anyone, especially the black backpacks and some crossbody bags in plain black. The product title says whether a bag is designed for men or women, but a simple black crossbody bag or backpack suits either. If you are buying for a couple, two crossbody bags in black and navy make a matching pair.

### Which bag is the safest in a crowd?

A crossbody bag with a zip, worn across the body with the bag in front of you. It stays in sight, it cannot slip off the shoulder, and the zip keeps hands out. A backpack is also secure if you keep valuables in an inside pocket against your back.

### How do I choose between leather and fabric?

Leather looks the smartest and ages well, but it prefers dry days and gentle care. Textured fabric and coated materials are lighter, resist light rain and wipe clean more easily. For a bag used every day in Egypt's heat and dust, fabric or pebbled leather is the most forgiving.
