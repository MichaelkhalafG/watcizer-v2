## How to read the colour code in the title

Many Naviforce titles end in a short group of letters, separated by slashes or hyphens, such as **S/B/S** or **RG/B/B**. That code usually describes the colours in this order: the case, the dial, then the bracelet or strap. **S** means silver, **G** gold, **RG** rose gold, **B** black, **W** white, **BE** blue, **GN** green and **BN** brown. So S/BE/S is a silver case with a blue dial on a silver bracelet, and RG/B/B is a rose-gold case with a black dial on a black strap. Once you know the code, you can scan a long list of models in seconds and spot the colour you want before you even open the photos, then confirm it in the product page photos.

## Bracelet or strap

A steel bracelet is the most durable and the most versatile choice; it needs sizing, which takes a few minutes in any watch shop or in our stores; most wrists need 2 to 4 links removed, taken in pairs from both sides of the clasp so it stays centred. Leather is softer and dressier but absorbs sweat, so it suits cooler months and evenings. Rubber and silicone are the practical choice for heat, sport and the beach-side café, and they need no sizing. If you are buying for someone else and do not know their wrist size, a strap with a buckle is the safest choice. Many Naviforce straps use a standard width, so a second strap in another colour can change the look of the same watch for summer and winter.

## Water resistance: what to expect

The Naviforce watches we list are generally rated for splashes rather than swimming: the product page states the rating, and most models are at the 30-metre level. That covers rain, washing your hands and everyday sweat. It does not cover swimming, the sea or a shower. If you want a watch to wear in the pool, choose a model that states a higher rating on its page, or keep the Naviforce for dry days.

## Getting long life from an affordable watch

A few simple habits make a Naviforce last. Keep perfume and sun cream away from plated gold and rose-gold cases, which dull with chemicals. Wipe the bracelet with a dry cloth after a hot day. Change the battery as soon as the watch starts losing time — usually every 2 to 3 years — so a weak battery never leaks inside. Do not press the buttons when the watch is wet. And store it in its box rather than loose with keys and coins, which scratch the glass and the plating.

## Questions and answers

### What do the letters at the end of a Naviforce title mean?

They usually describe the colours: first the case, then the dial, then the bracelet or strap. S is silver, G gold, RG rose gold, B black, W white, BE blue, GN green and BN brown. For example, S/GN/S is a silver case with a green dial on a silver bracelet. The photos on the product page always show the exact model, so check them too.

### Is a 48 mm Naviforce too big?

For most men, 48 mm is a statement size. Measure your wrist: below about 17 centimetres, a 44 or 45 mm model will look more balanced; above about 18 centimetres, 48 mm looks natural. A strap version sits closer to the wrist and feels lighter than the steel-bracelet version of the same model.

### Can women wear the men's Naviforce models?

Some women like the oversized look, and a 44 or 45 mm model on a leather or silicone strap can work well as a bold, sporty watch. For everyday wear, though, the 37 mm women's models are more comfortable and sit better under a sleeve. Both are on the same product pages, and the title says whether a model is for men or women.

### What warranty do Naviforce watches have?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the warranty card and the invoice. It does not cover water damage, a broken glass or wear to the plating. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.
