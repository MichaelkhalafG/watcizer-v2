Tory Burch launched her label in New York in 2004 and built it quickly into one of the best-known American names in women's accessories. Her signature is the **Double T** logo, two stacked letters drawn from her own initials, and a style that sits between classic and playful: good leather, clean shapes and a touch of colour. The bags in our shop show that balance well. This guide explains the shapes, the colours and how to choose and care for a Tory Burch bag.

## The shoulder bag: the core of the range

Most Tory Burch bags we carry are **shoulder bags**, and they are made to be the one bag you use every day. A structured body keeps its shape when it is full, a top flap or zip keeps your things secure, and a strap length that sits comfortably under the arm makes it easy to wear with a jacket or a dress. Inside, there is usually room for a wallet, a phone, keys, sunglasses and a small make-up pouch. Several models have a detachable or adjustable strap, so the same bag can be worn crossbody on busy days.

## Eleanor and the Double T

**Eleanor** is one of the brand's best-known designs: a leather shoulder bag with a fold-over flap and a large Double T clasp in metal on the front. The strap can often be worn single on the shoulder or doubled for a shorter drop, which changes the look from day to evening. Because the hardware is the star of the design, Eleanor looks best with simple clothes and minimal jewellery. If you want one Tory Burch bag that everyone recognises, it is this one. In black it suits evenings best, while brown and burgundy work better for daytime and the office.

## Crossbody bags and the tote

For hands-free days, a **crossbody bag** sits on a long strap across the body, keeping your phone and cards in front of you, which is the safest way to carry a bag in a mall or on a busy street. The **tote** is the roomiest option, with space for a tablet or a notebook, and it suits work and travel. Check the width, height and depth on each product page; the photos show the bags open as well as closed, so you can see the inside, the number of inner pockets and the type of closure.

## Choosing the colour

Black is the most common colour in our Tory Burch range, and the most useful: it goes with everything and hides daily wear. Brown and the deep wine-red **Tempranillo** are warm alternatives that look rich in autumn and winter. **Pebblestone** is a soft grey-beige neutral, and light cream reads fresh and elegant in summer, though it needs more care to stay clean. Burgundy and blue add colour without being loud. The [Tory Burch bags page](/brand/tory-burch) shows every colour side by side, and the photos are taken in natural light.

## Leather care in Egypt's climate

Good leather lasts for years if it is treated well. Keep the bag out of a parked car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside gets far hotter, and the heat can mark the leather and soften the edges. Wipe off dust with a soft, dry cloth, and blot any spill at once rather than rubbing. Store the bag empty, stuffed with paper and inside its dust bag, away from direct sun, which fades lighter colours such as cream and Pebblestone. Avoid household cleaners and wet wipes, which can strip the finish; a leather-care specialist is the right place for deep stains. When you travel, pack the bag inside its dust bag.

A Tory Burch bag is a gift that feels special and gets used every day, which is a rare combination. For a woman who works, a black or brown shoulder bag is the most useful choice; for someone who loves recognisable designs, an Eleanor with its Double T clasp; for a younger style, a crossbody bag in a colour such as blue or Tempranillo. ## A bag and a watch together

Tory Burch hardware is usually gold-tone, so it pairs naturally with a gold or rose-gold watch, such as the women's watches from [Michael Kors](/brand/michael-kors) in our range. A black Eleanor with a gold watch is a classic evening combination; a brown or Tempranillo shoulder bag with a watch on a brown leather strap is warm and relaxed. If you wear mostly silver, choose a darker leather where the gold hardware stands out less. In our two stores in Cairo you can hold the bag and try the watch together.

## Questions and answers

### What is the Double T?

It is Tory Burch's logo: two letter Ts stacked and mirrored, drawn from the designer's initials. On bags it appears as a metal clasp or plaque, most famously on the Eleanor. It is the quickest way to recognise the brand, and on most models it is gold-tone, which pairs well with gold jewellery and watches.

### Can the shoulder bags be worn crossbody?

Some can. Models with a detachable or long adjustable strap can be worn across the body as well as on the shoulder; the product page and the photos show the strap and its length. If a model has a fixed shoulder strap, it is designed to be carried on the shoulder only.

### Will a light cream bag stay clean?

With care, yes, but it needs more attention than black or brown. Keep it away from dark denim, which can transfer its dye, and from pens and make-up inside the bag. Wipe it gently with a dry cloth after use, and store it in its dust bag. If you want a light colour for daily use, Pebblestone is more forgiving than cream.
