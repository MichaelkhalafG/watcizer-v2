## Tommy Hilfiger, the largest name

Tommy Hilfiger supplies more than 1 in 3 pieces in this section. Its bags, wallets and belts carry the brand's red, white and navy details, the TH monogram or the classic stripe. Many come as gift sets that pair a wallet with a belt. If you like a clean, preppy look that goes with jeans and with a blazer, the [Tommy Hilfiger page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger) is the place to start.

## Calvin Klein and Armani Exchange

Calvin Klein brings a minimal style: black leather, the CK monogram, metal logo buckles and simple shapes. Its belt and wallet sets are among the most popular gifts in the shop. Armani Exchange offers a younger, urban take with bold logos on bags and wallets. Both suit men who prefer a modern look with little ornament.

## Coach, Tory Burch and Le Pliage

For women's bags, 3 names stand out. Coach offers leather and Signature canvas bags in many shapes, from shoulder bags to mini totes. Tory Burch brings refined leather shoulder and crossbody bags with the brand's double-T logo. Le Pliage contributes its famous folding nylon totes and leather versions in practical sizes. Each has a guide on its brand page.

## Guess, Pinko and Hugo Boss

Guess bags and wallets add colour and logo prints at friendly prices, popular for everyday use. Pinko offers Italian styling with its Love Birds buckle on compact bags. Hugo Boss brings men's bags and small leather goods in black and navy, a smart match for a Hugo Boss watch. Lacoste and Michael Kors add a few pieces with their own signatures.

## Colours that work everywhere

Black is by far the most common colour in the section, on more than 370 pieces, then brown, silver hardware tones, navy, white, blue and beige. Black accessories are the safest single choice and match any watch strap. Brown pairs naturally with leather watch straps and casual outfits. Navy and burgundy are refined alternatives, and lighter colours such as beige, pink and white suit summer.

## Matching a bag to a watch

Match the metal tones: a gold watch with gold hardware on the bag, a silver watch with silver hardware. Match the mood: a sports watch with a nylon tote or backpack, a dress watch with a structured leather bag. And match the colour family: a brown leather watch strap works with a brown or tan bag. These 3 rules keep an outfit coherent without effort.

## Matching a belt and wallet

The traditional rule is to match the belt to your shoes and the wallet to the belt. A black belt and a black wallet are the most versatile pair. Brown suits casual outfits and brown shoes. The Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein gift sets solve the question for you, with a matching pair in one box, ready to give.

## Caring for leather accessories

Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth and keep it away from rain, direct sun and heat. Store bags stuffed with paper in their dust bags so they keep their shape. Do not overfill wallets, which stretches the leather. Keep light-coloured pieces away from dark denim, which can transfer dye. With these simple habits, a leather accessory lasts 5 years and more.
