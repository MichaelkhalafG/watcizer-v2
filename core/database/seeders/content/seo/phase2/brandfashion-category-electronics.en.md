## Smartwatches

The smartwatches here include women's models that answer and make calls from the wrist, and a classic-series model in silver and navy. They connect to your phone by Bluetooth, show notifications and track steps and sleep. Check that the watch supports your phone's system before ordering; the product description lists compatibility and battery life. A smartwatch charges in about 2 hours and is worn like any watch, so it pairs well with a classic piece for evenings.

## Chargers for every device

A 20 W charger is enough for a phone. A 45 W charger suits tablets and small laptops, and a 65 or 67 W GaN charger can charge a laptop and a phone at once. In the car, a 45 W dual-port car charger with a pull ring keeps 2 phones fast charging. GaN chargers are smaller and cooler than older bricks, which matters in a travel bag.

## Cables that keep up

The cable sets the real speed. Type-C to Type-C cables come in 30, 60 and 100 W versions; Lightning or 8-pin cables suit iPhones up to the 14. The StarFlight series stops charging automatically when the battery is full, which helps battery health over the years. Lengths of 1.2 m reach comfortably from a socket to a bed or a car dashboard.

## Power banks, from pocket to weekend

The range starts with slim 10 thousand mAh models, some magnetic for MagSafe iPhones, some with a built-in retractable cable or a kickstand, and goes up to a 30 thousand mAh model with PD and QC fast charging at 22.5 W. Bigger is not always better: choose the capacity you will actually carry every day.

## Listening

Earbuds with ANC and ENC cancel noise around you and around your microphone, for calmer calls. Over-ear headphones with hybrid ANC suit travel and study. Wired half in-ear earphones with a 3.5 mm plug need no charging and suit laptops and older phones. The [audio page](/subtypes/audio) explains how to choose a fit.

## Mounts for the car and the bike

Car holders mount on the air vent, the dashboard or the windshield, several with 360 degree rotation and long arms. A bike mount holds the phone on the handlebar for navigation or fitness apps. Mount the phone where you can glance at it without taking your eyes off the road for more than a second.

### Do the smartwatches work with iPhone and Android?

Most connect to both through Bluetooth and a companion app, but features vary by model. The product description lists compatibility, battery life and functions such as calls, notifications and step tracking. ### Which power bank should I buy?

For daily use, a 10 thousand mAh model is light and gives about 2 phone charges. If you have a MagSafe iPhone, a magnetic model sticks to the back. For trips or sharing, the 30 thousand mAh model gives several charges. Check that it supports PD or QC fast charging for your phone.
