## Why buy accessories from 1 brand

Buying a charger, a cable and a power bank from the same brand makes speeds and connectors easy to match. Joyroom's chargers, cables and power banks follow the same standards, USB Power Delivery and Quick Charge, so a 20 W charger and a 3 A Joyroom cable fast charge an iPhone or an Android phone without guesswork.

## Standards worth knowing

PD means USB Power Delivery, used by iPhones and many Android phones. QC 3.0 means Quick Charge, used by many Samsung, Xiaomi and Oppo phones. GaN means gallium nitride, which makes chargers smaller and cooler. ANC means active noise cancelling. Product titles on this page use these 4 abbreviations, so knowing them makes comparing models quick.

## Questions and answers

### Are Joyroom chargers safe for an iPhone?

Yes. Joyroom chargers support USB Power Delivery, the standard iPhones use for fast charging, and the phone takes only the power it supports. A 20 W Joyroom charger with a Type-C cable fast charges an iPhone 15 or later; older iPhones need a Lightning cable. Avoid damaged cables of any brand.

### Which Joyroom products work with Android phones?

Almost all of them. Chargers, Type-C cables, power banks, earbuds, headphones and car holders work with Android phones and iPhones alike. Only the screen protectors and the MagSafe card holder are made for specific iPhone models, and the product title names the model each one fits.

### Can I return a Joyroom product?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange an unused product in its packaging, and we pay the return shipping. Screen protectors and earphones must be returned unopened. If a product does not work, send your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange an exchange.
