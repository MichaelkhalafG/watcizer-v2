A woman's wallet has to match two things: the bag it lives in and the way she pays. A long wallet suits a large handbag and cash; a compact one suits a smaller bag; a phone wallet suits days without a bag at all. Our women's Calvin Klein wallets cover those three cases, and this guide explains which to choose, how to organise it and how to keep it looking new.

## Three wallets, three bag sizes

Think of the wallet as part of the bag. The **long** Re-Lock suits a tote, a satchel or a large shoulder bag. The **compact** beige zip-around suits a medium shoulder bag or a crossbody bag. The **phone crossbody** replaces the bag altogether for short outings. Start from the bag you carry most and the right wallet follows.

## The Re-Lock long zip-around

The **Re-Lock** has a long, flat profile, square quilting on a black surface and a raised silver-tone CK metal logo. Its zip runs around three sides and opens wide, so every card and note is in view when you pay. The long shape keeps banknotes flat and unfolded, which many women prefer.

## The compact beige zip-around

The **beige** quilted wallet is smaller, with polished CK hardware and several card slots and compartments inside. It takes cards, cash and small essentials in a size that slips into a medium bag. The light colour is easy to spot at the bottom of a dark bag and looks soft next to neutral tones.

## The phone crossbody wallet

The black **phone crossbody** is a small vertical bag on a strap with a phone pouch and a card wallet. It carries a phone, a few cards and some notes, and leaves both hands free for a walk, a market, a concert or a wedding where a handbag would be in the way.

## Why quilting

Both zip-around wallets are **quilted**: stitched in a pattern that gives the surface a padded, slightly glossy look. Quilting looks dressier than a plain surface, catches the light softly and hides small marks and scratches, which helps a wallet look new for longer.

## Organising the inside

Put the cards you use daily, a bank card and an ID card, in the slots nearest the opening, and keep loyalty and spare cards further in. Keep notes in the long section and **coins** in the zipped pocket where there is one. A quick monthly tidy, removing receipts and expired cards, keeps the zip closing easily.

## How many cards

A zip-around wallet holds a typical set of bank cards, an ID card and a few loyalty cards. Keep it to about **10** cards so the wallet stays slim and the slots keep their grip; an overfilled wallet strains the zip and stretches the slots, and the wallet loses its shape.

## Colours

**Black** is the most practical wallet colour: it hides marks and goes with every bag. **Beige** is soft and fresh and easy to find inside a dark bag. If your bags are mostly black, a beige wallet adds contrast; if they are mostly light, a black wallet stands out and stays clean.

## Materials

The product page names the material of each wallet. Many Calvin Klein accessories use **synthetic** or leather-look materials, which are light, hold their colour and wipe clean easily. Keep the wallet away from water and long hours of heat, which can mark the surface and loosen the stitching.

## Caring for a zip-around

Open and close the **zip** slowly along its line, never at an angle, and never force it over a bulging stack of cards. Wipe the wallet with a soft, dry cloth. Keep the beige wallet away from dark jeans and newspaper ink, which can transfer colour onto light surfaces.
