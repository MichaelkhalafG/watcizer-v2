In Egypt a wallet still works hard: notes, coins, bank cards, an ID card and the odd receipt all go into it every day. That is why most women's Guess wallets on this page share the zip-around shape, which holds all of it and lets nothing fall out. This guide explains how the shape is organised, how the finishes differ and which wallet suits which bag and which person.

## Why the zip-around works for cash

A **zip-around** wallet opens on three sides, so the whole inside is visible at once: notes in the long middle section, cards in rows on either side and, on many models, coins in a zipped pocket in the centre. Nothing slides out at the bottom of a bag, and change stays separate from notes, which makes paying a taxi or a kiosk quicker.

## The quilted trio

Our quilted Guess wallets come in **black**, **white** and **pink**. The stitched pattern gives a soft, padded feel and a gentle shine. The black one adds small gold studs, the pink one gold-tone hardware, and the white one keeps it plain. Quilting looks dressy and pairs well with a quilted Guess bag such as Giully.

The **pebbled** wallet has a grained surface with no pattern at all. It is the quietest wallet on the page and the most forgiving: small scratches and fingerprints disappear into the texture. Choose it for a mother, a manager or anyone who prefers a classic look over a visible logo.

## The wristlet for short outings

A **wristlet** has a short strap that loops around the wrist, so it can be carried without a bag. Our brown monogram wristlet has 2 zipped sections, one for cards and one for notes, and may take a small phone. It suits a walk on the corniche, a quick trip to the shops or a wedding where a large bag would be in the way.

## A colour you can find in the bag

The wallet spends most of its life at the bottom of a bag, so contrast helps. A **pink** or **white** wallet is easy to spot inside a black bag; a black wallet is easy to spot inside a light beige or latte one. If you carry several bags, black is the safest single choice, because it looks right whichever bag you pick up.

## Matching a Guess bag

A long zip-around wallet fits comfortably in a shoulder bag, a satchel or a tote, such as a **Noelle** tote or a Cordelia satchel. For a mini or camera bag, take the wristlet instead. The [Guess bags](/brand/guess/bags) page lists every shape with its measurements, so you can check that the wallet fits before you order both.

## Keeping the wallet in shape

Do not let the wallet bulge: about **10** cards, a few notes and some coins is plenty, and receipts are best cleared out every week. Close the zip slowly along its line, never at an angle, and wipe the surface with a soft, dry cloth. Light colours dislike dark denim and newspaper ink, so keep a white or pink wallet away from both.

## Questions and answers

### How many cards fit in a Guess zip-around wallet?

A typical set of bank cards, an ID card and a few loyalty cards fit comfortably in the rows of slots on both sides. The exact number of slots differs by model, and the inside photos on the product page show them. Keep it to about 10 cards so the zip closes easily and the slots keep their shape.

### Is a zip-around wallet good for coins?

Yes. Many zip-around wallets have a zipped coin pocket in the centre, which keeps change apart from notes and cards. Because the outer zip closes on three sides, coins cannot escape even if the inner pocket is left open. The product photos show whether a model has the centre pocket.

### Which colour stays clean the longest?

Black, followed by the brown monogram. Both hide marks, and the pebbled black wallet also hides small scratches. White and pink look beautiful but show stains sooner; wipe them with a dry cloth now and then, and keep them away from dark jeans, which can transfer colour.

### Why are some Guess wallets out of stock?

They arrive in small quantities, and a colour can sell out between deliveries. The product page always shows the current stock. If the wallet you want is unavailable, ask us on WhatsApp whether it is coming back, or choose the same shape in another finish that is in stock now.

### Can I return a wallet?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The wallet must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
