Naviforce gives you the look of a large sports chronograph at the price of an everyday fashion watch, which is why it is one of the busiest brands in our men's range. With so many models, the trick is to choose by what you want the watch to do, then by size, strap and colour. ## Start with the display

Naviforce men's watches come in three kinds of dial. A plain **analogue** dial shows hands and markers only, the cleanest and easiest to read. A **chronograph-style** dial adds 3 sub-dials; on many models they work as a stopwatch and a day or 24-hour indicator, on others they are decorative, and the product page says which. An **analogue-digital** dial combines hands with a small screen for an alarm, a stopwatch and a backlight. Choose analogue for work, chronograph-style for the look, and analogue-digital if you want the tools.

## Then the size: 45 and 48 mm lead

Most men's Naviforce watches measure 45 millimetres, and a large group is 48 mm, with others from 41 to 47 mm. These are big watches by any standard. On a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres, 45 mm looks bold but balanced; 48 mm suits a broad wrist above about 18.5 centimetres. If your wrist is slimmer, look at the 41 to 44 mm models. Measuring your wrist with a tape takes ten seconds and saves an exchange.

## Steel, leather, rubber or silicone

Steel bracelets are the most common on this page, followed by leather, rubber and silicone. **Steel** looks the most expensive and lasts the longest; it needs sizing, usually by removing a few links. **Leather** gives a warmer, more classic look for the office. **Rubber** and **silicone** are the comfortable summer and sport choice, easy to rinse, with a buckle that fits almost any wrist. For one watch in every season, steel; for the gym and the beach-side café, silicone.

## Reading the colour code

Many titles end in a short code that describes the colours: the case, then the dial, then the strap, with **S** for silver, **G** gold, **RG** rose gold, **B** black, **W** white, **BE** blue and **GN** green. So a code such as B/B/B is an all-black watch, and S/BE/S a silver case with a blue dial on a silver bracelet. Reading the code lets you scan a long list quickly; the photos always confirm the exact model.

## Colours that work

**All black** is the most versatile and hides scratches best. **Silver with a blue or white dial** looks classic and smart. **Gold or rose-gold cases** stand out and look best with a black dial, which keeps them from looking flashy. **Gunmetal** and green dials give a modern, technical feel. If you buy one watch, choose all black or silver; if it is a second watch, a gold or green model is the fun one.

## Water: what to expect

The men's Naviforce watches here are generally made for splashes rather than swimming: most list the 30-metre level on the product page. Rain, washing your hands and everyday sweat are fine; swimming, the sea and the shower are not. Never press the buttons when the watch is wet. If you want a watch to swim with, choose a model that lists a higher rating, or keep the Naviforce for dry days.

## Making an affordable watch last

A few habits keep a Naviforce looking good for years. Keep perfume and sun cream off gold and rose-gold plating, which dulls with chemicals. Wipe the case and bracelet with a dry cloth after a hot day. Change the battery as soon as the watch starts to lose time, usually every 2 to 3 years, so a weak battery never leaks inside. Store the watch in its box, not loose with keys and coins, which scratch the glass and the case.

## Questions and answers

### Do the sub-dials on a Naviforce chronograph work?

On many models they do, as a stopwatch and as day or 24-hour indicators; on some designs they are decorative. The product page lists the functions of each model. If a working stopwatch matters to you, check that the page names a chronograph function, or ask our customer service on WhatsApp before you order.

### Is 48 mm too big for me?

For most men it is a statement size. Measure your wrist: below about 17 centimetres, a 44 or 45 mm model looks more balanced; above about 18.5 centimetres, 48 mm looks natural. A silicone or rubber strap makes the watch feel lighter than the steel version of the same size.

### Can I wear a Naviforce in the shower?

No. Most models are made for splashes, at the 30-metre level, so rain and hand-washing are fine but showers, swimming and the sea are not. Steam and soap can get past the seals. Take the watch off before you wash, and dry it if it gets wet.

### What warranty does a Naviforce have?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the warranty card and the invoice. It does not cover water damage, a broken glass or worn plating. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.
