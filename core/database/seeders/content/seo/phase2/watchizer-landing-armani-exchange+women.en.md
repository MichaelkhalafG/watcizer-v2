Armani Exchange dresses a woman for the city: sharp black pieces, a little silver shine and a confident logo. ## Morning: a tote for the commute

The day starts with a **tote**: long handles for the shoulder, room for a notebook, a wallet and a water bottle, and on most models a detachable strap. A black tote with a tonal A|X plaque or a grained finish and a metal logo charm looks smart in an office and survives a busy commute.

## Midday: a satchel for meetings

For a meeting or an appointment, the structured black **satchel** looks the most professional. Its rounded top handles, silver-tone AX logo and three inner sections keep papers and essentials in order, and it stands upright on a desk or a chair.

## Afternoon: a crossbody for errands

When the afternoon turns into errands, switch to a **crossbody** bag. The black embossed logo crossbody has a silver-tone zip and an adjustable strap; the white logo crossbody has a structured shape with black piping; and the camera bag in black and dark grey adds an all-over logo print. All keep your hands free.

## Evening: a chain bag for dinner

For dinner or a celebration, a **chain** bag adds shine. We carry a black chain crossbody with a silver-tone AX clasp and a magnetic closure, a dark brown chain flap shoulder bag, a black and silver monogram chain bag and a white and grey monogram chain bag. ## The crescent bag: day or night

The **crescent** shoulder bag, with its curved shape and silver-tone A|X buckle, works at any hour. It is compact enough for an evening yet holds a wallet, a phone and keys for the day, which makes it a good choice when you go straight from work to dinner.

## Weekend trips and travel

For a weekend away, the embossed-logo **backpack** carries the day's things and leaves both hands free at the airport or the station, and a small crossbody bag keeps a passport and a phone close. Black pieces are the most practical on the road, as they hide marks and match whatever you pack.

## Black, brown and white

**Black** leads this range and goes with every outfit. The dark **brown** chain bag is a warm alternative that suits camel, beige and denim. **White** and off-white pieces look fresh in summer and lift a black or navy outfit. Monogram patterns in black and silver or white and grey add texture without bright colour.

## Choosing your logo level

Armani Exchange lets you decide how visible the brand should be. A **tonal** plaque or embossed lettering is quiet and refined. A silver-tone AX clasp or charm adds a touch of shine. An all-over monogram or geometric print is the boldest statement. Mix one bold piece with quiet ones for a balanced look.

## Silver hardware and jewellery

The clasps, buckles and chains are mostly **silver-tone**. They look best with silver or white-gold jewellery and a steel watch. If your jewellery is gold, choose a bag with a tonal or embossed logo, so the hardware does not compete with your rings and bracelets.

## Styling with black outfits

An all-black outfit with a black Armani Exchange bag looks sleek and modern; add the **silver** of a chain strap or a metal logo for a highlight. For contrast, carry the white logo crossbody or the white and grey monogram bag with black, which makes the bag the focus of the look.

## Styling with denim and casual clothes

With jeans, a T-shirt and trainers, the camera bag, the embossed crossbody or the **backpack** feel natural. A brown chain bag with a denim jacket is a relaxed, warm combination. Keep the dressier satchel and the monogram chain bags for days when you want to look more put together.

## Caring for chain straps

Chains keep their shine when they stay dry and clean. Wipe them with a dry **cloth** after use, keep perfume and creams away from the metal, and avoid catching the chain on knitwear, which can pull threads. Store a chain bag with the chain inside the bag, so it does not scratch the surface.

## Caring for the bag itself

Wipe the surface with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry it in the shade; avoid alcohol and strong cleaners. Stuff the bag with paper when you store it and keep it in its dust bag. Keep **white** and off-white bags away from dark denim and newspapers, which can transfer colour.

Yes. Several chain straps have a softer shoulder section, and the bags themselves are practical flap and crossbody designs with secure closures. The chain looks dressy, but the bag works for a normal day. Wipe the chain now and then, and avoid catching it on knitwear.

A tonal plaque, black on black, or embossed lettering. Both show the brand without contrast or shine, which suits offices and minimal outfits. Silver-tone clasps and all-over monograms are more visible, so choose them when you want the bag to stand out.
