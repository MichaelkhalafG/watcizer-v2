Guess men's watches are made to be seen. They take the confidence of the brand's Los Angeles fashion roots and put it into large cases, layered dials and bold colours, at a price well below the luxury watches they sometimes resemble. This page gathers every men's piece we carry, and the guide below explains the lines one by one, then helps you choose a size, a strap and a colour.

## Phoenix

**Phoenix** is the most common line on this page. It is a large multi-function watch: the sub-dials show the day, the date and a 24-hour scale rather than a stopwatch, which keeps the dial busy and sporty without extra buttons. It often comes on a silicone strap, in black with gold or blue details. If you want the classic big Guess look, start here.

## Dashboard

**Dashboard** takes its inspiration from a car's instrument panel. The dial is designed like a set of gauges, with layered rings and contrasting markers, and it reads as technical and sporty. It suits men who like cars and detailed watches, and it pairs naturally with casual clothes and trainers.

## Poseidon and Fleet

**Poseidon** and **Fleet** borrow from diving watches: a rotating-style bezel, bold markers and a robust case. They are the most rugged looks in our men's Guess range, ideal with a T-shirt and jeans. Remember that the look comes from diving watches, but the water rating on the product page is what tells you whether you can swim with it.

## Continental and Headline

**Continental** and **Headline** are the calmer choices, with cleaner dials and a more classic layout. They are the ones to wear with a shirt for work or for a family dinner, when a full sports chronograph would feel too loud. A Continental on a steel bracelet is the most versatile men's Guess we carry.

## Sizes: 42 to 46 mm

The most common case size on this page is 46 millimetres, followed by 44 and 42 mm. On a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres, 44 mm looks balanced and 46 mm makes a statement; above 18.5 centimetres, 46 mm looks natural. For a slimmer wrist, choose 42 mm or a silicone-strap version, which sits closer to the skin and looks smaller than a steel bracelet.

## Silicone or steel

Silicone straps are common on men's Guess watches for good reason: they are soft, light, cool in the heat and easy to rinse after the gym or a sweaty day, and they fasten with a buckle that fits almost any wrist. Steel bracelets look dressier and last longer; they need sizing, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty.

## Black, gold, blue and green

Black is by far the most common colour here, on its own or with **gold** details that give it a bolder, dressier look. **Blue** dials are fresh and sporty, and **green** is the modern choice for a second watch. If you want one watch for everything, black on steel; if you want something lively for weekends, blue or green on silicone.

## Water and care

Check the water rating on each product page; many men's Guess watches list 50 metres, enough for rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool, but not for the sea or diving, and the pushers should never be pressed under water. Rinse a silicone strap with fresh water after the gym, keep perfume off gold-tone details, and change the battery as soon as the watch slows, usually every 2 to 3 years.

## Quartz and setting the dial

The men's Guess watches on this page run on **quartz** movements: accurate to within seconds a month, with no winding and no need to wear them every day. On a multi-function dial the day and the date are set through the crown, and the 24-hour hand moves with the main hands, so it always shows whether it is morning or evening. If the day and the date fall out of step after a battery change, bring the watch to our stores in Cairo and we set it for you in a few minutes.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between multi-function and chronograph?

A multi-function watch uses its sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale; it has no stopwatch. A chronograph has a stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown, and its sub-dials count the elapsed time. Phoenix is mostly multi-function; the product page names the functions of each model.

### Which men's Guess watch is best for work?

A Continental or Headline on a steel bracelet, with a black, blue or silver dial, in 42 or 44 mm. They are calm enough for an office, comfortable under a shirt cuff and still recognisably Guess. Save the big Phoenix and diver-style models for weekends and evenings out.

### Is a 46 mm Guess too big?

On a wrist above about 18 centimetres it looks bold but balanced; on a slimmer wrist it can overhang. Measure your wrist and compare the case size with a watch you already own. A silicone-strap version wears smaller than a steel one, and our stores in Cairo let you try both sizes.
