## What a Mini Focus watch looks like

A typical Mini Focus watch has a round steel case of 43 to 45 mm, three sub-dials, bold hour markers and a date window. In our range 14 models measure 44 mm and 9 measure 43 mm; a few are 45 or 48 mm, which is very large, and two are 42 mm for a smaller wrist. Black is the most common dial colour, followed by blue, green and silver, and many models pair the dial with a two-tone or gold-tone case for contrast.

The style is sporty rather than formal. It suits jeans, a polo shirt or a casual jacket better than a suit, and it makes a strong first watch for a young man who wants something that looks substantial on the wrist. If you prefer a quiet dress watch, a smaller three-hand model from another brand will suit you better.

## Steel bracelet or rubber strap

Most of our Mini Focus watches, 26 of 34, come on a stainless steel bracelet; 7 have a rubber strap and one a leather strap. The steel bracelet looks dressier and lasts longer, and it needs to be sized to your wrist: the links come out with a small pin, and within the warranty we size it free of charge in our stores. The rubber strap is lighter, more comfortable in the heat of an Egyptian summer and easy to rinse after the gym or the beach.

If you spend the day outdoors or sweat a lot, the rubber version is the more practical choice. If the watch is for work, a family occasion or a gift, the steel version usually makes the better first impression.

## Choosing the size

Mini Focus cases run large, so the size is the first thing to check. A 44 mm case looks balanced on most men's wrists of 17 to 19 cm; a wrist under about 17 cm is more comfortable with 42 or 43 mm, and a 48 mm case suits only a broad wrist and a bold taste. The lugs, the parts that hold the strap, should not reach past the edges of your wrist.

Thickness matters too, because a chronograph case is thicker than a three-hand watch and will not slip under a tight shirt cuff. If you wear long sleeves every day, choose a 43 mm model, or try the watch with the shirt you wear most before you decide.

To measure your wrist at home, wrap a strip of paper around it just below the bone, mark where it overlaps and measure it with a ruler. Most men measure between 16 and 20 cm, and the size guide above works from that number.

## Water resistance in daily life

Nearly every Mini Focus at Watchizer carries a 30 m rating, printed as 30 m or 3 ATM. Think of it as a raincoat rather than a swimsuit: a downpour, a splash from the sink or washing your hands will not hurt it, while a swim, a shower or a day at the sea will. The figure comes from a pressure test on a watch that is not moving, which is why real-world water is treated more cautiously.

Two habits protect the seals. Leave the crown pressed home and the pushers alone whenever the watch is wet, and take it off before steam: a hot shower or a sauna ages the gaskets faster than any cold water does.

## Getting the best from an affordable watch

A Mini Focus watch rewards a little care. Wipe a steel bracelet once a week and clean it with a soft brush now and then; let a leather strap dry naturally if it gets wet. All our Mini Focus models run on a quartz movement with a battery, which usually lasts 2 to 3 years; when the second hand starts to jump every few seconds, the battery is low.

Within the warranty we change the battery free of charge at our stores, so bring the watch with its invoice. A watch that stops after a hard knock or shows moisture under the glass should come to us rather than be opened at home.

Heat is the other thing to watch. A watch left on a car dashboard in summer can reach 60 degrees or more, which ages the seals and the strap; keep it in the shade or in a pocket. And if you wear a gold-tone model, put on perfume and lotion before the watch rather than after, so the plating keeps its colour for longer.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 44 mm Mini Focus too big for me?

On most men's wrists of 17 to 19 cm a 44 mm case looks balanced, because it is the size Mini Focus is designed around. If your wrist is under about 17 cm, choose a 42 or 43 mm model instead. The lugs should not go past the edges of your wrist; if they do, the watch is too large for you.

### Do the sub-dials on a Mini Focus really work?

On most models they do: the sub-dials usually show the day, the date or a 24-hour indicator, and some work as a stopwatch. The functions differ from model to model, so read the product page before you buy if a working stopwatch matters to you. If you are unsure, ask us on WhatsApp and we will check the exact model.
