Coach is the New York leather house known for practical, beautifully finished bags, and its designs are made to be carried every day for years. Our range is large and fully in stock, so this guide helps you narrow it down: first the material, then the hardware tone named in each title, then the shape, the colour and the named styles.

## Leather or Signature canvas

Coach bags come in two main materials. **Leather** has a smooth or pebbled grain, softens with use and gives a refined look. **Signature coated canvas** carries the interlocking C pattern in a durable, wipe-clean finish, often trimmed with leather. Choose leather for a classic, quiet look and Signature canvas for the instantly recognisable Coach style.

## Reading the hardware tone

Many titles begin with a metal tone before the colour: **brass**, gold or silver. It tells you the finish of the clasp, the zips and the logo. Brass is warm and slightly antique, gold is bright and dressy, and silver is cool and modern. Match it to your watch and jewellery for a coordinated look.

## Shoulder bags

**Shoulder** bags are the largest group on this page. They sit snugly under the arm, open quickly and hold a wallet, a phone, sunglasses and keys. We carry them in black, maple, walnut, burgundy, navy, khaki, chalk, pink and ivory, in leather and Signature canvas, so this is where most women will find their everyday bag.

## Crossbody bags

A **crossbody** bag rides across the body on a long strap, leaving both hands free and keeping the bag in front of you. Our Coach crossbody bags come in black, blue, khaki, powder pink, tan rust and gold with black, ideal for errands, travel and markets, when security and freedom matter most.

## Top-handle handbags

A **handbag** with a top handle is carried in the hand or the crook of the arm, often with a detachable strap for the shoulder. It is the most polished Coach shape, right for the office, family visits and dinners. We carry handbags in black, navy, brown, maple with dark brown, and Signature canvas with gold hardware.

## Caring for leather

Wipe leather with a soft, dry cloth, keep it away from rain and direct sun, and let it dry naturally if it gets wet. Store the bag stuffed with paper in its **dust bag** so it keeps its shape, and keep light leathers away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Caring for Signature canvas

Signature coated canvas is more forgiving than leather: wipe it with a slightly damp cloth and dry it in the shade. The leather trims still prefer a dry cloth. Avoid alcohol and strong cleaners on both, and never leave a bag in a hot car, where heat can mark the **coating**.

## Questions and answers

### What is Coach Signature canvas?

It is Coach's coated canvas printed with the brand's interlocking C pattern, usually trimmed with leather. The coating makes it durable, light and easy to wipe clean, and the pattern makes the bag recognisable as Coach at a glance. It is a practical choice for a bag used every day.

### What do brass, gold and silver mean in the titles?

They describe the tone of the bag's hardware: the clasp, the zips, the rings and the logo. Brass is warm and slightly antique, gold is bright and dressy, and silver is cool and modern. Choosing a tone that matches your watch and jewellery makes your whole look feel coordinated.

### Which Coach bag is best for every day?

A leather shoulder bag in black or maple. It holds the essentials, opens quickly and goes with almost every outfit. If you want your hands free, choose a crossbody bag; if you carry a laptop or a lot of things, the backpack is the better daily bag.
