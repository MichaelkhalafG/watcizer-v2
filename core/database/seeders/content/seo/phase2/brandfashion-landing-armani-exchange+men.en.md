Most men carry the same five things every day: a phone, a wallet, keys, earbuds and sunglasses. Armani Exchange's men's pieces are built around that short list, with small bags that keep it together without bulk. This guide explains what is in stock, how each piece is worn, how to put together a daily carry for work, weekends and travel, and how to keep the finish looking new.

## The two crossbody bags

Both in-stock crossbody bags are compact, flat and black, with a **pebbled** leather-look surface that hides small marks. One is all black, for a quiet look that goes with anything. The other adds a single vertical white stripe down the front, which gives a plain outfit a graphic, urban accent. Choose the stripe if you like one detail that stands out.

## The hand pouch

The black textured **hand pouch** shares the same pebbled finish. Carried on its own, it holds a phone, cards and keys for a short evening out. Slipped into a larger bag, it becomes an organiser for chargers, cables and a power bank, so nothing tangles at the bottom of a backpack or a suitcase. It is the most flexible piece on this page.

## What fits inside

A compact crossbody bag is sized for the essentials: a large phone, a slim **wallet** or card holder, keys, earbuds and a pair of sunglasses in a soft case. It is not meant for a tablet or a laptop. If you need room for a notebook or a water bottle, look out for the backpacks below, which arrive in small numbers.

## Wearing a crossbody: chest or hip

The strap is adjustable, so the same bag can sit in two places. Shortened, it rides high on the **chest**, close to the body, which is the most secure position in a crowd or on the metro. Lengthened, it hangs at the hip across the body, a more relaxed look for weekends. Avoid a very long strap that lets the bag swing below the hip.

## With a smart-casual outfit

A black crossbody bag works with chinos, a polo or an **Oxford** shirt and loafers or clean white trainers. Keep the bag on the outside of a light jacket rather than underneath it, so the jacket keeps its shape. The all-black bag is the more discreet choice here; the striped one suits a plain navy or grey outfit that needs one accent.

## With jeans and a T-shirt

With denim, a T-shirt and trainers, either bag looks natural. The white stripe echoes a white T-shirt or white soles, and the all-black bag pairs well with black jeans for a sleek, monochrome look. A steel watch from [Diesel](/brand/diesel) or [Emporio Armani](/brand/emporio-armani) in our range completes it.

## For travel

At an airport or in a new city, a crossbody bag keeps your passport, phone and cards in front of you, where you can see them. Pack the **pouch** inside your carry-on for cables and chargers, so you can lift it out at security in one move. Wear the bag on the chest in busy places, and close every zip before you walk away from a counter.

## One bag or two

If you buy one piece, make it a crossbody bag: it covers work, weekends and travel. If you want two, add the hand pouch, which organises a backpack or a suitcase and works on its own for evenings. A crossbody bag and a pouch in the same **black** finish look like a matched set, which makes them a neat combination.

## Matching the metals

Look at the zip pulls and the logo in the product photos and match them to your other accessories. Black and silver tones sit easily with a steel watch, a silver ring or silver **cufflinks**. If you wear gold, let the watch be the one bright piece, so the metals do not compete.

## Caring for the finish

Wipe the pebbled surface with a soft, dry **cloth** after use, and with a slightly damp one if it gets dusty; dry it in the shade. Keep perfume, hand cream and alcohol-based cleaners away from it. Empty the bag at night so it keeps its shape, and store it away from direct sun, which fades black finishes over time.
