Hugo Boss was founded in 1924 in Metzingen, Germany, and built its name on sharp, modern tailoring. Under the **BOSS** label it now dresses people for the office as much as for the evening, and its watches are designed to finish that look: clean, confident and easy to read. They are made under licence by a specialist watch company and run mainly on quartz. This guide helps you understand the features you will see on the product pages and choose the right Hugo Boss for the way you dress.

## Reading the features on the dial

Hugo Boss dials pack in a lot, so it helps to know the words. A **chronograph** is a stopwatch: two pushers beside the crown start, stop and reset it, and the small sub-dials count its minutes. A **tachymeter**, printed around the edge on racing models such as **Grand Prix**, converts the stopwatch into average speed. A **date window** sits at three, four or six o'clock. A **second time zone**, on travel models, shows the time at home and abroad together; the product page says when a model has one. Knowing these terms makes the product page specifications much quicker to read.

## The lines and their characters

**Grand Prix** and **Peak** are the sporty end of the range, with racing details and bold sub-dials. **Candor** and **Troper** are everyday chronographs, clean enough for the office. **Centre Court**, named after the main court of a tennis tournament, has a fresh, sporty dial. **Dapper** and **Avery** are the dress watches: slimmer cases, calmer faces and often leather straps. If you are choosing your first Hugo Boss, a Candor on a steel bracelet is the most versatile; if you already own a sports watch, a Dapper on leather is a natural second one. ## Black, blue or green

Black dials are the most common in our Hugo Boss range, followed by silver and blue. Green has become one of the brand's signature dial colours in recent years, and a green dial on a steel bracelet is an easy way to own something a little different from black. Green works surprisingly well with navy and grey suits and with brown leather shoes. Blue is the safest colourful choice; black is the most formal; silver is the most classic and the easiest to match. ## The 44 mm question

The most common case size in our range is 44 millimetres, with others from 41 to 46 mm. On an average men's wrist, 44 mm reads as modern and confident. If you prefer a classic proportion, or you wear fitted shirts, a 41 or 42 mm model sits more discreetly. The case thickness matters too, especially for chronographs, which are thicker than three-hand watches; the product page gives both measurements.

## Daily care for steel and leather

Rinse a steel bracelet with fresh water after the sea and dry it with a soft cloth; dust and salt collect between the links. Give a leather strap a day off in the heat, and keep it away from water and perfume. Never press chronograph pushers under water. If the seconds hand of a quartz model starts jumping several seconds at a time, the battery is getting weak and should be changed soon, usually after 2 to 3 years.

## Questions and answers

### Which Hugo Boss watch suits an office dress code?

A three-hand watch or a slim chronograph in 41 to 43 mm, with a black, blue or silver dial, on a steel bracelet or a black or brown leather strap. Dapper and Avery are made for this, and a Candor on steel is a good choice if you want a chronograph that still looks smart. Avoid very large sports models under a fitted shirt cuff.

### Is a green dial easy to wear?

Yes, more than most people expect. Green sits between the formality of black and the brightness of blue, and it goes well with navy, grey, beige and brown. On a steel bracelet it looks modern; on brown leather it looks warm and classic. If you are unsure, try it in our stores beside a black-dial version of the same model.

### Why does the chronograph hand not return to twelve?

After a knock or a battery change, the stopwatch hands of a quartz chronograph can lose their zero position, so they stop slightly off twelve after a reset. It is not a fault of the movement and it can be corrected in a few minutes. Bring the watch to our stores and we reset it; while the watch is under warranty this is free.

### Does a Hugo Boss watch come with a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and the same is printed on the card in the box. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. A broken glass, damage from a fall and water above the rating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I exchange a watch for another dial colour?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange.
