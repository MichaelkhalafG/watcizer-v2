With a range this large, the hard part is not finding a watch but narrowing the choice. This guide shows how the section is organised, what each big brand is known for, and a simple order of decisions that takes you from thousands of models to 2 or 3 you will love.

## Start with a style

The section has its own style pages. [Chronographs](/subtypes/chronograph) are the largest group, with stopwatch dials for men and women. [Casual watches](/subtypes/casual) are everyday pieces, [dress watches](/subtypes/dress) are slim and formal, [sport watches](/subtypes/sport) are built tough, [automatic watches](/subtypes/automatic) wind themselves, and [digital watches](/subtypes/digital-watch) add alarms and timers. ## Michael Kors and Guess

Michael Kors is the largest name in the section, with nearly a thousand models, many of them women's watches in gold, rose gold and silver with crystal details, alongside men's chronographs. Guess adds lively, colourful designs and multifunction dials at friendly prices. Both are favourites when the watch is part of the outfit.

## Tommy Hilfiger and Hugo Boss

Tommy Hilfiger brings a clean, sporty look in navy, red and white, with a large range of chronographs and multi-dial watches for men and women. Hugo Boss offers a more formal, modern style in black, blue and steel, from slim dress watches to sporty chronographs and a few automatic skeletons. Both suit a man who wants a watch for work and weekends.

## Emporio Armani, Fossil and Diesel

Emporio Armani mixes Italian design with practical sizes, including the automatic Sea Explorer diver. Fossil is known for its vintage-inspired designs and open-heart automatics that show the balance wheel. Diesel makes the boldest watches here: oversized cases, thick straps and dials with several time zones.

## Naviforce, Mini Focus and Alba

For value, Naviforce and Mini Focus offer large, sporty cases, analog-digital displays and strong colours at the most affordable prices, popular with students and young men. Alba, from the Seiko group, adds dependable quartz and automatic movements in its Active, Fashion, Flagship Signa and Prestige lines. Maserati brings motorsport styling to steel chronographs.

## Men's and women's watches

About 2 in 3 watches here are for men, the rest for women, with a few unisex models. Men's watches mostly measure 40 to 46 mm; women's 28 to 38 mm. The gender filter narrows the list quickly, but many women choose a men's design of 38 to 40 mm for a bolder look, and many slim men's dress watches suit women too.

## Movement: quartz or automatic

Quartz watches make up the vast majority of the section. They are accurate, light and need only a battery every 2 to 3 years. Automatic watches, from Emporio Armani, Seiko, Fossil, Maserati, Alba and Hugo Boss, wind themselves as you move and need a service every 4 to 5 years. Choose quartz for ease, automatic for the craft.

## Strap: steel, leather or rubber

Stainless steel bracelets account for about 3 in 5 watches. They are durable and suit both work and evenings. Leather straps look warmer and suit dress watches. Silicone and rubber suit sport and summer, because they dry quickly and do not mind sweat. Our stores can shorten a steel bracelet so it fits from the first day.

## Dial colours

Black is the most common dial in the section, then blue, white and silver. Black and blue are the safest everyday choices. White and silver look fresh and are easiest to read. Green, burgundy, champagne and mother-of-pearl dials add personality; many of them are women's models from Michael Kors and Alba Fashion.

### What size of watch should I choose?

For men, 40 to 44 mm suits most wrists; for women, 30 to 36 mm. If your wrist is slim, choose the smaller end of the range, and check the case thickness as well as the diameter. Our stores can let you try different sizes on the wrist before you decide.

### Can I exchange a watch I bought online?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its box and with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup.
