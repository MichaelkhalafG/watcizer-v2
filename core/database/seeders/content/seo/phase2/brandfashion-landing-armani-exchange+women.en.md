Every woman has a style she returns to, and Armani Exchange has a bag for most of them. This guide does not sort the range by shape but by wardrobe: minimalist, classic, statement and casual. Find the description that sounds most like you, and the bags that suit it follow, along with advice on silver hardware, care and gifts.

## If your style is minimalist

A minimalist wardrobe wants clean lines and a quiet logo. The **crescent** shoulder bag, with its curved shape and small silver-tone A|X buckle, and the structured tote with a tonal A|X plaque, black on black, are the natural choices. Neither competes with a simple outfit; both make it look finished.

## If your style is classic

A classic wardrobe of shirts, tailored trousers and dresses calls for structure. The black **satchel**, with rounded top handles, a silver-tone AX logo and three inner sections, looks polished and professional. A grained black tote with a detachable logo charm is the larger classic option for long days.

## If your style is statement

If you like a bag to be noticed, choose a **monogram**. The black and silver and white and grey monogram chain bags, and the black and grey monogram tote, carry all-over AX lettering. The camera bag adds an all-over logo print. Keep the rest of the outfit calm so the bag does the talking.

## If your style is casual

For jeans, trainers and weekend clothes, a **crossbody** bag feels right. The black embossed logo crossbody, with a silver-tone zip, and the white logo crossbody, with black piping, carry the essentials hands-free. The brown chain flap bag adds warmth to denim and camel.

## If your style is on the move

If your days are spent between university, the gym, the metro and a café, choose the black embossed-logo **backpack**. Its padded straps and top handle make it comfortable for long days, it holds a notebook, a water bottle and a jacket, and its clean black design suits women and men alike. Pair it with a small crossbody bag for evenings.

## The chain bags

Several bags hang from a silver **chain**: a black chain crossbody with a magnetic closure, a dark brown chain flap shoulder bag and the monogram chain bags. A chain strap dresses a bag up instantly, which makes these the bags to carry to dinners, engagements and weddings.

## The small Liz

The **Liz** is a small shoulder bag with a shiny finish, sized for a phone, a card holder and keys. It is a light, pretty bag for evenings and outings, and a good choice when you want a little shine without the weight of a chain.

## Silver-tone details

Clasps, buckles, charms and chains are mostly **silver-tone**. Silver looks cool and modern and matches a steel watch and silver or white-gold jewellery. If you mostly wear gold, choose a tonal or embossed logo bag, so the metal stays discreet and does not clash with your jewellery.

## Colours

**Black** leads and suits every wardrobe. **Brown** is the warm alternative, lovely with beige, camel and denim. **White** and off-white bags look fresh in summer and stand out against dark outfits, and the grey and silver monograms add pattern while staying neutral.

## One bag, several styles

If you want one bag that crosses styles, choose a black **tote** with a detachable strap: it is classic on its handles, casual on the strap, and its tonal or embossed logo suits a minimalist look. Add a small chain bag later for evenings, and the two cover almost every occasion.

## Caring for chains and logos

Wipe chains, clasps and metal logos with a dry **cloth** after use to keep their shine, and keep perfume and creams away from the metal. Store chain bags with the chain tucked inside, so it does not scratch the bag's surface, and avoid catching chains on knitwear.

## Caring for the bag

Wipe the surface with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry it in the shade; avoid alcohol and strong cleaners. Stuff the bag with paper when you store it, keep it in its **dust bag** and away from direct sun, and keep white bags away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### Which Armani Exchange bag suits a minimalist style?

The crescent shoulder bag or the structured tote with a tonal A|X plaque. Both have clean shapes and a quiet logo that does not compete with a simple outfit. Black on black is the most understated combination, and it pairs well with a steel watch and fine silver jewellery.

### Are the monogram bags too bold for every day?

Not if the rest of the outfit is calm. A monogram bag with plain clothes in black, white or denim looks confident rather than busy. If you prefer something softer, the white and grey monogram is lighter than the black and silver one and suits daytime well.

### Which bag can I wear to a wedding?

A chain bag: the black chain crossbody, the brown chain flap bag or a monogram chain bag. The silver chain dresses the bag up, and the compact size carries a phone, a card holder and a lipstick. The shiny Liz shoulder bag is another light evening choice.
