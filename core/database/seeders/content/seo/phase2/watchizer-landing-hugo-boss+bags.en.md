A good men's bag has to do three jobs: carry what you need, keep it organised and look right with what you wear. The Hugo Boss bags we carry are all black, so they match any outfit, and they are designed around those three jobs. This guide sorts them by how you will use them, a daily crossbody, a laptop backpack for work or a convertible backpack for travel, and explains the details that set each one apart.

## BOSS and HUGO

Hugo Boss sells under two names. **BOSS** is the classic line, with clean shapes, debossed logos and the brand's tricolor stripe. **HUGO** is the younger, bolder line, here with a square logo badge in a contrasting colour. Both lines appear on this page, and the product title tells you which one each bag belongs to.

## The everyday crossbody bag

Four of our Hugo Boss bags are **crossbody** bags, worn across the body on an adjustable strap. They hold a phone, a wallet, keys, earbuds and a power bank, and keep them in front of you in a crowd. Choose one if you carry little and want your hands free, on the metro, in a market or at an airport.

## Organised inside

The crossbody bags are built with several zipped sections. One **BOSS** model has 3 top-zipped sections plus a front and a back zip pocket; others add an inner zipped pocket and open slip pockets for cards. Separate sections mean you find your keys without emptying the bag, and valuables can sit in a back pocket against your body.

## Logos and stripes

The details tell the crossbody bags apart. One has a **striped signature strap** in the brand's colours, one a square HUGO logo badge, one a tricolor logo with a textured bottom panel, and the compact BOSS model a white printed logo with a text-detailed strap. All are black, so the detail you prefer is the deciding factor.

## The technical laptop backpacks

Two backpacks, one from **BOSS** with tricolor stripes and one from HUGO with a brown logo, are made of matte technical fabric. Inside, each has a padded laptop sleeve; the BOSS model adds side slip pockets, an internal zipped pocket and dividers. They suit a daily commute to the office or university, with a laptop, a charger and a notebook.

## The convertible travel backpacks

The two **convertible** backpacks are the largest bags here. Side compression buckles let you expand them for clothes on a short trip or pull them in for a slim profile at the office. One has a padded laptop sleeve with an elastic security strap opposite a wide pocket for a tablet or notebook; the other has a debossed BOSS logo on its fold-over flap.

## Choosing by what you carry

If you carry a phone, a wallet and keys, a **crossbody** bag is enough. If you carry a laptop every day, choose a technical backpack. If you travel for a weekend or fly often for work, choose a convertible backpack that can hold a change of clothes as well as the laptop. Measure your laptop and compare it with the sleeve size on the product page.

## Wearing a crossbody bag well

Adjust the strap so the bag sits at about **hip** height, high enough to rest a hand on it and low enough not to bounce as you walk. Wear it in front in crowds and on public transport, and on your back or side the rest of the time. Over a jacket, lengthen the strap a little so the bag does not pull the fabric.

## Looking after a technical bag

Technical fabric resists light rain and wipes clean with a damp cloth. Empty the bag now and then and shake out dust and crumbs, which wear the lining. Never overfill a backpack beyond its zips, and keep the laptop in its padded **sleeve** rather than loose in the main section, where a hard knock reaches it more easily.

The technical and convertible backpacks have padded laptop sleeves. Laptop sizes vary, so compare your laptop's measurements with the sleeve size given on the product page, or ask us on WhatsApp with your laptop model and we check for you before you order.

### What is the difference between BOSS and HUGO?

They are two lines of the same company. BOSS is the classic line, with clean shapes and discreet logos such as the debossed BOSS mark and the tricolor stripe. HUGO is the younger, bolder line, with more visible branding such as the square logo badge. Both use the same practical designs.
