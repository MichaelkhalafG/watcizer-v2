Emporio Armani dresses a woman the way the brand dresses its runway: in calm colours, clean shapes and a few well-chosen details. This page gathers the watches and bags we carry for women, and the guide below is about wearing them together, at work, in the evening, at weekends and on a journey, then about choosing a gift and caring for both a steel watch and a faux-leather bag.

## What you will find here

Watches make up most of this page: **dress watches** in steel or gold tone, mostly between 30 and 34 mm, with white, silver, mother-of-pearl or grey dials. Next come the **MyEA** totes and a few other bags and wallets. Stock changes often, so the in-stock pieces at any moment may be a small, carefully chosen group.

## At work

For the office, a silver **Gianni** with a white dial and the black MyEA tote make a quiet, polished pair. The tote holds a notebook, a water bottle and a day's essentials, and its removable pouch keeps keys and cards together. The watch is easy to read in meetings and slips under a blazer cuff without catching.

## In the evening

Evenings call for one shining piece. The gold-tone **oval** watch, with its Roman numerals and fine multilink bracelet, is the one to wear to a wedding or a formal dinner. Pair it with a small bag and gold jewellery, and leave the tote at home; its pouch can stand in as a slim clutch for a phone and a lipstick.

## At the weekend

At the weekend, colour can come from the bag. The **khaki green** MyEA tote brings a relaxed, military-inspired tone that works with white trainers, denim and linen. A two-tone Gianni T-Bar on the wrist links the gold hardware of the bag with any silver jewellery you wear.

## On a journey

For travel, the tote's detachable shoulder strap frees your hands at the airport, and the pouch keeps a passport and boarding pass apart from everything else. Choose a steel watch for the trip: it shrugs off heat and handling better than leather, and if its page lists **50 metres**, it can stay on for a short swim in the hotel pool.

## How the MyEA totes work

Both **MyEA** totes are built as a system: a roomy main bag with 2 top handles and a removable inner pouch. The khaki green one adds a detachable logo strap for the shoulder; the black one closes with a magnet. You can carry the tote alone, the pouch alone, or both together, which is why one bag covers so many occasions.

## A colour palette that works

Emporio Armani's women's pieces live in a short palette: **black**, khaki green, silver, gold and white. Everything on this page goes with everything else, which makes it easy to build a small, coherent wardrobe. Start with black and silver, then add gold for evenings and khaki for weekends.

## Watch faces worth knowing

The **Sinfonia** has a mother-of-pearl dial that shimmers in soft colours, the oval model a grey sunray dial that catches light in a sweep, and the Gianni and Gino clean white or pearl dials. A shimmering dial suits evenings and gifts; a plain white dial is the easiest to wear every day.

## Choosing a size

Most watches here measure 30 to 34 mm, the classic size for a women's dress watch. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, **32** to 34 mm looks balanced; 30 mm reads like jewellery. If you are buying for someone else, a 34 mm model with a bracelet that can be sized is the safest choice.

## Gifts for her

For a mother, a silver Gianni. For a wife or a fiancée, the oval gold-tone watch or the Sinfonia. For a colleague or a graduate, a MyEA tote, practical and smart. For a couple, two Gino watches, which we list for both women and men. Keep the invoice so any piece can be exchanged within 14 days.

## Caring for the watch

These are **quartz** dress watches with 30- or 50-metre ratings: rain and handwashing are fine, the shower is not. Wipe the bracelet with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day, put the watch on after perfume and creams, and change the battery every 2 to 3 years, as soon as it slows.

## Caring for the tote

The MyEA totes have **faux-leather** finishes, textured or grained. Wipe them with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry them in the shade, never with alcohol or strong cleaners. Stuff the bag with paper when you store it, so it keeps its shape, and keep the khaki tote away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### What does MyEA mean?

MyEA is the name of an Emporio Armani bag line built around one roomy tote that you can carry in several ways. The totes we carry come with a removable inner pouch, and one has a detachable shoulder strap, so a single bag can be used for work, travel and weekends.

### Can I order a watch and a tote together?

Yes. Add both to the basket and check out once; we confirm the whole order with you before it ships. With cash on delivery you can inspect each piece before paying, and if one is not right you can return it within 14 days and keep the other.

### Which Emporio Armani watch suits every outfit?

A silver Gianni with a white dial. Silver steel goes with every colour and every kind of jewellery, the white dial is easy to read, and the classic size sits neatly under any sleeve. For a warmer look that still matches silver jewellery, the two-tone Gianni T-Bar is the alternative.

### Does an Emporio Armani watch have a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I return part of an order?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange any piece, and we pay the return shipping. It must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a watch bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup.
