## Chains explained

A snake chain is smooth and flexible, made of tight plates that look like a single cord; it lies flat and looks refined. A link chain is made of small connected rings and moves more freely. A beaded chain alternates small balls with chain sections and adds texture. On this page 4 necklaces use snake chains, which are the most versatile with any neckline.

## Choosing a length

Most fashion necklaces are around 45 cm long, so the pendant sits just below the collarbone. That length suits round and V necklines and blouses with an open collar. For high necklines, a longer chain works better. If you layer two necklaces, keep about 5 cm between their lengths so the pendants do not tangle.

## Building a set

A complete look needs 3 pieces at most: a necklace, a watch and one bracelet. Keep the metal consistent, gold with gold, and let one piece lead. If the pendant is bold, like the pharaoh, choose a slim watch and a delicate bracelet. If the watch is large and sparkling, a simple heart pendant keeps the look balanced.

## Caring for gold-tone jewellery

Gold-tone jewellery is plated, so treat it gently. Put it on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take it off before showering, swimming or sport. Wipe it with a soft dry cloth after wearing, and store it in its pouch away from humidity. A quick wipe every 7 days keeps the shine. Keep each necklace separate to avoid scratches and tangled chains.
