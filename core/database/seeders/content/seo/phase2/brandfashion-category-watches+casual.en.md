An everyday watch earns its place by being worn, day after day, with whatever you put on. This guide explains what to look for in a casual watch, how the brands on this page compare, and how to keep your watch looking fresh with a few minutes of care a month.

## What casual means here

On this page, casual means a watch you can wear from morning to evening without changing it: a readable dial, a comfortable strap, a case between sporty and formal. Most have 3 hands and a date; some add a small chronograph. The steel bracelets and simple dials make them easy to match with jeans, a shirt, a skirt or a dress.

## Alba's 4 lines

Alba provides about 56 watches here, more than a third of the page. Active is its sporty line, with bold dials and some automatic models. Fashion is mostly for women, in pink, champagne, mother-of-pearl and silver-white dials. Flagship Signa and Prestige are more refined, with chronographs and leather options. Alba is part of the Seiko group, a reassuring name for the movement inside.

## Michael Kors for women

Michael Kors supplies about 29 casual watches, mostly women's, in gold, rose gold and silver, many with crystal details on the bezel or the dial. They are designed to be noticed, and they pair naturally with jewellery. If you like a watch that doubles as a bracelet, this is the brand to browse first.

## Hugo Boss and Guess

Hugo Boss offers about 21 models with a clean, modern design in black, blue and grey, mostly for men, on steel or leather. Guess offers about 17 lively models with colourful dials and multifunction displays, for both men and women. Together they cover the range from understated to playful.

## Naviforce, Mini Focus and Emporio Armani

Naviforce and Mini Focus add bold, affordable men's watches with large cases and contrasting dials, ideal for students and young men. Emporio Armani adds a few refined pieces for those who want an Italian name. The [Naviforce page](/brand/naviforce) gathers every design of the brand, casual and sporty.

## Women's or men's

About 76 watches on this page are for women and 69 for men. Women's models mostly measure 26 to 36 mm, men's 40 to 44 mm. Some 22 mm women's pieces are as delicate as jewellery. Many women like a men's 38 to 40 mm model for a bolder look, and the gender filter keeps the list clear either way.

## Steel, leather, rubber or silicone

About 2 in 3 casual watches here have a steel bracelet, which suits every season and handles sweat and splashes. Leather straps, on about 24 models, look warmer for autumn and winter. Rubber and silicone straps, on about 17 models, are the light summer choice. One model even uses a denim-look strap for a relaxed outfit.

## Dial colours

Black, white and blue dials are the most common, followed by silver and gold. Black and blue are the safest choices; white and silver are the easiest to read. Gold dials on women's models give a warm, festive look, and Alba's pink and champagne dials are a soft alternative to gold for a younger style.

## Easy ownership

Almost every casual watch here is quartz: accurate to a few seconds a month, with a battery that lasts 2 to 3 years. A few Alba Active models are automatic, for those who prefer a mechanical movement. Either way, a casual watch asks very little of you beyond an occasional wipe and a battery change.

## Keeping it fresh

Wipe the case and bracelet with a soft cloth every 7 days, and wash a steel bracelet with mild soap and a soft brush every month to remove dust between the links. Keep leather away from water and perfume. Rubber and silicone straps can be rinsed with water after a hot day.

## Questions and answers

### Is a casual watch good enough for work?

Yes, in most workplaces. A casual watch on a steel bracelet with a black, blue or white dial looks neat with office clothes. For formal events with a suit, a slim dress watch on leather is more elegant, but for daily work a casual watch is the practical, comfortable choice.

### Which brand on this page is best for women?

Michael Kors and Alba Fashion offer the widest choice for women, in gold, rose gold, silver and two-tone, with crystal details and soft dial colours. Guess adds colourful, playful designs. Use the gender filter to see only women's models, and the size filter to find cases that suit a slim wrist.

### Do casual watches resist water?

Most resist splashes, rain and hand washing, and many are rated 50 m, enough for a shower. Check the rating on each product page before swimming, and never wear a leather strap in water. For the pool or the sea, choose a model rated 100 m on rubber or steel.

### How long does the battery last?

Usually 2 to 3 years for a three-hand quartz watch, a little less for chronographs. When the seconds hand starts jumping several seconds at once, the battery is low. Our stores replace batteries and check the seals at the same time, so the watch stays protected.
