Michael Kors founded his New York label in 1981 and turned it into one of the best-known names in accessible luxury, with handbags and watches at its centre. His watches are designed the way his jewellery-inspired accessories are: polished metal, generous sparkle and shapes that look expensive without being fragile. Rather than describe every line, this guide helps you choose by occasion, by wrist size and by colour, so you can find the right watch quickly in a large range.

## Choosing by occasion

For everyday wear and the office, a plain steel bracelet with a clean dial is the most useful Michael Kors you can own. The **Runway** line is built for this: a simple round case, baton markers and a link bracelet that looks smart with work clothes and casual outfits alike. For evenings, weddings and engagement parties, look at the crystal-set models such as **Darci**, whose ring of stones catches the light, or the smaller, slimmer pieces in the **Mini** and **Slim** versions that sit on the wrist like a bracelet. If you like a sportier look, the **Parker** chronographs add three sub-dials and often a crystal bezel, which makes them busy, bold and easy to notice.

## Choosing by wrist size

The most common case size in our Michael Kors range is 39 millimetres, followed by 38, 40 and 42 mm, and the range goes down to 36 mm and below. A quick rule: measure your wrist with a tape. Under about 15 centimetres, 36 to 38 mm looks balanced; between 15 and 17 centimetres, 38 to 40 mm is the sweet spot; above that, a 42 mm or a chronograph will not look too large. The bracelet's width matters as much as the case: a wide link bracelet makes any watch look bigger.

## Choosing the colour of the metal

Gold, rose gold and silver are the three families, and two-tone bracelets mix them. Pick the metal you already wear in rings and earrings, because a watch that matches your jewellery looks like part of a set. Rose gold flatters warm skin tones and goes well with pink, nude and white clothes; yellow gold looks richest against dark colours; silver is the easiest to wear every day and the most neutral as a gift when you do not know the person's taste. The [women's Michael Kors watches page](/brand/michael-kors/watches/women) filters by bracelet and dial colour.

## Getting the bracelet right

Almost every Michael Kors watch we list has a stainless steel bracelet, so sizing is the one thing to plan for. The bracelet has removable links, held by small pins, and a folding clasp. Take out links in pairs — usually 2 to 4 in all — one from each side of the clasp, so the clasp stays centred under your wrist. We size bracelets for free in our stores while the watch is under warranty; keep the removed links in the box, because they are needed if you ever want the bracelet longer. A watch should be loose enough to slide a finger under the bracelet, especially in summer, when wrists swell a little in the heat.

## His-and-hers pairs

Michael Kors also works well as a pair: a women's watch and a men's chronograph in the same metal, for an engagement, a wedding anniversary or a couple's gift. The easiest way to build a pair is to choose the metal first — 2 watches in rose gold, gold or silver — and then pick each watch for its wearer: a 38 or 39 mm Runway or Darci for her, a 44 mm chronograph for him. The dials do not have to match exactly; the same metal tone is what makes them look like a set. Ordering both together means one delivery and one confirmation call, and each watch keeps its own warranty card.

If one of you prefers leather and the other steel, that is fine: choose the same colour for the case and let the strap reflect each person's taste.

## Keeping the shine

A Michael Kors watch is plated steel with crystals, so treat it like jewellery. Put it on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, not before. Take it off before the beach, the pool and the gym, because salt, chlorine and sweat dull the plating fastest. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after wearing it and store it in its box or a soft pouch, away from other jewellery that can scratch the polished surfaces. With that routine the finish keeps its colour for years. Crystals that fall out after a knock are not covered by the [warranty](/warranty), which is one more reason to take the watch off for sport.

## Questions and answers

### Which Michael Kors watch is the best gift for a woman?

If you do not know her taste, a 38 or 39 mm Runway in rose gold or silver is the safest choice: simple, elegant and easy to match. If she loves sparkle, choose a Darci with a crystal bezel. Keep the invoice, so the watch can be exchanged within 14 days if the colour or the size is not right.

### Does a 39 mm watch look too big on a small wrist?

It can, on a wrist under about 15 centimetres, especially with a wide bracelet. In that case, a 36 or 37 mm model, or a Mini version, will look better balanced. Compare the case size on the product page with a watch you own, and measure across the case without the crown.

### How many links should I remove from the bracelet?

It depends on your wrist, but most women need two to four links removed from a Michael Kors bracelet. Remove them in pairs from both sides of the clasp so it stays centred. If you prefer, bring the watch to our stores, where we size it for free while it is under warranty, and keep the spare links in the box.

### Will the rose gold colour last?

Yes, with normal care. The colour is a plating over stainless steel, and it lasts for years if you keep perfume, creams, salt water and chlorine away from it. Friction on desks and laptop edges is what wears the clasp first. Wipe the watch dry after hot days and keep it in its box when you are not wearing it.

### What warranty does a Michael Kors watch have?

Each product page shows the warranty length and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and the invoice together. Scratches, a broken glass, lost crystals after a knock and water damage are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains how to claim.
