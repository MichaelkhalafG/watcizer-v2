The best way to choose a bag is to picture how you will carry it: in your hand, on your shoulder, across your body or on your back. Each way suits a different day, and Calvin Klein makes a women's bag for every one of them. This guide is organised around those four ways to carry, then covers colours, materials, care and ordering.

## Carried on the shoulder: the tote

A **tote** has two long handles that rest on the shoulder and a wide opening that makes everything easy to reach. It is the bag for long days: work, university, a doctor's appointment followed by shopping. Our in-stock totes include a black monogram tote and colour-block totes in brown, khaki and white or black and brown.

## Carried on the shoulder: the shoulder bag

A **shoulder** bag is smaller than a tote and sits snugly under the arm on a single strap. It holds a wallet, a phone, sunglasses and keys and opens in a second. We carry it in vanilla, caramel and brown, in brown, khaki and white, and in the Calvin Klein Jeans Minimal Monogram style in black.

## Dressed up: the chain strap

One Calvin Klein Jeans shoulder bag hangs from a **silver chain** and has a raised CK monogram on the front. A chain strap turns a day bag into an evening bag, right for a dinner, an engagement party or a wedding. Silver hardware pairs naturally with a steel watch and silver jewellery.

## Carried across the body: the crossbody

A **crossbody** bag rides on a long strap across the body, so both hands are free and the bag stays in front of you. It is the safest choice on public transport and in markets. Our embossed crossbody in vanilla, khaki and caramel has a soft, textured surface and a compact shape for the essentials.

## Carried on the back: the backpack

The **backpack** in vanilla, caramel and brown spreads the weight over both shoulders, which is kind to your back on long days of walking or studying. Its colour block makes it look polished rather than sporty, so it works with a dress or a smart jacket as well as with jeans.

## Carried in the hand: the flap bag

The Calvin Klein Jeans **Minimal Monogram** flap bag closes with a silver-tone CK clasp and can be carried in the hand or on its strap. It is structured and neat, a good choice when you want a bag that looks put together without being formal.

## Choosing by your week

For workdays, the tote or the backpack. For errands and weekends, the crossbody bag. For evenings, the chain bag or the flap bag. If you are buying one bag for the whole week, a **shoulder** bag in a neutral colour block covers the most occasions with the least effort.

## Choosing a colour family

The bags fall into two families. **Warm neutrals**, vanilla, caramel, khaki, brown and white, are soft and easy with beige, white and denim. **Black** with the CK monogram is sharper and suits a city look. Choosing one family makes it easy to add a matching wallet later.

## Synthetic leather

Many of these bags are made of synthetic leather; some product pages describe it as 100% **polyurethane**, or PU. It is light, keeps its colour, resists light rain and wipes clean. Some styles in the range are listed as leather. The product page names the material of each bag.

## Looking after the strap and handles

Straps and handles take the most wear. Do not overload a bag so the handles stretch, and avoid hanging a heavy bag on a hook for weeks. Wipe a **chain** strap with a dry cloth to keep its shine, and keep perfume and creams off light-coloured handles, which can mark them.

## Storing a bag

Empty the bag, stuff it with paper so it keeps its shape, and keep it in its **dust bag**, away from direct sun. Store light vanilla and white bags away from dark denim and newspapers, which can transfer colour, and never stack heavy items on top of a structured bag.

## Questions and answers

### Which women's Calvin Klein bag is best for work?

A tote or the backpack. Both carry a notebook, a water bottle and a long wallet, and the black monogram tote and the colour-block totes look smart enough for an office. If you prefer a lighter bag, a neutral shoulder bag holds the essentials and works from the office to dinner.

### Can the chain bag be worn every day?

Yes. A chain strap looks dressier than a fabric or leather-look strap, but the bag itself is a practical shoulder bag. Wipe the chain with a dry cloth now and then to keep it shining, and avoid catching it on knitwear, which can pull threads.

### Is synthetic leather durable?

Yes, for everyday use. PU synthetic leather resists light rain, holds its colour and wipes clean easily. Its weak points are long hours of strong sun and heat, which can mark the surface, and sharp objects, which can scratch it. Stored in its dust bag, a synthetic bag stays in good shape for a long time.

### What if the bag I want is out of stock?

Many styles arrive in small numbers. The product page always shows the current stock; if the bag you want is unavailable, ask us on WhatsApp whether it is expected again, or choose a similar shape in the same colour family that is in stock now.

### Can I return a bag?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund goes back by the payment method you used.
