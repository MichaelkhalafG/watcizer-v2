A wallet is a small thing that is used dozens of times a day, which is why a good one is worth choosing carefully. On this page most Tommy Hilfiger wallets come as part of a set — wallet and belt, sometimes with a separate card holder — boxed and ready to give. ## Bifold wallets and card cases

The **bifold** is the classic men's wallet: it folds once, holds cards in slots on both sides and keeps notes flat in a long pocket. It fits a trouser pocket or a jacket easily and suits most men for daily use. A **card case** or **card holder** is slimmer, with a few slots and no note section; it suits a front pocket, a small crossbody bag or anyone who now pays mostly by card or phone. Several sets on this page include both, so you can carry the card holder on light days and the full wallet when you need cash.

## Automatic, reversible and classic belts

The belts inside the sets come in three types. An **automatic buckle** belt has no holes: the strap slides through the buckle and locks with a small lever at any length, so it fits perfectly and adjusts in a second. A **reversible** belt is black on one side and brown on the other; twist the buckle and it matches either colour of shoes. A **classic** pin-buckle belt uses holes, like any traditional belt. An automatic belt is the easiest to size for a gift, because it can be trimmed and adjusted without punching new holes.

## Patterns: plain, monogram, grid, plaid and stripe

Plain smooth or textured leather is the most discreet and the most formal. The **monogram** repeats the brand's initials across the leather, and the **grid** and **plaid** patterns add a subtle check. The **TH stripe**, in red, white and navy, is the most recognisable Tommy Hilfiger detail and appears as a band on the wallet or as the colour of a belt. For an office wardrobe, choose plain or grid; for a younger, casual style, the stripe or monogram.

## Choosing for yourself

Count your cards. If you carry 8 or more and still use cash, a bifold with at least 6 slots and a note pocket is right. If you carry 4 or 5 cards, a card case keeps your pocket flat. Then think about the belt: if you wear black and brown shoes, a reversible belt does both; if you change waist size or want a perfect fit, an automatic buckle is the most practical. Match the leather of the belt to your watch strap when you can.

## Looking after leather goods

Leather wallets and belts last longest when they stay dry and are not overfilled. Do not keep a wallet in a back pocket all day in summer; heat, sweat and pressure wear it fastest. Empty old receipts regularly so it keeps its shape, and wipe a belt with a dry cloth after a hot day; if you can, alternate 2 belts so each one dries fully. If a belt is too long, a leather shop can shorten a classic belt at the buckle end, and an automatic belt can usually be trimmed at the tip.

## Questions and answers

### What is an automatic buckle belt?

It is a belt without holes. The strap slides into the buckle and a small internal mechanism holds it at any point; press the lever on the buckle to release it. It adjusts in small steps, so it always fits exactly, and the strap can usually be shortened at the tip if it is too long.

### Is the leather genuine?

### How do I know the belt will fit?
