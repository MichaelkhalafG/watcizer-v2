The women's Tommy Hilfiger range at Brand Fashion is mostly bags and watches, and the best way to shop it is to think in pairs: a bag and a watch that work together for a particular kind of day. This page sets out five such pairings, explains how to choose colours and metals across them, and links to the pages where the watches and the bags are explained in detail.

## The workday pair

For the office, choose a structured shoulder bag or a tote in black, navy or beige, with a zip and room for a tablet, and pair it with a 38 mm steel watch in silver or two-tone. The bag keeps its shape on a desk, the watch is calm enough for meetings and easy to read, and silver goes with every outfit. If your workplace is relaxed, a backpack with a padded sleeve can replace the tote.

## The weekend pair

For shopping, cafés and errands, a crossbody bag is the most useful choice: light, secure and hands-free. Pair it with a watch on a silicone strap in a light summer colour, or a rose-gold steel model if you prefer something dressier. A navy crossbody with a white or blue silicone strap feels fresh and unmistakably Tommy Hilfiger. A crossbody of about 20 centimetres holds a phone, a card holder, keys and sunglasses, which is all a weekend needs.

## The travel pair

At airports and in new cities, keep your passport, boarding pass and phone in a crossbody bag in front of you, and carry the rest in a tote or a backpack. Choose a steel-bracelet watch that you never have to take off, and check that it lists 50 metres of water resistance if you want it in the hotel pool. Monogram canvas bags are the best travel companions because they shrug off scuffs and wipe clean.

## The evening pair

For dinners, engagements and weddings, carry a smaller handbag or a crossbody in black or red, and choose a gold or rose-gold watch, ideally a 32 or 36 mm model that sits like a bracelet. Keep other jewellery in the same metal as the watch, and let either the bag or the watch be the statement piece, not both.

## The gift pair

For a gift, the safest combination is a 38 mm watch on a steel bracelet in silver or rose gold, plus a black or navy shoulder bag. The bracelet can be sized after delivery and bags have no size to guess, so there is nothing to get wrong. Keep the invoices, so either piece can be exchanged within 14 days for another colour.

## Choosing colours across bag and watch

Think in families. **Black and silver** is the most formal and the easiest to match. **Navy, red and white** is the brand's own palette, cheerful and confident. **Beige with gold or rose gold** is soft and warm, lovely in spring and summer and with light clothes. Choose one family per outfit and your bag, watch and jewellery will look planned rather than collected.

## Metals: silver, gold or rose gold

Match the watch metal to the bag's hardware and your jewellery. Most bags on this page use silver-tone hardware, which pairs naturally with silver steel watches. Gold and rose-gold watches look best with bags in warm colours such as beige, brown or red, where the hardware matters less. Two-tone watches bridge both, if you wear silver and gold jewellery together. As a rule of 1: one metal tone per outfit, repeated in the watch, the bag hardware and your earrings.

## Sizes at a glance

For the **watch**, 38 mm suits most women's wrists of about 14 to 16 centimetres, with 32 to 36 mm for a finer look and 40 mm and above for a bold one. For the **bag**, a crossbody holds 4 essentials — phone, cards, keys and lipstick — a medium shoulder bag adds a wallet and sunglasses, and a tote or a backpack takes a tablet or a laptop. Check the inside width on the product page against your largest item.

## Building your first set

If you are starting from nothing, buy in this order. First, a black or navy shoulder bag that you will use every day; it is the piece that works hardest. Second, a 38 mm watch in the metal you wear most. Third, a crossbody bag for weekends and travel, in a second colour from the same family. Three pieces chosen this way cover work, weekends, travel and most evenings, and everything you add later will match what you already own.

If you want a fourth piece later, make it a small evening bag in a bold colour such as red; it adds variety without leaving your colour family.

## Caring for your pieces

Put on your watch after perfume and creams, which dull plating, and wipe it after a hot day. Wipe bags with a soft cloth, keep them out of a hot parked car and away from dark denim, and store them stuffed with paper in their dust bags. Store the watch apart from other jewellery so it does not scratch, and change a quartz battery as soon as it slows, usually every 2 to 3 years.

## Questions and answers

### Can I order a bag and a watch together?

Yes. Add both to the cart and they arrive in one delivery after one confirmation call. With cash on delivery you can inspect each piece in front of the courier before you pay, and each keeps its own right to be returned or exchanged within 14 days.

### Which watch goes with a navy bag?

A watch with a navy or blue dial, a navy strap or a silver steel bracelet. Navy and silver are a classic Tommy Hilfiger pairing; a rose-gold watch also works beautifully with navy for a softer, warmer look that suits evenings.

### Is beige practical for every day?

It is lovely and versatile, but it shows marks sooner than black or navy. Keep it away from dark denim, keep make-up and pens in an inner pouch and wipe it gently after use. Monogram canvas in beige is easier to keep clean than smooth beige finishes.

### What is the safest gift for a woman on this page?

A 38 mm silver or rose-gold steel watch, or a black or navy shoulder bag, or both together. The watch bracelet can be sized after delivery, the bag has no size to guess, and the colours suit almost any wardrobe.

### Can I exchange one piece if I buy two?

Yes. Each item can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. It must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a watch bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp.
