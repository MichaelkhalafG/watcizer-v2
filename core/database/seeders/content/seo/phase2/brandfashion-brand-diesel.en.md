Renzo Rosso founded Diesel in Italy in 1978, and the brand built its reputation on bold denim and an attitude that never apologised for being loud. Its watches, made under licence by a large watch group, follow the same rule: they are big, industrial and full of detail, designed to be the first thing people notice. This guide explains the three lines that define our Diesel range, how big each one really is, and how to choose and look after one.

## Mr. Daddy: up to four time zones on one dial

**Mr. Daddy** is the Diesel most people picture. Its large case carries a main dial and several small sub-dials, each of which can show a different time zone, so you can keep Cairo, Dubai, London and New York on your wrist at once. Each small dial is set with its own crown, and the instructions in the box show which crown controls which dial. The look is deliberately busy and mechanical, with exposed screws and a heavy crown guard. The best-known version measures around 57 millimetres, one of the largest cases in our whole range.

## Mega Chief: the big chronograph

**Mega Chief** is Diesel's oversized chronograph, usually around 51 millimetres. It has a working stopwatch with two pushers, sub-dials for the counted minutes and seconds, and bold numerals that stay readable at a glance. It is a little smaller and more wearable than Mr. Daddy, and it is the model we suggest to customers who want the Diesel look for daily wear. Black, gunmetal and gold-tone versions on steel or leather let you choose between a sporty and a dressy feel.

## Little Daddy: the Diesel look, smaller

**Little Daddy** takes the Mr. Daddy design and scales it down for smaller wrists, keeping the multi-dial face and the industrial details. It suits men who love the style but find 57 millimetres too much, and it also works for women who like a bold, oversized watch. The [Diesel page](/brand/diesel) shows every size side by side.

## How big is big? Sizes in context

Our Diesel range runs from about 46 to 59 millimetres, with 51 and 57 mm the most common. A typical men's watch is 40 to 44 mm, so even the smaller Diesel models are large. Measure your wrist with a tape: under about 17 centimetres, start with a 46 to 51 mm model; between 17 and 19 centimetres, 51 to 55 mm looks bold but balanced; above that, a 57 or 59 mm Mr. Daddy is in proportion. Remember that the lugs extend past the case, so a 57 mm watch covers the full width of an average wrist.

## Leather or steel on a big watch

Leather straps and steel bracelets are almost equally common in our Diesel range. A thick leather strap is lighter and more comfortable for long days, and it softens the industrial look; brown leather with a gold-tone case is a classic Diesel combination, and a brown-leather **Mega Chief** is one of the most wearable pieces in the range. A steel bracelet is heavier and more durable, and on a black or gunmetal case it creates the full, solid look the brand is known for. If you have never worn a heavy watch, start with leather.

## Colours and finishes

Black dominates Diesel: black cases, black dials and black straps give the most aggressive, modern version of each line, from **Mr. Daddy** to **Mega Chief**, and they hide wear well. Gunmetal grey is the technical alternative, softer than black and very good with denim and grey clothes. Gold-tone cases with brown leather are the warm, vintage-industrial option, at their best with a white shirt or a beige jacket. Some models add red or orange details on the hands or the chronograph scale, a small touch of colour that makes the dial easier to read. If this is your first Diesel, black is the safest choice; if you already own one, gold-tone on brown leather makes a striking second watch.

If you are torn between two colours, think about the clothes you wear most: dark colours and denim lean towards black and gunmetal, light colours and beige towards gold-tone.

## Looking after an oversized watch

Size brings its own care. Large cases catch door frames, car doors and desk edges, so be aware of the watch on your wrist for the first few days. The product page states the water resistance: many Diesel models list 50 or 100 metres, enough for rain and a pool, but never press the chronograph pushers under water. Plated black and gold-tone cases show wear on the edges over time, which many owners see as part of the character. Keep leather dry and away from perfume, and rinse steel after the sea.

## Questions and answers

### How do I set the time zones on a Mr. Daddy?

Each small dial has its own crown on the side of the case. Pull out the crown for the dial you want, turn it until the hands show the local time of that city, and push it back in. The main dial is set with the main crown, as on any watch. The instruction booklet in the box shows which crown belongs to which dial.

### Is Mega Chief smaller than Mr. Daddy?

Yes. A Mega Chief is usually around 51 mm, while the best-known Mr. Daddy is around 57 mm. That difference is very visible on the wrist. If you want the Diesel style for everyday wear, Mega Chief is the easier choice; Mr. Daddy is the statement piece for evenings and weekends.

### Are Diesel watches heavy?

The large steel models are heavier than most watches, especially on a steel bracelet. On a leather strap the same model feels noticeably lighter. Most people get used to the weight within a few days. If comfort is your priority, choose a leather-strap version or a Little Daddy.

### Does a Diesel watch come with a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and so does the card in the box. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Damage from knocks, a broken glass and water above the rating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.
