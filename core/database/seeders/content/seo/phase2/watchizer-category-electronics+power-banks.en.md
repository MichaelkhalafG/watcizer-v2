A power bank is the difference between a phone that dies at 4 in the afternoon and one that lasts until you are home. This guide explains what capacity means, how fast charging works on a power bank, and which of the 3 models suits your day.

## What mAh means

The capacity of a power bank is measured in milliamp-hours, mAh. A typical phone battery holds 3 to 5 thousand mAh. Because some energy is lost in the transfer, a 10 thousand mAh power bank charges most phones about 2 times, and a 20 thousand mAh model about 4 times. A tablet needs more, so the larger model is the better choice for it.

This is the model for long days and trips. ## Fast charging from a power bank

Fast charging works only when 3 things match: the power bank, the cable and the phone. A 22.5 W power bank with a 3 A cable fast charges most phones from about 0 to 50 percent in around 30 minutes. With an old or thin cable, the same power bank charges much more slowly. The [chargers page](/subtypes/chargers) has cables rated for fast charging.

## Recharging the power bank

Recharge the power bank with a wall charger of 18 to 20 W and a good cable; it will refill much faster than from a laptop port. A 20 thousand mAh model takes several hours to fill completely, so charging it overnight is the easiest habit. The display shows when it reaches 100 percent.

## Travelling with a power bank

Airlines ask passengers to carry power banks in hand luggage, never in checked bags, and set limits on capacity. Both the 10 and 20 thousand mAh models are common travel sizes, but rules differ between airlines, so check yours before you fly. Keep the power bank switched off and protected from knocks during the flight.

## Looking after the battery

Lithium batteries last longest when they are not left empty or full for weeks. If you store a power bank, charge it to about 50 percent and top it up every 3 months. Avoid leaving it in a hot car in summer; heat above 40 degrees ages the cells. Never use a power bank that is swollen, cracked or very hot.

## Choosing the right capacity

Choose 10 thousand mAh if you need 1 or 2 phone charges and want something light. Choose 20 thousand mAh if you charge a tablet, share with others, or spend whole days away from a socket. Choose the wired model if you hate carrying cables. All 3 work with Android phones and iPhones, and with earbuds, smartwatches and most tablets.

## Questions and answers

### How many times will it charge my phone?

A 10 thousand mAh power bank charges most phones about 2 times, and a 20 thousand mAh model about 4 times. The exact number depends on your phone's battery size and on energy lost as heat during the transfer. A tablet takes several times more energy than a phone, so it needs the larger model.

### Can I take a power bank on a plane?

Airlines generally require power banks to travel in hand luggage, not in checked bags, and set limits on capacity. The 10 and 20 thousand mAh models are common travel sizes, but rules vary between airlines and countries, so check with your airline before you fly and keep the power bank switched off.

### Why does my phone charge slowly from it?

The most common reason is the cable. Use a 3 A cable that supports fast charging, and make sure your phone supports PD for iPhone or QC for many Android phones. Charging 2 devices at once also splits the power, so each one charges more slowly than it would alone.
