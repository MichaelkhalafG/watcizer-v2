A bracelet is the easiest way to give a watch some company. This guide explains the styles on this page, how to choose a size, how to stack a bracelet with your watch, and how to keep the beads and chains looking bright.

## Styles on this page

There are 3 main families. Snake chain bracelets are smooth, flexible silver-tone chains threaded with beads. Cable chain bracelets use small linked rings and usually carry a single charm. Beaded bracelets are strung on an elastic or a cord, often black beads with a crystal-style centrepiece. Each title names the chain, the bead colours and the charm, so you can see what you are choosing.

## The charms

Charms are what make each piece personal. On this page you will find more than 10 different charms: a palm tree, a four-leaf clover, a crown, a horseshoe, angel wings, an arrow, an evil eye, flowers, circles and geometric shapes, most set with small crystal-style stones. Many people choose a charm for its meaning: the clover and horseshoe for luck, the evil eye for protection, the crown for confidence.

## Choosing a size

Measure your wrist just above the bone with a tape or a strip of paper, then add 1 to 2 cm for a comfortable fit. Elastic beaded bracelets stretch to fit most wrists between 15 and 18 cm. Chain bracelets usually have a clasp with an extension chain, and adjustable pieces like the Lacoste Arthor can be set in seconds.

## Stacking with a watch

The simplest rule is to wear the bracelet on the same wrist as the watch, on the side away from your hand, and to match the metal: silver-tone chains with a steel watch, gold-tone beads with a gold watch. A slim watch of 28 to 34 mm stacks best with 1 or 2 bracelets. With a larger watch, one beaded bracelet is enough. The [watches page](/subtypes/watches) helps you find a matching piece.

## Wearing several bracelets

Mixing 2 or 3 bracelets is popular: for example, a silver snake chain, a black beaded bracelet and a single charm bracelet. Keep 1 colour in common, such as black or silver, so the stack looks deliberate. Start with the thinnest piece next to the hand and add bolder ones further up the wrist.

## Caring for your bracelets

Put your bracelets on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, and take them off before swimming, showering or sport. Wipe them with a soft dry cloth after wearing. Store each one in a separate pouch or box, at room temperature below 30 degrees, so the chains do not tangle and the stones do not scratch each other. Avoid pulling elastic bracelets over your hand roughly; roll them on instead.

## Questions and answers

### Will a beaded bracelet fit my wrist?

Most beaded bracelets here are strung on elastic and fit wrists between about 15 and 18 cm. Chain bracelets usually have a clasp with a short extension chain. Measure your wrist just above the bone and add 1 to 2 cm; if you are unsure, ask our team on WhatsApp before ordering.

### Can I wear the bracelet in the shower?

It is better not to. Water, soap and chlorine can dull the beads and the metal finish and weaken elastic over time. Take the bracelet off before showering, swimming or sport, and put it on after perfume and lotion. With that small habit it keeps its shine much longer and the charm stays bright.
