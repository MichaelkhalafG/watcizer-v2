Before you choose a bag, it helps to know what makes one well made. The Hugo Boss bags on this page share a few features worth looking for in any men's bag: a padded place for a laptop, separate zipped pockets, adjustable straps and materials that cope with daily use. This guide explains those features one by one and shows which of our bags has each, so you can match a bag to the way you live.

## Two names, one company

The bags carry one of two labels. **BOSS** signals the classic line, with discreet logos and the brand's tricolor stripe; **HUGO** signals the younger line, with a bolder square badge. The construction is equally practical in both, so choose by the look you prefer rather than by the label alone.

## Feature 1: a padded laptop sleeve

A padded sleeve keeps a laptop off the bottom of the bag and away from keys and chargers. Both **technical backpacks** and one of the convertible travel backpacks have one; the travel model adds an elastic security strap that holds the laptop in place. Check the laptop size you need against the measurements on the product page.

## Feature 2: separate zipped pockets

Separate pockets keep small things where you can find them. The compact **BOSS** crossbody bag has 3 zipped top sections plus a front and a back zip pocket, and the other crossbody bags add inner zipped and slip pockets. A back pocket that sits against your body is the safest place for a phone or a wallet.

## Feature 3: expandable capacity

The two **convertible** travel backpacks have side compression buckles. Loosen them and the bag grows to take a change of clothes for a night away; tighten them and it slims down for the office. One bag can therefore cover a working week and a weekend trip without looking oversized on a Monday.

## Feature 4: straps that adjust

Every crossbody bag here has an **adjustable** strap, some woven with the brand's stripes or text, so it can sit at hip height over a T-shirt or a jacket. The backpacks have padded shoulder straps, and on the HUGO backpack the straps and back panel carry jacquard webbing with the brand name.

## Feature 5: materials for daily use

The backpacks are made of **matte technical fabric**, light, resistant to light rain and easy to wipe clean. When they are in stock, the leather handbags bring the opposite character: a full-grain leather look that ages with use and suits a suit and a briefcase-style day. The product page names the material of each bag.

## Matching a bag to your day

For a commute with a laptop, a **technical backpack**. For flights and weekends, a convertible travel backpack. For errands, cafés and city walks, a crossbody bag. For formal offices and meetings, a leather handbag when one is available. If one bag must do everything, the convertible backpack is the most flexible.

## Security on the move

Wear a crossbody bag in front of you on the **metro** and in crowds, with the zips facing your body. On a backpack, keep valuables in the inner zipped pocket rather than the front one. At airports, a crossbody bag for the passport and phone plus a backpack for the laptop keeps everything close and easy to reach at security.

## Black, with a detail

All of our Hugo Boss bags are **black**, which keeps them easy to match and hides marks. The differences are in the details: tricolor stripes, a square logo badge, a debossed logo on a flap or a text-printed strap. Pick the detail you like, because the colour will suit everything you own.

## Caring for technical fabric and leather

Wipe technical fabric with a damp cloth and let it dry in the shade; avoid strong detergents, which can dull the matte finish. Leather prefers a dry cloth and occasional conditioning, and dislikes heavy rain. For both, empty the bag now and then, shake out crumbs and never force a zip over an overfilled pocket, which strains the **teeth**.

## Questions and answers

### Which Hugo Boss bag holds a laptop?

The two technical backpacks and one of the convertible travel backpacks have padded laptop sleeves. Laptops differ in size, so compare yours with the measurements on the product page, or send us your laptop model on WhatsApp and we check before you order.

### Is a Hugo Boss crossbody bag big enough for a tablet?

Most crossbody bags here are designed for a phone, a wallet, keys, earbuds and a power bank rather than a tablet. If you need to carry a tablet or a notebook, a backpack is the better choice. The product page lists the measurements of each bag, so you can compare them with your tablet.

### Are the Hugo Boss backpacks waterproof?

The technical fabric resists light rain and splashes, which is enough for a walk to the car or a short shower. It is not designed for heavy rain or for being submerged, so keep a laptop in its padded sleeve and avoid leaving the bag on wet ground.
