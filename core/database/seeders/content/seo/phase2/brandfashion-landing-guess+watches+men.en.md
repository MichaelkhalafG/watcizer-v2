At work, a steel bracelet and a clean dial look the most polished. **Connoisseur** shows the day and the date on a black steel case, and **Continental** comes on leather in gold with black or rose gold with brown. Choose a black, blue or silver dial in 42 or 44 mm, which slides under a shirt cuff without catching.

## For the gym and summer: silicone and rubber

Silicone and rubber straps are light, cool and easy to rinse, so they suit hot days and active weekends. **Phoenix**, **Poseidon**, **Formula** and **Headline** all come on silicone here, in black, blue, green, yellow and pink. ## For evenings: gold and black

Black with gold is the most typical Guess evening look. The gold-and-black **Phoenix**, **Delta** and **Jet** models pair a black dial or strap with gold-tone hands and markers, bold enough for a wedding or a dinner and still easy to read. Keep the rest of your accessories simple, so the watch is the one bright point.

## For chronograph fans

A chronograph adds a stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown, with sub-dials that count the elapsed time. **Fleet**, **Octane**, **Surge**, **Anchor** and **Jolt** are among the chronograph lines we carry. Many other models use a multi-function layout instead, with sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale; the product page names the functions of each.

## Tonneau cases

A few Guess models use a **tonneau** case, a barrel shape that is wider in the middle and curves at the ends. It looks different from the round watches that fill most of the page and it often sits comfortably on the wrist, following the curve of the arm. It is a good choice if you want a Guess that stands out without being larger.

## Reading the water rating

Specifications sometimes give water resistance in **ATM** or bar rather than metres: 3 ATM equals 30 metres, 5 ATM equals 50 metres and 10 ATM equals 100 metres. At 30 metres the watch is for splashes only; at 50 metres a short swim in a pool is fine; at 100 metres or more you can swim in the sea. Never press the pushers under water.

## Glass: mineral or sapphire

Most of our men's Guess watches list **mineral** glass, hardened glass that is clear and resists everyday knocks well. Some models list sapphire, which is harder still and resists scratches better. The glass is named in the specifications of each product; whichever it is, keep the watch away from rough walls, metal tools and gravel.

## Choosing the size

The most common sizes here are 44 and 46 mm, followed by 42, 43 and 45 mm. On a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres, 42 to 44 mm looks balanced; above about 18.5 centimetres, 46 mm looks natural. A watch on silicone or rubber wears a little smaller than the same case on a steel bracelet, because the strap sits closer to the skin.

## Steel bracelets and sizing

Steel bracelets are the most common strap on this page, and they look the dressiest. They need sizing, usually by removing 2 to 4 links in pairs from both sides of the clasp, which any watch shop does in minutes. Leather straps are the warmer, classic alternative; keep them dry and let them air after a hot day.

## Battery and care

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows, so a weak cell never leaks inside. Wipe the case and bracelet after a hot day, rinse silicone in fresh water after the gym, and keep perfume off gold-tone plating.

## Questions and answers

### What does 5 ATM mean on a Guess watch?

ATM is a unit of pressure, and 5 ATM corresponds to 50 metres of water resistance. In practice that covers rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool, but not the sea or diving. 3 ATM means 30 metres, for splashes only, and 10 ATM means 100 metres, which allows swimming in the sea.

### Which men's Guess watch is best for the gym?

A model on a silicone or rubber strap with a rating of at least 50 metres, such as a Phoenix or Poseidon on silicone. The strap is light, rinses clean after a workout and does not hold sweat like leather. Take the watch off for weight training, where a bar can knock the case and the glass.

### Is mineral glass scratch resistant?

It resists everyday knocks well, but it can scratch against harder materials such as stone, concrete or metal tools. Sapphire is harder and resists scratches better. Whichever glass your model has, avoid brushing the watch against walls and remove it for heavy work, where the risk of a deep scratch is highest.
