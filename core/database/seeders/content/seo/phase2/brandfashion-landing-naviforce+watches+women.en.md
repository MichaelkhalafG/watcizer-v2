A women's Naviforce is one of the easiest ways to own a smart steel watch without spending much, and it makes an easy gift for sisters, friends and colleagues. Because the range is built around one well-judged size, choosing comes down to the metal, the dial and the strap. This guide explains each choice and what to expect from the watch day to day.

## The 37 mm case

Almost every women's Naviforce on this page measures 37 millimetres. That size is large enough to read at a glance and look modern, yet balanced on most women's wrists of 14 to 16 centimetres. It sits between a delicate jewellery watch and an oversized fashion watch, which is why it suits office days and weekends equally. If you prefer something finer, a few **32** and **33** mm models give a more classic look on a slim wrist.

## Silver, gold or rose gold

The steel bracelets come in three tones. **Silver** is the easiest to wear every day and goes with every outfit and piece of jewellery. **Gold** looks warm and festive, at its best with yellow-gold earrings and rings. **Rose gold** is soft and feminine and flatters warm skin tones. Many models match the bracelet and the dial in the same tone for a seamless, jewellery-like effect.

## Reading the title code

Naviforce titles often end in a short code for the colours, in this order: case, dial, strap. **S** is silver, **G** gold, **RG** rose gold, **W** white, **B** black, **BE** blue and **GN** green. So RG/W/RG means a rose-gold case, a white dial and a rose-gold bracelet, and S/BE/S a silver case with a blue dial on a silver bracelet. The photos on each page confirm the exact combination.

## Dial colours

**White** and **silver** dials are the most classic and easy to match. **Black** looks sharper and more modern, especially on a silver or rose-gold bracelet. **Blue** and **green** dials add a touch of colour that still looks polished. If you want one watch for everything, a white or silver dial on a silver bracelet is the safest choice; for a second watch, a rose-gold case with a coloured dial.

## Bracelet, leather or silicone

Most women's models are on **steel bracelets**, durable and easy to clean; they need sizing, usually by removing 2 to 4 links in pairs from both sides of the clasp, which takes a few minutes in our stores or any watch shop. A few are on **leather**, softer and warmer for the office and cooler months. **Silicone** straps, where available, are the light, sporty choice for summer and travel, and fit most wrists with a simple buckle.

## Everyday wear

A 37 mm steel watch is a true everyday companion: it goes from the office to a café to a family dinner without looking out of place. Put it on after perfume and hand cream, which dull plating, and take it off before washing up or cleaning, when water and chemicals are most likely to reach it. Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day.

## Water: what to expect

The women's Naviforce watches here are made for splashes: most list the 30-metre level on the product page. Rain and washing your hands are fine, but swimming, the sea, the shower and the dishes are not. If water ever fogs the inside of the glass, stop wearing the watch and bring it to us before the movement is affected.

## Battery and care

These are quartz watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. When the watch starts losing time, change the battery promptly so a weak cell never leaks inside. Store the watch in its box rather than loose in a jewellery dish, where harder pieces can scratch the case and the glass.

## Matching with jewellery

Keep one metal tone across the watch, the bracelets and the rings, and the whole look appears planned. A rose-gold Naviforce pairs beautifully with pearls and soft colours; a gold one with gold hoops and warm clothes; a silver one with almost anything. A 2-watch wardrobe of one silver and one rose-gold model covers every outfit, from a white office shirt to a dark evening dress, and lets you match whichever jewellery you choose that morning. If you wear an engagement ring or a gold wedding band every day, make your everyday watch gold or rose gold to match it.

## Questions and answers

### Is 37 mm a good size for a woman?

For most women, yes. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 37 mm is easy to read and modern in proportion, without looking oversized. If your wrist is very slim or you prefer a classic jewellery look, choose one of the 32 or 33 mm models instead.

### What does RG/W/RG mean in the title?

It is the colour code: rose-gold case, white dial, rose-gold bracelet. The code always lists the case first, then the dial, then the bracelet or strap. S is silver, G gold, B black and BE blue. The photos on the product page show the exact model, so check them as well.

### Will the gold colour fade?

The gold and rose-gold tones are plating over stainless steel. With normal care they last a long time; what wears them is friction and chemicals such as perfume, creams, sea water and cleaning products. Put the watch on after applying them, and wipe it after a hot day.

### Does a women's Naviforce have a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.
