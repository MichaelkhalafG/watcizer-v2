Tommy Hilfiger started his label in New York in 1985, and its red, white and navy flag became one of the most recognisable logos in casual fashion. The brand's strength is consistency: a watch, a wallet and a bag from Tommy Hilfiger look like they belong together, which makes it easy to choose two or three pieces at once. This guide walks through each part of the range we carry — watches, bags, wallets and sets — and what to look at in each.

## Men's watches: the named lines

Most Tommy Hilfiger watches at Brand Fashion are men's models, and the titles usually carry the line's name. **Stewart**, **Baker** and **Tyson** are sporty chronographs with sub-dials and strong markers. **Bryan**, **Henry** and **Weston** lean dressier, with cleaner dials and leather straps that sit well under a shirt cuff. **TH85** is the brand's integrated-bracelet design, a steel case that flows into its bracelet in the style of the 1970s, and **Lars** and **Legend** sit between sport and dress.

The most common case size in the men's range is 44 millimetres, followed by 46 mm for the larger sports models. Steel bracelets lead, then leather, then rubber and silicone for the sportier pieces. The [men's Tommy Hilfiger watches page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/watches/men) lets you filter by strap and dial colour.

## Women's watches

The women's Tommy Hilfiger watches we list are mostly 38 millimetres across, a balanced size that suits most women's wrists, with smaller 32 and 36 mm models for a finer look. Steel bracelets in silver, gold and rose-gold tones are the most common, and a group of models comes on soft silicone straps in light colours for summer. They keep the brand's clean style: simple dials, small logo details and a sporty touch in the bezel or the strap. The [women's Tommy Hilfiger watches page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/watches/women) shows them together.

## Bags for men

Men's bags are a real strength of the Tommy Hilfiger range here. Most are **crossbody bags** — small, flat bags worn across the chest that carry a phone, keys, a wallet and sunglasses — and **messenger bags** large enough for a tablet or documents. They come in black, navy and brown, often with the monogram print or a small flag badge. A crossbody bag is the practical choice for daily life in a busy city, because it keeps your things in front of you and your hands free. The [men's Tommy Hilfiger bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/men) lists them with their measurements.

## Putting a matching set together

Because the brand's style is consistent, it is easy to build a set. Choose one colour family — navy and brown, or black and silver — and stay with it across 3 pieces: the watch strap, the wallet and the belt. A brown leather watch strap with a brown wallet-and-belt set is the classic combination for work; a black steel watch with a black crossbody bag works for everything else. For a gift, a watch and a wallet-and-belt set from the same family make a complete present without the risk of guessing sizes.

## Looking after leather and steel

Leather wallets, belts and straps last longest when they are kept dry and allowed to rest. In summer, rotate 2 belts or straps if you can, and wipe off sweat at the end of the day. Steel watch bracelets need a rinse with fresh water after the sea and a dry cloth; bags need to be emptied and stuffed with paper when they are stored, so they keep their shape. Keep perfume away from leather and from plated watch cases, because it leaves marks on both.

## Questions and answers

### Which Tommy Hilfiger watch is best as a first watch for a young man?

A 44 mm three-hand model or a simple chronograph on a steel bracelet, in black or navy, is the safest first choice: it suits university, work and evenings, and steel survives daily wear better than leather. Lines such as Bryan or Henry are calm and versatile; Stewart and Tyson are sportier if he prefers a bolder look.

### Is the wallet-and-belt set packed as a gift?

Yes, the sets come in a gift box with the wallet and the belt together, so they are ready to give as they arrive. The product page shows the box and lists the belt's length and the wallet's size. Check the belt length against the person's waist size, and if you are unsure, remember that a leather belt can usually be shortened at the buckle end by a leather shop.

### How do I choose a men's crossbody bag?

Think about what you carry every day. If it is a phone, keys and a wallet, a small flat crossbody bag is enough and stays light. If you add a tablet, a charger or papers, choose a messenger bag and check its width on the product page. An adjustable strap matters too: it lets you wear the bag high on the chest or lower on the hip.

### Do Tommy Hilfiger watches come with a warranty?

Watches come with a warranty, and its length and who provides it are written on each product page. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the warranty card and the invoice together, since you need both. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains what is covered and what is not, such as a broken glass or water damage.

### Can I exchange a bag or a wallet?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay for the return shipping. The item must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund is made by the payment method you used.
