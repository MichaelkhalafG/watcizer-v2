Fossil's women's watches are easy to live with: steel bracelets that last, clear dials and just enough shine to feel special. Rather than listing every model, this guide helps you narrow the range by three questions, round or rectangular, simple or detailed, plain or sparkling, and then covers size, metal, water, care and ordering.

## Round or rectangular

Most women's Fossils are **round**, the classic and versatile shape. The **Raquel** is the exception: a rectangular case with a three-hand dial and a date, in gold tone, silver or two-tone, some with mother-of-pearl dials. A rectangular watch has a vintage, jewellery-like character and pairs beautifully with thin bangles.

## Simple: three hands and a date

For a calm, easy-to-read watch, choose a **three-hand** model, often with a date window. The **Jacqueline**, one of the names that appears most often in our titles, is the classic example: a clean round dial in silver, gold or two-tone. The Raquel follows the same simple layout in its rectangular case.

## Detailed: multi-function and chronograph

For a richer dial, a **multi-function** model adds small sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale, and a few chronographs add a stopwatch. The **Riley** pairs a multi-function dial with crystal accents in navy, and the **Cecile** is another multi-function design at 38 mm. These look sportier and more detailed.

## Sparkle: crystal accents

Some models add **crystals** around the bezel or on the markers. The **Neely** and several silver and rose-gold models catch the light without overwhelming the design, which makes them right for both a normal day and an evening out. Remove crystal watches for sport and housework, where a knock can loosen a stone.

## Carlie: small, light and modern

The **Carlie** is a slim 35 mm watch with a mix of numerals and markers, in rose gold or all black. It is a natural everyday choice for university or the office, light on the wrist and easy to read, and the black version is one of the most modern looks in the range.

## Sizes: from Raquel to 38 mm

Most round models measure 34 to 38 mm; **36** mm is the most common and looks balanced on a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres. A few cases are smaller, at 29 to 31 mm, for a delicate look, and the rectangular Raquel is about 23 to 26 mm wide. Compare with a watch you already own before choosing.

## Choosing the metal

**Gold** tone is the most common here, warm and festive. **Rose gold** flatters warm skin tones, **silver** goes with white-gold and silver jewellery, and **two-tone** steel matches both, which makes it a smart choice when you wear both metals or buy as a gift.

## Steel or leather

Most models are on **steel** bracelets: durable, easy to clean and right for every season. They are sized by removing links, which any watch shop does in minutes. A few are on **leather**, softer and warmer for the office and the cooler months; keep leather dry and let it air after a hot day.

## Water: reading the rating

The specifications list water resistance in metres or **ATM**: 3 ATM is 30 metres, for splashes only; 5 ATM is 50 metres, the most common rating here, for rain, washing your hands and a short pool swim; 10 ATM is 100 metres and allows swimming in the sea. Take any watch off before a hot shower.

## Matching your jewellery

Keep one metal tone across the watch and your everyday rings and bracelets. A gold Jacqueline goes with yellow-gold rings, a silver Neely with white gold and pearls, a rose-gold Carlie with rose-gold pieces. A rectangular **Raquel** in two-tone links gold and silver if you wear both.

## His and hers

Brand Fashion also carries men's Fossils, so a couple can share one style. Match the metal rather than the model: a gold-tone **Jacqueline** with a gold-accented Townsman, or a silver Carlie with a steel Grant. The sizes differ, but a shared tone and a similar dial colour make the pair look planned, a thoughtful idea for an engagement or an anniversary.

## Battery and care

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows, so a weak cell never leaks inside. Put the watch on after perfume and creams, and store it in its box, away from harder jewellery.

## Choosing one as a gift

For a mother, a Jacqueline in gold or two-tone. For a student, a Carlie in rose gold. For someone who loves vintage style, a Raquel with a mother-of-pearl dial. For someone who likes detail, a Riley or Cecile. Keep the invoice, so the watch can be exchanged within 14 days if the size or colour is not right.

## Questions and answers

### What is the Fossil Raquel?

It is a women's Fossil line with a rectangular case, a three-hand dial and a date window. We carry it in gold tone, silver and two-tone, some with mother-of-pearl or green dials. The rectangular shape gives it a vintage, jewellery-like look that differs from the round watches on the rest of the page.

### Which women's Fossil is the easiest to wear every day?

A three-hand Jacqueline in silver or two-tone, at 36 mm. It is simple, easy to read, matches every outfit and has a date window for everyday use. If you prefer a lighter, more modern look, a Carlie is the alternative, and a steel bracelet on either keeps care to a minimum.

### Can I swim with a women's Fossil?

Check the rating on the product page. At 5 ATM or 50 metres, a short swim in a pool is fine; at 10 ATM or 100 metres, the sea too. A 3 ATM watch is for splashes only. A leather strap should stay out of the water whatever the rating, and always rinse the watch after the pool.
