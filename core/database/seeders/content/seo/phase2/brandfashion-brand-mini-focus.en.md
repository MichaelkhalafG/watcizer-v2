## Choosing the size

Men's Mini Focus cases run large. The most common size in our range is 44 mm, with 52 models, followed by 43 and 45 mm; a 44 mm case looks balanced on most men's wrists of 17 to 19 cm, and a wrist under about 17 cm is more comfortable with 42 or 43 mm. The women's watches are mostly 30 to 32 mm, a classic size that sits neatly under a sleeve.

To measure your wrist at home, wrap a strip of paper around it just below the bone, mark where it overlaps and measure it with a ruler. The lugs, the parts that hold the strap, should not reach past the edges of your wrist; if they do, choose a smaller case.

## Steel bracelet, silicone or rubber strap

A steel bracelet is the most common choice in our Mini Focus range, on 173 models; 57 have a silicone strap, 27 a rubber strap and 8 a leather strap. The steel bracelet looks dressier and lasts longer, and it needs to be sized to your wrist: the links come out with a small pin, and within the warranty we size it free of charge at our two stores in Cairo.

Silicone and rubber straps are lighter, more comfortable in the heat of an Egyptian summer and easy to rinse after the gym or the beach. A leather strap is warm and classic but should stay away from water. For a watch you will wear every day in every season, steel is the safest single choice; silicone makes a good second watch for sport and summer.

Colour is the other choice. Black is the most common dial in our Mini Focus range, on 109 models, followed by blue on 69 and silver on 36; green, gold and white dials make up most of the rest. A black or blue dial on a steel bracelet is the easiest to wear with anything, while a green or gold dial makes a stronger statement and works best as a second watch.

## Water resistance in daily life

Most of our Mini Focus watches are rated to 30 m, shown on the dial or the case back as 30 m or 3 ATM, and a smaller group is rated to 50 m. A 30 m rating covers rain, splashes and hand washing, not swimming or the shower. A 50 m rating adds a light swim in shallow water. The ratings are measured in a laboratory with the watch at rest, so treat them as protection against everyday water.

Keep the crown pushed in, never press the chronograph pushers under water, and take the watch off before a hot shower or a sauna, because steam and heat are harder on the seals than cold water.

## Looking after a Mini Focus watch

All our Mini Focus models run on a quartz movement with a battery, which usually lasts 2 to 3 years; when the second hand starts to jump every few seconds, the battery is low, and within the warranty we change it free of charge at our stores. Wipe a steel bracelet once a week and rinse a silicone strap after sport.

Heat is the other thing to watch: a watch left on a car dashboard in summer can reach 60 degrees or more, which ages the seals and the strap. If you wear a gold-tone or rose-gold model, put on perfume and lotion before the watch, not after, so the plating keeps its colour for longer.
