Karl Lagerfeld was one of the most influential designers of the last half-century, known for his work at the great Paris houses and for his own label, which carries his name and, often, his famous silhouette with sunglasses and a white ponytail. The accessories under the **Karl Lagerfeld Paris** name translate that look into practical pieces: graphic, black-and-white, with a touch of irony. This guide explains the belts and bags in our range and how to choose and wear them.

## The house style in a few details

Karl Lagerfeld's look was unmistakable: a high white collar, dark glasses, fingerless gloves and a silver ponytail, all in black and white. The accessories borrow from that wardrobe. You will see the designer's **silhouette** printed or cast in metal on some bags, the name written in clean capitals, and a palette that rarely strays far from black, white and grey, with metal as the only shine. That restraint is what makes the pieces easy to combine: a black Karl Lagerfeld belt or bag goes with almost anything, and the graphic logo gives it personality without colour.

## Men's belts with signature buckles

Most of our Karl Lagerfeld pieces for men are **leather belts**. They come in black leather with polished metal buckles, some round, some carrying the brand's name or monogram, in silver or gold tone. A belt is the easiest designer accessory to wear every day: it sits with jeans, chinos or a suit, and the buckle is the only detail that shows. Choose silver tone if your watch case is steel, gold tone if your watch is gold or two-tone, and match the leather colour to your shoes. A round buckle looks more modern, while a buckle carrying the name announces the brand more clearly.

## Maybelle: the women's bag family

**Maybelle** is the bag family that appears most in our women's Karl Lagerfeld range. It comes in several shapes. The **crossbody** is compact, worn on a long strap across the body, for a phone, cards and keys. The **camera bag** is a small, boxy crossbody with a zipped top, practical for errands and travel. The **satchel** is larger and more structured, with a top handle and a detachable strap, for work days and outings that need more space. Most carry the Karl Lagerfeld name or the designer's silhouette as a metal or printed detail.

## Black, taupe and a pop of colour

Black is the house colour of Karl Lagerfeld and the most versatile choice for both belts and bags. **Taupe**, a soft grey-brown, is a warm neutral that goes with beige, white and denim and feels lighter in summer. A few pieces add a bright **fuchsia**, a confident colour that turns a black or white outfit into something memorable. Gold-tone and silver-tone hardware sit beside each other in the range, so you can match your jewellery and watch.

## Choosing a bag by your day

Empty your current bag and look at what you carry. If it is only a phone, cards and keys, a Maybelle crossbody or camera bag is enough and keeps you light. If you add a wallet, sunglasses and a pouch, choose the satchel. If you carry a laptop, look elsewhere in our catalogue for a tote, because Maybelle bags are designed for lighter days. ## Caring for leather and coated materials

Wipe leather belts with a dry cloth after a hot day and let them rest between uses in summer, so sweat can dry. Bags made of coated materials wipe clean with a slightly damp cloth; never use household cleaners or alcohol, which can strip the print. Keep bags out of a hot parked car, where a 35-degree day can soften coatings, and store them empty, stuffed with paper and away from sunlight, which fades taupe and fuchsia.

## Questions and answers

### What does Karl Lagerfeld Paris mean on a product?

It is the name under which the designer's accessories line is sold, carrying his name and graphic style. The pieces keep the house look — black and white, metal details, the Karl silhouette — in everyday bags and belts. The product page shows exactly which logo or detail each model has.

### How do I choose the right belt length?

Measure a belt you already wear, from the buckle to the hole you use most, and compare it with the length on the product page. The buckle should close on a middle hole, leaving room to adjust. If a belt is a little long, a leather shop can usually shorten it at the buckle end.

### Is the camera bag big enough for daily use?

It is made for the essentials: a phone, a card holder, keys, a lipstick and perhaps small sunglasses. That is enough for errands, shopping and travel days. If you carry a full wallet or a power bank, the satchel will be more comfortable. The product page lists the measurements so you can check.
