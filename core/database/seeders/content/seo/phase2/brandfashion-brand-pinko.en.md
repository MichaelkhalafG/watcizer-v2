Pinko grew from a small Italian label into a fashion house known for clothes and accessories with a confident, feminine attitude. Its bags are recognisable for their metal details, above all a clasp formed by two birds facing each other, and for shapes that mix elegance with a little boldness. This guide explains what to look for in a Pinko bag, how to choose between our everyday and evening pieces, and how to wear and protect them.

## What makes a bag a Pinko

Three things set Pinko bags apart. The **hardware**, often a bird clasp, a logo plaque or a chain, is large and polished, and it is the focal point of the design. The **shapes** are compact and structured, with neat flaps and clean edges that keep their form. And the **finish** leans glossy and feminine, with quilting or smooth leather that catches the light. Together they make a bag that dresses up a plain outfit instantly, which makes it a natural choice for occasions and for days when you want to feel put together.

## Everyday leather shoulder bags

Many of our Pinko pieces are **leather shoulder bags and handbags** sized for daily use. They hold a wallet, a phone, keys, sunglasses and a small pouch, and they sit comfortably under the arm or in the hand. A top handle lets the same bag look formal for a meeting; a shoulder strap makes it easy for the commute. Before ordering, compare the width and height on the product page with the wallet you carry, because structured bags keep their shape and will not stretch for a bulky wallet. Inside there is usually a zipped pocket for a phone or keys and a slip pocket for cards; look at the photos of the bag open to see how it is organised before you decide.

## Crossbody bags for evenings and events

The **crossbody** Pinko bags are smaller, made to carry the essentials of an evening — a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick — on a long strap that leaves your hands free. Many use a chain or a chain-and-leather strap that can be doubled to wear on the shoulder. They are ideal for weddings, engagements and dinners, where you need your hands for greeting guests, holding a glass or taking photos. The [Pinko page](/brand/pinko) lists every model with its measurements.

## Black, white and burgundy

**Black** is the most versatile Pinko and works with every outfit and season. **White** is the statement colour of summer and of evening events, striking against black or bright clothes, but it needs gentle care. **Burgundy** is a warm, rich alternative for autumn and winter that pairs well with black, grey and beige. If you choose one bag, black covers everything; if you already have black, a white Pinko for summer events is the most rewarding second bag. A white bag with gold-tone hardware looks especially beautiful in wedding and summer-evening photos.

## Wearing the hardware well

Because the metal is the star of a Pinko bag, keep your other accessories calm. Match the bag's metal tone — gold or silver — with your earrings and your watch, and avoid wearing many competing pieces at once. A slim bracelet watch, such as a women's Guess or Michael Kors model from our range in the same tone, balances the bag without fighting it. On a simple black dress or a white shirt, a Pinko bag and one piece of jewellery are all you need.

## Protecting leather, chains and white finishes

Wipe the bag with a soft, dry cloth after use, and keep it away from perfume and creams, which mark leather and dull metal. Never leave it in a hot car, where a 35-degree day can soften leather and glue. Store it empty and stuffed with paper, inside its dust bag, with the chain laid inside so it does not scratch the leather. For white bags, keep dark denim away and wipe any mark at once, before it settles. If the metal loses some of its shine, a gentle wipe with a dry microfibre cloth usually brings it back.

## Questions and answers

### Is a Pinko bag suitable for daily use?

Yes, especially the leather shoulder bags and handbags in black, which are sized for a wallet, a phone, keys and sunglasses and keep their structure through daily use. The smaller crossbody pieces are better kept for evenings and events, when you carry only the essentials.

### What is the bird clasp?

It is Pinko's signature closure: a metal clasp shaped like two stylised birds facing each other, used on many of the brand's bags. It acts as both the fastening and the main decoration of the bag. The product photos show whether a model has it, and in which metal tone.

### How do I carry a chain bag comfortably?

Wear it crossbody on the full chain for long evenings, so the weight rests on your shoulder and your hands stay free; double the chain to wear it on the shoulder for a shorter, dressier look. If the chain has a leather band woven through, that part sits on your shoulder and makes it more comfortable.

### Will a white Pinko stay white?

With care, yes. Keep it away from dark denim and new dark clothes, whose dye can transfer, keep pens and make-up in a pouch inside, and wipe it gently after each use. Store it in its dust bag, out of the sun. Treated this way, a white bag keeps its fresh look for many seasons.

### Can I exchange a Pinko bag?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. The bag must be unused and in its original packaging with its tags attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange or the refund, by the payment method you used.
