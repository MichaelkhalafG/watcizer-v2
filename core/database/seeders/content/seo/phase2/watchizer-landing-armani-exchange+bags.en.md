Armani Exchange is the most urban, casual line of the Armani family, and its bags show it: clean black shapes, bold logos and practical details made for city days. Our range is small and almost entirely in stock, so this guide takes the shapes one by one, explains the logo styles and helps you pick the right bag for work, evenings and weekends.

## Totes for the working day

Several Armani Exchange **totes** carry a full day: long double handles for the shoulder, a roomy interior and, on most, a detachable shoulder strap. We carry a structured black tote with a tonal A|X plaque, a grained black tote with a detachable metal logo charm, a black and ash grey tote with a geometric logo print, and an off-white tote with embossed logos and a silver-tone charm.

## The structured satchel

The black **satchel** has a structured body, double rounded top handles and a silver-tone AX logo on the front. Inside, three sections keep a wallet, a phone and papers apart. A satchel looks the most polished of all these shapes and is the one to carry to an office or a formal appointment.

## The crescent shoulder bag

The **crescent** bag has a sleek curved shape that sits neatly under the arm, a silver-tone A|X logo buckle and an adjustable shoulder strap. It holds a wallet, a phone and keys and looks modern and minimal, equally at home with a dress or with jeans.

## The camera bag

The **camera** bag in black and dark grey is a compact, boxy crossbody with an all-over logo print, a bold tonal AX plaque and an adjustable strap. It carries a phone, a card holder, keys and a lipstick, and keeps both hands free, which makes it ideal for errands, concerts and travel.

## The monogram chain bag

The white and grey **monogram** chain bag has an all-over AX lettering pattern, a silver-tone AX clasp on the flap and a silver chain strap. It is the dressiest bag on the page, made for dinners and celebrations, and its light colours look fresh against black or navy outfits.

## The embossed-logo backpack

The black **backpack** has an all-over embossed logo, a top carry handle and adjustable padded shoulder straps. It suits university, commuting and travel, carrying the day's things while staying close to the brand's clean city style. It is also a good unisex choice.

## Reading the logo styles

Armani Exchange uses its logo in several ways. A **tonal** plaque, black on black, is the quietest. Embossed lettering adds texture without colour. A silver-tone metal logo or charm adds a little shine. An all-over geometric print or monogram is the boldest. Choose the level of branding that suits your style.

## Black, off-white and grey

**Black** dominates the range: practical, smart and easy to match with any outfit or shoe. The **off-white** tote and the white and grey chain bag are the lighter alternatives for spring and summer. Black and ash grey or dark grey prints sit in between, adding pattern without losing the urban look.

## Silver-tone hardware

Most of these bags use **silver-tone** metal for logos, buckles, clasps and chains. Silver looks cool and modern and pairs naturally with a steel watch and silver jewellery. If you mostly wear gold, a tonal or embossed logo bag keeps the hardware discreet.

## Detachable straps and inner sections

Several of these bags give you options. Most totes come with a **detachable** shoulder strap, so they can be carried by hand or on the shoulder, and the camera bag and crescent bag have adjustable straps. Inside, the satchel's three sections and the totes' pockets keep keys and a phone apart from the rest, which saves time when you pay or travel.

## Choosing a bag for your day

For work, the satchel or a structured tote. For errands and travel, the camera bag or the backpack. For evenings, the monogram chain bag or the crescent shoulder bag. If you want one bag for everything, a black **tote** with a detachable strap covers the most occasions.

## Looking after an Armani Exchange bag

Wipe the surface with a soft, slightly damp **cloth** and dry it in the shade; avoid alcohol and strong cleaners. Wipe chains and metal logos with a dry cloth to keep their shine. Store the bag stuffed with paper in its dust bag, and keep the off-white and white bags away from dark denim, which can transfer dye.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between A|X and AX on the bags?

Both are logos of Armani Exchange. A|X, with the vertical bar, appears on several plaques, buckles and charms, while AX appears on clasps, prints and lettering. They mark the same brand; the difference is only in the design of each bag.

### Which Armani Exchange bag is best for work?

The structured satchel or a structured tote. Both hold papers, a wallet and a phone, and keep their shape, which looks smart in an office. The satchel's three inner sections keep things organised; a tote with a detachable strap gives you the choice of carrying it by hand or on the shoulder.

### Is the backpack only for men?

No. The embossed-logo backpack is a clean black design that suits women and men alike. Its padded straps make it comfortable for long days at university or in the city, and its black colour goes with any outfit, from jeans to a smart jacket.
