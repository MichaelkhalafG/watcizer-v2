The right Coach wallet depends on your day. Some women pay by card and carry almost nothing; others still need room for notes and coins; others want a wallet that replaces a bag. This guide sorts our Coach wallets by those habits, then helps you pick a print, care for Signature canvas and choose one as a gift.

## If you pay mostly by card

A **card holder** or a zip card case is all you need: a few slots for cards and, on the zip cases, room for folded notes and coins. They slip into a pocket, a mini bag or the front of a larger bag. We carry a strawberry print card holder in beige and zip card cases with cherry and floral prints.

## If you still carry cash

If you pay taxis, kiosks and shops in **cash**, choose a wallet with a full-length note section and a coin pocket. The medium corner zip wallet keeps notes flat and change separate, and the small zip-around closes on three sides, so coins cannot escape at the bottom of a bag.

## If you want one hand free at the till

A **snap** wallet opens with a press of the thumb, which is quick when you are holding a phone or a shopping bag in the other hand. Our floral print and Signature snap wallets fold compactly and suit small and medium handbags.

The prints give each wallet a personality. **Floral** sprays and leaves feel romantic and fresh. The floral heart is sweet and youthful. **Cherry** and strawberry prints are playful and fun. ## Matching a print to a bag

A printed wallet looks best inside a **plain** bag, where it adds a flash of colour each time you open it. With a Signature canvas bag, a plain Signature wallet keeps the look calm and coordinated. If your bag is black, a light wallet in white, beige or pink is also easier to find.

## Signature canvas, explained

**Signature canvas** is Coach's coated canvas printed with the interlocking C pattern and usually trimmed with leather. The coating makes it light, resistant to light rain and easy to wipe clean, so it keeps its colour and print through years of daily use, which suits a wallet that is opened many times a day.

## Leather details

The small zip-around combines **brown leather** with Signature canvas, and many wallets have leather trims and tabs. Leather prefers a dry cloth, so wipe the canvas and the leather separately, and let any damp patches dry naturally, away from direct heat.

## Keeping a wallet tidy

Clear out receipts every week, keep cards to about **10** in the smaller sizes and avoid forcing a zip or a snap over a bulging stack. A tidy wallet closes easily, keeps its shape and makes paying quicker, especially at a busy till.

## Caring for prints

Prints stay bright when they stay clean and dry. Wipe the wallet with a slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol or strong cleaners, and keep it away from dark denim and **newspaper** ink, which can transfer colour onto light canvas. Store it in its box when you are not using it.
