## Case size

The case size is the diameter of the watch without the crown, in millimetres. It is the single most useful figure: compare it with a watch you already own. As a rough guide, 28 to 34 mm looks petite, 36 to 40 mm balanced, 42 to 44 mm sporty and 46 mm or more bold. Lug-to-lug length, where listed, tells you whether the watch overhangs a slim wrist.

## Movement

Almost every Guess watch here runs on a **quartz** movement: a battery-powered movement that is accurate to within seconds a month and needs no winding. The battery usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Quartz also means the watch keeps time even when it sits in a drawer for weeks, which suits anyone who owns more than one watch.

## Dial functions

A **three-hand** dial shows the hours, minutes and seconds, sometimes with a date. A **day-date** dial adds the day of the week. A **multi-function** dial uses small sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale. A **chronograph** adds a stopwatch, run by 2 pushers beside the crown. The product page names the functions of every model.

## Glass

Most of our Guess watches list **mineral** glass, which is clear and handles everyday knocks well; some list sapphire, which resists scratches better. Whichever your model has, the main risk is rubbing against walls, stone and metal tools. Take the watch off for heavy work and store it in its box rather than with keys and coins.

## Water rating

Water resistance appears in metres or in ATM: 3 ATM is 30 metres, 5 ATM is 50 metres and 10 ATM is 100 metres. At 30 metres keep the watch to splashes. At 50 metres a short swim in a pool is fine. At 100 metres or more you can swim in the sea. Never press the pushers of a chronograph under water.

## Band

**Steel** bracelets are the most common band here, dressy and long-lasting; they are sized by removing links. **Mesh** is a supple woven steel that often adjusts with a sliding clasp. **Silicone** and **rubber** are light and rinse clean, ideal for summer and sport. **Leather** looks warm and classic but prefers dry days.

## Colour: case and dial

Titles usually name the case colour first and the dial second. **Gold** and **rose gold** cases are plated stainless steel, warm and festive; **silver** is plain polished steel; **two-tone** mixes both; **gunmetal** is a dark grey. A black or blue dial is the easiest to read, and a white or silver dial looks the most classic.

## Sizes that suit either wrist

Some sizes work for men and women alike. A 38 to 40 mm women's model can suit a man with a slim wrist who likes a smaller watch, and a 42 mm men's model on silicone can suit a woman who prefers a large, sporty look. Judge by the case size and the strap, not by the label alone.

## Matching watches for couples

For a couple, keep the same metal tone and let the sizes differ: a 44 mm rose-gold men's watch with a 36 mm rose-gold women's model, or two silver steel watches with blue dials. Matching tones look planned without being identical, and a couple's pair also makes a thoughtful engagement or anniversary gift.

## Battery and care

Change the battery as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside. Wipe the case and bracelet after a hot day, rinse silicone in fresh water after the gym, and put the watch on after perfume and creams, which dull plating. Store it in its **box**, away from harder pieces that can scratch it.

## Questions and answers

### How do I tell a men's Guess watch from a women's one?

The title usually says men's or women's, and the reference code often ends in G for gents or L for ladies. The case size is the clearest guide: most men's models are 42 mm or more, most women's 40 mm or less.

### Which Guess watch is the safest gift if I do not know the wrist size?

A model on a silicone or rubber strap, or a women's watch on a mesh bracelet with a sliding clasp. Both fit almost any wrist without visiting a watch shop. Choose a metal tone that matches the jewellery the person already wears, and keep the invoice in case an exchange is needed.

### Can a steel bracelet be shortened after delivery?

Yes. A link bracelet is shortened by removing links, usually in pairs from both sides of the clasp, which any watch shop does in minutes. Try the watch on before shortening it, though: a bracelet that has been shortened can no longer be returned, so be sure of the model first.

### Does a Guess watch have a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it. Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I exchange it for another model?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange.
