Rather than starting from shapes, this guide starts from your day: the office, the evening, the weekend, travel and university. For each, it suggests the shape, the size and the brands on this page that do it best, then covers materials, colours and care.

## For the office

A work bag needs room for a tablet or documents, a structure that stands on a desk, and a quiet colour. Look at shoulder bags and totes of 30 cm or more in black, brown or beige. Tory Burch and Coach make structured leather shoulder bags; Calvin Klein offers minimal black totes. If you carry a laptop, check the dimensions in the product description.

## For the evening

For weddings, engagements and dinners, a small bag with a chain or a detachable strap works as a crossbody bag or a clutch. Pinko's compact bags with the Love Birds buckle, Guess's gold-hardware minis and Tory Burch's small crossbody bags are the favourites. Match the hardware to your watch and jewellery: gold with gold, silver with silver.

## For the weekend

Weekends call for hands-free bags. A medium crossbody bag holds a phone, a wallet, keys and sunglasses and stays close to the body in crowded places. Tommy Hilfiger and Guess offer many in logo prints and colours. Coach's Signature canvas crossbody bags resist scratches and look good with jeans.

## For travel

Le Pliage's folding nylon totes are the travel classic: light, roomy and flat when empty, in sizes from S to L. For flights, a tote that closes with a zip is safer than an open one. Men's backpacks from Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein carry a laptop and a jacket for a day of meetings in another city.

## For university

Students need a bag that holds a laptop, notebooks and a water bottle without hurting the shoulder. Backpacks and large totes from Tommy Hilfiger, Guess and Le Pliage are the most popular choices. In September, when the school year starts, these sell quickly, so choose early to find the colour you want.

## Bags for men

About 217 bags on this page are for men: crossbody bags, messenger bags, backpacks and slim pouches from Tommy Hilfiger, Calvin Klein, Emporio Armani, Armani Exchange and Hugo Boss. A small crossbody bag is the modern way to carry essentials when pockets are full. Black and navy are the safest colours.

## Brand by brand

Tommy Hilfiger, with more than 300 bags, covers every use with its TH monogram and stripes. Guess, with more than 200, brings colour and logo prints at friendly prices. Coach offers leather and Signature canvas. Tory Burch makes structured leather with the double-T logo. Le Pliage specialises in folding totes. Pinko and Karl Lagerfeld add a fashion edge.

## Leather, canvas or nylon

Leather is rich and long-lasting but heavier and sensitive to rain. Coated canvas, used by Coach and Guess, resists scratches and stains. Nylon, used by Le Pliage, is the lightest and easiest to clean. For 1 bag that handles everything, coated canvas or nylon with leather trim is the most forgiving choice.

## Colours that work

Black is the most common colour here, on more than 200 bags, followed by brown, white and beige. Black and brown go with everything and hide marks. Beige and white are fresh for summer but need more care. If you already own a black bag, a brown or beige one is the most useful second bag.

## Size, step by step

Under 20 cm: phone, cards and keys. 20 to 30 cm: add a wallet, sunglasses and a small pouch. 30 to 40 cm: a tablet or documents. Over 40 cm, or a tote: a laptop and a day's worth of things. The product description lists the dimensions of every bag, so compare before you choose.

## Care that keeps a bag new

Stuff bags with paper and store them in their dust bags, away from direct sun. Wipe leather with a dry cloth and canvas or nylon with a damp one. Do not hang heavy bags by their straps for weeks, and keep light colours away from dark denim. A good bag treated this way looks new for 3 years and more.

For a student, a Le Pliage tote. For a man, a Tommy Hilfiger crossbody. Every bag can be exchanged within 14 days if unused.

A structured shoulder bag or a tote of 30 cm or more in black, brown or beige suits most offices. Tory Burch and Coach make elegant leather options; Calvin Klein offers minimal ones. If you carry a laptop, choose a tote whose description states the laptop size it holds.

### Will a Le Pliage tote hold a laptop?

The larger sizes usually will. Sizes M and L hold most 13 to 14 inch laptops, while size S is meant for daily essentials. Check the dimensions in the description before ordering, and use a laptop sleeve inside the tote to protect the computer from knocks.
