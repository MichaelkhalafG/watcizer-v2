A Just Cavalli watch is chosen for how it looks on the wrist as much as for telling the time. This page explains the 3 models we carry, how to choose the right tone and size, and how to wear and care for a fashion watch so it stays bright for years.

## The 3 models

The collection here has 3 women's watches. Two carry the Just Cavalli name and one carries the Roberto Cavalli name. All 3 are quartz, all 3 sit on stainless steel bracelets, and their cases range from 30 to 35 mm. The dials are white or silver, and the bracelets come in silver, gold, rose gold and two-tone combinations.

## Silver, gold or rose gold

Silver steel is the most versatile tone and suits cool skin tones, white gold jewellery and every colour of clothing. Gold looks warm and festive and pairs with gold jewellery and earthy colours. Rose gold sits between the 2, soft and modern, and flatters most skin tones. Two-tone models combine silver and gold, so they match both kinds of jewellery.

## Choosing the size

A 30 mm case is delicate and suits slim wrists and a jewellery look. A 35 mm case is more visible and easier to read, and suits a medium wrist or a bolder style. Measure your wrist just above the bone: under 15 cm, the smaller case usually looks best; above 16 cm, the larger case looks balanced.

## White and silver dials

White and silver dials are the most elegant choice for a fashion watch. They keep the face bright and easy to read, and they let the bracelet and case details take the attention. They also suit every occasion, from the office to a wedding, which makes them the safest choice if you own only 1 watch.

## Quartz for a dress watch

Quartz is the right movement for a fashion watch. It keeps excellent time, needs no winding, and runs for about 2 to 3 years on a battery. If you wear the watch only on special occasions, it will still show the right time when you pick it up, which an automatic watch would not.

## Fitting the bracelet

Stainless steel bracelets are shortened by removing links, which our stores in Arkadia Mall and Heliopolis do for you. Leave enough room to slide 1 finger under the bracelet, so the watch sits comfortably and does not mark the skin in summer. If you order online, our team can tell you how many links to remove from your wrist measurement.

## Caring for the watch

Put the watch on after your perfume and lotion, and take it off before showering or swimming; fashion watches resist splashes but are not made for water. Wipe the bracelet with a soft dry cloth after wearing. Store it in its box, away from direct sun and from other jewellery that could scratch it. Replace the battery every 2 to 3 years.

## Questions and answers

### Which Just Cavalli size suits a slim wrist?

The 30 mm model suits a slim wrist best, with a delicate, jewellery-like look. If your wrist measures more than about 16 cm, the 35 mm case looks more balanced. Both bracelets can be shortened in our stores, so the watch sits comfortably whichever size you choose.

### Can I wear a Just Cavalli watch in water?

Fashion watches like these resist splashes and hand washing, but they are not made for showering or swimming. Take the watch off before contact with water, perfume and lotion, and dry it if it gets wet. This protects the seals, the dial and the bracelet's finish for years.

### How often does the battery need changing?

About every 2 to 3 years. When the seconds hand starts jumping several seconds at a time, the battery is low. Our stores replace batteries and check the water seals at the same time, so the watch keeps its protection against splashes and dust.

### Does the watch come in a gift box?
