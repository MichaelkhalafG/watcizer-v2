A protector is cheap insurance for the most expensive part of a phone or a tablet. This guide explains how to identify your exact device, which version suits how you use it, and how to fit it cleanly on the first try.

## Find your exact model first

Protectors are cut to the millimetre. On an iPhone, open Settings, then General, then About, and read the model name. On a tablet, the model name is in the settings or on the back of the device. Then choose the product that names that exact model. The iPhone 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max all have different sizes and camera cut-outs.

## Clear or privacy?

Clear glass is best for photos, video and drawing on a tablet. Privacy glass narrows the viewing angle to about 30 degrees either side, so people beside you on public transport or in an office cannot read the screen. It looks slightly darker, so you may raise the brightness a little. Choose by where you use the device most.

## A clean first fit

Fit the protector in a small room with little air movement, ideally a bathroom after a hot shower, when dust settles. Wash your hands, clean the screen with the wet wipe, dry it with the cloth and lift remaining dust with the sticker. Place the protector using the frame or the camera cut-out, then press from the centre outwards. Small bubbles usually fade within 24 hours.

## Living with a case

Most protectors are cut slightly smaller than the screen so that a case does not lift their edges. With a thick case that has raised edges, check that the protector is described as case-friendly. The 2 work together: the case absorbs knocks on the corners and the back, the protector takes scratches and impacts on the face.

## The MagSafe card holder

The slim magnetic card holder attaches to the back of an iPhone 12 or later and carries a few cards, so you can leave a wallet at home for short trips. Take it off before wireless charging and keep magnetic-strip cards in their own slot.

## When to change a protector

Change it when it cracks, when an edge lifts or when scratches start to bother you. A cracked protector has done its job, but its sharp edges can catch a finger, so replace it soon. Many customers keep a spare at home, since 1 drop can break the first.

## Daily care

Clean the protector with a soft dry cloth or a lightly damp one. Avoid alcohol and household cleaners, which strip the oleophobic coating that keeps fingerprints away. Keep the device away from sand at the beach and from keys and coins in the same pocket. With care, a protector stays clear for 12 months or more.

## Common mistakes

Choosing the protector for a nearby model, such as the 17 instead of the 17 Pro, leaves an uncovered edge or a misplaced camera hole. Fitting it in a dusty room leaves white specks under the glass. Pressing a bubble hard in the first hour can trap dust; wait 24 hours, as most bubbles disappear by themselves.

## Questions and answers

### How do I know which protector fits my iPhone?

Open Settings, then General, then About, and read the model name. Choose the protector whose title names exactly that model, for example the iPhone 17 Pro or the 17 Pro Max. The 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max all differ in size, so a protector for one does not fit another.

### Do you have protectors for tablets?

Yes. The page includes a series of tablet screen protectors for popular tablet sizes. Each product description lists the tablet models it fits. If you are not sure which one matches your tablet, send us its model name on WhatsApp and we will tell you before you order.

### Will a privacy protector affect Face ID?

No. A good tempered glass protector, clear or privacy, leaves Face ID, the front camera and the touch response working normally. The privacy effect only darkens the screen when it is seen from the side. If touch seems uneven, a speck of dust under 1 area is the usual cause.

### Can I return a screen protector?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, as long as it is unopened in its sealed packaging; we pay the return shipping. A protector that has been fitted cannot be reused, so it cannot be returned. If you received the wrong model, tell us on WhatsApp and we arrange an exchange.
