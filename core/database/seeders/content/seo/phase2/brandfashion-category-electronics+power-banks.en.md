The best power bank is the one you actually carry. This guide sorts the 8 models on this page into 3 groups, magnetic, compact with cables, and high capacity, and helps you pick by phone, by day and by weight.

## Group 1: magnetic and wireless

Magnetic power banks attach to the back of an iPhone 12 or later with MagSafe magnets and charge it wirelessly, so there is no cable to carry. One model adds a fold-out kickstand, handy for video calls, and a built-in cable for a second device. The 3-in-1 magnetic model charges by magnet, by cable and through its own port. Wireless charging is a little slower than cable charging, but far more convenient.

The 20 thousand mAh model shows the remaining charge on an LCD display and charges at 22.5 W. ## Matching the power bank to your phone

For a MagSafe iPhone, a magnetic model is the most convenient. For an Android phone, choose a model with QC or AFC and a Type-C cable. For a phone that uses Lightning, check that the cable or port matches, or carry your own cable. The product title states the outputs of every model.

## Capacity and real charges

A power bank loses part of its energy as heat and in voltage conversion, so 10 thousand mAh gives roughly 2 charges of a typical phone, 20 thousand about 4, and 30 thousand about 6. Wireless charging loses a little more than cable charging. A tablet needs 2 to 3 times the energy of a phone.

## Weight in the pocket

A 10 thousand mAh model is about the weight of a phone and fits a pocket or a small bag. A 20 thousand model is roughly twice that, and the 30 thousand model belongs in a backpack. If you will carry it every day, choose the smallest capacity that gets you through your longest normal day.

## Recharging the power bank

Use a wall charger of 18 to 20 W with a good Type-C cable. A 10 thousand mAh model refills in a few hours; the 30 thousand model is best charged overnight. Many models allow pass-through charging, charging the power bank and the phone together, which is useful in a hotel with 1 socket.

## Flying with a power bank

Airlines ask passengers to keep power banks in hand luggage, not in checked bags, and set limits on capacity. The 10 and 20 thousand mAh models are common travel sizes; check the larger model against your airline's rules before you fly, because they vary between airlines and countries.

## Battery health

Do not leave a power bank empty or full for months. Store it at about half charge and top it up every 3 months. Keep it out of a hot car, where cabin temperatures in summer can pass 50 degrees. A swollen, cracked or very hot power bank must not be used.

## Questions and answers

### Does a magnetic power bank work with Android?

Magnetic wireless power banks are made for MagSafe iPhones, iPhone 12 and later. Android phones with wireless charging can charge if you hold them in place or use a magnetic ring, but the fit is not guaranteed. For Android, a cable model with QC or AFC is the better choice.

### Which power bank charges a tablet?

The 20 and 30 thousand mAh models are best for tablets, because a tablet needs 2 to 3 times the energy of a phone. Use a Type-C cable and check that the tablet accepts 18 W or more. The 30 thousand model can charge a tablet fully and still leave power for a phone.

### What do PD, QC and AFC mean?

They are fast-charging standards. PD, USB Power Delivery, is used by iPhones and many Android phones. QC, Quick Charge, is used by many Xiaomi, Oppo and other Android phones, and AFC, Adaptive Fast Charging, by Samsung. A power bank supporting all 3 fast charges almost any phone.
