A women's Guess watch is worn as much for its sparkle as for the time. With a range this wide, the size and the bracelet narrow the choice quickly, and the specifications on each product page answer the practical questions about water and glass. This guide starts with size, moves through bracelets and occasions, and ends with care, stock and ordering.

## Petite, classic or bold

Sizes on this page run from 28 to 40 mm. **Petite** cases of 28 to 32 mm look like jewellery and suit a slim wrist or a classic taste. **Classic** sizes of 34 to 37 mm, with 36 mm the most common of all, are balanced on most wrists of 14 to 16 centimetres. **Bold** sizes of 38 to 40 mm look modern and are easy to read at a glance.

## Steel bracelet watches

More than half of the women's Guess watches here are on steel bracelets, in gold, rose gold, silver or two-tone. **Constellation**, **Lily** and **G Twist** are typical: polished, bright and as much a bracelet as a watch. Steel lasts the longest and suits every season; it needs sizing by removing links, which any watch shop does in minutes.

## Crystals and glitter for occasions

For weddings, engagements and evenings, choose a model set with crystals or finished with glitter. **Afterglow**, **Crush** and **Bedazzle** carry crystals around the dial or on the markers, and the silver G Twist has a glitter finish that catches light at every movement. Wear them with simple jewellery, so the watch is the main sparkle.

## Silicone and rubber for summer

Silicone and rubber straps are light, cool and easy to rinse, which makes them ideal for summer, the beach café and travel. **Athena**, **Princess**, **Confetti**, **Swirl** and **Raven** pair rose-gold or gold cases with white, blue or multicoloured straps. A buckle strap fits almost any wrist without sizing, which helps when the watch is a gift.

## Mesh bracelets

A **mesh** bracelet is woven from fine steel threads and lies flat and supple on the wrist. **Maiden**, **Lenox** and some **Fame** models use it, in rose gold, gold and two-tone. Many mesh bracelets adjust with a sliding clasp rather than removable links, so you can set the length at home; the photos show how each one closes.

## Chronograph and multi-function

A few women's Guess models have sub-dials. A **chronograph** adds a stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown; a multi-function dial uses the sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale. Both give a sporty, detailed look; the product page names the functions of each model.

## Reading the water rating

The specifications may list water resistance in metres or in **ATM**: 3 ATM is 30 metres, 5 ATM is 50 metres and 10 ATM is 100 metres. A 30-metre watch is for splashes, such as rain and washing your hands. At 50 metres a short swim in a pool is fine; at 100 metres or more the sea is fine too. Take any watch off for a hot shower.

## Choosing the metal tone

**Rose gold** flatters warm skin tones and looks soft beside pearls. **Gold** is festive and matches yellow-gold rings and bangles. **Silver** suits cool skin tones and goes with white gold and silver jewellery. **Two-tone** steel mixes silver and gold, so it matches whatever jewellery you choose that morning.

## Wearing the watch with bracelets

A steel or mesh Guess watch stacks well with 1 or 2 thin bangles in the same metal tone. Wear the watch closest to the hand and the bangles above it, so they do not knock the glass. Leave crystal models on their own; their sparkle is enough, and a crowded wrist can scratch the stones.

## Choosing one as a gift

For a fiancée or a wife, a crystal model in the metal tone of her jewellery. For a student, a rose-gold watch on white silicone, cheerful and light. For a mother, a classic 36 mm steel watch with a white or silver dial. If you do not know her wrist size, a silicone strap or a mesh bracelet with a sliding clasp is the safest choice.

## Battery and care

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows. Put the watch on after perfume and hand cream, which dull plating and crystals, and keep it in its box rather than loose in a jewellery dish.

## Questions and answers

### What does the letter L in a Guess reference mean?

Guess references usually end in a letter that shows who the watch was designed for: L for ladies and G for gents. It helps when a title does not say women's or men's. Size matters more than the letter, though; a small men's model or a large women's one can suit either wrist.

### Which size is best for a slim wrist?

A petite case of 28 to 32 mm, or a 34 mm model on a mesh bracelet, which lies flat and closes with a sliding clasp. On a wrist below about 14 centimetres, a 36 mm case can still work if the bracelet is fine and the dial is light in colour.

### Will the gold or rose-gold colour fade?

The gold and rose-gold tones are plating over stainless steel. With normal care they last a long time; friction and chemicals such as perfume, creams, sea water and cleaning products are what wear them. Put the watch on after applying them and wipe it with a soft cloth after a hot day.

### Does a women's Guess watch have a warranty?

Where there is one, it covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Water damage, a broken glass and worn plating are not covered.
