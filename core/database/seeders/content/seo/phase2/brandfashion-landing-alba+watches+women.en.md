A women's Alba is a small, precise watch that keeps working quietly for years. Most of the range sits in the classic petite sizes that suited women's watches for generations, and the Japanese quartz movements need little more than a battery every few years. This guide explains what those petite sizes look like on the wrist, which details to check in the specifications and how to pick a dial, a strap and a gift.

## Petite sizes: 28 to 32 mm

Most women's Albas here measure **31** mm, with many at 28, 29, 30 and 32 mm. These are classic proportions: on a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 30 to 32 mm looks graceful and discreet, slipping under a fitted sleeve without catching. If you prefer a larger, modern look, a few models are bigger; check the case size on each page.

## Why small can be practical

A petite watch is light, so you forget you are wearing it, and it sits close to the wrist, away from door frames and desk edges. It pairs naturally with bracelets and rings, and it looks appropriate in the most formal settings. For anyone who wears a watch every day at work, a 30 to 32 mm **Alba** is a comfortable companion.

## Sapphire glass on many models

Many of our women's Albas list **sapphire** glass, which is far harder than mineral glass and resists scratches very well. It keeps the dial clear for years, even when the watch brushes against keys, handbag clasps and walls. Others list mineral glass, which is clear and handles everyday knocks well. Each product page names the glass.

## The Fashion collection

**Fashion** makes up a large part of the range: slim steel watches with dials in silver-white, black, blue, green, champagne and light pink, and several in **mother of pearl**, marked MOP. A shimmering MOP dial turns a simple steel watch into something that feels like jewellery.

## Active, Standard and Prestige

**Active** brings a sportier, fresher look; **Standard** is simple and practical, sometimes with luminous hands and markers that glow in the dark; **Prestige** is the dressier line, refined and quiet. The product title names the collection of every watch, so you can filter by the style you want.

## Steel bracelets

Most women's Albas are on **steel** bracelets, in silver or two-tone. Steel is durable, easy to clean and right for every season. Bracelets need sizing by removing links, which any watch shop does in minutes; for a petite watch, a well-sized bracelet that lets one finger slip under it looks neatest.

## Leather straps

About one in six models is on **leather**, sometimes in the same colour as the dial. Leather is soft and warm for the office and cooler months. Keep it dry, let it air after a hot day and replace it when it wears; a new strap gives the watch a fresh look.

## Water: reading the rating

The specifications show water resistance in metres or ATM. **5 ATM** or 50 metres, the most common here, allows rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool, but not the sea. 3 ATM or 30 metres means splashes only, and 10 ATM or 100 metres allows the sea. Leather straps should stay dry in any case.

## Choosing a dial

**Silver-white** is the most classic and easiest to match. **Black** looks sharp and modern, **blue** and green fresh, **champagne** warm next to gold jewellery, and **light pink** soft and feminine. A mother-of-pearl dial is the special choice for a gift or an evening.

## Silver or two-tone

**Silver** steel goes with white gold, silver and pearls. **Two-tone** steel, silver with gold, also matches yellow-gold rings and bangles, which makes it practical if you wear both metals or buy for someone whose taste you do not know.

## Japanese quartz

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside. A quartz watch keeps time even after weeks in a drawer, so it is always ready to wear.

## Wearing an Alba to work

For an office, a 30 to 32 mm Alba on steel with a silver-white or black dial is ideal: discreet, easy to read and comfortable at a keyboard all day. A two-tone bracelet adds warmth without looking flashy, and a **Prestige** model adds a refined touch for meetings.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 31 mm watch too small?

Not for a classic women's watch. 31 mm is a traditional size that looks graceful on most wrists of about 14 to 16 centimetres and sits neatly under a sleeve. If you prefer the larger look of modern fashion watches, choose one of the bigger models on the page or compare sizes with a watch you own.

### What is the advantage of sapphire glass?

Sapphire is much harder than mineral glass, so it resists scratches from keys, clasps and everyday knocks far better and keeps the dial clear for years. It can still break under a very hard impact, but for daily wear it is the most durable glass a watch can have.

### Can I wear a women's Alba in the pool?

Only if its page lists 5 ATM or 50 metres or more, and then for a short swim. A 3 ATM or 30-metre watch is for splashes only, and a leather strap should always stay dry. Rinse the watch with fresh water after the pool and dry it with a soft cloth.
