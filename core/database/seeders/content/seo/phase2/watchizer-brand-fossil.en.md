Fossil was founded in Texas in 1984 and built its reputation on vintage-inspired watches at fair prices, sold in a tin box that many owners still keep. Today the Fossil group is one of the largest fashion-watch makers in the world and also produces watches under licence for other well-known labels. Its own Fossil line keeps the warm, slightly retro style it started with: brown leather, warm dial colours and clear, easy-to-read faces. This guide explains the range we carry.

## The men's lines: Grant, Nate, Townsman and more

The line name appears in most Fossil titles, and each line has a clear character. **Grant** is the classic: a round case, Roman numerals and a chronograph, usually on brown leather. **Townsman** is similar in spirit but leans more formal, with cleaner dials and slimmer hands. **Nate** is the big, rugged one, with a thick case, bold numerals and often a black or gunmetal finish. **Neutra**, **Dillinger** and **Wakefield** fill the gaps, from minimal dials to field-watch styles.

Most men's Fossil watches we carry measure 44 millimetres across. That is a generous size that reads as confident without being oversized, and it works well on an average wrist. For a slimmer wrist, look at the three-hand models, which are thinner than the chronographs and sit closer to the skin. The [men's Fossil watches page](/brand/fossil/men) puts them all side by side.

## Automatic, skeleton and open heart

Eleven of our Fossil watches are automatic, and they are worth seeing in person in our stores. An automatic movement winds itself as you move your wrist, so it needs no battery. A **skeleton** dial is cut away so you can see the gears and the balance wheel turning; an **open-heart** dial shows just a window over the balance wheel, which is a quieter way to show the mechanism.

Two things are worth knowing before you choose one. First, an automatic keeps running for about a day and a half after you take it off; if it stops, wind it with the crown or simply wear it and set the time. Second, it is a little less precise than quartz — a few seconds a day is normal — which is the price of a mechanism you can watch.

## Leather or steel

Leather is part of the Fossil identity, and more than a third of the watches we list come on it — about 1 in 3 — mostly in brown and black. A leather strap softens with wear and takes the shape of your wrist, but it does not like water or heavy sweat; give it a day off in summer, and keep a steel or silicone watch for the beach. Steel bracelets are more durable and easier to clean, and we size them for free in our stores while the watch is under warranty.

A useful detail: many Fossil leather straps use a standard width and quick-release pins, so you can change the look of a watch with a second strap in a few seconds.

## Chronograph or three-hand

Around a third of our Fossil watches are chronographs, with 3 small sub-dials and 2 pushers beside the crown that start, stop and reset a stopwatch. They look busy and sporty, and they are the natural choice if you like a technical face or actually time things — a run, a parking meter, the oven. A three-hand watch, with only hours, minutes and seconds, is thinner, lighter and easier to read at a glance, and it sits better under a shirt cuff. If the watch is for work and formal events, choose three hands; if it is for weekends and you like detail, choose the chronograph. Both kinds are on the same product pages, and the title says which one it is.

If you cannot decide, try both sizes in our stores: a 44 mm chronograph is often heavier than you expect, and that a three-hand model of the same diameter looks smaller and lighter on the wrist.

## Women's Fossil watches

The women's range is smaller and leans towards rose gold, gold and silver steel, mostly in 34 to 38 millimetre cases with a few tiny 23 to 26 mm models for those who like a jewellery-sized watch. Lines like **Carlie**, **Jacqueline** and **Riley** combine slim cases with mother-of-pearl or crystal-set dials. They suit daily wear at work and are popular as gifts for mothers and sisters. For a very small wrist, the 34 mm rose-gold models are the most delicate choice, and a mesh bracelet adjusts to any size by sliding the clasp along it, with no links to remove. ## Caring for a Fossil watch

The Fossil watches we list are rated for 50 metres of water resistance, which means washing your hands and rain are fine but swimming with a leather strap is not. Keep an automatic away from strong magnets, such as speakers, magnetic bag clasps and tablet covers, which can make it run fast. Clean a steel case with a soft cloth and a little water, and let a leather strap dry naturally, away from direct sun. If you put an automatic away for weeks, set it again when you take it out; that is normal and not a fault.

## Questions and answers

### Does an automatic Fossil need a battery?

No. An automatic movement is powered by a spring that is wound by the motion of your wrist, through a weighted rotor you can often see on the back. It never needs a battery. If you leave it off for more than a day or two it stops; give the crown about 20 turns or wear it for a while, then set the time and date.

### Which is better as a gift, quartz or automatic?

For someone who wants a watch that is always right and needs no attention, quartz is the better choice. For someone who enjoys mechanisms, or who will wear the watch every day, an automatic with a skeleton or open-heart dial makes a more memorable gift. Both styles come with a warranty stated on the product page.

### Can I change the strap of a Fossil watch?

Usually yes. Many Fossil models use a standard strap width, printed in the specifications on the product page, and some have quick-release pins that let you change the strap without tools. Steel bracelets can be swapped for leather, too. If you are unsure, bring the watch to our stores in Cairo and we will check the width for you.

### What does the warranty cover?

It does not cover the strap's normal wear, a broken glass, damage from a fall or water above the rating. Keep the card and the invoice together. The [warranty page](/warranty) gives the full list.
