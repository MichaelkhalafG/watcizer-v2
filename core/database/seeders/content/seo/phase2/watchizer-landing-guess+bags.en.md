A Guess bag is an easy way to add the brand's playful, glamorous style to an everyday outfit. The designs range from quiet black shoulder bags to quilted off-white crossbody bags and bright pink totes covered in the logo. This guide explains the logo, the named lines we carry and the shapes, then helps you choose a colour and care for the bag.

## The 4G logo and the monogram

The **4G** pattern is four interlocking G letters, repeated across the bag; it is the most recognisable Guess design. On some bags it is printed in two tones of brown or grey, on others it is embossed into a single colour, so it shows only as a texture. If you want a bag that everyone recognises as Guess, choose a 4G print; if you prefer something discreet, choose a plain or embossed model with a small metal logo.

## Noelle

**Noelle** bags pair the 4G print with clean shapes and plain trims. We carry it as a black crossbody bag and, as Noelle II, a grey tote. The crossbody bag is the practical choice for errands and travel, small enough to keep in front of you; the tote holds a day's worth of things for work or university.

## Quilted styles: Assia and Fedana

**Assia** and **Fedana** use quilted stitching, which gives the surface a soft, padded look and catches the light. Both come in off-white here: Assia as a crossbody bag and Fedana as a shoulder bag. Quilting looks dressy enough for a wedding or a dinner and still works with jeans, which makes these two the most versatile evening bags on the page.

## Totes: Latona, Open Road and Cessily

Totes are the bags for a full day. **Latona** is a mini tote in black, neat and structured, for the essentials. **Open Road** is a large tote in a bright pink with an embossed monogram, roomy enough for a scarf, a water bottle and a small notebook. **Cessily** is a tweed tote in a red mix of colours, the most textured bag we carry, with a soft, wintery look.

## Kerima and the bamboo-style handle

**Kerima** is a beige 4G handbag with a curved handle in a bamboo style, a classic detail borrowed from vintage handbags. It is carried by hand or in the crook of the arm rather than on the shoulder, and it turns a simple dress or a plain shirt into a finished outfit.

## Kasinta and the mini bags

**Kasinta** is a white mini bag with a top handle and a logo, small enough for a phone, a card holder and keys. Mini bags suit evenings, weddings and short outings, when you carry little. If you need room for a wallet, sunglasses and a power bank as well, choose a shoulder bag or a tote instead.

## Downtown Chic

**Downtown Chic** is a fuchsia shoulder bag with a magnetic flap closure, which opens with one hand and closes by itself. It is the boldest colour on the page, a good choice for a second bag that brightens a black or white outfit.

## Crossbody, shoulder or handbag

A **crossbody** bag is worn across the body on a long strap, keeping your hands free and the bag in sight, which makes it the safest in a crowd or on public transport. A **shoulder** bag sits under the arm and is quicker to open. A **handbag** with a top handle looks the most formal. The single **backpack** we carry, in black, spreads the weight over 2 shoulders for long days.

## Colours: black, coal, latte and pink

Black is by far the most common colour here; it goes with everything and hides marks. **Coal** is a softer black-grey, **latte** a warm light brown, and **beige** and **off-white** look fresh in summer. **Pink** and **fuchsia** are the playful choices. For one bag that matches every outfit, black or coal; for a second, latte or pink.

## Looking after a Guess bag

Most Guess bags use coated or faux-leather materials; the product page names the material of each. Wipe the bag with a soft, slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol or a strong cleaner, and let it dry away from direct sun. Store it stuffed with paper in its **dust bag** so it keeps its shape, and keep light-coloured bags away from dark jeans, which can transfer dye.

## One bag for each part of the week

If you are building a small collection, three bags cover almost everything. For work or university, a tote such as **Noelle II** or Latona, which keeps papers and a water bottle upright. For errands and travel, a black crossbody bag with a zip. For evenings and weddings, a quilted Assia or a Kasinta mini bag. Start with the one you will use most often, which for most women is the crossbody bag.

## Questions and answers

### Are Guess bags made of real leather?

Most Guess bags use coated fabric or faux leather, which is lighter than leather, resists light rain and wipes clean easily. The product page names the material of each bag. Faux leather prefers a dry cloth and shade; keep it away from long hours of direct sun and from heat, which can mark the surface.

### What does 4G mean?

4G is the name of the Guess pattern made of four interlocking G letters. It can be printed in two tones or embossed into a single colour. A bag described as 4G or monogram carries this pattern, and the photos on the product page show which version it is.

### Which Guess bag is best for every day?

A black or coal crossbody bag with a zip. It holds a phone, a wallet and keys, it stays in front of you in a crowd, and black hides marks. If you carry more, a tote such as Noelle II is the better daily bag, and a mini bag is best kept for evenings.
