Guess watches are chosen for their look first: big, confident cases for men, sparkling and colourful designs for women, all at a fashion price. With dozens of models on one page, the easiest way to choose is by what the dial does, then by size, strap and colour. This guide covers those choices across the whole Guess range we carry and points you to the men's and women's pages when you know which one you want.

## Men's and women's at a glance

About three quarters of our Guess watches are men's models, mostly between 42 and 46 mm, sporty and full of detail. The women's models are fewer and smaller, mostly 36 to 40 mm, in rose gold, gold and silver with crystals. The [women's Guess watches](/brand/guess/watches/women) page gathers them, and the [Guess brand page](/brand/guess) shows bags and wallets too.

## Three-hand and day-date

The simplest dials show only the hours, the minutes and the seconds, sometimes with a date window. **Connoisseur** adds a day-and-date display on a black steel case at 42 mm, a classic layout for the office. These quiet dials are the easiest to read and the most versatile under a shirt cuff.

## Multi-function dials

A multi-function dial uses small sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale, without a stopwatch. **Axle** is a black steel example at 45 mm, and many models in the **Phoenix** and **Dashboard** lines work the same way. You get the busy, sporty look of a chronograph with no buttons to learn.

## Chronographs

A chronograph adds a stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown. We carry it in **Anchor**, **Caliber**, **Fleet**, **Octane**, **Surge** and **Pinnacle**, and in **Jolt**, a blue and rose-gold model at 48 mm. Chronographs are the most technical-looking Guess watches; the product page names the functions of each one.

## A skeleton-style dial

**Legend** is the one skeleton-style watch on the page: its open dial shows part of the mechanism behind it, on a black case with a silicone strap at 44 mm. It runs on a quartz movement, as its product page states, so it needs no winding. It is a bold choice for anyone who likes to see what is inside a watch, and a good conversation piece on a night out.

## The largest cases

Most men's Guess watches here measure 42 to 46 mm, but a few go further. **Formula** measures 47 mm, Jolt 48 mm and **Genesis** 52 mm, the biggest watch we carry from the brand. These suit a broad wrist of about 19 centimetres or more, or anyone who wants a watch that is seen from across the room.

## Lines that list 50 metres

Several men's models list a 50-metre water rating on their product pages, including **Dashboard**, **Resistance**, **Vinyl** and some Phoenix models. That is enough for rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool, but not for the sea or diving. Where the page lists 30 metres, keep the watch to splashes only.

## Two-tone and gunmetal

Two-tone watches mix silver and gold, which lets one watch match both kinds of jewellery. **Vinyl** and Dashboard come in two-tone versions, and **Odyssey** pairs a blue dial with a gunmetal case, a dark grey steel that looks modern and hides small marks better than polished silver.

## Leather for a dressier look

Leather straps make a Guess watch look warmer and more classic. **Continental** comes on leather in gold with black and in rose gold with brown, and **Delancy** pairs rose gold with black leather at 44 mm. Leather prefers dry days, so keep it away from water and sweat, and let it air after a hot day.

## Rubber, silicone and steel

Rubber and silicone straps are light, cool and easy to rinse, which makes them the most common choice for sporty Guess models. Steel bracelets look dressier and last longer; they need sizing, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty. Mesh bracelets, on several women's models, are supple and adjust with a clasp.

## Choosing the size

For men, a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres suits 42 to 44 mm, and a broader wrist carries 46 mm or more. For women, 36 to 38 mm is balanced on a wrist of 14 to 16 centimetres, and 40 mm looks bold. Measure your wrist with a tape and compare the case size with a watch you already own.

## Battery and care

The Guess watches we carry run on **quartz** movements, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows, keep perfume off gold-tone plating, and never press the pushers of a chronograph under water.

## Questions and answers

### Are Guess watches good for everyday wear?

Yes, for normal daily life. They have stainless steel cases and quartz movements, which are accurate and need little care beyond a new battery every few years. Treat them as fashion watches: take them off for heavy sport and the sea, and keep the plating away from perfume and creams.

### Which Guess watch is best for a couple?

Choose the same metal tone for both: a rose-gold Continental for him and a rose-gold women's model with crystals for her, or two silver steel watches for a calmer look. Matching tones look planned without being identical, and both pages show the colour of every model clearly.

### Can a woman wear a men's Guess watch?

Yes, if she likes a large watch. A 42 mm men's model on silicone sits closer to the wrist and looks smaller than a steel bracelet of the same size. On a wrist below about 15 centimetres, a 46 mm case will overhang, so a women's model of 38 to 40 mm is the better choice.

### What warranty comes with a Guess watch?

Each product page states the warranty length and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and the invoice together. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains what is and is not covered.

### Can I exchange it for another model?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange.
