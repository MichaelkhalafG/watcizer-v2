## Tommy Hilfiger, the backbone

Tommy Hilfiger supplies more than 500 pieces: bags for women, crossbody bags and backpacks for men, wallets with the TH logo or the brand's grid, and belt and wallet gift sets. The red, white and navy details make the brand easy to recognise, and its sizes and prices make it a natural first choice for a gift.

## Calvin Klein and Guess

Calvin Klein brings minimal black leather, the CK monogram and some of the most popular belt and wallet sets in the shop. Guess offers colourful logo bags and wallets for women at friendly prices, with gold hardware and monogram prints. Between them they cover the quiet and the playful ends of the department.

## Coach, Tory Burch and Le Pliage

For women's leather goods, Coach offers Signature canvas bags, card cases and phone wallets, often with florals and fruit prints. Tory Burch brings structured leather shoulder and crossbody bags with the double-T logo. Le Pliage adds its folding nylon totes in sizes from S to L. These 3 are the department's most requested bag brands.

## Emporio Armani, Lacoste and Hugo Boss

Emporio Armani contributes bags, totes and small leather goods with Italian styling. Lacoste offers wallets, belts and bracelets with the crocodile logo for men. Hugo Boss and Armani Exchange add men's bags and wallets in black and navy. These suit men who want their accessories to match a watch from the same brand.

## Men's and women's

The department is split almost evenly, with slightly more than half of the pieces for men, a little under half for women, and about 80 unisex pieces. Men's pieces are mostly wallets, belts and crossbody bags; women's pieces are mostly bags, card cases and jewellery. The gender filter keeps each list focused.

## Colours

Black is the most common colour by far, then brown, white, blue and beige. Black accessories are the safest single choice. Brown suits casual outfits and leather watch straps. Lighter colours look fresh in summer. Coach and Guess add pinks, prints and two-tone combinations for women who want colour.

## Matching accessories to a watch

Match the metal first: gold hardware with a gold watch, silver with steel. Match the leather next: a brown watch strap with a brown wallet and belt. Then match the style: a sports watch with a nylon bag or backpack, a dress watch with structured leather. The [watches page](/subtypes/watches) has thousands of watches to pair with.

## Care for leather and canvas

Wipe leather with a dry soft cloth and keep it out of rain and strong sun. Clean coated canvas with a slightly damp cloth. Store bags stuffed with paper in their dust bags. Do not overfill wallets. With these habits, a leather accessory lasts 5 years or more.
