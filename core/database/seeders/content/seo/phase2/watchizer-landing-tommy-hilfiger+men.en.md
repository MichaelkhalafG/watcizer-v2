What a man needs from his accessories changes as his life changes: a student wants something tough and casual, a groom something polished, a father something that lasts. Tommy Hilfiger's men's range at Watchizer is wide enough to follow those stages. This guide goes through six of them and the watch, wallet, belt and bag that suit each, then covers sizes, straps and care.

## What the men's range covers

There are about **337** watches, 40 wallets, 74 belts, sets and other accessories, and 51 bags on this page, and almost all are in stock. The watches are mostly 44 mm, with 46 mm models for larger wrists. Wallets are mostly bifolds; bags are mostly crossbody and messenger styles. Most watches are rated 50 m.

## The student

A student needs a watch that survives lectures, football and weekends. A **rubber**-strap watch in navy or black is light, easy to clean and relaxed; 53 men's models here are on rubber or silicone. Add a slim bifold wallet and a small crossbody bag that keeps a phone, keys and earbuds in one place on campus.

## The first job

The first job calls for a step up. Choose a watch on a **stainless steel** bracelet, with a blue or black dial, which looks smart with a shirt and a jacket. Pair it with a black leather belt and a bifold wallet in black or brown. A messenger bag carries a notebook and a tablet to meetings and looks more grown-up than a backpack.

## The groom

On his wedding day and the weeks around it, a man wants something polished. A watch with a gold-tone or two-tone case on a steel bracelet, or a black dial on black leather, looks right with a suit. A boxed **wallet-and-belt set** in black is a thoughtful gift from the bride or the family, ready to give as it arrives.

## The father

For a father, comfort and reliability matter. A **leather**-strap watch with a clear, uncluttered dial is easy to read and comfortable all day; 92 men's models here are on leather. A brown leather belt and a matching bifold wallet complete a classic look he will wear for years.

## The traveller

A man who travels needs things that keep him organised. Wear a steel watch with a clear dial and a 50 m rating, carry a **crossbody** bag in front of you for a passport, cards and a phone, and choose a wallet with enough card slots for travel cards and currencies. Keep the belt simple, so security checks are quick.

## The sportsman

For training, the gym or the beach, rubber and silicone straps cope with sweat and water better than leather or steel. Choose a **sporty** dial in black, navy or with a red accent. Check the water rating: 50 m (5 ATM) allows a short pool swim, 30 m means splashes only and 100 m or more is fine for the sea.

## The signature stripe

The navy, red and white **stripe** is Tommy Hilfiger's signature, and it appears on watch straps, dials, wallets and bags across the range. If you like the brand to be recognisable, choose pieces with the stripe. If you prefer a quieter look, choose plain black, navy or brown pieces with a small logo.

## Watch sizes

Most men's watches here are **44** mm, which suits an average to large wrist; 46 mm and above suit a large wrist or a man who likes a bold watch. On a wrist below about 17 cm, choose a 40 to 43 mm model. A chronograph looks larger than a three-hand watch of the same size because its dial is busier.

## Steel, leather or rubber

About **135** men's watches are on steel bracelets, 92 on leather and 53 on rubber or silicone. Steel is the most durable and smart; leather is warm and classic but should stay dry; rubber is for sport and water. We size steel bracelets free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty.

## Wallets

Most of the men's wallets are **bifolds**, the classic shape that folds once and fits a trouser pocket, with card slots and a note section. They come in black, brown and navy, many with the brand's flag logo or stripe. A slim bifold keeps the pocket flat; a fuller one holds more cards and receipts.

## Belts and gift sets

The belts and sets include leather belts with metal buckles and boxed **sets** that pair a wallet with a belt, some with a card holder or key chain. Because the pieces match in leather and colour, a set takes the guesswork out of a gift. Check the belt length on the product page against his trouser size.

## Bags

The men's bags are mostly compact **crossbody** bags and messengers, with hand bags and clutches, several in gift boxes. A crossbody bag carries the daily essentials hands-free; a messenger takes a tablet and papers. The [men's bags page](/brand/tommy-hilfiger/bags/men) explains every shape and size in detail.

## Caring for watches and leather

Wipe a steel bracelet with a soft, dry **cloth** after a hot day, and rinse a rubber strap after sport. Keep leather straps, belts and wallets away from water, perfume and direct sun, and let a wallet dry flat if it gets wet. Change a quartz watch's battery as soon as it starts losing time.
