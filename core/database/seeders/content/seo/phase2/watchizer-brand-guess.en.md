Guess was founded in Los Angeles in 1981 by the Marciano brothers, and it made its name with jeans and bold advertising before moving into accessories. Its watches kept that confidence: they are designed to be seen, with large cases, strong colours and plenty of detail on the dial. They run on quartz movements, which keeps them accurate and easy to own. This guide covers the watches first, then the bags and wallets we carry.

## Men's Guess watches: bold and sporty

Most men's Guess watches in our range are large: the most common case size is 46 millimetres, followed by 44 and 42 mm. They are built to make an impression, with multi-function dials that show the day, the date and a 24-hour scale, and in some models a working chronograph. Named lines in the titles include **Phoenix**, with a sporty multi-function dial; **Dashboard**, inspired by a car's instrument panel; **Poseidon** and **Fleet**, with a diver's look; and **Continental** and **Headline**, a little quieter for daily wear.

Black is by far the most common colour, on its own or with gold-tone details, and many models come on a silicone strap. Silicone is soft, light and comfortable in the heat, which is why it suits a watch worn all day in an Egyptian summer. The steel-bracelet versions are heavier and dressier. The [men's Guess watches page](/brand/guess/men) lets you filter by strap and colour.

## Women's Guess watches: crystals and rose gold

The women's models are smaller — mostly 36 to 40 millimetres, a few at 31 mm — and the look moves from sporty to sparkling. Gold-tone and rose-gold steel lead the range, often with crystals around the dial or on the bracelet. Lines such as **Twist**, **Fame**, **Athena** and **Lily** show the brand's playful side: twisted bezels, mesh bracelets and dials that catch the light. They make popular gifts because they look like jewellery and need no special care beyond what a bracelet needs. The [women's Guess watches page](/brand/guess/watches/women) shows them all together.

## How to compare two Guess watches

Start with the case size, because Guess sizes vary more than most brands: a 46 mm men's case and a 42 mm one look like different watches on the wrist. Then look at the strap. Silicone needs no sizing and can be rinsed; a steel bracelet has to be sized, which we do for free in our stores while the watch is under warranty. Finally, check the water resistance written on the product page. Many Guess models are rated at 50 metres, which covers rain, splashes and washing your hands but not diving; the pushers of a chronograph should never be pressed under water.

## Guess handbags

The Guess bags we carry are women's bags, and most of them are shoulder bags, crossbody bags and totes in the brand's two signature looks: the all-over **4G** logo print and the quilted finish with gold-tone hardware. Black is the most common colour, then brown, with latte, coal, beige and a few lighter shades for summer. The [Guess bags page](/brand/guess/bags) lists them with photos from several sides and the measurements in centimetres.

When you choose a bag, think about how you will carry it. A crossbody bag keeps your hands free and is the safest in a crowd, because it sits in front of you. A shoulder bag holds more and looks smarter for work. A tote is the most practical if you carry a laptop or a water bottle, so check the width on the product page against what you need to fit inside.

When the bag arrives, check it before you take the tags off: open and close every zip, look at the stitching where the handles meet the body, and make sure the strap clips hold. Keep the tags on until you are sure, because a return needs the bag unused and in its original packaging with its tags. If you find a fault in the stitching or a zip, take a photo and send it to customer service on WhatsApp with your order number, and we arrange an exchange.

## Looking after Guess pieces

Keep perfume, hand cream and sun cream away from both the watches and the bags; they dull the gold-tone plating and can stain the logo print. Wipe a silicone strap with a damp cloth when it collects dust. Store a bag stuffed with paper and away from direct sun, which fades the lighter colours, and never leave it in a parked car in summer: on a 35-degree day the inside of a car gets far hotter, and the heat can soften the coating.

## Questions and answers

### Are Guess watches only for young people?

No, although their bold style is popular with younger buyers. The quieter lines, such as Continental and Headline for men or the slim steel women's models, look right at any age and at work. If you want something more discreet, choose a smaller case, a black or silver dial and a steel bracelet rather than a bright silicone strap.

### Do I need to size a Guess silicone strap?

No. Silicone straps fasten with a buckle and a row of holes, like a belt, so they fit most wrists straight from the box. Only steel and mesh bracelets need sizing. For those, bring the watch to one of our two stores in Cairo, or ask any watch shop; we size them for free while the watch is under warranty.

### What warranty does a Guess watch have?

Each product page states how long the warranty is and who provides it, and the same is printed on the card in the box. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and your invoice together. Scratches, a broken glass and water damage beyond the rating are not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### How do I know a Guess bag is the size I want?

Each bag's product page lists its width, height and depth in centimetres, and the photos show it from several angles. Measure a bag you already own and compare the numbers. If you have a question about a particular bag, send our customer service a message on WhatsApp with the product link before you order.

### Can I return a bag or a watch if I change my mind?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, and we pay the return shipping. The item has to be unused, in its original packaging, with its tags still attached. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund is made by the payment method you used once the item reaches us.
