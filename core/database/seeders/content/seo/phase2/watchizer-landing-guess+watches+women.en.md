A women's Guess watch sits between jewellery and a fashion accessory: it tells the time, but above all it adds shine and colour to the wrist. The range we carry is built on a few clear choices, the metal tone, the amount of sparkle, the size and the strap, and the guide below walks through each one, line by line, so you can find the watch that matches your style.

## Rose gold leads

More than half of our women's Guess watches are in **rose gold**, a soft pink-gold tone that flatters most skin tones and looks warm next to gold or pearl jewellery. Gold follows, then silver. If you already wear rose-gold rings or a rose-gold bracelet, this is the easiest tone to match; if your jewellery is white gold or silver, choose a silver model such as Lily or Crush.

## G Twist

**G Twist** plays with the Guess G in its design and is one of the most recognisable women's lines from the brand. We carry it in two versions at 40 mm: a silver model with a glitter finish, and a gold model set with crystals. Both are on steel, and both look as much like a bracelet as a watch.

## Crystals: Afterglow, Crush, Lily and Bedazzle

Several of our women's Guess watches are set with **crystals** around the dial or on the markers. **Afterglow** is rose gold at 36 mm, **Lily** is silver at 36 mm, **Bedazzle** is rose gold at 40 mm, and **Crush** is silver at 42 mm, the largest women's model we carry. Crystals catch the light at every movement of the wrist, which makes these the watches for weddings, engagements and evenings out.

## Fame: the smallest

**Fame** is the most delicate line here, at 31 millimetres. We carry it in gold on a fine mesh bracelet with crystals and in gold on black leather. A 31 mm case reads like a piece of jewellery and suits a slim wrist or anyone who prefers a classic, discreet watch.

## Mesh bracelets: Lenox and Maiden

A **mesh** bracelet is woven from fine steel threads, so it is supple, light and sits flat around the wrist. **Lenox** pairs silver and gold at 38 mm, and **Maiden** is rose gold at 36 mm. Many mesh bracelets adjust with a sliding clasp rather than removable links; the product page shows how each one closes.

## Silicone in white and rose gold

For a sportier, summery look, **Confetti**, **Raven** and **Princess** pair a rose-gold case with a white silicone strap, and **Swirl** follows the same idea. **Athena** combines rose gold with blue silicone at 39 mm. Silicone is light, cool in the heat and fastens with a simple buckle, so it suits almost any wrist without sizing.

## Constellation and the steel classics

**Constellation** is a rose-gold steel watch at 38 mm, with the clean, polished look of a classic dress watch. Steel bracelets like this one last the longest and look the most formal; they need sizing by removing links, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty.

## Choosing the size

Most of our women's Guess watches measure 36 to 40 mm. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 36 or 38 mm looks balanced and feminine, while 40 mm looks modern and bold. A 31 mm Fame suits a very slim wrist or a jewellery look, and the 42 mm Crush is for anyone who likes a large, eye-catching watch.

## Water and care

Check the water rating on each product page before you wear the watch near water. A 30-metre rating means splashes only: rain and washing your hands are fine, swimming is not. A 50-metre rating also allows a short swim in a pool, but never the sea. Put the watch on after perfume and hand cream, which dull plating and crystals, and wipe it with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day.

## Battery and accuracy

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside, and store the watch in its box rather than loose in a jewellery dish, where harder pieces can scratch the crystals and the glass.

## Questions and answers

### Are the crystals on a Guess watch diamonds?

No. They are crystals, cut and set to catch the light like small stones, which is what keeps these watches at a fashion price. They are fixed firmly, but they can loosen after a hard knock, so take the watch off for sport, housework and anything that might strike the wrist.

### Which size suits a woman best?

For most women, 36 to 38 mm is the balanced choice, easy to read and elegant. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, 40 mm looks modern and bold. If you prefer a jewellery look or have a very slim wrist, choose the 31 mm Fame instead.

### Will the rose-gold colour fade?

The rose-gold and gold tones are plating over stainless steel. With normal care they last a long time; what wears them is friction and chemicals such as perfume, creams, sea water and cleaning products. Put the watch on after applying them, and wipe it after a hot day.

### What warranty comes with a women's Guess watch?

Each product page states the warranty length and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and the invoice together. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains what is and is not covered.

### Can I exchange it for another model?
