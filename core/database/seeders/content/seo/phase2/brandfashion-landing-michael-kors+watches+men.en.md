Michael Kors built its name on women's watches, and many of the men's models in our range are bought by women, as gifts or to match their own. That is useful to know, because it explains the style: polished, confident and a little glamorous, even on a sporty chronograph. This guide helps you choose by build, strap and colour, and explains the features you will see in the specifications.

## A look built on shine

Men's Michael Kors watches are not quiet tool watches. Polished steel bezels, crisp applied markers, gold-tone and gunmetal finishes and a small **MK** logo give them the brand's signature gloss. If you like a watch that catches the light at dinner or in photos, this is the right range; if you prefer a matte, understated look, choose a black or gunmetal model on rubber, which is the most discreet option here.

## Choosing by your build

These watches are large, so match the size to your frame rather than only your wrist. The most common size is 44 millimetres, followed by 45 and 48 mm, with 42 and 43 mm models for slimmer builds. As a guide, a wrist under about 17 centimetres suits 42 to 44 mm; 17 to 19 centimetres suits 44 to 46 mm; broader wrists and taller builds carry 48 mm naturally. A watch that fits your frame looks intentional; one that is too big looks borrowed.

## Steel, silicone, rubber or leather

**Steel** bracelets are the most common and the dressiest, and they last the longest; they need sizing, usually by removing 2 to 6 links. **Silicone** and **rubber** straps are the summer and sport choice, light, cool and easy to rinse, with a buckle that fits almost any wrist. **Leather** straps are fewer, and they give a chronograph a warmer, more classic feel for the office and evenings. If you buy one watch for every day, steel is the safest; if you want comfort in the heat, silicone.

## Colours and finishes

Black and blue dials lead, with silver, champagne and grey beside them. Cases come in plain steel, **gold tone** for a dressier, more festive look, and **gunmetal**, a dark grey that reads modern and technical. A few models add a bright accent in turquoise, red or orange. Gold-tone cases look best with dark clothes and a matching belt buckle; gunmetal goes with almost anything, including denim and black.

## The named lines

**Dylan** is the boldest, a large chronograph often on silicone. **Lexington** is the dressier chronograph, usually on a polished steel bracelet. **Bayville** and **Saunder** are further men's designs, each with its own dial layout; the product photos show the differences side by side. Searching a line name in the box above shows every colour of it, the quickest way to compare.

## Chronograph or multi-function

Most models here are **chronographs**, with 2 pushers that start, stop and reset a stopwatch. Some are **multi-function**, using the sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale instead, without a stopwatch. The product page names the functions. If you will actually time things, choose a chronograph; if you only like the look, either type works.

## With a suit or with jeans

The same watch can work with very different clothes if you change the strap or the setting. With a suit, choose a steel or leather model with a calm black, blue or silver dial, and let the cuff cover half the case. With jeans and a T-shirt, a silicone strap or a colourful accent is perfectly at home. For smart-casual, such as chinos and a shirt, a gunmetal or black chronograph on steel bridges both. If you wear one watch all 7 days of the week, steel with a dark dial is the most adaptable.

If you like variety without buying a new watch, an extra silicone strap for your steel watch gives you a different summer look at little cost.

## Water and care

Most models list 50 metres of water resistance on the product page, enough for rain, washing your hands and a short pool swim, but not for the sea or diving; never press the pushers under water. Rinse silicone and steel with fresh water after sweat or chlorine, and keep perfume off gold-tone cases, which can dull. Change the battery as soon as the watch slows, usually every 2 to 3 years.

## Questions and answers

### Which men's Michael Kors size suits most men?

44 millimetres. It looks modern on a wrist of about 17 to 19 centimetres, which covers most men. Slimmer wrists suit 42 or 43 mm; broad wrists can carry 48 mm. If you are unsure, compare the case size with a watch he already wears.

### Is a gold-tone watch easy to wear?

It is dressier than plain steel and looks best with dark clothes, a black or brown belt and a matching buckle. For everyday wear, plain steel or gunmetal is easier. A gold-tone chronograph is a striking second watch for evenings and occasions.

### Can I swim with these watches?

Models rated at 50 metres can handle a short swim in a pool, but not the sea or diving. Never press the chronograph pushers under water, and rinse the watch with fresh water afterwards. The rating is on each product page.

### Do the watches come with a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains the details.

### Can I exchange the watch for another size?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange.
