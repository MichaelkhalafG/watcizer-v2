Calvin Klein opened his New York house in 1968 and turned restraint into a signature: the brand's accessories are recognisable precisely because they do so little, with clean shapes, neutral colours and a short, confident logo. Its younger line, Calvin Klein Jeans, adds bolder logos and monogram prints for a more casual mood. This guide is organised by who you are shopping for, so you can go straight to the right part of a large range.

## For her: shoulder bags, totes and crossbody bags

The women's Calvin Klein bags in our range cover the three everyday shapes. A **shoulder bag** is the all-rounder, structured enough for the office and simple enough for the weekend. A **tote** is the practical choice for work or university, with room for a tablet, a notebook and a water bottle. A **crossbody bag** is the one for errands, travel and crowded places, worn across the body with your phone and cards in front of you. Black is the most common colour and the easiest to match; the CK logo usually appears as a small metal plate or an embossed monogram. The [women's Calvin Klein bags page](/brand/calvin-klein/bags/women) lists them all.

## For him: crossbody bags and men's wallets

Among the men's bags, the **crossbody bag** is the most practical: a flat, light bag worn across the chest or the hip, sized for a phone, a wallet, keys and sunglasses, so pockets stay empty and trousers keep their shape. It is especially handy for travel, when you need your passport and tickets within reach. The [men's Calvin Klein bags page](/brand/calvin-klein/bags/men) shows them with their measurements. Men's **wallets** are the largest single group in the range: bifold wallets with card slots and a note section, many in genuine leather with a textured or embossed CK finish. The [men's Calvin Klein wallets page](/brand/calvin-klein/wallets/men) gathers them.

## Wallets for women

The women's wallets are a smaller group, mostly zip-around designs that hold cards, notes, coins and sometimes a phone, so they can double as a clutch on an evening out. They usually come in black or calm light shades, some with a small wrist loop. Choosing the wallet in the same colour and finish as a Calvin Klein bag gives you a coordinated set. The [women's Calvin Klein wallets page](/brand/calvin-klein/wallets/women) lists them.

## Calvin Klein or Calvin Klein Jeans

Both lines appear in our range, and the title tells you which. **Calvin Klein** pieces are the most minimal: smooth or finely textured leather, a small logo and neutral colours that suit work and formal wear. **Calvin Klein Jeans** pieces are louder and more casual: all-over monograms, bigger logos and a style that goes with denim and trainers. Owning one of each lets you switch by occasion: a minimal wallet for the office and a Jeans crossbody for the weekend.

## Looking after leather and coated materials

Genuine leather softens and darkens slightly with use, which is part of its charm; keep it dry, wipe it with a soft cloth and, in summer, give it at least 1 day of rest between uses. Coated and synthetic materials, common on bags, resist light rain and wipe clean easily with a slightly damp cloth. Avoid household cleaners, alcohol and wet wipes on both. Store bags empty and stuffed with paper, away from direct sun, and never leave them in a hot parked car, where heat can soften coatings and glue.

## Questions and answers

### Is the leather genuine?

Many Calvin Klein wallets and belts in our range are genuine leather, and the product title or description says so when they are. Some bags use coated or synthetic materials chosen for lightness and easy care. If the material matters to you, check the product page, or ask our customer service on WhatsApp before you order.

### Which wallet suits a man who carries many cards?

A bifold wallet with at least 6 to 8 card slots and a note section is the right choice, ideally with an extra pocket behind the slots. If you carry more than about 10 cards, choose a slightly thicker wallet rather than overfilling a slim one, which loses its shape quickly. The product page lists the slots of each model.

### Can a crossbody bag be worn on the shoulder?

Most can. A crossbody bag has a long adjustable strap: shorten it to wear the bag on the shoulder or high on the chest, lengthen it to wear it across the body at the hip. The product page gives the strap length, and the photos show the bag worn.

### Is the wallet-and-belt set ready as a gift?

Yes, the sets come in a gift box with both pieces inside, ready to give as they arrive. The product page shows the box and lists the belt's length. If you would like a note with the gift, tell our customer service when we call to confirm your order.

### Can I return a Calvin Klein item?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The item must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and a belt must not have been cut. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup; the refund is made by the payment method you used.
