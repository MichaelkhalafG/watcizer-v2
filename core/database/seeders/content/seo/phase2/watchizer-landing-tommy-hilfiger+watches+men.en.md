## Step 1: pick the line

The line name is in almost every title, and it tells you the watch's personality. **TH85** is the integrated-bracelet design, where the steel case flows straight into the bracelet in a 1970s spirit; it is the most fashionable shape in the range. **Stewart**, **Baker** and **Tyson** are sporty chronographs with three sub-dials. **Bryan**, **Henry** and **Weston** are cleaner and dressier, often on leather. **Lars**, **Legend** and **Larson** sit in between, sporty enough for the weekend and calm enough for the office. **Axel** and **Neo** bring bolder, more modern dials. Searching the line name in the box above the list shows every colour of it at once.

## Step 2: choose the dial colour

Black is the most common dial on this page, followed by blue and silver. A **black** dial is the most versatile and the easiest to read; it suits both a T-shirt and a suit. **Blue**, the brand's own navy, is the most Tommy Hilfiger of all, and it pairs naturally with denim, white shirts and brown leather. **Silver** and **white** dials look the most classic and formal. Less common colours, such as green, grey, beige and red accents, are the ones to choose if you already own a black or blue watch and want something different.

## Step 3: steel, leather or rubber

On this page, steel bracelets are the most common, followed by leather and then rubber. **Steel** is the most durable and the most versatile, and we size it free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty. **Leather** is the dressiest and softens with wear, but it prefers dry days, so it suits an office watch. **Rubber** is the summer and sport option: comfortable in the heat, easy to rinse and with a buckle that fits most wrists without any sizing. If you wear one watch every day in Egypt's summer, steel or rubber is the practical choice.

## Step 4: match it to the occasion

For the office, choose a three-hand or a simple chronograph with a black, blue or silver dial on steel or leather. For the weekend and sport, a rubber-strap chronograph from Stewart or Tyson. For evenings and weddings, a TH85 on its steel bracelet or a dressy Bryan on black leather. If you can only own one, a 44 mm watch with a blue dial on a steel bracelet covers almost every situation.

## The 44 mm standard

Around two thirds of the watches on this page measure 44 millimetres, with a group at 46 mm and a few at 40 to 43 mm. On a typical men's wrist of 17 to 18 centimetres, 44 mm looks modern and balanced. Under about 16.5 centimetres, look for the 40 to 42 mm models, or choose leather, which sits closer to the wrist. The case size is on every product page, so you can filter quickly.

## Chronographs, multi-function and three-hand

Many Tommy Hilfiger men's watches have three small sub-dials, but not all of them are chronographs. A **chronograph** has two pushers beside the crown that start and stop a stopwatch. A **multi-function** dial uses the sub-dials for the day, the date and a 24-hour scale instead. A **three-hand** watch shows only hours, minutes and seconds, and is the thinnest and the easiest to read. The product page names the functions, so check it if you want a working stopwatch.

## Keeping it like new

Most of the watches on this page list 50 metres of water resistance: rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but the sea and diving are not, and the pushers should never be pressed under water. Rinse a rubber strap or a steel bracelet with fresh water after a sweaty day and dry it with a soft cloth. Give leather a rest on hot days. Change the quartz battery as soon as the watch starts losing time, usually every 2 to 3 years, so it never leaks inside the movement.

## Questions and answers

### Which men's Tommy Hilfiger watch is the most versatile?

A 44 mm watch with a blue or black dial on a steel bracelet. It works at the office, at university, at weekends and in the evening, and steel survives daily wear better than leather. Lars and Bryan are good lines for this; if you prefer a chronograph look, choose a Stewart or Baker on steel.

### What is the TH85?

It is Tommy Hilfiger's integrated-bracelet watch: the steel case and the bracelet are designed as one flowing piece, in the style of the sporty steel watches of the 1970s. It looks dressier than a chronograph and more distinctive than a classic three-hand watch, which makes it a popular choice for evenings and as a gift.

### Is a 46 mm Tommy Hilfiger too big?

On a wrist above about 18 centimetres it looks bold and balanced; on a slimmer wrist it can overhang. Compare the case size with a watch you already own, or try both 44 and 46 mm in our stores. Rubber-strap versions feel lighter on the wrist than steel versions of the same size.
