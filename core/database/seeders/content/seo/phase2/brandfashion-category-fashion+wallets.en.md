A wallet should fit what you carry, not the other way round. This guide starts from your pocket and your habits, cards, cash, coins and phone, and matches them to the wallet shapes and brands on this page.

## What do you carry?

Count your cards and think about cash. With 4 to 6 cards and little cash, a card holder is the slimmest choice. With 6 to 10 cards and notes every day, a bifold works best. With receipts, coins and more cards, a trifold or a zip-around wallet keeps everything closed. With a phone in hand all day, a phone wallet or wristlet carries both.

## Where do you carry it?

A front trouser pocket suits a card holder or a slim bifold under 1.5 cm thick. A jacket's inside pocket takes a long or vertical wallet. A back pocket is common but bends the wallet and strains the back when you sit, so a slim model is kinder. A handbag suits a zip-around wallet, which keeps coins and cards from spilling.

## Leather and textures

Smooth leather looks classic and ages well. Crosshatch and grained textures hide scratches. Coated canvas, used by Coach and Guess, resists stains and keeps its shape. Embossed logos are discreet; printed monograms are bold. Choose by how hard you are on your wallet: textured or coated finishes forgive daily wear best.

## Making a wallet last

Do not overfill it; extra cards stretch the slots for good. Empty old receipts every week. Keep it out of rain and wipe it with a dry cloth. Avoid the back pocket if you sit for long hours. With these habits, a good leather wallet lasts 3 years and often far longer.

A card holder or a slim bifold. A card holder takes about 4 to 8 cards and a few folded notes; a slim bifold adds a note compartment. Avoid thick trifolds in a front pocket. Coach, Calvin Klein and Lacoste all offer slim designs on this page.
