## What a Naviforce watch looks like

The Naviforce models we list share a style: a large round or cushion-shaped case, three sub-dials that give the look of a chronograph, bold hour markers and a date window. The most common case size in our range is 44 millimetres, and a good number are 45 to 48 mm, which is big even for a men's watch. Silver, black and gold-tone steel cases sit beside rose-gold and two-tone versions, and dials come in black, blue, green, burgundy and grey.

The style is closer to a sports watch than to a dress watch. It suits jeans, a polo shirt or a gym bag better than a suit, and it is popular as a first watch for a teenager or a gift for a young man.

## Steel bracelet or rubber strap

Our Naviforce range divides almost evenly between steel bracelets and rubber straps, with a few on leather. The steel bracelet looks dressier and lasts longer, and it needs to be sized to your wrist: the links come out with a small pin, and we size it for free in our stores. The rubber strap is lighter, more comfortable in Egyptian summer heat and easy to rinse after the gym or the beach; it fastens with a classic buckle and fits most wrists without any adjustment.

If you sweat a lot or spend the day outdoors, the rubber version is the more practical choice. If the watch is for work, a family event or a gift, the steel version usually makes the better impression.

## Analogue, chronograph or analogue-digital

Most Naviforce titles say "Chronograph", and on many models the sub-dials really do work as a stopwatch, a day and a 24-hour indicator. The product page states the functions, so read it before you buy if a working stopwatch matters to you. About 1 in 8 of our Naviforce watches is an analogue-digital model, with hands on top and a small digital screen below that shows the time, an alarm, a stopwatch and a backlight. Those suit someone who wants the look of a classic watch and the tools of a digital one.

## Choosing the colour

Colour is where Naviforce gives you the most choice, so it is worth deciding before you scroll. An all-black watch — black case, black dial, black rubber strap — is the most discreet and hides scratches best, and black is the most common colour in our range. Silver steel with a blue or white dial looks the most classic and goes with office clothes. Gold-tone and rose-gold cases stand out and look best with a black or burgundy dial, which keeps them from looking flashy. If you want a watch for every day of the week, a silver case on a steel bracelet is the safest single choice; a bright colour works best as a second watch. The [men's Naviforce page](/brand/naviforce/men) filters by dial and strap colour.

If the watch is a gift and you do not know their taste, choose black or silver, which are the easiest to like, and keep the invoice so it can be exchanged within the exchange period if they want another colour.

## Water resistance in daily life

The Naviforce models we carry are mostly listed at 30 metres of water resistance. That is enough for rain, sweat and washing your hands, and not enough for swimming, the sea or a shower. Do not press the buttons when the watch is wet, and keep the crown pushed in all the way. If the glass fogs on the inside after a hot day, moisture has got in; stop wearing it and bring it to us before the movement is damaged.

## Getting the best from an affordable watch

Naviforce keeps its prices low, which is why we can offer a large chronograph at a price close to a basic fashion watch. A few habits help it last. Keep it away from perfume and sun cream, which dull the plating on gold-tone and rose-gold cases. Wipe the case and bracelet with a dry cloth after a hot day. Change the battery — usually every 2 to 3 years — as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak battery never leaks inside, and store it in its box rather than loose in a drawer with keys.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 46 mm Naviforce too big for me?

It depends on your wrist. Measure your wrist with a tape: below about 17 centimetres, a 44 or 46 mm case will look very large, and a 41 or 42 mm model is a better fit. Above about 18 centimetres, 46 mm looks natural. The rubber versions sit a little closer to the wrist and feel smaller than the steel ones of the same diameter.

### Do the sub-dials on a Naviforce really work?

On most chronograph models they do, as a stopwatch and as day and 24-hour indicators, but a few designs use sub-dials only as decoration. The functions are listed on each product page. If you need a working stopwatch, check that the page names a chronograph function, or message our customer service on WhatsApp before ordering.

### Can I swim with a Naviforce watch?

Not with the models we list, which are rated at 30 metres. That rating means splashes and rain only, so take the watch off for the pool, the sea and the shower. Water is the most common cause of a fogged glass and a stopped movement, and damage from water beyond the rating is not covered by the warranty.

### What warranty does a Naviforce watch have?

The warranty length and who provides it are written on each product page and on the card in the box. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card with your invoice; you need both to use it. Damage from water, falls or opening the case is not covered. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains how to claim it.

### Can I exchange the watch if the size is wrong?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been cut. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the pickup, then refund you by the payment method you used.
