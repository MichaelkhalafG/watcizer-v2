A casual watch has one job: to look right on a normal day. It should be easy to read, comfortable for hours and sturdy enough for daily life. This page explains how to choose one, which lines suit which taste, and how to keep it looking good.

## What makes a watch casual

A casual watch sits between a dress watch and a sports watch. The dial is clear, often with 3 hands and a date, the case is usually steel, and the strap is chosen for comfort. It is not as slim as a dress watch nor as chunky as a diver, which is exactly why it works for school, the office, shopping and a dinner with friends.

## Alba, the largest line here

Alba supplies more than a third of the watches on this page. The Active line is the sportiest, with bold dials on steel or silicone. The Fashion line is mostly women's, in pink, champagne, mother-of-pearl, green and silver-white dials. Flagship Signa and Prestige add more refined designs, some with a chronograph. Alba belongs to the Seiko group, which gives these watches a reputation for dependable movements.

## Michael Kors and Guess

Michael Kors is the choice for women who like a little sparkle: gold, rose gold and silver cases, many with crystal details, in sizes that suit a slim wrist. Guess offers lively designs for men and women, often with colourful dials and multifunction displays. Both brands make watches that read as jewellery as much as timekeepers, ideal when the watch is part of the outfit.

## Hugo Boss, Naviforce and Mini Focus

Hugo Boss brings a cleaner, more grown-up casual style, with blue, black and grey dials on steel or leather. Naviforce and Mini Focus are the bold, affordable options, with large cases, contrasting hands and plenty of colour. The [brand page of Naviforce](/brand/naviforce) gathers all of its designs if you want to see the full range.

## Picking a dial

Black is the most common dial on this page, then white, blue, silver, gold and green. A white or silver dial looks fresh and is the easiest to read in low light. Black and blue are the safest everyday choices. Green, pink and champagne dials add personality; on women's Alba Fashion models they pair beautifully with a gold or two-tone bracelet.

## Steel bracelet, leather or silicone

About 2 in 3 watches here come on a stainless steel bracelet, which is durable and handles water and sweat. Leather straps look warmer and suit autumn and winter. Silicone is the summer and sport choice: light, soft and easy to wash. If you buy a watch on steel, our stores can shorten the bracelet so it fits on day one.

## Quartz for everyday ease

Nearly every casual watch here is quartz, which means it keeps excellent time and needs no attention beyond a battery change every 2 or 3 years. A handful are automatic for those who prefer a mechanical movement. For a watch you want to put on and forget, quartz is the practical answer.

## Water, sun and sport

Most casual watches resist splashes and hand washing; many manage 50 m, enough for a shower. Check the rating on the product page before swimming. Keep leather straps away from water and perfume, and give steel bracelets a gentle wash with mild soap and a soft brush every few weeks to remove dust from between the links.

## Matching a casual watch to your style

Think about what you wear most. With denim and trainers, a Naviforce or Guess on silicone or a bold Mini Focus suits the relaxed look. With chinos and a shirt, an Alba Prestige or Hugo Boss on brown leather is smarter. With dresses and blouses, an Alba Fashion or Michael Kors in gold or rose gold works like a bracelet. If your style changes from day to day, a silver steel watch with a white dial is the 1 piece that goes with everything.

## Questions and answers

### Is a casual watch suitable for the office?

Yes, in most offices. A casual watch with a steel bracelet and a black, blue or white dial looks neat with a shirt and trousers. For very formal settings or a suit and tie, a slimmer dress watch on leather is more elegant, but for daily work a casual watch is the most practical choice.

### Which casual watch is best for a small wrist?

Look at cases between 28 and 36 mm for women and 38 to 40 mm for men, with a bracelet that can be shortened. Alba Fashion and many Michael Kors models are made for slim wrists. The case size is on every product page, and our stores can adjust a steel bracelet for a perfect fit.

### Can I wear a casual watch in the shower?

Only if the product page states at least 50 m water resistance, and never with a leather strap. Hot water and soap can age the seals over time, so many owners simply take the watch off. For swimming, choose a model rated 100 m on a steel or silicone strap.

### How often does the battery need changing?

Usually every 2 to 3 years for a simple three-hand quartz watch. Models with chronographs or extra functions use a little more power. When the seconds hand starts jumping in steps of several seconds, the battery is low; our stores can replace it and check the water seals at the same time.
