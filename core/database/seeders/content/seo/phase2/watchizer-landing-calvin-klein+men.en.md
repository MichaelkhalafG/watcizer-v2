Calvin Klein's menswear is built on a minimal idea: clean shapes, black and brown, and a logo used with restraint. Its accessories follow the same rule, which makes them easy to wear every day and easy to give. This page brings together everything we carry for men, and the guide below explains the gift sets, belts, wallets and small accessories, and how to choose between them.

## Wallet and belt gift sets

More than half of this page is made up of **gift sets** that pair a bifold wallet with a matching belt, almost all in black. They are sold as a pair, ready to give, and the wallet and belt share the same CK logo or monogram so they look like a set. For birthdays, graduations and Eid, a set is the easiest complete gift.

## What a set includes

Each set combines a **bifold** wallet, with card slots, a note compartment and on many models a coin pocket, and a belt with a CK buckle. Some sets use a large embossed CK logo, others an all-over monogram, and a few pair the wallet with a card holder instead. The product photos show both pieces and the buckle of each set.

## Calvin Klein or Calvin Klein Jeans

Two labels appear in the titles. **Calvin Klein** is the main line, with the most minimal designs. **Calvin Klein Jeans** is the more casual line, with bolder logos and monograms. Both are black-led and easy to match; choose the label whose look you prefer for the person who will wear it.

## Belts and their buckles

The belts come with several buckle styles. A **CK logo** buckle shows the letters in silver tone or black; a **plaque** buckle is a smooth metal plate; a **pin** buckle is the classic tongue-and-hole design; and an **automatic** buckle slides and locks without holes, so the fit can be adjusted finely. Choose by the look you want and how formal the outfit is.

## The reversible belt

One belt is **reversible**: black on one side and brown with a pattern on the other, with a metallic CK buckle that turns to switch faces. It is two belts in one, black for a suit and black shoes, brown for jeans, chinos and brown shoes, and a practical choice when packing light for a trip.

## Bifold and vertical wallets

The standalone wallets include **bifold** designs, which fold in half and slip into a trouser pocket, and **vertical** wallets, which stand upright and fit well in a jacket pocket. Finishes include embossed CK logos, all-over monograms, a crosshatch texture with a metal logo, and black, grey or dark brown colours.

## Materials

Many of these wallets and belts are described as **leather-look**: a coated material with the appearance of leather that is light and easy to wipe clean. The product page names the material of each piece. Whatever the material, keep belts and wallets away from water and heat, and wipe them with a soft, dry cloth.

## Keychains and the key holder

For a small gift or a stocking filler, there are **keychains** in black with silver metal rings, in textured, leather-look and fabric monogram finishes. The **key holder** pouch has a fold-over flap and a silver CK logo, so keys stay together without scratching a phone or a wallet in the same pocket.

## Crossbody bags and a travel pouch

Two men's **crossbody** bags, one in tan brown and black and one from Calvin Klein Jeans in black with a green logo, carry a phone, a wallet, keys and earbuds. The dark grey **travel pouch** organises chargers, cables or grooming items in a suitcase or a backpack.

## Matching belt, wallet and shoes

A simple rule keeps an outfit tidy: match the **belt** to your shoes. A black belt goes with black shoes and a suit; a brown belt with brown shoes, jeans and chinos. The wallet stays out of sight most of the time, so it can be black whatever you wear, which is why the black gift sets suit almost everyone.

## Choosing a belt size

Most men choose a belt one size above their trouser waist, so the buckle closes on the middle hole. If you are buying a gift and do not know the size, an **automatic** buckle belt, which adjusts without holes, or a belt that can be trimmed is the safer choice. Check the length on the product page.

### How do I choose the right belt size?

Measure the waist of the trousers you wear most often, then choose a belt one size above it so the buckle closes on the middle hole. If you are unsure, choose an automatic buckle belt, which adjusts without holes, or ask us on WhatsApp and we help you with the length.
