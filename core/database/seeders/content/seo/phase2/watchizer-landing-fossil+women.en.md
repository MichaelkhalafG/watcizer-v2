Fossil's women's watches mix vintage charm with everyday practicality: clear dials, steel bracelets that last and a touch of sparkle where it counts. The range we carry is easy to navigate once you know the main lines and sizes, and every model shares the same reassuring 50-metre water rating. This guide introduces the lines, then helps you choose a size, a metal and a dial.

## Carlie: small and graceful

The **Carlie** is a slim, feminine watch at 35 mm, with a delicate dial and a mix of numerals and markers. We carry it with a rose-gold dial on steel and in an all-black version that looks modern and chic. It is a natural everyday watch for the office or university, light on the wrist and easy to read.

## Jacqueline: the classic round watch

The **Jacqueline** is a clean, round 36 mm watch with a simple dial and a date window on many models. We carry it in silver and gold together and in gold with a white dial. Its calm design suits every age, which makes it one of the easiest Fossil watches to give as a gift.

## Riley: multi-function with sparkle

The **Riley** pairs a multi-function dial with crystal accents, at 38 mm. Its sub-dials show the day, the date and a 24-hour scale, giving a rich, detailed look. We carry it in navy blue, a colour that feels fresh and elegant and pairs well with silver or white jewellery.

## Multi-function dials

Besides the Riley, several models use a **multi-function** layout in rose gold or silver, at 35 to 38 mm. The small sub-dials make a watch look more detailed and sporty without the pushers of a chronograph, and they are practical too: the day and date are always in view.

## Crystal accents

A few of our women's Fossils carry **crystals** on the bezel or around the dial, in silver or rose gold. They catch the light without overwhelming the design, so the watch works for a dinner or a wedding as well as for a normal day. Take crystal models off for sport and housework, where a knock can loosen a stone.

## Raquel: rectangular, with pearl and green dials

At the small end sit the **Raquel** models, with a rectangular case about 23 to 26 mm wide and a three-hand dial with a date. Two have pearl-effect dials, in two-tone and in silver, and one pairs a green dial with a gold-tone case. A rectangular watch reads like jewellery and suits a slim wrist or anyone who likes a vintage, bracelet-style look.

## Rose gold leads

**Rose gold** is the most common tone on this page, warm and flattering on most skin tones. Silver follows, then gold and two-tone. If you wear rose-gold rings or a rose-gold bracelet, it is the easiest match; for white gold and silver jewellery, choose a silver model.

## Two-tone and brown

One model combines **two-tone** steel with a brown, rose-gold accented look at 39 mm, and others mix silver and gold. Two-tone steel matches both silver and gold jewellery, which makes it a practical choice when you wear both or when you buy for someone whose taste you do not know.

## Choosing a size

Most models measure 35 to 38 mm. On a wrist of about 14 to 16 centimetres, **36** mm looks balanced and modern, 38 mm a little bolder, and 35 mm graceful. The rectangular Raquel suits a very slim wrist or a jewellery look. Compare the case size with a watch you already own.

## Water: 50 metres

Every women's Fossil here lists **50 metres**: rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but not the sea. Take the watch off before a hot shower, when steam and soap can reach the seals, and rinse it with fresh water after the pool, then dry it with a soft cloth.

## Steel bracelets and sizing

Most models are on steel bracelets, which need sizing by removing links. We do it free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty. A well-sized bracelet should let one finger slip under it; too tight and it marks the skin, too loose and it slides over the hand.

## Battery and care

These are **quartz** watches, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch slows. Put the watch on after perfume and hand cream, which dull plating, and store it in its box rather than with harder jewellery that can scratch it.

## Questions and answers

### What is a multi-function Fossil watch?

It is a watch with small sub-dials that show the day, the date and a 24-hour scale, in addition to the time. It looks like a chronograph but has no stopwatch and no extra pushers. It is practical for anyone who likes to see the date at a glance and enjoys a detailed dial.

### Is a 23 mm Raquel too small?

On the Raquel, 23 mm is the width of a rectangular case, which is taller than it is wide, so it looks less small than a 23 mm round watch would. Its appeal is the jewellery look of vintage women's watches. For a larger, easier-to-read dial, choose a 35 or 36 mm round model.

### Can I swim with a women's Fossil watch?

Every model here lists 50 metres, which allows a short swim in a pool. It is not meant for the sea or for diving, and the watch should come off before a hot shower. After the pool, rinse it with fresh water and dry it with a soft cloth.

### What warranty comes with a Fossil watch?

Each product page states the warranty length and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. It covers the movement against manufacturing defects. Keep the card and the invoice together. The [warranty page](/warranty) explains what is and is not covered.

### Can I exchange it for another model?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange it, and we pay the return shipping. The watch must be unused, in its original packaging with its tags, and the bracelet must not have been shortened. Send us your order number on WhatsApp and we arrange the exchange.
