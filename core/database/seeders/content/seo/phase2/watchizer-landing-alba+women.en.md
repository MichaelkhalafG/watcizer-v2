Alba's women's watches combine Japanese reliability with a lot of colour. Where many brands offer a few safe dials, Alba gives you green, light pink, champagne, red and several shades of mother of pearl, often in a classic steel case that will last for years. This guide explains the collections, the dial finishes and the straps, so you can find the shade that suits you.

## Fashion: the heart of the range

**Fashion** is by far the largest collection in our women's Alba range. Its watches are slim and feminine, with steel cases and a wide palette of dials: silver-white, black, blue, light blue, green, champagne and light pink. They are made for everyday wear, at the office, at university or out with friends.

## Mother-of-pearl dials

Several Fashion models have **mother-of-pearl** dials, marked MOP in the titles. Nacre, the inner layer of a shell, shimmers with soft reflections, and Alba tints it in white, blue, green, champagne and red. Each dial is slightly different from the next, which gives the watch a personal, jewellery-like character.

## Coloured leather straps

Some Fashion models pair the dial with a **leather** strap in the same colour: blue with blue, red with red, or white mother of pearl with brown. A coloured strap turns a classic watch into a statement accessory and is easy to change for a new look when it wears out.

## Steel and mesh bracelets

Most of our women's Albas are on **steel** bracelets, in silver or two-tone, the durable choice for every day. One Fashion model has a fine steel **mesh** bracelet, supple and flat on the wrist. ## Active for women

The women's **Active** models are sporty and fresh, with blue, green or light pink-gold dials on steel bracelets. They suit an active day, a walk or a trip, and they sit well with casual clothes and trainers. If you want one sporty Alba, start here.

## Standard and luminous dials

The **Standard** collection keeps things simple and practical. Two of its women's models have **luminous** dials, with hands and markers that glow in the dark, which is useful in a cinema, at night or early in the morning. Others pair black or grey-brown dials with silver steel.

## Prestige and Flagship Signa

**Prestige** is Alba's dressier line; our women's Prestige has a black dial on steel and lists sapphire glass, which resists scratches very well. The **Flagship Signa** model pairs a pink and white dial with steel. Both suit an office and an evening equally well.

## A red chronograph

One model stands out: a women's **chronograph** with a red dial on red leather. A stopwatch, started and stopped by 2 pushers beside the crown, gives it a sporty, technical look, and the bold colour makes it a fun second watch beside a classic silver one.

## Choosing a dial colour

**Silver-white** and black are the safest choices and match everything. **Blue** and green look fresh and modern. **Champagne** is warm and elegant, a good match for gold jewellery. **Light pink** is soft and feminine, and **red** is the boldest. For a first Alba, silver-white on steel; for a second, a coloured mother-of-pearl dial.

## Silver or two-tone

**Silver** steel goes with white-gold and silver jewellery and with every outfit. **Two-tone** steel, silver with gold, also matches gold rings and bracelets, which makes it practical if you wear both metals. A gold or champagne dial on silver steel adds warmth without needing a gold case.

## Japanese quartz

Most of our women's Albas run on Japanese **quartz**, accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that usually lasts 2 to 3 years. Change it as soon as the watch starts losing time, so a weak cell never leaks inside. A quartz watch keeps running even if it sits in a drawer for weeks.

## Reading the specifications

Each product page lists the case size, the glass, the water rating and the movement. Compare the **case size** with a watch you already own. For water, 30 metres means splashes only, 50 metres a short swim in a pool, and 100 metres or more the sea; leather straps should stay dry in any case.

## Care for leather and steel

Wipe a steel bracelet with a soft, dry cloth after a hot day, and put the watch on after perfume and hand cream. Keep **leather** straps away from water and let them air before storing the watch. Store the watch in its box rather than loose with other jewellery, which can scratch the case.

## Questions and answers

### What does MOP mean in an Alba title?

MOP stands for mother of pearl, the shiny inner layer of a shell, used for the dial. It shimmers with soft colours as the light moves, and Alba tints it in shades such as white, blue, green, champagne and red. Because it is natural, every MOP dial is slightly different.

### What is a luminous dial?

A luminous dial has hands and markers coated with a material that stores light and glows in the dark for some time afterwards. It lets you read the time in a cinema, at night or early in the morning without switching on a light. The glow is strongest just after the watch has been in light.

### Is an Alba a good everyday watch for women?

Yes. Alba is the Japanese brand of the Seiko group, and its watches are made to be reliable, with steel cases and Japanese quartz movements. With a battery change every few years and normal care, a women's Alba can be worn every day for a long time.
