Fossil built its name on vintage-inspired American design: warm leather straps, readable dials and watches that look good with jeans as much as with a jacket. The men's range we carry follows that spirit, with chronographs for everyday wear and automatics for anyone who likes to see a movement at work. This guide goes through the lines one by one, then helps you choose a strap, a size and a dial colour.

## Grant: Roman numerals on leather

The **Grant** is a classic Fossil chronograph with Roman numerals on the dial, usually on a leather strap. We carry it with blue, navy, black and silver dials, on black or brown leather, at 44 mm. It is the line to choose if you want a watch that looks traditional and dressy without being formal.

## Nate: the big one

The **Nate** is the largest Fossil here, at 50 mm, with a rugged chronograph dial and bold numerals. It comes on black steel, brown leather and black leather, with brown or black dials. A 50 mm case is a statement on the wrist, best suited to a broad wrist of about 19 centimetres or more.

## Townsman: the dressier chronograph

The **Townsman** is a refined chronograph for the office and evenings, with a cleaner dial and slim hands. We carry it on black and brown leather and in a black and gold version. At 44 mm, it sits comfortably under a shirt cuff and pairs well with a leather belt in the same colour.

## Neutra: minimal and modern

The **Neutra** takes a minimalist route: a clean dial with simple markers, on brown or black leather. We carry a grey Neutra chronograph at 42 mm and a rose-gold Neutra with black leather. It suits men who prefer a quiet, modern design over a busy, sporty one.

## Machine, Bowman, Dillinger and Wakefield

Other lines add variety. **Machine** has an industrial, bold look on a black steel bracelet. **Bowman** is a 46 mm black chronograph, **Dillinger** pairs a blue dial with brown leather, and **Wakefield** is a black chronograph on black leather. Each product page shows the exact layout and size.

## Pilot 54: an aviation look

The **Pilot 54** has a cream dial on brown leather, inspired by the instruments of early aircraft. Large, clear numerals and a vintage colour make it easy to read and full of character. It is a good second watch for someone who already owns a black-dial everyday watch.

## Automatic, skeleton and open heart

About a fifth of our men's Fossils are **automatic**: mechanical watches that wind with the movement of your wrist. A **skeleton** dial is cut away to show much of the movement, while an **open-heart** dial has a single window that reveals the balance wheel ticking. Both make the movement part of the design.

## Leather in brown, black and blue

Leather straps make up about half of the page. **Brown** leather is the classic Fossil look and goes with jeans, chinos and brown shoes. **Black** leather is the formal choice for suits and black shoes. **Blue** leather is the fresh option, especially with a blue or navy dial.

## Steel, gunmetal and grey

Steel bracelets are the durable, all-season choice; they need sizing, which we do free of charge in our two stores in Cairo while the watch is under warranty. **Gunmetal** and grey steel look darker and more modern than polished silver and hide small marks better.

## Dial colours

**Black** is the most common dial here and the easiest to read. **Blue** and navy look fresh and smart, **cream** gives a vintage feel, and **brown** pairs naturally with a brown strap. For one watch for everything, choose black on steel; for a second, blue or cream on leather.

## Sizes: 44 and 50 mm

Most men's Fossil watches here measure **44** mm, which looks balanced on a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres. The Nate and some chronographs reach 50 mm, and a few models are 42 or 45 mm. A leather strap makes a watch look slightly smaller than a steel bracelet of the same size.

## Water: 50 metres

Almost every model lists **50 metres**: rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool are fine, but not the sea or diving, and never press the pushers under water. Leather straps should stay out of water altogether, so take a leather-strap watch off before the pool.

## Looking after leather

Leather lasts longest when it stays dry. Let it air after a hot day before putting the watch in its box, keep perfume and creams off it and wipe it with a dry **cloth**. A leather strap is a wearing part and can be replaced when it is tired, which gives the watch a fresh look.

## Questions and answers

### Is a 50 mm Fossil Nate too big?

On a wrist of about 19 centimetres or more it looks bold but natural; on a slimmer wrist it can overhang the edges. If you like the Nate's style but have a smaller wrist, a 44 mm Grant or Townsman chronograph gives a similar sporty look in a more balanced size.

### What is the difference between a skeleton and an open-heart dial?

A skeleton dial is cut away across much of its surface, showing the gears and bridges of the movement. An open-heart dial keeps most of the dial solid and has one small window, usually showing the balance wheel as it swings back and forth. Both are found on automatic Fossils.

### Can the leather strap be replaced?

Yes. A leather strap wears with time, especially in heat and humidity, and can be replaced with a new one of the same width. The product page lists the details of each watch, and a new strap gives a well-loved watch a fresh look without changing the case.
