## Quartz or automatic

Most of the men's Emporio Armani watches here run on **quartz**: accurate to within seconds a month, with a battery that lasts about 2 to 3 years. The **automatic** models, a smaller group, are mechanical and wind with the movement of your wrist. They are less precise than quartz, gaining or losing some seconds a day, but they need no battery and have a smooth, sweeping seconds hand.

## Living with an automatic

If you take an automatic off for a day or two, it may stop. Wind it with a few turns of the crown, then set the time and the date. Avoid setting the date between about 9 in the evening and 3 in the morning, when the date mechanism is working. Wearing it every day keeps it running on its own.

## The 43 mm case

The **43** mm case is by far the most common size here, followed by 46 and 42 mm. On a wrist of about 17 to 18 centimetres, 43 mm looks balanced and modern; on a slimmer wrist, choose a 40 to 42 mm model. A leather strap makes the watch look a little smaller than a steel bracelet of the same size.

## Water and glass

Most models list **50 metres**, or 5 ATM: enough for rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool, but not the sea. Models listing 100 metres can go to the sea, and those listing 30 metres are for splashes only. Most have mineral glass; some list sapphire, which resists scratches better. Never press the pushers under water.

## Steel, leather or rubber

**Steel** bracelets are the most common choice and the most durable; they are sized by removing links. **Leather** gives a warmer, classic look for the office but prefers dry days. **Rubber** and silicone, on a few sporty models, are the choice for summer and the beach café, and rinse clean in fresh water.

## Questions and answers

### Is an automatic Emporio Armani better than a quartz one?

Neither is better; they are different. A quartz watch is more accurate and needs only a new battery every few years. An automatic is mechanical, winds itself as you wear it and has a smooth, sweeping seconds hand, which many people value. Choose the automatic if you enjoy mechanics and wear the watch daily.

### Can I swim with a men's Emporio Armani watch?

Check the rating on the product page. At 50 metres, or 5 ATM, a short swim in a pool is fine, but not the sea or diving. At 100 metres you can swim in the sea. At 30 metres, keep it to splashes. Never press the pushers under water, and rinse the watch after the pool.

### What is a GMT watch?

A GMT watch shows a second time zone, usually with an extra hand that goes round the dial once every 24 hours. You set the main hands to local time and the extra hand to home time, so you can see both at once. It is useful for travellers and for anyone with family abroad.
