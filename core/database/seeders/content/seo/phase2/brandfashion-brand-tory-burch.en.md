Designer Tory Burch founded her company in New York in 2004 with the idea of making beautiful, wearable pieces for real days, and within a few years her Double T logo had become familiar far beyond the United States. The bags carry that philosophy: refined leather, shapes that keep their structure and a design that dresses up a simple outfit. Our range is mostly shoulder bags and handbags, so this guide explains how to tell them apart, how to choose a colour for each season and how to look after fine leather.

## Shoulder bag or handbag?

The two words appear in most of our Tory Burch titles, and the difference is how you carry the bag. A **shoulder bag** has a strap long enough to sit under your arm, leaving your hands free; it is the everyday workhorse. A **handbag** is designed to be carried by its top handle, in the hand or the crook of the arm, and looks more formal and composed. Many of our models do both: a top handle for meetings and a strap for the commute. The product photos show each bag carried, which helps more than any description, so look at them before you decide which way of carrying suits your day.

## The Eleanor and the Double T clasp

The **Eleanor** is built around a large Double T clasp in metal, set on a fold-over flap of smooth or textured leather. It is the most recognisable Tory Burch design in our range and the one to choose if you want the brand to be obvious at a glance. Its strap can usually be worn long or doubled, which makes it as useful at dinner as at work. Wear it with plain clothes, so the hardware can be the only jewellery your outfit needs.

## Planning what fits

Before choosing, empty your current bag onto a table and look at what you really carry. A phone, a card holder and keys — 3 items — fit almost any of our Tory Burch bags. A full-size wallet, sunglasses and a small pouch need a medium shoulder bag. A tablet, a notebook or a water bottle need the tote. Compare the width, height and depth on the product page with those items; Tory Burch bags are structured, so they hold their shape but do not stretch to take more than they were made for.

Black is the colour of most of our Tory Burch bags and the one that works in every season. ## The bag as a gift

A Tory Burch bag is a gift that marks an occasion: a graduation, a new job, an engagement or a milestone birthday. Because handbags have no size to guess, the decision is the colour and the shape. Black is the safest; brown or Tempranillo is warmer and more personal; light cream suits someone who loves soft, bright clothes. A shoulder bag with a top handle is the most versatile gift, and the Eleanor is the most recognisable. Keep the invoice, so she can exchange it within 14 days for another colour if she prefers, as long as it is unused, in its packaging and with its tags.

If you want the surprise to feel complete, add a small handwritten card; a refined gift is finished by a personal word.

## Fine leather, fine care

The leather on these bags is soft and finished with care, and a little attention keeps it beautiful. Wipe the bag with a soft, dry cloth after use, and dab any spill immediately without rubbing. Keep it away from a hot car in summer, where the heat on a 35-degree day can mark the leather, and from strong sun when stored. Stuff it with paper so the structure holds, and keep it in its dust bag, with the strap inside, so the hardware does not scratch the leather. Never use household cleaners, alcohol or wet wipes.

## Pairing with jewellery and a watch

The Double T and the other metal details are usually gold-tone. They sit naturally with gold earrings, a gold bracelet and a gold or rose-gold watch; a Michael Kors or Guess women's watch from our range in the same tone completes the look. With silver jewellery, a black or Pebblestone bag keeps the contrast calm. As a rule, let the bag's hardware set the metal for the rest of the outfit.

## Questions and answers

### Which Tory Burch bag is best for every day?

A medium shoulder bag in black leather with a zip or a secure flap is the most practical daily choice. It fits a wallet, a phone, keys and sunglasses, sits comfortably under the arm and goes with work and weekend clothes. If you want the brand's signature look, an Eleanor in black does the same job with more presence.

### Is Tempranillo red or brown?

It sits between the two: a deep, wine-like red with brown undertones, named after a Spanish grape. In daylight it reads as a rich burgundy; indoors it can look almost brown. It pairs well with black, camel, navy and denim, and it is a beautiful choice for autumn and winter.

### Does the leather scratch easily?

Smooth leather can show fine marks from keys and rings over time, which is part of how leather ages. Textured leathers hide them better. Keep sharp objects in an inner pocket, and wipe the bag regularly; small marks on smooth leather often soften with gentle rubbing from a clean, dry fingertip.
