## A men's range built on 43 mm

Most of the men's Emporio Armani watches we list measure 43 millimetres, which is the brand's signature size: substantial on the wrist but never oversized. A smaller group sits at 41 and 42 mm for slimmer wrists, and the sportier models reach 44 and 46 mm. ## The automatic models

Our Emporio Armani range includes more than 40 automatic models, a much larger share than most fashion brands offer. Many show the movement through an open section of the dial or a transparent case back, so you can watch the balance wheel beat. An automatic winds itself from the motion of your wrist, needs no battery and keeps running for about a day and a half after you take it off. It is less precise than quartz by a few seconds a day, which is normal for any mechanical watch. If you enjoy the idea of a watch that runs on its own energy, this is the part of the range to explore first.

## Ceramic and two-tone

A few Emporio Armani models use **ceramic** for the case or the bracelet. Ceramic is very hard and scratch-resistant, keeps its deep colour for years and feels smooth and warm against the skin; it is also lighter than steel. It can crack under a hard knock, though, so it suits a watch for the office and evenings rather than sport. Two-tone steel, mixing silver and gold-tone links, is the other dressy option, and it pairs well with a dark suit or a white shirt.

## Matching the watch to what you wear

Emporio Armani is designed to go with tailored clothes, so it is worth matching the watch to your wardrobe. With a navy or grey suit, a blue or silver dial on a steel bracelet is the cleanest choice. With a black suit or for evenings, a black dial, or a black ceramic model, looks sharpest. Brown leather straps belong with brown shoes and a brown belt; black leather with black shoes. For smart-casual days — chinos and a shirt — a chronograph on leather bridges both worlds. Keep the metal of the watch, the belt buckle and any ring in the same tone, and the whole outfit looks planned. A slim 41 or 42 mm model on leather slips under a fitted shirt cuff far more easily than a 46 mm chronograph on steel.

If the watch is for a particular occasion, such as a wedding or a job interview, start from the clothes you will wear that day and choose the watch to suit them, not the other way round; a calm, matching watch makes a better impression than a striking one that clashes with the suit.

## Leather, steel and daily care

Steel bracelets are the most common choice in the range and the easiest to look after: rinse with fresh water after the sea, dry with a soft cloth and have them sized to your wrist, which takes a few minutes in our stores. Leather straps are the second most common and give the dress models their softness; rotate them in summer, and keep them away from water and perfume. Most watches list 50 metres of water resistance or close to it on their pages, which covers rain, washing your hands and a short swim in a pool. Never press the pushers of a chronograph under water.

## Questions and answers

### What is the difference between an automatic and a quartz Emporio Armani?

A quartz watch runs on a battery, is accurate to a few seconds a month and needs no attention apart from a new battery every 2 to 3 years. An automatic runs on a spring wound by your wrist, needs no battery, and can gain or lose a few seconds a day. Many people choose an automatic for the pleasure of the mechanism rather than for precision.

### Is a ceramic watch fragile?

Ceramic resists scratches much better than steel and keeps its colour, so in daily wear it looks new for longer. Its weakness is a hard impact: a fall onto a tiled floor can crack it, where steel would only dent. Treat a ceramic watch with the same care as fine glass, and take it off for sport or heavy work.

### Which size suits me, 41, 43 or 46 mm?

Measure your wrist. Under about 17 centimetres, 41 or 42 mm looks balanced; between 17 and 19 centimetres, 43 mm is the natural choice; above that, 44 to 46 mm will not look too large. A leather strap makes any size look a little smaller than a steel bracelet does, because it sits closer to the wrist.

### Does an Emporio Armani watch come with a warranty?

Each product page states whether the watch has a warranty, how long it is and who provides it, and the card in the box shows the same. A warranty covers the movement against manufacturing defects and needs the card and the invoice. Damage from knocks, a broken glass and water above the rating are not covered.
