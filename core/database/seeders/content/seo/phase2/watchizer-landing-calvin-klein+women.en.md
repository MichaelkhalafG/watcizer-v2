Calvin Klein is the brand for a woman who likes things simple and well made. Its bags and wallets share a short palette and a quiet logo, which makes it easy to build a small set where every piece goes with the others. This guide is about that set: how the bags and wallets pair up, which colours work together, and how to put together a look for work, weekends and evenings.

## What you will find here

Bags make up most of this page: **totes**, shoulder bags, a crossbody bag, a backpack and a toiletry bag, in neutral colour blocks or in black with the CK monogram. Three **wallets** complete them: two quilted zip-arounds, in black and in beige, and a long monogram wallet from Calvin Klein Jeans. The [women's Calvin Klein bags](/brand/calvin-klein/bags/women) page explains every bag shape.

## A palette that matches itself

The colours on this page belong together. **Vanilla**, caramel, khaki, brown and white sit in one warm, neutral family; black and the CK monogram in a cooler, city family. Pick one family and every bag and wallet you add will match the first, which is the simplest way to look put together without effort.

## Pairing a bag and a wallet

For a warm, neutral look, pair a vanilla and caramel bag with the **beige** quilted zip-around wallet. For a city look, pair a black monogram tote with the black **Re-Lock**. For something playful, the black and lemon Calvin Klein Jeans long wallet adds a bright flash of colour inside a black bag.

## The Calvin Klein Jeans long wallet

The long wallet from **Calvin Klein Jeans** has a vertical colour-block design in black and light lemon with the CK monogram, and is made of synthetic polyurethane, often called PU. Its long shape keeps notes flat and holds many cards, and the bright lemon makes it easy to find in a dark bag.

## A set for work

For a working day, carry a **tote** or the backpack, which hold a notebook, a water bottle and a long wallet. Keep the toiletry bag inside for small essentials. Choose black or the black and brown colour block for an office, where a calm, neat look matters most.

## A set for the weekend

For weekends and errands, switch to the embossed **crossbody** bag in vanilla, khaki and caramel, which keeps your hands free, with the compact beige wallet inside. The pair is light, practical and goes with denim, linen and white trainers.

## A set for the evening

For dinners and celebrations, choose a smaller bag with a little shine, the Calvin Klein Jeans **chain** shoulder bag or the Minimal Monogram flap bag with its silver-tone CK clasp. A slim wallet or a card holder is enough inside; leave the long wallet at home.

## Travelling with Calvin Klein

On a trip, the backpack carries the day's essentials and leaves your hands free at the airport, the **toiletry** bag keeps makeup and skincare together in your suitcase, and the zip-around wallet keeps cards and cash secure. Black pieces are the most practical for travel, as they hide marks.

## Building the set over time

You do not need to buy everything at once. Start with the piece you will use most, usually a **shoulder** bag or a tote, in the colour family you prefer. Add the matching wallet next, then a crossbody bag for weekends and, if you travel, the toiletry bag. Each new piece will go with the others because the palette stays the same.

## Quilting, monogram or plain

Quilting, as on the Re-Lock and the beige wallet, looks soft and dressy. The **CK monogram** is the most recognisable Calvin Klein pattern and looks confident on one piece at a time. Plain colour blocks are the quietest of all. Mixing one patterned piece with plain ones keeps a look balanced.

## Synthetic materials

Many pieces here are made of **synthetic** or PU leather: light, colourfast and easy to wipe clean. The product page names the material of each bag and wallet. Keep them in the shade and away from strong heat, and wipe them with a soft, slightly damp cloth, never with alcohol.

## Storing your set

Store bags stuffed with paper in their dust bags, so they keep their shape, and wallets in their **boxes**. Keep light vanilla, beige and white pieces away from dark denim, which can transfer dye, and never hang a heavy bag by its handles for weeks, which stretches them.
