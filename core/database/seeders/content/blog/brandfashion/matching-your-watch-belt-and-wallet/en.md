The watch is usually the first thing people notice on your wrist, but it rarely works alone. A belt, a wallet, a bag and your shoes all sit in the same picture. When they agree with each other, the whole outfit looks considered — even if it took you thirty seconds. When they clash, even a beautiful watch looks like an afterthought. The good news is that a few simple rules do almost all the work.

## Rule 1: match your leathers

If your watch is on a leather strap, let it lead the other leathers you wear.

- **Brown strap** — brown belt and brown shoes, in roughly the same shade. They do not have to be identical; a little difference in tone looks natural. What looks wrong is a brown strap with black shoes and a black belt.
- **Black strap** — black belt and black shoes. This is the most formal combination there is: suits, evening events, the office on an important day.
- **Tan, cognac or a lighter leather** — a relaxed, summery look. Pair it with lighter shoes, suede or canvas, and a belt in a similar warm tone.

Your wallet follows the same family when you can: it comes out at the till and on the table, and a matching leather finishes the picture. Browse our [belts](/subtypes/belts) and [wallets](/subtypes/wallets) by colour.

## Rule 2: match your metals

A steel bracelet or a metal case brings a metal colour into the outfit. Let the other metals follow it:

- **Silver-tone watch** — a silver-tone belt buckle, cufflinks and jewellery.
- **Gold-tone watch** — gold-tone buckle and accents.
- **Two-tone watch** — the easiest of all: it works with either, which makes it the most flexible choice if you wear both metals.

The belt buckle is the one people forget. A gold buckle under a silver watch is a small thing, but it is exactly the kind of small thing that makes an outfit look unplanned.

## Rule 3: decide how formal you are

Before you match anything, decide what the day is.

- **Formal** — a slim [dress watch](/subtypes/dress) on black or dark brown leather, a matching belt with a simple buckle, a slim wallet that does not spoil the line of a jacket. Keep everything quiet and in one colour family.
- **Smart casual** — a steel bracelet or brown leather, a belt in the same family, a wallet in leather or a smooth material. You have more room to play with colour here.
- **Casual** — a sporty watch, a rubber strap or a canvas strap, a belt with more texture, a cardholder. This is where a coloured strap or a bold dial can be the one thing that stands out.

Mixing levels is what usually goes wrong: a chunky sports watch with a dinner suit, or a delicate dress watch with gym clothes. Matching the formality matters more than matching the exact colour.

## Rule 4: let one piece stand out

Matching does not mean everything has to be the same. The best outfits have one piece that draws the eye and several that support it:

- If the watch has a bold dial or a bright strap, keep the belt and wallet simple.
- If you are carrying a statement bag, let the watch be classic.
- One pattern or one bright colour at a time is enough.

## The bag counts too

For anyone carrying a bag, it sits in the same frame as the watch. The same rules apply: leathers in the same family, metal hardware in the same tone as the watch, and one statement at a time. A structured leather bag pairs naturally with a dress watch; a softer or sportier bag with a casual one. See our [bags](/subtypes/bags).

## A quick reference

- **Black leather strap** — Belt: Black leather; Wallet: Black leather; Works for: Formal, evening
- **Brown leather strap** — Belt: Brown leather; Wallet: Brown leather; Works for: Office, smart casual
- **Steel bracelet, silver tone** — Belt: Black or brown, silver buckle; Wallet: Any leather; Works for: Almost everything
- **Gold-tone bracelet** — Belt: Brown or black, gold buckle; Wallet: Brown or black; Works for: Smart casual, evening
- **Rubber or canvas strap** — Belt: Canvas or casual leather; Wallet: Cardholder; Works for: Weekend, sport, summer

## Building a small, versatile set

You do not need a drawer full of accessories to get this right. A small set covers most of life:

1. One steel-bracelet watch that goes with everything.
2. One black and one brown leather belt, both with a simple silver-tone buckle.
3. One wallet in a neutral leather.

Add a dress watch on leather when you need one for formal occasions, and a casual or sporty watch for the weekend, and you have a complete wardrobe.
