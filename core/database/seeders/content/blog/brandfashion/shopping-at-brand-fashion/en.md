Before you order from a shop for the first time, you want to know the practical things: what they sell, how quickly it arrives, how you pay, and what happens if it is not right. This page answers all of it in one place, so you can shop with Brand Fashion knowing exactly what to expect.

## What we sell

Brand Fashion is a store for watches and the accessories that go with them. On the site you will find [watches](/category/watches) for men and women, and in [fashion](/category/fashion) our [bags](/subtypes/bags), [wallets](/subtypes/wallets), [belts](/subtypes/belts), [jewellery and bracelets](/subtypes/bracelets), perfumes and a small range of electronics. Everything is shown with its price, its details and its photos, and you can shop in Arabic or English.

## Original, and you can check it

Every watch we sell is original, and it comes with the evidence: a serial number and its stamps on the watch, the brand's own box and catalogue, a warranty card and a sales invoice. The warranty runs **up to 3 years, depending on the watch** — the length for each watch is on its product page — and covers the movement against manufacturing defects.

## Delivery: every governorate, within 24 hours

We deliver to **every governorate in Egypt within 24 hours**. The delivery cost depends on where you are and is shown in the cart before you pay, as soon as you choose your governorate. **Orders over EGP 10,000 ship free** — the cart tells you how much more you need to reach it.

## Payment: the way that suits you

**All payment methods and instalment programmes are available** — instalments help when your order is a bigger one. Whatever you choose, the total on the checkout page is the total you pay.

## Discount codes

From time to time we run offers with a discount code. Type it in the cart, and the cart shows the discount and the new total straight away. If a code cannot be used on your cart, the cart says why — for example, if it has expired or does not include the items you chose.

## Returns and exchanges: 14 days

If something is not right, you can **return or exchange it within 14 days**. The item must be in its original, unopened state, with its seals intact. Return shipping is paid by the customer; once the item has reached us and been inspected, the refund is made by the same payment method you used.

Our advice: if you are unsure about a size or a style, ask us before you order, or see it in store first. And keep the packaging closed until you are sure — that keeps the exchange open.

## After you buy: service and care

A watch bought from us stays looked after. Within the warranty period, **battery replacement and bracelet sizing are free** — bring the watch to either store with its warranty card and invoice. Other work, such as a repair outside the warranty or damage from an accident, is carried out for a fee. If you own a watch you did not buy from us, we can still look at it, for a fee that depends on the fault. Keep the warranty card and the invoice together and somewhere safe: they are what you will need, whether for service, for the warranty, or one day if you sell the watch.

## Our stores

Prefer to see it in person, try a watch on your wrist or compare two bags side by side? You are welcome at either of our stores, **every day from 10:00 to midnight**:

- **Arcadia Mall**, Corniche El Nil, Maspero extension, next to the Fairmont, second floor, Cairo.
- **Heliopolis**, 90 El-Thawra Street.

You can browse online, then come and see the piece before you decide — or the other way round.

## Before you order: a short checklist

1. Choose your item and check its details and warranty on the product page.
2. Add it to the cart and choose your governorate to see the delivery cost — or free shipping over EGP 10,000.
3. Enter a discount code if you have one.
4. Pay the way you prefer — all payment methods and instalment programmes are available.
5. Keep your confirmation e-mail and, when it arrives, the warranty card and invoice.
