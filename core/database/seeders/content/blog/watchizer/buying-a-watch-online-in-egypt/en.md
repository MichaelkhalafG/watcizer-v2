Buying a watch online saves you a trip, gives you a wider choice than any single shop window, and lets you compare at your own pace. It also asks you to trust a seller you cannot see. The good news is that a serious seller makes almost everything checkable before you pay. Here is what to look at, in the order that matters.

## 1. Is the watch original — and can the seller show it?

The single most important question. An original watch carries its own evidence, and the seller should be able to describe all of it before you order:

- **A serial number and stamps** on the case, cleanly engraved, that match the paperwork.
- **The brand's own box and catalogue** or instruction booklet, for that model.
- **A warranty card and a sales invoice**, with the warranty's length stated.

At Watchizer every watch is sold with its serial number, its brand box and catalogue, a warranty card and a sales invoice. If a seller cannot tell you what comes in the box, or will not give you an invoice, treat that as your answer.

## 2. Who stands behind the warranty, and for how long?

A warranty is only as useful as the people who honour it. Check three things: how long it lasts, what it covers, and who you go to when you need it.

Our warranty runs **up to 3 years, depending on the watch**, and covers the movement against manufacturing defects. The exact length is on each product page. Depending on the brand, it is our store's warranty or the warranty of the brand's official agent; the card that comes with the watch says which, and how to use it. Within the warranty, battery replacement and bracelet sizing are free.

## 3. The price: what you see should be what you pay

Before you order, make sure the price on the page is the full price of the watch, and check what delivery costs where you live. A sale price should be clearly shown against the regular price, not hidden in a code you only discover at checkout.

At Watchizer, delivery cost depends on your governorate and is shown before you place the order — and **orders over EGP 10,000 ship free**. The cart tells you how far you are from the threshold.

## 4. Delivery: how fast, and to where

A watch is a small, valuable parcel. Ask how long delivery takes and whether it reaches you. We deliver to **every governorate in Egypt within 24 hours**.

## 5. Payment: the methods you are comfortable with

You should be able to pay in the way that suits you. Watchizer accepts **all the usual payment methods, with instalments available through most banks** — useful when the watch is a bigger purchase. Whichever you choose, the total you are charged is the total shown on the checkout page.

## 6. Returns and exchanges: read the conditions before you need them

Every serious shop has a return policy; the details are what matter. At Watchizer you can **return or exchange a watch within 14 days**, provided it is in its original, unopened state with the seals intact. Return shipping is paid by the customer, and once the watch has been received and inspected, the refund goes back by the same payment method you used.

Knowing these conditions before you order means no surprises: if you are unsure about a model or a size, ask before you buy, or see it in store first.

An online shop should still be reachable by a human. Look for a phone number, a WhatsApp line and a physical address. ## A short checklist before you press "order"

1. The seller describes the serial number, the brand box and the catalogue.
2. A warranty card with a stated length, and a sales invoice, come with the watch.
3. The price, the delivery cost and any discount are clear before you pay.
4. Delivery reaches your governorate, and you know how long it takes.
5. You can pay the way you want, including instalments if you need them.
6. You have read the return and exchange conditions.
7. There is a real person to call.
